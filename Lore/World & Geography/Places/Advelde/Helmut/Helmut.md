- Hometown of [[Chein Po]] 
- To the Northeast of [[Dornthalm]] just at the foothills of the gray mountains
- Has a close-knit marketplace, while residences tended to be few and far between in the surrounding land.
	- Even the houses closest to the heart of the market of Helmut were a half-day's journey from the market

- Name is Germanic, meaning helmet, healthy, whole, and spirit
	- Named Helmut by a collective agreement of the representatives of the residents, as many of the families that settled there were hardy, resourceful, and thrived in times of hardship
