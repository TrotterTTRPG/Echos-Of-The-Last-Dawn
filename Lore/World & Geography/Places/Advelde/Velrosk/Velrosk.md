Located on the Western Coast of [[Advelde]]

Description: Velrosk is a bustling trade city built on stilts and canals. Ships from every corner of Advelde dock here, bringing spices, dyes, and rare woods from distant lands

Personality: Vibrant, chaotic, full of sailor's taverns, smugglers' guilds and lively markets. Music and festivals never seem to end, and locals embrace a "live for today" philosophy.
