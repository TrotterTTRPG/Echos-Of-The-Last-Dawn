Sister town of [[Solstice]].
Home to the [[Verdant Glade]], an ancient magical place where celebrations are performed every spring and autumn equinox to celebrate the seasons.
The structures here are buildings built out of the very trees themselves, where it is somewhat hard to discern where the forest and the settlement transition.
This place is home to many spring and autumn Eladrin, though other races do reside here as well, predominately other elves. This is a place more in tune with nature, and has a peaceful druidic calling to it.
