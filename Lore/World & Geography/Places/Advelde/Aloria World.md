A fantastical amusement park founded by the enigmatic [[Aloria]].

Currently run by [[Brumble]] since [[Aloria]] left.

[[Vivatrix]] worked there as a fortune teller. She believed that her fortune telling abilities were true (using a tarot deck), but she was really just guessing.
- She left after being disillusioned about [[Brumble]]'s greed and left to make her own fortune.
