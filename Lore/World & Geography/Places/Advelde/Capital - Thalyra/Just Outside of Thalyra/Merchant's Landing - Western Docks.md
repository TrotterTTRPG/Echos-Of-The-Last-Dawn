**Location:** West of [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Trade & Merchant Shipping

The Western Docks serve as the ports for ships heading to [[Velrosk]].
It's used mainly by merchant vessels bringing supplies between [[Velrosk]] & [[Thalyra]]

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Long, sturdy piers with wide gangways for cargo handling.
- Large warehouses and storage buildings line the shore, painted in muted colors to avoid distracting ships’ crews.
- Cranes, pulleys, and small carts for moving goods between ships and warehouses.
- The air smells of salt, tar, and packed spices or grain.
- Crowded and bustling during daylight hours; quieter at night except for overnight guards.

**Traffic & Ships**
- Mainly merchant vessels traveling between [[Velrosk]] and [[Thalyra]].
- Cargo includes imported goods, raw materials, and artisan supplies.
- Occasional smaller passenger or courier vessels, but trade dominates.

**Staff & Security**
- Dockmasters oversee loading/unloading schedules.
- Workers include stevedores, warehouse clerks, and gang laborers.
- Light guard presence — mostly [[Thalyra]] city guards ([[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]]) or contracted security to prevent theft and maintain order.

**Reputation & Role**
- Efficient and practical — designed for moving goods, not luxury or leisure.
- Vital for supplying the city and maintaining trade connections with Velrosk.
