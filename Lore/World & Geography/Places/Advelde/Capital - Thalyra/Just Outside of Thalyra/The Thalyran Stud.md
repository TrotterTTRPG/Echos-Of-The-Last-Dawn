To the West of [[Thalyra]]
Owned by [[Caleb Whitmere]] and daughter [[Lydia Whitmere]]

**Layout & Features**
- *The Entry Road & Front Paddocks*
	- A dirt road leads south, southwest from the western gate of [[Thalyra]] to a stone archway marked with a carved sun and horse’s head — the symbol of the Stud. Beyond it lies a broad open yard bordered by smaller paddocks, where horses available for sale or rent graze and stretch.
	- Visitors are greeted by a small stablehouse — half shop, half office — where a stablehand or apprentice greets travelers, records transactions, and arranges rentals.
	- The front paddocks each hold a handful of sleek, well-groomed riding horses, often of varied coloration and temperament to suit different riders.
	- A covered hitching area provides shade for visiting wagons or riders trying out mounts.
- *The Public Barn*
	- A medium-sized barn sits near the paddocks, freshly painted and neatly maintained. Inside are:
	- Stalls for horses awaiting sale or temporary boarding
	- A small tack room for fitting saddles and bridles
	- An office loft above where the Master of Stables keeps meticulous records of lineage, temperament, and sale prices.
	- It’s a busy, approachable place — clean, well-organized, and faintly perfumed with hay and oil.
- *The Inner Grounds (Private Area)*
	- A sturdy wooden fence and locked gate separate the public paddocks from the main training and breeding grounds farther back. Only employees and apprentices are allowed beyond this point.
	- Here, the operation becomes much larger:
		- The main barn is an impressive structure — long, low, and built from dark timber, designed to hold mares, foals, and stallions.
		- Several training rings and fenced lanes are scattered nearby, where handlers walk young horses or run them through basic riding drills.
		- Pastures stretch westward from here, dotted with watering troughs, shade trees, and low stone walls dividing grazing zones.
	- At dawn and dusk, the sound of galloping hooves can often be heard from the city walls.
- *Storehouse*
	- A nearby storehouse holds hay, grain, and blacksmithing supplies for shoeing and repairs.
	- The workers tend to be locals from the Bronze or Silver Districts, often families that have passed the trade down for generations.

**Management & Operations**
- *Master of Stables*: Oversees the farm’s daily operations and negotiates directly with [[Thalyra]]’s merchants, couriers, and noble households.
- *Stablehands & Apprentices*: Handle feeding, cleaning, and early training; some specialize in foal care or shoeing.
- *Traders & Couriers*: Frequently stop by to rent horses for trips between [[Thalyra]] and neighboring settlements.
- Prices are fair, but the Stud's reputation rests on trust: if a horse is rented here, it’s known to be sound and steady.
