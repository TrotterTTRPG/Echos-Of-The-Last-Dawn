**Location:** East of [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Passenger & Luxury Shipping, Some Trade

The Eastern Docks serve as the ports for ships heading to [[Iridia]] and [[Dornthalm]].
It's used in equal part by ships bring supplies to those locales and nobles looking to vacation in [[Iridia]].

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Piers built with polished wood and brass fittings, giving a refined, welcoming look.
- Smaller warehouses for high-value or delicate goods, often owned privately by noble shipping companies.
- Waiting pavilions, shaded seating areas, and small inns or cafes for travelers.
- Less chaotic than the eastern docks — quieter, more orderly, and more scenic.

**Traffic & Ships**
- Passenger ships for travel to [[Iridia]], [[Dornthalm]], or other nearby cities.
- Ships owned by nobles or wealthy merchants, often outfitted for comfort.
- Some trade ships destined for [[Dornthalm]]

**Staff & Security**
- Dockmasters coordinate arrivals and departures, sometimes working directly for noble families.
- Crew includes stewards, porters, and attendants trained in luxury service.
- Security is discreet but effective, including city guards and private mercenaries for noble passengers.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as the gateway for [[Thalyra]]’s elite travelers.
- A hub for nobles, diplomats, and adventurers traveling in comfort.
- Symbolizes prestige and refinement compared to the utilitarian eastern docks.
