**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Functional inn for traders and traveling professionals

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Modest, clean rooms
- A small stable out back for horses and carts
- Private meeting rooms for trade discussions.

**Clientele:**
- Visiting merchants
- Couriers
- Adventurers delivering goods.

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine).

**Staff:** Managed by [[Tomas Veyne]], human businessman, with several assistants who handle stables and logistics.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Convenient
- Practical
- Reliable
- Rarely crowded
- Focused on service and utility rather than charm.
