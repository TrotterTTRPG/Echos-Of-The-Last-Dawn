Location:  Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
Type: Public Park (Modest, Peaceful, Underused)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- A small but well-maintained park tucked between trade offices and townhouse rows.
- Defined by gentle rolling lawns, neat gravel paths, and a few marble benches engraved with merchant guild crests.
- Centered around a simple silver-and-stone sundial, often used as a meeting marker but rarely crowded.
- The faint smell of polished metal and perfumed ink lingers from nearby artisan workshops.
- Quiet at nearly all hours — birdsong and the rustle of leaves are usually the only sounds.

**Features**
- A reflecting pond ringed with lilies and trimmed shrubs, occasionally catching coin tosses from passersby making small wishes.
- Covered pergolas provide shade but are rarely occupied; a few wandering artists sometimes sketch or write there.
- Notice board occasionally holds merchant announcements, calls for apprentices, or small postings from the Adventurers’ Docket, though few stop to read them.
- Pathway lighting glows with soft silver light after dusk, making it a calm but somewhat lonely nighttime walk.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as “the quiet heart of commerce” — lovely, calm, and usually empty.
- A favorite resting spot for travelers, couriers, and scribes passing through between errands.
- Locals see it as a symbol of the Silver District’s nature — polished, practical, and quietly prosperous, but never indulgent.
