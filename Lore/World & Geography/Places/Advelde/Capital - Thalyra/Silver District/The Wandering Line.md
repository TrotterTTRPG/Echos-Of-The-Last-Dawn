District: Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
Type: Cartographer’s Shop

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- A tidy, well-lit storefront. Its gilded sign depicts a quill drawing a winding golden line across a parchment.
- The air smells faintly of parchment, ink, and candle wax.
- The walls are lined with framed maps — detailed renderings of [[Advelde]]’s regions, including [[Thalyra]], [[Dornthalm]], [[Velrosk]], and [[Iridia]] - each signed by the shop’s elusive cartographer.
- Central display tables hold rolled maps in leather tubes, compass sets, and blank journals.
- A large back wall bears an enormous map of [[Advelde]] pinned to corkboard, with colored strings marking trade routes and new discoveries.
- The front counter is cluttered with receipts, a perpetually steaming teapot, and a cashbox that squeaks when opened.

Staff
- *Owner (Shopkeeper)*: [[Dorian Pellwater]] - middle-aged human man with kind eyes and a perpetually bewildered expression. Wears a vest with ink stains he doesn’t remember getting.
	- Doesn’t know much about maps but talks about his wife’s talent with pride and love.
	- Known for saying things like, “I couldn’t tell you where it is on a map, but my wife could draw it from memory.”
- *Cartographer (Absent but Renowned)*: Elira Pellwater — his wife, a renowned human cartographer rarely in town. Travels [[Advelde]] to chart new roads, rivers, ruins, and borders. Sends back new works via courier every few weeks.

Reputation & Role
Known for the most accurate and artistic maps in Advelde — prized by merchants, scholars, and adventurers alike.
Customers often come more for Elira’s reputation than for Dorian’s expertise.
The shop serves as a quiet meeting place for travelers and scholars, who sometimes leave notes or pins on the large Advelde map marking their journeys.
Dorian occasionally commissions adventurers to escort Elira or deliver messages and supplies to her while she’s in the field.
