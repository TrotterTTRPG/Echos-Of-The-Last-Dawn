**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Hard Liquor / Distillery

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Polished brick and timber building with copper stills visible through windows.
- Subtle aroma of grains, sugar, and barrel-aged spirits.
- Sleek tasting room with wooden counters, glass decanters, and small tables.
- Mid-level sophistication — functional but appealing to those with refined tastes.

**Note:** Many Inns and Taverns will distill their own liquor, but they often supplement with this well-known type.

**Staff**
- Master Distiller: [[Murmur]], Kenku man, precise and skilled in flavor and aging.
- Assistants: Technicians, samplers, and attendants for tasting room service.

**Specialties & Services**
- Whiskey, rum, brandy, and specialty liqueurs.
- Limited edition barrels or seasonal flavors sold directly in the shop.
- Occasionally provides small batches for merchants, inns, or private events.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known for quality spirits that are strong, smooth, and reliably crafted.
- Appeals to middle-class and wealthy clients in the Silver District.
- Respected for skill, quality, and modest elegance in presentation.
