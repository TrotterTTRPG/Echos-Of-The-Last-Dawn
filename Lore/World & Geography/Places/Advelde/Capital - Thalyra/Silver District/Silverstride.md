**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Cobblers / Fancier Footwear

**Note:** These shoes are also displayed at [[The Silken Crown]] to be purchased alongside clothing - sort of like a consignment type situation.

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Sleek, polished interior with display cases holding fine leather shoes, boots, and slippers.
- Fitting area with plush carpets, mirrors, and soft lighting.
- Workbench tucked away behind a velvet curtain for custom creations.
- Subtle decorative touches — silver trim, engraved wooden shelves, and small potted plants — signal elegance.

**Staff**
- Owner/Tailor: [[Elyssa Stormcloak]], half-orc craftswoman renowned for stylish, refined designs who doesn't really want the recognition or renown
- Assistants: Skilled shoemakers who specialize in decorative stitching, delicate leatherwork, and tailored fits.

**Reputation & Role**
- Specializes in fashionable, stylish shoes for merchants, minor nobles, and middle-class elites.
- Known for elegant designs, comfort, and quality craftsmanship.
- Often commissions custom shoes for formal events, business appearances, or important travel.
