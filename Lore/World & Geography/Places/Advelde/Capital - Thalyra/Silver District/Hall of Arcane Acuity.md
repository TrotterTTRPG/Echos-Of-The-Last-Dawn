In [[Thalyra]]
Connected to [[The Crucible of Crowns]]

A research laboratory conducting studies on monsters, trying to learn what they can about the arcane from them as they seem to be 
inherently arcane in nature. 

Mostly supplied by monster hunters, the monsters are studied in a variety of manner. 
They are dissected, observed, eaten and processed.
Some of this research is to gain detailed knowledge of their capabilities, and the design of strategy to counter these abilities.
[[The Crucible of Crowns]] is connected to their monster pits.

**Exterior Design**
- *Architecture Style*
	- Constructed in the same elegant, Elven-inspired style seen throughout [[Thalyra]]  - graceful lines, pale stone, flowering metalwork—but noticeably heavier in profile.
	- The building appears smaller than expected, barely larger than a manor house.
	- Instead of delicate curves and open façades, the Hall embraces straight lines, buttressed walls, and narrow, arched windows that give little view inside.
- *Materials & Colors*
	- Thick stone walls of moon-pale granite shot with silver veining, subtly warded.
	- Minimal glass—only a few slitted windows set high on the façade.
	- Silver filigree or sigils are etched into the stone, not ornamental but functional—designed to suppress or dampen magical energies.
- *Entrance*
	- A single, wide archway recessed deep into the wall, framed with runes.
	- 1ft thick doors reinforced with wrought metal bands.
	- The doors rarely stand fully open; visitors must be admitted intentionally.
	- The entrance has a small metal sign on the front: "By appointment only" with no notes on how to acquire said appointment

**Hidden Depths** - Although the ground-level structure occupies little space, the true size of the Hall lies underground.
Subterranean Levels
- **Level 1** — Administrative & Research
	- Offices
	- Labs
	- Containment observation rooms
- **Level 2** — Experimental Chambers
	- Enchanted laboratories
	- Dissection/analysis rooms
	- Magic-dampening test facilities
- **Level 3** — Monster Quarries
	- Massive reinforced pens
	- Elemental containment cells
	- Transport tunnels leading directly to the arena connection
- **Level 4** — Deep Vault
	- Long-term containment for dangerous beings
	- Arcane battery chambers—backup energy sources
	- Sealed lockdown corridors
- Each level expands laterally well beyond the footprint of the surface structure, creating a secretive web of research and containment beneath [[Thalyra]]’s streets.

**Connection to [[The Crucible of Crowns]]**
- An underground passage connects the monster pens to the older arena.
- When a creature is cleared for combat or disposal, it can be:
	- Teleported to the arena floor; or
	- Moved through reinforced tunnelways accompanied by specialized handlers.
- This maintains safety and limits public knowledge of what’s being stored below.

**Atmosphere**
- Inside, the upper floors feel academic: quiet, sterile, clinical.
- The deeper levels grow more mechanical, warded, and eerie—glowing sigils on the stone, low thrumming of magical dampers, muffled roars or noises far below.
- Visitors describe the feeling: *“Like stepping somewhere you are not meant to be.”*
