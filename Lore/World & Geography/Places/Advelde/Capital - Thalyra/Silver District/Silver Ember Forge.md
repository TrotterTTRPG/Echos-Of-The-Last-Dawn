**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Specialty:** Custom craftsmanship, ornamental weapons, experimental materials

Atmosphere & Structure
- The front room doubles as a showroom, displaying completed works and prototypes.
- Behind that lies the workshop, where magic-infused bellows and crystal-forges hum softly.
- The air smells faintly of ozone and lavender oil — not soot.
- Arcane inscriptions line the walls, powering both ventilation and enchantment stabilizers.

**Owner & Staff**
- Master Artificer: [[Erevan Korynth]], a silver-haired high elf with delicate hands and an ego to match. He views smithing as both science and symphony.
- Assistant: [[Miri Flint]], a half-orc metalworker who handles practical commissions (and often saves Erevan from overpromising).

**Reputation & Role**
- The Silver Ember is known for finesse and innovation. Their commissions are costly but unmatched in craftsmanship. The wealthy commission dueling swords, spell foci, or family heirlooms here — and ambitious adventurers often come hoping for that one perfect weapon to define their legend.
