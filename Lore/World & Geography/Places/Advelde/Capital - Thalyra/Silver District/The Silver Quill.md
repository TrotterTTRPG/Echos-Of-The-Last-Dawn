**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Mid-tier inn with artistic touches

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Crisp, airy rooms
- Polished floors
- A small reading lounge with books, maps, and a chess set.

**Clientele:**
- Merchants
- Minor nobles
- Traveling scholars

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine).

**Staff:** Owned by [[Feylen Merrith]], elven innkeeper, assisted by humans and halflings.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Quiet, refined, but not ostentatious.
- Popular for conducting informal business meetings or study breaks.
