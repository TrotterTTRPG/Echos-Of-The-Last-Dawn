(Aroma Joes)

**Location**: Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Reputation**: Sleek, modern, and quickly becoming the go-to for anyone trying to look like they have taste.
**Description**:
- A sign in looping script announces Fragrant Jones, the Silver District’s latest import.
- The space smells of bright citrus and roasted beans, with sunlight filtering through enchanted glass that shifts color with the hour.
- Every countertop gleams, every barista wears a smart uniform, and the menu changes weekly with flavor fads sweeping the upper wards.

Owned by [[Junis Vale]], a half-elf entrepreneur with an eye for aesthetics and a gift for marketing, Fragrant Jones has quickly become a hit with students, junior researchers, and Silver District trend-chasers. Some say it’s trying too hard to be “artisan.” Others love that it’s trying at all.

Clientele: Apprentices, mid-tier merchants, and mid-level bureaucrats - people with aspirations and an image to maintain.

Signature Item: The Silver Drizzle — espresso blended with frothed oatmilk and topped with luminescent syrup that faintly glows in dim light.

Vibe: Polished, aromatic, and ever-so-slightly pretentious — perfect for posting up with a tome and pretending to study.
