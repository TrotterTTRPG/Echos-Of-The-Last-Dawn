**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Trade / Merchant School

**Focus:**
- Any age; teaches business, trade laws, bookkeeping, navigation, and merchant networks.
- Field trips to markets, guilds, and trade hubs in [[Thalyra]] and beyond.

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Classrooms with desks, ledgers, maps of trade routes, and product samples.
- Offices for simulations of negotiations and trade agreements.
- Modest but efficient; bustling with students and merchants.

**Staff:**
- Experienced merchants, clerks, and trade guild members as instructors.
- Administrators managing classes and apprenticeships.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Produces skilled merchants and trade specialists.
- Known for practicality, connections, and real-world training.
