**Location:** Silver District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Jewelry & Gemstone Boutique

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Elegant storefront with polished silver and glass displays showcasing fine jewelry, rare gems, and precious metals.
- Soft lighting highlights the sparkle of rings, necklaces, tiaras, and brooches.
- Private viewing rooms for high-value or rented pieces, with velvet-lined display cases and subtle security measures.
- The air smells faintly of polished wood and a hint of incense, creating a refined, luxurious atmosphere.

**Staff**
- Owner/Jeweler: [[Marcel Durelle]], half-elf man known for impeccable taste and eye for quality.
- Attendants: Trained in customer service, knowledgeable about gemstones, and discreet with high-profile clients.
- Security & Support Staff: Hidden guards and clerks to handle inventory, appraisals, and the jewel rental program.

**Note:** [[Marcel Durelle]] has faced a lot of pressure to move this store to the Gold District in [[Thalyra]], but he refuses. He's happy where he is and he believes that the gems, metals, and jewelry are for everyone who can afford it regardless of where they live.

**Specialties & Services**
- Fine jewelry, precious metals, and quality gemstones for purchase.
- Custom jewelry design and repair services.
- Noble Rental Program: Allows wealthy patrons to rent exquisite jewelry for events without purchasing. Pieces are insured and carefully tracked.
- Appraisals, cleaning, and secure storage for high-value items.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as the best source for high-quality jewelry in the Silver District.
- Attracts merchants, minor nobles, and adventurers seeking elegant gifts or event-ready adornments.
- The rental program adds convenience for elite patrons, making Glimmerhold both prestigious and practical.
- Trusted for discretion, craftsmanship, and quality — a symbol of taste and refinement.
