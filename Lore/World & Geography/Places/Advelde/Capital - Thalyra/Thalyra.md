Located in the fertile green plains in the Southeast of [[Advelde]]

The land was fertile and the rivers abundant, so it's no surprise that this is where 'The Capital City' rests.

**Description**: Thalyra is a jewel of the Gilded Age: sprawling gardens, gleaming marble towers, and wide boulevards lines with flowing trees. It's heart is the Hall of Seasons, where rulers meet beneath a vast stained-glass done depicting the cycles of the year. 

**Personality**: Cosmopolitan, cultural hub, home to philosophers, artisans and wealthy merchant guilds

**Population**: Approximately 38,000

**Government**: Many factions struggle for control of the city, but a consortium of wealthy merchants and a court of aristocrats make up the most powerful

Meetings of government officials occur in the [[Hall of Seasons]] within the Gold District

There are 3 districts in concentric rings. The Bronze District is the outer most ring, next is the Silver District, and the innermost is the Gold District.
