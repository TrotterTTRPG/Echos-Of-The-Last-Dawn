The Criminal Underground in Thalyra is fairly new, but like much of the city, is mostly derived from elsewhere.
There are facets of the major criminal groups found in the other cities here, and they all vie for power.

Because there is a very strong law and order force in [[Thalyra]] - the criminal networks here are extra cautious, and tend to keep much lower numbers than they do elsewhere. 

Because of the harsh environment for criminals, it has breed an odd combination of cooperation, and ruthlessness between the different criminal organizations.

One of the larger groups is the [[Tharvayne Family]]. They are a family that has long been the secret arm of the [[Calhoune Family]] in Thalyra.
The [[Calhoune Family]] has long siphoned off funds to have deeds done off the books and in the shadows.

[[Reginald Tharvayne]] is on the perimeter of this criminal family, and has sought to heighten himself in the government, as well as in the family.

[[The Moonhelm Society]] has a strong presence in the city. They are generally more focused on humanitarian goals than crime, but that element is present as well.

[[Faris Harith]] wore something that looks like a family crest. The crest is gold on the left, and blue on the right. In the gold, a knight stands under the sun in a protective stance with sword drawn, and in the blue, a pale moon lights an empty knights helm with a sapling sprouting from the faceguard.

This is the symbol of [[The Moonhelm Society]] also know as the [[The Moonhelm Family]]
