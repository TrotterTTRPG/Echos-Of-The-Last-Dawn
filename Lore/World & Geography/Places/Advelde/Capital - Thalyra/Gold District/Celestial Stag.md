**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Reputation:** Prestigious, serene, and the benchmark for quality coffee and conversation.  
**Description:**  
- Celestial Stag stands like a shrine to refinement - a tall, stone building with star-patterned windows and brass fixtures that catch the light like constellations.
- Inside, soft music plays under the murmur of quiet conversation.
- The air smells faintly of roasted moonseed and sweet incense.
- A massive mural of a celestial stag dominates the back wall, its painted eyes seeming to follow you with calm benevolence.

Owned by Elarion Vos, a silver-haired Aasimar who treats coffee-making as a sacred art, the Stag prides itself on excellence and atmosphere. Patrons come here not just to drink, but to _be seen drinking_.

**Clientele:** Diplomats, wealthy merchants, visiting scholars, and the occasional adventurer who’s finally earned enough to splurge. Even those from the lower wards make the pilgrimage occasionally — just to say they’ve had a cup here.

**Signature Item:** _The Constellation Brew_ — a velvety blend that shimmers faintly like starlight on water, served in porcelain cups painted with constellations unique to each patron’s birthdate.

**Vibe:** Calm, expensive, and quietly proud — a temple to caffeine and self-importance.
