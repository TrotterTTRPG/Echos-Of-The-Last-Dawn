In Gold District of [[Thalyra]]

Where the head of [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] resides 

**Exterior**
- Two statues flank the doorway - one of a guard standing at ease, the other with sword drawn and shield raised. Together, they symbolize peace and vigilance in balance.
- The front doors are crafted of oak reinforced with brass bands, engraved with the emblem of Thalyra: a silver sunburst crossed by a sword.
- A flagstone courtyard surrounds the Hall, edged with neatly trimmed hedges and benches where off-duty guards or petitioners often wait.
- Two ceremonial guards always stand watch, dressed in polished bronze breastplates and blue-and-gold sashes rather than full armor - more for formality than function.

**Interior**
- *Main Foyer:*
	- Upon entering, visitors find a high-ceilinged atrium lit by sunlight pouring through a circular skylight. Murals along the walls depict the founding of [[Thalyra]] and the early defenders who kept peace during the city’s growth. There’s a calm dignity to the space — no ostentation, but every detail is deliberate.
	- A wide staircase leads to the Captain’s offices above, while corridors on either side lead to administrative chambers and meeting rooms. The air smells faintly of parchment, oil, and polished bronze.
- *The Hall of Inquiry (Ground Floor):*
	- A public chamber where citizens and nobles alike can bring complaints, reports, or petitions. A desk of polished walnut divides the room, with clerks handling paperwork while a lieutenant listens to concerns.
- *Captain’s Office (Second Floor):*
	- The Captain of the Guard maintains a commanding view of the Gold District through tall arched windows. The office is organized and warm, with dark wood furnishings, maps of [[Thalyra]] marked with patrol routes, and an elegant sword displayed on the wall behind the desk — a ceremonial weapon awarded for service.
	- A small adjoining room serves as a private conference space for strategic meetings or discussions with city officials. Despite its location among the elite, the Captain’s quarters are humble — a deliberate reminder that their duty is to serve the city, not rule it.
- *Lower Level (Restricted Access):*
	- A reinforced cellar contains records and sealed evidence, guarded by enchantments to deter tampering.
