Holds branches of the government in [[Thalyra]].
The upper house, known as [[The Gems of the Crown]], and the lower house, known as [[The Gold of the Crown]]

[[The Gold of the Crown]] (lower house) is comprised of 30 members, 10 from each district. These members are voted into their positions. They hold their positions indefinitely, unless there are motions to remove or replace them. Most elected officials quit before they are removed. The Bronze District has the most turnover

[[The Gems of the Crown]] (upper house) has five seats, which are determined by the lower house, through a secret voting process. They handle the distribution of government funds, and as such can create or kill any city initiative.

[[The Gold of the Crown]] is tasked with carrying out will of the upper house.
