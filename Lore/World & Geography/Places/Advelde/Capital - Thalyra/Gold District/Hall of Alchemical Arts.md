**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Alchemy Research & Potion-Making Institution

The beating heart of Thalyra’s alchemical innovation, the Hall of Alchemical Arts hums with the sounds of bubbling glass and faint explosions. The alchemists of its namesake department labor to refine transmutation, distillation, and the interplay of arcane reagents — bridging the gap between science and sorcery.  
Its name is a reminder that creation is as much art as it is science — and that even beauty can be volatile when bottled.

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Building is two stories, with the front resembling a refined storefront but the bulk of the structure houses labs, workshops, and storage.
	- *Ground Floor:* storefront and small teaching / demonstration area.
	- *Second Floor:* labs for apprentices, specialized brewing chambers, and storage for ingredients.
- Architecture leans practical but elegant, with polished stone floors, brass piping, and glass skylights over the main labs to allow natural light.
- Secret or semi-hidden spaces: small private lab for the Head Alchemist for forbidden or highly dangerous brews.
- Constant aroma of herbs, metals, and brewing reagents, with occasional smoke or minor magical residue.

**Staff**
- *Head Alchemist:* [[Belvyre Flintflower]], a female halfling with brown curly hair she wears in pigtails/space buns. She always has massive goggle on her head. She's missing a few teeth of past failures and is always covered in a layer of some kind of colored powder.
- *Apprentices:* Several young alchemists capable of producing basic potions reliably, e.g., minor healing, antidotes, minor buffs.
- *Security & Wards:* Standard magical protections on rare or dangerous ingredients; most conflicts with Arcane Acuity handled politically rather than magically.

**Specialties & Services**
- Basic potion production: apprentices can produce mundane or simple potions en masse.
- Advanced potions: only Head Alchemist can brew complex or rare potions; preparation is time-consuming and resource-intensive.
- Research: experimentation on combining ingredients, rare reagents, and refining brewing techniques.
- Storefront & Sales: small selection of pre-made potions available for adventurers, mostly basic or mid-level; rare brews usually by appointment or private negotiation.
- Back-alley deals: occasional secret transactions with adventurers or other parties to bypass Department of Arcane Acuity’s control over rare ingredients.

**Internal Politics / Reputation**
- *Conflict with Department of Arcane Acuity at the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]]:* the Hall of Alchemical Arts believes they should handle dangerous or exotic materials first, but are often given only leftovers (“dregs”) after Arcane Acuity experiments.
- *Head Alchemist’s reputation*: renowned for skill, extremely disciplined, and highly respected in [[Dornthalm]] and beyond. Keeps apprentices capable but limits what outsiders can access.
