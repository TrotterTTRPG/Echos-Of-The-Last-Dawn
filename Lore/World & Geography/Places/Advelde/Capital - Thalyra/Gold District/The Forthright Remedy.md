**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Luxury Infirmary / Housecall Clinic

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Small, elegant storefront selling medical supplies, rare herbs, and minor potions.
- Actual treatment happens primarily via house calls to private residences of nobles, merchants, or influential adventurers.
- Front of shop features a polished reception and display of luxury healing items; back storage has portable medical kits for traveling staff.

**Staff**
- *Head Healer:* [[Selene Veyrin]], Triton druid with a no-nonsense manner that many find comforting in times of crisis or uncertainty.
- *Store Attendant:* [[Seraphina Windstrum]], an Owlin Bard known for her calm agreeable voice and her spirituality (I'm thinking like the type of fake spiritual that you might find at a crystal shop in a wealthy city)

**Specialties & Services**
- Tailored, discreet healing for wealthy patrons in their own homes.
- Minor magical healing, potions, and luxury remedies.
- Occasional preventative treatments or check-ups for elite families.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known for privacy, exclusivity, and high-quality care.
- Used primarily by nobles, diplomats, and affluent adventurers.
- Seen as the gold standard for personal, discreet healthcare — a mark of prestige.
