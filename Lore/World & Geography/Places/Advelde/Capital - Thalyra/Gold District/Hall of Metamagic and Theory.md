**Location**: Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Arcane Research Institution / Magical Shopfront

Within these austere walls, scholars of the Department of Metamagic and Theory unravel the principles that govern spellcraft itself. Here, they experiment with the structure of magic — bending its shape, testing its boundaries, and redefining how arcana interacts with the world.  
Its name speaks to the pursuit of understanding _how_ and _why_ magic works, rather than merely wielding it — the study of the art behind the art.

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Building is a two-story, squat, square building that takes up quit a bit of room. It's design is more Roman will white columns and filigree that span it's entire height. 
- Each floor serves a different purpose
	- **Ground Floor** — Public and Semi-Public Areas
		1. *Magical Shopfront*
			- Visible from the street, with display windows of spell scrolls and minor magical items.
			- Small counter area with knowledgeable researchers. Everyone has to take a shift working in the shop, so you'll often find them reading/studying and slightly distracted.
			- Some shelves behind the counter with securely locked rarer items.
			- Atmosphere: polished marble floors, soft ambient magical light, quiet hum of wards.
			- Purpose: fund research and provide an approachable “face” of the institution to the public.
		2. *Meeting Rooms*
			- One or two small meeting rooms for visiting scholars, adventurers, or donors.
			- Equipped with tables, chairs, and magically-enhanced writing materials.
			- Can be used for consultations, negotiations for artifact loans, or small lectures.
			- Atmosphere: formal but welcoming, with carved panels depicting historical magical discoveries.
		3. *. Offices*
			- Head Magister’s office (I[[lyana Corvess]]) and several researcher offices.
			- Decorated with personal touches: arcane diagrams, globes of magical energy, and small trophies from past experiments.
			- Offices include secure filing areas for research notes, rare manuscripts, and artifact logs.
		4. *Library*
			- Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves of ancient tomes, spellbooks, and maps.
			- Reading tables with enchanted lights and minor wards to prevent magic leaks.
			- Atmosphere: warm and hushed, smells of parchment, ink, and faint traces of magical residue.
			- Note:  Most books, scrolls, and tomes here are copies of ones that exist in [[Dornthalm]]. Some are unique to [[Thalyra]], though - such as books written by [[Thalyra]]n citizens or books about [[Thalyra]]
	- **Second Floor** — Research and Specialized Areas
		1. *General Research Labs*
			- Open areas with benches, arcane apparatus, and containment circles.
			- Used for experimentation with new spells, magical theory, and artifact study.
			- Small safety wards and containment bubbles to prevent accidental magical mishaps.
			- Atmosphere: faint hum of arcane energy, occasional flashes of experimental magic.
		2. *Cursed Artifact Containment Wing*
			- Secured rooms behind heavy magical and physical locks.
			- Each cursed item contained in warded cabinets or magical containment fields.
			- Signage and subtle magical warnings indicate the nature of danger.
			- Staff use special keys, gloves, or protective spells to handle these items.
			- Atmosphere: tense and eerie, dim lighting, soft alarms in case wards fail.
		3. *Small Seminar/Instruction Room*
			- For teaching apprentices, holding staff meetings, or reviewing experiments.
			- Contains tables, enchanted chalkboards, and floating arcane displays.
			- Can double as a place to showcase progress on spell research for patrons or donors.
		4. *Private Offices / Labs for Senior Researchers*
			- A few smaller, private labs where lead researchers work on sensitive projects.
			- Equipped with customized containment circles, personal libraries, and experimental apparatus.
			- Atmosphere: highly focused, quiet, and controlled; a place for meticulous work without distractions.

**Staff**
- Head Magister: [[Ilyana Corvess]], elven wizard specializing in metamagic and spellcraft theory.
- Researchers: A mix of wizards, artificers, and arcanists, some apprenticed, some full members.
- Store Attendants: Knowledgeable staff who manage sales, inventory, and occasionally give minor guidance to customers.
- Security: Wards and enchanted locks protect experimental areas and cursed items.

**Specialties & Services**
- *Primary goal:* discovering and creating new spells. Current focus is on Comprehend Languages for Old Languages.
- Study and catalog magical artifacts, including research on cursed items.
- Provides spell scrolls and minor magical items for purchase to fund research.
- Offers consultations or demonstrations for visiting scholars, adventurers, and mages.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as [[Thalyra]]’s foremost experimental magic hub.
- A place where adventurers may bring artifacts or rare spell knowledge.
- Reputable for innovation and expertise, though some locals find the building slightly intimidating or uninviting due to its secretive nature.
- Balances research and commerce: magical sales fund the ongoing development of new spells and artifact studies.
