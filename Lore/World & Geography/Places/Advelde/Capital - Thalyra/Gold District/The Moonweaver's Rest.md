**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Boutique inn and tavern (discreet, some rumors of a brothel)

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Small but exquisitely decorated common room with soft, enchanted lighting and plush seating.
- Serene courtyard featuring rare flora and subtle fountains, perfect for quiet conversations.
- Private rooms are elegantly appointed; some can be discreetly rented for more intimate purposes.
- Patrons rarely see the full extent of the inn — many areas are behind hidden doors, curtained alcoves, or stairways that only insiders know about.

**Clientele:**
- Nobles, wealthy merchants, elite adventurers, and influential figures.
- Some patrons visit specifically for the rumored “discreet companionship” the inn quietly provides — though nothing is overtly advertised.

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine).

**Staff:**
- Managed by [[Selene Arvani]], elven woman known for discretion and charm.
- Staff includes attendants trained in etiquette, security, and discretion; some specialize in providing companionship or entertainment for select guests.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Highly exclusive, offering privacy, luxury, and discretion above all.
- Known among [[Thalyra]]’s elite as a place to conduct business, relax, or indulge in pleasures quietly.
- Patrons who know the inn’s secrets speak of it in hushed tones — the line between tavern, inn, and brothel is intentionally blurred, giving it an aura of mystery.
