**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Fine art gallery and cultural salon

**Atmosphere & Layout**
- *Main Hall:* A broad central gallery with high ceilings and skylights enchanted to mimic daylight, displaying large-scale paintings, tapestries, and magical sculptures that subtly move when observed.
- *Upper Salon:* A more intimate setting for private showings, small performances, or art auctions.
- *The Atrium of Reflections:* A serene courtyard filled with enchanted mirrors, delicate fountains, and living sculptures made of shifting light and water.
- *Back Rooms:* Workshops and studios for guest artists, plus secure storage for pieces too valuable for display.
- Music from unseen sources hums gently through the air — a spell tuned to each exhibit’s theme. The scent of oils, parchment, and faint perfume lingers.

**Curator & Staff**
- *Curator*: [[Marceline Virel]], a composed human woman with silver-streaked hair, sharp intuition, and an impeccable sense for both art and social maneuvering. She speaks softly but wields her influence deftly — her approval can make or break a rising artist’s career.
- *Archivist*: [[Rin Thalos]], a reclusive gnome who handles the cataloging of pieces, often muttering about “sentient paintings” and “temperamental canvases.”
- *Guards*: Discreet but ever-present, wearing polished breastplates engraved with the Gilded Frame’s emblem — a stylized golden windowpane.

Exhibits & Reputation
- The Gilded Frame is the cultural centerpiece of [[Thalyra]]. Artists from across [[Advelde]] dream of being featured here. Displays include:
	- *Traditional Works*: Portraits of nobles, landscapes of [[Thalyra]]’s architecture, and depictions of mythic battles.
	- *Arcane Art*: Paintings that shift subtly depending on the viewer’s emotions, sculptures animated by bound illusions, and tapestries that tell stories aloud when touched.
- What's auspiciously missing:
	- *Historical Collections*: There are no exhibits showcasing relics of [[Thalyra]]’s past — ancient city maps or early religious art, etc.

**Reputation:**
Elegant, exclusive, and highly political. Admission is free for the public on certain festival days, but the wealthy prefer the private evening unveilings — where every conversation doubles as negotiation.
