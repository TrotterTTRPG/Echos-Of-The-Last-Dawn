In the Gold District of [[Thalyra]]
Connected by an underground tunnel to the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]]

Where promises become contractual and alliances take form, the Hall of Accord is [[Thalyra]]’s grand nexus of negotiation and contract. Merchants, nobles, and emissaries alike gather beneath its vaulted ceilings to draft treaties, seal bargains, and forge pacts both mundane and magical. Every word spoken here is measured — every signature, binding.  
The name reflects its solemn purpose: to transform discourse into covenant, and ensure that honor, once written, endures.

---

This is the place where contracts are made.

Rising from the heart of Thalyra's Gold Ring, the Hall of Accord is a masterpiece of civic grandeur and quiet purpose - a place where ambition meet bureaucracy and dreams are weighed against coin and contract.

The structure itself is built in sweeping tiers of pale marble and gild-veined stone. It's facade is adorned with carved reliefs of clasped hands, quills, and scales of balance. Sunlight from the wide avenues catches the gilded trim along it colonnades, making the building seems to shimmer like a beacon of opportunity. High above, an enormous domed roof of glass and bronze catches the light of day and glows softly at night, illuminated within by enchanted globes that mimic starlight.

Inside, the Hall hums with activity. The grand atrium is a vast circular chamber floored in intricate mosaics depicting the five great cities of Advelde - Thalyra, Iridia, Dornthalms, Velrosk, and the lost ruins of Orest. The air smells faintly of parchment, oil, and incense. A long row of registration desks are staffed by clerks in gold-trimmed uniforms who manage the endless stream of hopeful adventurers, merchants, and emissaries seeking contracts.

Behind them are many meeting rooms including the largest nestled beneath the gilded dome - the Concord Chamber.

The chamber is a vast, circular hall. Its floor descends in gentle tiers of pale marble toward a central dais. Above, the domed glass is crowned by a great etched glass oculus. Its surface is traced with fine, interlocking geometric patterns that catch the sunlight and scatter in faint, shifting rainbows. When the light moves across the chamber, it paints the marble and gold in prismatic color - never gaudy, but mesmerizingly alive.

Tall stone columns ring the room, their bases carved with scenes of pass accords - peace treaties, trade compacts, and oaths sworn. Between the pillars, narrow alcoves hold statues of fame mediators and reformers, each cast in serene contemplation.
