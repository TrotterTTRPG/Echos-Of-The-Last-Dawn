**Location:** The Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Private-Style Park (Prestige & Secrecy Focused)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- An elegant, manicured garden surrounded by ornate wrought-iron fences entwined with climbing ivy and gold-tipped thorns.
- Pathways of white marble and polished stone wind through intricate hedges, sculpted topiaries, and softly bubbling fountains.
- Subtle magic maintains its pristine beauty — dew never lingers, and flowers never wilt.
- Decorative lampposts glow with soft golden light after sunset.

**Features**
- Secluded alcoves and gazebo nooks provide private meeting spots for nobles, merchants, and politicians.
- Exotic flora — many rare or near-extinct plants, including glowing moon orchids, golden thistle, and whispervines that hum softly at night.
- Reflecting pools enchanted to show glimpses of distant landscapes when touched.
- Occasional performances by bards or illusionists, often invitation-only events.

**Reputation & Role**
- A common meeting place for powerful figures to discuss business, form alliances, or exchange favors in relative privacy.
- Locals joke that “more deals are sealed in the Auravale Gardens than in the [[Hall of Accord]]”
- Tourists rarely linger long; it’s beautiful but carries an unmistakable air of wealth and watchfulness.
