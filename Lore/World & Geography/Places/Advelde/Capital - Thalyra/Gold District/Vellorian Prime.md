Vellorian Prime

Master Butchers of the Gold District

Location: Gold District, Thalyra
Sign: A gilt bull’s head etched in ivory and obsidian
Scent: Clean salt, fresh herbs, and faint citrus oil

Vellorian Prime sits on a quiet, marble-paved street between a vintner and a perfumer, its wide glass windows revealing immaculate stone counters and hanging brass scales polished to a mirror sheen. Nothing here smells of blood. Every surface is pristine, every cut wrapped in linen or waxed paper stamped with a gold seal.

Behind the counter stands [[Master Ilyon Vellor]], a tall, elegant elf with silver-blond hair tied back in a precise knot. His movements are economical and exact, and he speaks in calm, measured tones, never raising his voice — even when correcting a noble on how a cut should be prepared.

What They Sell

Prime cuts of beef, veal, lamb, and game, trimmed with surgical precision

Aged meats stored in magically regulated cold rooms

Rare imports: snow-fed elk, highland aurochs, pearl-fed boar

Custom orders for banquets, holy days, and arena feasts

Clientele

Noble households and wealthy merchants

Temples and scholars hosting formal gatherings

Arena patrons arranging post-victory feasts for champions

Reputation

Vellorian Prime is renowned not just for quality, but discretion. Orders are recorded in cipher, deliveries are unmarked, and servants are trained never to speak of who buys what. A whispered rumor claims that if you can pay enough, the shop can source anything that once walked on four legs — legal or otherwise.

Subtle Hook

Recently, Master Vellor has refused certain commissions tied to the upcoming Day of Challenging, claiming that “some flesh should not be celebrated.” Whether this is personal conviction… or knowledge of something coming, remains unclear.
