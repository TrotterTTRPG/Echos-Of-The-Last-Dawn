**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Arcane School

**Focus:**
- Children with high intelligence or magical talent trained in arcane arts.
- Combines theory, practical spellcasting, magical research, and magical history.
- Students come from all districts, but mostly from Gold District.

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Grand building with classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and magical practice halls.
- Dormitories for boarding students, observation towers, and controlled experimentation rooms.

**Atmosphere:** intellectually intense, inspiring, and slightly intimidating.

**Staff:**
- Archmages, wizards, and skilled instructors in magical theory and practice.
- Assistant teachers and magical apprentices for hands-on instruction.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Elite institution for developing magical talent.
- Known for discipline, rigor, and innovation in magical research.
