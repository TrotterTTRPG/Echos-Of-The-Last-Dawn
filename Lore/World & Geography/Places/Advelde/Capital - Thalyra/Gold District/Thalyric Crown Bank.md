In the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]

Connected by an underground tunnel to the [[Hall of Accord]]

The Thalyric Crown Bank is less  a building and more of statement - of prosperity, of stability, and of who truly holds power in Thalyra. 

From the outside, the bank resembles a temple more than a place of commerce. Tall, fluted columns of pale veined stone rise to meet and arche portico engraved with a crown encircled by a coiled serpent - the symbol of wisdom in guardianship and of profit unending. The architecture evoked the solemnity of a temple, but its design whispers of defense first, beauty second.

Massive, iron-banded doors seal the main entrance, guarded day and night by soldier. Even the air inside feels heavy - still and sound-dampened, as if the building itself discourages reckless noise or ambition.

The main hall is vast but austere, with high-arched ceilings. A ring or arcane wards is woven through the foundation stones, their sigils faintly glowing beneath the marble when spells are cast too close.

At the far end of the hall stands a raised dais with a semicircle of desks built from burnished brass and walnut. Clients approach each desk one by one, under the watchful gaze of guards and unseen divination wards that pulse faintly like the heartbeat of the building itself.

Below, the vault levels extend deep underground, protected by a labrythine series of corridors, runic locks, and enchantments. 

Even the thieves' guilds speak of the Crown Bank with reverence - not as a target, but as a warning.
