In the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]

Connected by an underground tunnel to the [[Hall of Accord]]

**Exterior**
- The tallest building in Thalyra - it overtops every government building, every noble estate, every civic landmark in the city
- Built from pale, heavily veined stone
- The design is severe and vertical: clean lines, minimal ornamentation, the aesthetic of something that has no interest in being charming
- Walls are visibly thick, especially on the lower floors
- Narrow, deeply-set windows line the lower floors; the upper floors have larger windows
- The main entrance is large massive iron-banded doors that are open during banking hours and are guarded at all hours by guards that are distinctly not of [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] 
- Atop the roof sits the largest arcane cannon in the city, mounted on a rotating base capable of targeting any approach

**Main Hall**
- Sound-dampened upon entry - voices carry only so far, footsteps muffle without explanation, the effect is clearly deliberate
- Vast, high-arched ceilings that make you feel small
- Completely austere: pale grey and cream marble floors, no tapestries, no softness, no ornament
- There's a long walk to a raised dais at the far end. The dais holds a semicircle of desks in burnished brass and dark walnut.
	- Clients approach one at a time, in the bank's own 
	- **This is where most normal transactions occur**
	- Manned by Senior Desk Officer [[Seravael Dawnmere]] and Desk Officers [[Dorak Ashfen]], [[Milla Twocopper]], [[Cassel Vorne]], [[Brek Ironmantle]]
- Guards are stationed at appropriate intervals

**Second Floor**
- Offices of bank officials
- Thick, dark walnut doors with brass handles - no name plates on any doors
- Largest Office belongs to [[Edran Braxton]]

**Upper Floors**
- Not accessible to most
- Houses the private offices of Thalyra's most powerful people
- The higher the floor, the more powerful the occupant
- The bank is their true address: their money is kept below, their decisions are made above

**The Vaults**
- Extend deep underground beneath the main hall
- Accessible only through a single corridor, and only with escort
- Protected by a labyrinthine series of passages that do not follow intuitive geometry
- Runic locks seal every door; enchantments shift and cycle on their own schedule
- No map of the vault layout is publicly available

**The Tunnel**
- A hidden underground passage connects the bank directly to the [[Hall of Accord]]
- Unmarked on any public map
- Its existence is known only to those who need to use it
