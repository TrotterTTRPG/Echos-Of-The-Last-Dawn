(Located outside the [[Hall of Accord]], Gold District in [[Thalyra]])

In the center of the courtyard just outside of the [[Hall of Accord]].
Consists of 2 magically reinforced oak panels side by side, framed in silver and brass, enchanted to resist weather, tampering, and “creative editing.”

**The Quest Board**
- The left side of the structure is the Quest Board, where townsfolk, merchants, and officials post requests for adventuring aid. Each notice is neatly categorized and color-coded:
	- Bronze Placards – Local or low-risk tasks (guard duty, missing persons, pest control)
	- Silver Placards – Moderate-risk jobs (monster tracking, artifact retrieval, escort missions)
	- Gold Placards – High-risk or prestige contracts (rare hunts, political escort, ancient site investigation)
- A magical quill at the base of the board automatically copies accepted postings into the Hall’s internal records. Once a party accepts a quest, their insignia is magically branded onto the listing — marking it as “claimed.”
- If a group fails to complete the job, the posting reappears at sunrise with a subtle red shimmer and an updated reward. Repeat failures can result in temporary rank suspension — the embarrassment of which often stings worse than the loss of coin.

**The Ranking Board**
- To the right stands the gleaming Ranking Board, a source of endless pride (and rivalry). Crafted of mirrored silver, it constantly shifts and reorders itself as accomplishments are verified by the Hall’s clerks.
- The top panel reads in grand lettering: **“The Adventurer’s Docket — Current Standings”**
- Below it, the names and insignias of registered adventuring groups glow softly, ranked by:
	1. Completed contracts and reliability
	2. Prestige of tasks undertaken
	3. Favor from sponsors and patrons
	4. Public reputation (both official and rumor)
