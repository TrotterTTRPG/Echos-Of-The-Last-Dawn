**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Luxury inn and tavern

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Opulent common room with plush seating, golden chandeliers, and private dining rooms.
- Rooms are elegantly furnished with soft rugs and embroidered curtains.

**Clientele:**
- Nobles
- Wealthy merchants
- Diplomats
- Adventurers of high standing

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine).

**Staff:** Managed by [[Marcellus Veyra]], Leonin man with impeccable manners; staff includes trained attendants, stablehands, and discreet security.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Prestigious
- Exclusive
- Well-regarded
- Ideal for negotiations, lavish receptions, or simply showing off wealth
