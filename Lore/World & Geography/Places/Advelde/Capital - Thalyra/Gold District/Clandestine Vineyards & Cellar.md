**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Luxury Wine Maker / Vineyard

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Boutique building with stone floors, arched windows, and tasting rooms with velvet seating.
- Rare imported wines and local vintage selections displayed in polished racks.
- Aromas of oak barrels, fermenting grapes, and subtle floral notes in the air.
- Private rooms for elite tastings and wine-pairing events.

**Note:** Many Inns and Taverns will ferment their own wine, but they will occasionally supplement with this well-known type.

**Staff**
- Winemaker: [[Lucien Arvalis]], Half-elven man, known for sophisticated palettes and rare vintages.
- Assistants: Sommeliers, cellar masters, and attendants trained in presentation and service.

**Specialties & Services**
- Luxury wines, rare vintages, and custom blends for nobles, diplomats, and affluent adventurers.
- Private tastings, special orders, and wine-pairing events.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as the gold standard of wines in [[Thalyra]].
- A destination for wealthy patrons and high-society gatherings.
- Represents sophistication, exclusivity, and indulgence.
