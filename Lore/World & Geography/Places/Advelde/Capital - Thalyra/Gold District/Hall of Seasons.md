In Gold District in  [[Thalyra]]  

The Hall of Seasons serves as the central government assembly of [[Thalyra]] — a grand, circular complex representing the city’s devotion to balance and cyclical order. 
This is where diplomats all across [[Advelde]] commune to plan and debate. Though this is where assembly occurs, most of the upper level government officials have offices in [[The Crown Building]]. 
From this hall, policies are shaped and alliances formed. The Hall of Seasons stands as both a symbol of continuity and a reminder that all things—power included—ebb and flow with time.

**Exterior Design**
_Architecture Style_
- Built in the same elegant Elven-inspired design as much of [[Thalyra]] — graceful arches, curving bridges, and radiant symmetry.
- The building forms a great circle divided into four wings radiating from a domed central atrium, each distinct in color and motif to match its season.
_Materials & Colors_
- Smooth white marble shot through with veins of rose, gold, green, and blue crystal, shifting subtly with the light.
- The dome at its center is made of magically tinted glass that reflects the hue of the current season.
- Each wing bears carved emblems and flora tied to its element — blooming vines for Spring, blazing suns for Summer, falling leaves for Autumn, and snowflakes etched in silver for Winter.
_Entrance_
- Four main archways, one for each season, converge on the **Rotunda of Equinox** at the Hall’s heart.
- The public may enter through the Spring Gate, which opens into a lush garden and fountain courtyard.
- The other wings are open only to those with official standing or invitation.
