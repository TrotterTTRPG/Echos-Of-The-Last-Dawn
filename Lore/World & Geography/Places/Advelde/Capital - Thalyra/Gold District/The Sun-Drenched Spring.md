**Location:** Gold District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Luxury Bathhouse & Spa

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- A grand building with ornate golden trims, arched windows, and doors carved with flowing water motifs.
- Inside, the main hall opens onto a massive central bathing pool, fed by magically heated water that maintains a perfect temperature.
- Smaller private pools branch off along quiet corridors, some adorned with rare stones, exotic flowers, and subtle enchanted lighting.
- Gentle, ambient music plays from unseen sources, and faint steam carries scents of jasmine, sandalwood, and citrus.
- The bathhouse has multiple floors:
- Ground floor: communal pools, changing rooms, and massage areas.
- Upper floors: private suites, treatment rooms, and observation decks overlooking tranquil gardens.
- Water is enchanted in subtle ways — it may soothe fatigue, ease minor aches, or refresh the mind.

**Staff**
- Manager: [[Aurelia Veyrin]], human woman with an impeccable sense of elegance and discretion.
- Bath Attendants: Trained in etiquette, hospitality, and minor restorative magic.
- Specialists: Some provide luxury treatments, aromatherapy, or discreet massage services.
- Staff are mostly discreet, soft-spoken, and always attentive; their presence is calming rather than obtrusive.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as one of [[Thalyra]]’s most exclusive relaxation destinations, visited by nobles, wealthy merchants, diplomats, and elite adventurers.
- A place for business to be conducted quietly, deals to be forged subtly, or simply to relax away from the public eye.
- Offers privacy, luxury, and rejuvenation - not just physical, but mental. Patrons often leave feeling refreshed and enlightened.
- Rumors suggest some discreet arrangements occur behind closed doors, though the primary focus is luxury and wellness.
- Seen as a social hub for [[Thalyra]]’s elite in a way that blends leisure, influence, and prestige.
