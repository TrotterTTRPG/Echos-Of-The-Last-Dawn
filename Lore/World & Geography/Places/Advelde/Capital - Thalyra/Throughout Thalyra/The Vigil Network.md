Overview
Throughout [[Thalyra]], tall silvered posts line the district walls, main thoroughfares, and gateways between districts. These are the Vigil Beacons — an interconnected magical alarm and defense system that can alert the entire city in moments. Though simple to the eye, they are a masterwork of arcane engineering jointly maintained by the [[Thalyra]].

Although this alarm system is set up, it seems unnecessary. You would be hard pressed to find anyone in the city who has ever seen the alarms as anything else than the "pulse of the vigil". Even if it went off, most citizens wouldn't know what each color meant other than just alarm. 

**Physical Description**
- Each beacon is mounted atop a polished silver-steel post, 10–15 feet tall.
- The posts are spaced evenly:
	- Along each district wall (typically every 100–150 feet).
	- Flanking major roads leading through gatehouses or plazas.
- The top of each post holds a crystal orb the size of a melon, faintly glowing with soft light.
- Fine runes spiral down the pole—barely visible during the day, but gleaming faintly under moonlight.
- The runes are inscribed in mirrored script, reflecting [[Thalyra]]’s dual emphasis on illumination and warding.

Functionality
- Normal State
	- Emits a gentle silver glow, pulsing faintly every few seconds — a symbol of peace and readiness.
	- Residents use them as time markers at night: “the pulse of the Vigil.”
- Activation / Alarm State
	- When triggered (by authorized personnel or automatic detection wards):
		- The orbs flare to full brightness, projecting colored light into the sky.
		- A harmonic tone resonates outward — not deafening, but carrying clearly through the district.
	- The tone and color vary depending on the threat type.
	- The lights pulse towards the threat
	- The alarm sounds louder near the threat

| Color | Sound                   | Meaning          |
| ----- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| Red   | Resonant, low horn tone | Martial threat   |
| Blue  | Bell-like chime         | Magical anomaly  |
| Green | Rising tritone hum      | Natural Disaster |
| White | Steady tone             | Evacuation Order |
