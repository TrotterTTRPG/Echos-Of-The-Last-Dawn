**Location:** Throughout [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Individualized Home Education

**Focus:**
- High-class children receive tailored instruction at home.
- Covers reading, writing, history, etiquette, fine arts, basic magic, languages.

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Education happens in homes or estates, with small classrooms or libraries.
- Highly personalized and often luxurious, reflecting family status.

**Staff:**
- Tutors specializing in one or multiple subjects, often elite or highly skilled.
- Some tutors double as advisors to families or household managers.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Exclusive, high-quality education for the wealthy.
- Prepares children for elite positions in society or magical aptitude programs.
