**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** General Clothing & Tailoring Shop (Practical with Occasional Fancy Items)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Modest shop with sturdy wooden racks, simple mannequins, and a display of colorful but practical fabrics.
- Walls lined with shelves holding rolls of cloth, spools of thread, buttons, and leather trims.
- Small tailoring table near the back with tools scattered about — scissors, needles, measuring tapes.
- A cozy, bustling feel; customers chat while getting their clothes repaired or fitted.

**Staff**
- *Owner/Tailor:* [[Mira Fenn]], human woman with nimble fingers and a friendly smile.
- *Apprentices:* 1–2 young humans or halflings learning tailoring basics, assisting with minor repairs.

**Reputation & Role**
- Specializes in everyday clothes for workers, guards, and commoners, with a few fancier garments available for festivals or ceremonies.
- Known for reliable service, reasonable prices, and quick turnaround.
- Occasionally commissioned by adventurers for travel-ready gear or small cloaks enchanted by local wizards (mundane tailoring only, no magic tailoring).
