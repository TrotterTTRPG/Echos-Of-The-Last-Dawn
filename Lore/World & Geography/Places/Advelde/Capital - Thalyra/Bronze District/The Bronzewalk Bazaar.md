**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Open-Air Market (Artisans, Gatherers, & Traveling Merchants)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- A sprawling plaza near the edge of the Bronze District, marked by rows of canvas awnings, wooden stalls, and colorful banners that shift with the seasons.
- The air is rich with the smells of roasting meats, fresh herbs, tanned leather, and iron being worked at portable forges.
- Paths of worn cobblestone wind through the stalls in a loose circle, with a central well and stage often used by street performers or public criers.
- Chaotic but vibrant — bartering, shouting, and laughter echo through the space from dawn until dusk.
- At night, hanging lanterns and string lights give the bazaar a warm, flickering glow as late sellers linger and share drinks.

**Vendors & Goods**
- *Hunters & Gatherers:* Sell freshly caught game, pelts, wild herbs, mushrooms, and honey.
- *Artisans & Crafters:* Offer handmade wares — pottery, jewelry, trinkets, woodcarvings, toys, and charms.
- *Tinkerers:* Gnomes and artificers with small mechanical creations or simple clockwork curios.
- *Food Stalls:* Roast skewers, soups, pastries, and small-batch ales that change daily.
- *Traveling Merchants:* Occasionally bring in rare imports from other cities or villages, drawing crowds.

Staff & Organization
- *Market Master:* [[Thora “Tinvoice” Raddock]] — stout dwarven woman with a booming voice and a scar across her chin. Keeps the peace and ensures every vendor pays their dues.
	- Known for settling disputes with a single glare and the phrase, “If you’re sellin’ here, you’re under my tent.”
- *Assistants*: A few guards assigned to patrol, mostly to prevent pickpocketing or fights over pricing.
- *Local Kids*: Run errands for merchants, deliver small goods, and gossip about who sold what for how much.

Reputation & Role
- Considered the heart of [[Thalyra]]’s working class — lively, practical, and full of honest trade.
- Frequent site of small performances and rumor exchanges, especially near the central stage.
- On rare occasions, agents from the Silver District quietly visit to scout promising artisans or new products worth investing in.
