**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Childcare & Early Education

**Focus:**
- Early education for children, basic literacy, numeracy, hygiene, social skills.
- Emphasis on practical life skills and preparation for apprenticeships.
- Children of high school age often leave to apprentice in their chosen trades.

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Warm, modest building with classrooms, small play areas, and a central courtyard.
- Bright, welcoming, and slightly chaotic due to young children.
- Walls decorated with simple maps, charts, and craft projects.

**Staff:**
- Headmistress/Headmaster overseeing staff.
- Teachers skilled in literacy, math, crafts, and basic magic (optional).
- Caretakers for younger children.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Known for nurturing and practical education.
- Essential foundation for apprenticeships and early life learning.

Note: Children from [[Starlight Haven]] come here to get an education. They may be able to pick up a trade afterwards, but often children who aren't adopted by that point end up at [[The Bronze Bastion Academy]]
