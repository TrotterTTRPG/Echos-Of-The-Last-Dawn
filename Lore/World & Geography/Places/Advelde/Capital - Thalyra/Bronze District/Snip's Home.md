**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]

**Barber:** [[Lina “Snip” Dorrin]], a Gnomish woman who balances precariously on a stool while wielding sharp scissors

**Type:** Home-Based Barber / Budget Alternative

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Small room in Lina’s home converted into a modest barber space
- Functional but cozy, with simple chairs and mirrors

**Specialties & Services:**
- Non-guard haircuts for locals who don’t want the standard military-style cuts
- Quick, practical, and affordable

**Reputation & Role:**
- Popular among working-class locals seeking inexpensive, practical, or slightly stylish haircuts without going to official barbers.

The official barber in the Bronze District is [[Clovis Irontrim]] at [[The Sentinel's Shear]], but he seems only capable of performing basic military haircuts. That's why [[Lina “Snip” Dorrin]] opened up her own shop, unofficially to fill the gap.
