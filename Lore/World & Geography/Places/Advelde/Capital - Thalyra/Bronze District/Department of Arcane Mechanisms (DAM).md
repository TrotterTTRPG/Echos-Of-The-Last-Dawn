Located in the Bronze District in [[Thalyra]] and attached to the [[Department of Public Works]]

Headed by [[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]]

*"Magic should not merely be studied; it should be built."*

A municipal research and engineering department tasked with integrating arcane power, beast essences, and mechanical systems to create tools that improve city infrastructure, defense, and industry

Most staff are artificers, but the department also employs alchemists and enchanters

**Divisions within the department:**
- Essence Integration Division - Researchers who extract, refine, and stabilize beast essences
- Arcano-Mechanical Engineering Division - The artificers who design the machines themselves

Example machines:
- **Cargo Golems**
	- Large quadrupedal machines
	- Powered by auroch or bulette essence
	- Used in construction and shipping
- **Storm Turbines**
	- Channel bound air-elemental energy
	- Used to provide power through magical means
- **Tunnelers**
	- Massive burrowing machines
	- Powered by ankheg essence

**Moral Philosophy of [[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]]:**
- He believes beasts are part of a natural arcane ecosystem and their essences can be used responsibly.
	- "An ankheg heart rotting in the wilderness serves no one. The same heart digging tunnels for new departments or study can save lives."
- He does have limits:
	- He refuses to experiment on sentient creatures
	- He rejects necromantic energy sources
	- He dislikes war machines even when they're explicitly commissioned for defense

**Secret (Optional) Plot Hook:**
[[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]]'s current research is around a massive prototype that would allow [[Thalyra]] to harvest ambient magical energy from the environment itself. If successful, the city owuld become the first self-powered arcane metropolis in the world, but the machine requires an extremely powerful essence.
