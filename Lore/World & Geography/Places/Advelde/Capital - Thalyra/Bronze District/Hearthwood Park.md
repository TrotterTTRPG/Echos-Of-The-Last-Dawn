**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Public Park (Community & Family Focused)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- A sprawling, welcoming green space surrounded by modest stone walls with wide, open gates.
- Shaded by tall oak and ash trees with winding cobblestone paths connecting open lawns, flower beds, and small ponds.
- Scattered benches, picnic tables, and open gazebos encourage gathering and conversation.
- Central fountain with carved figures of families and workers—symbols of unity and everyday life.
- During the day, the air hums with laughter, chatter, and the jingling of street vendors’ carts.

**Features**
- Play areas with climbing stones, carved wooden creatures, and open fields for games.
- Community garden plots where locals grow vegetables and herbs, overseen by a retired druid who volunteers his time.
- Small stage used for impromptu performances, music, and public announcements.
- Lantern-lit paths that make it a cozy evening walk spot for couples and families alike.

**Reputation & Role**
- Guards occasionally patrol but are relaxed and friendly here.
- Known as a place where even the poorest feel a sense of belonging and peace.
