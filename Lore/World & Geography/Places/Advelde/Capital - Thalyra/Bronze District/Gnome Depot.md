**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** General Store (Mundane Goods & Adventuring Supplies)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Large, warehouse-style building with an orange-and-brass sign bolted above broad double doors.
- Wide aisles lined with shelves stacked high with camping gear, rope, rations, tents, lanterns, oil, ball bearings, mess kits, journals, and other adventuring basics.
- Overhead signs mark each section (e.g., “Rope & Rigging,” “Tents & Tarps,” “Mess & Maintenance”).
- Smells of sawdust, oiled metal, and faint alcohol from a perpetually leaking barrel.
- Constant background noise — carts rolling, hammering from the repair corner, and workers shouting for items in distant aisles.
- Feels chaotic but friendly; a popular meeting place for adventurers and laborers alike.

**Staff**
- *Owner*: [[Gernwick Boltbluff]] — rock gnome, fast-talking, perpetually smudged with soot, two quills behind his ears. Known for saying, “If I can’t find it, it probably hasn’t been invented yet!”
- *Assistants*: A rotating crew of young gnomes, dwarves, and humans working for steady coin.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as the most reliable place in [[Thalyra]] for practical, mundane supplies — no magic items, no nonsense.
- Beloved by locals and adventurers for its humor, dependability, and “organized chaos” energy.
- The go-to spot for outfitting expeditions, replacing worn tools, and grabbing last-minute essentials before a quest.
