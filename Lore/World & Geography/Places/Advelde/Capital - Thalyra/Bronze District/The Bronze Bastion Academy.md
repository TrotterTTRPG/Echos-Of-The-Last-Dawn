**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Military / Guard High School

**Focus:**
- Training high school-age students to become town guards or soldiers.
- Combat drills, tactics, physical conditioning, minor magical tactics if available.
- Discipline and loyalty emphasized over academic subjects.

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Courtyards for drills, indoor training rooms, armories, and barracks-style dorms.
- Stern and orderly environment; clang of weapons and drills common.

**Staff:**
- Veteran guards as instructors, some minor clerics for first aid and healing.
- Headmaster experienced in military training.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Produces reliable, disciplined guards for the city.
- Emphasizes tradition and practical skills over theoretical knowledge.

Note: Many children from [[Starlight Haven]] who haven't been adopted yet end up going to this school and becoming guards. Some do find apprenticeships, but that's rare.
