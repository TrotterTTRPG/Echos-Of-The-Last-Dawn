**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Orphanage / Charitable Institution

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Modest but well-kept building with simple rooms for sleeping, small common areas, and a central dining area.
- Outdoor courtyard with a small garden and play area for children.
- Warm, welcoming interior, with handmade toys, books, and basic furnishings.
- Quietly bustling with caretakers and children; cheerful but practical atmosphere.

**Staff**
- *Head Caretaker:* [[Frizzle]], Earth Genasi woman with a kind demeanor and practical sense. Despite her outward appearance being gruff and uninviting, she's incredibly caring and warm towards the children
- *Assistants:* Nurses, tutors, and cooks to tend to children’s physical, educational, and emotional needs.
- *Support Staff:* Housekeepers and occasional volunteers.

**Specialties & Services**
- Provides housing, food, and basic education for children without guardians.
- Focuses on safety, hygiene, and general upbringing, preparing children for apprenticeships or eventual independent living.
- Accepts children from families of all classes, though mostly those left by commoners.

**Reputation & Role**
- Funded mainly by noble donations, considered an easy and respectable charitable investment.
- Seen as reliable and necessary, but not luxurious — emphasis is on care and practicality, not comfort.
- A place where adventurers, guards, or merchants might drop off foundlings, or sometimes children of adventurers in need of care.
