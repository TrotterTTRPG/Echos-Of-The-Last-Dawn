**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Beer Brewery

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Large brick building with tall chimneys and rows of wooden fermentation vats inside.
- Strong smell of malt, hops, and yeast fills the air.
- Open tasting room at the front with wooden tables and benches for patrons.
- Walls lined with barrels ready for storage or sale; casual, lively atmosphere.

**Note:** Many Inns and Taverns will brew their own beer, but they often supplement with this well-known type.

**Staff**
- *Head Brewer:* [[Borin Maltwright]], human, robust and friendly, known for hearty, consistent ales. Always red in the face and always seems like he's about to keel over, but proclaims he's healthy as a horse
- *Assistants:* Brewers, coopers, and servers handling the tasting room.

**Specialties & Services**
- Large-scale beer production — ales, stouts, porters, and seasonal brews.
- Local favorite for working-class citizens, guards, and adventurers.
- Tours and tastings available occasionally for public engagement.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known for reliable, hearty beer at affordable prices.
- Community gathering spot, social hub for locals.
- Practical, robust brews rather than experimental or fancy flavors.
