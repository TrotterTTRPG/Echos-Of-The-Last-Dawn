Location: Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
Type: Working-class inn and tavern

**Outside
- Broad, squat tavern made of dark timber and smokestained stone
- A weathered wooden sign hangs from two iron chains above the door with a painted emblem - a frothing tankard cross with a smith's hammer. It's been touched up multiple times.
- Warm light shines on the street from behind diamond paned windows that are slightly fogged from the heat coming within.
- From the street, the scent of yeast, malt, and slow-cooked stew drifts out each time the heavy oak door swings open 

**Layout & Atmosphere:** 
- Dimly lit, sturdy wooden beams, low ceilings, large common room with roaring hearth.
- Rooms are simple but clean.
- The smell of ale and stew pervades the air.
- More rowdy and boisterous than [[The Bronze Lantern]]

**Clientele:**
- Laborers
- Guards
- Adventurers on a budget.

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine). Since this is in the Bronze District, the wine would be less likely to be stocked or would be prohibitably expensive.

**Staff:** Run by a boisterous dwarf named [[Harrik Stonebrow]] and a handful of human servers.

**Reputation & Role:**
- Reliable
- Hearty food and drink
- A place for storytelling, minor brawls, and bartering tips for adventurers
- Known for cheap ale and a no-nonsense vibe.
