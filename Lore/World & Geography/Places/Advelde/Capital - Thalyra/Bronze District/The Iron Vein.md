**Location**: Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Specialty**: Weapon and armor repair, standard issue guard gear, bulk forging

**Atmosphere & Structure**
- Rows of anvils, forges, and quenching troughs stretch through open-air courtyards.
- The scent of oil, soot, and molten metal fills the air.
- Apprentices scurry between stations carrying ingots, while senior smiths argue over technique.
- A long, muraled wall shows the Iron Vein’s history — weapons forged for city defense, names of fallen guards etched into the stone.

**Owner & Staff**
- Master Smith: [[Brunna Steelhand]], a broad-shouldered dwarven woman with burn scars on her forearms and a voice that carries across the yard. She’s a perfectionist about function, not flair.
- The workers are mostly locals or guards’ family members, proud of their craft.

**Reputation & Role**
- Reliable. No-nonsense. The Iron Vein is where soldiers and guards go when something breaks — and where apprentices go if they’re serious about learning the trade.
- They also handle metal reclamation — melting down damaged armor, salvaging scraps, and reforging them into new gear, ensuring nothing goes to waste.
