**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Public Infirmary / Walk-In Clinic

**Note:** Most patrons only have to pay the cost of materials. The cost of labor is often covered by wealthier merchants/nobles. It's seen as a very easy type of charity (it is looked at favorably and requires no effort, just money)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Modest, functional building with a warm glow from lanterns and hearths.
- Several treatment rooms with basic beds, shelves of herbs, bandages, and medical supplies.
- Waiting area with benches and a small water fountain for patients.
- Always bustling during the day, quieter at night with a few overnight patients or staff on call.

**Staff**
- Head Healer: [[Maren Fallow]], human Life cleric skilled in general medicine.
- Assistants: Apprentices, nurses, and a few minor clerics who help with treatments and patient care.
- Support Staff: Apothecary helpers, cooks for patient meals, cleaners, and clerks.

**Specialties & Services**
- Walk-in medical care for common ailments, injuries, and illnesses.
- Minor magical healing (laying on hands, minor restorative spells) and mundane treatments.
- Affordable for working-class citizens; open to anyone in the district.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as dependable and inclusive, welcoming all social classes.
- Essential for city laborers, guards, and adventurers who need prompt treatment.
- Seen as practical, compassionate, and trustworthy, though not luxurious.
