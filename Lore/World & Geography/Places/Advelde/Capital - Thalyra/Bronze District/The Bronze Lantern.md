**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Modest, quieter inn

**Layout & Atmosphere:**
- Small courtyard
- Ivy along the walls
- Modest furnishings
- Lanterns hanging along pathways
- More peaceful than [[The Iron Tankard]]

**Clientele:**
- Traveling merchants
- Families
- Adventurers seeking rest without rowdiness.

**Note:** They will often brew/distill/ferment their own beer/liquor/wine, but will often stock more well known products from [[Oak Barrel Brewing Co]] (beer), [[Emberglass Distillery]] (liquor), and [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] (wine). Since this is in the Bronze District, the wine would be less likely to be stocked or would be prohibitably expensive.

**Staff:** Managed by [[Lysa Dorrin]], human woman with a calm demeanor; staff are friendly and practical.

**Reputation & Role:**
- A reliable night’s rest, clean rooms, and a secure place for valuables.
- Favored by those who want less chaos than typical Bronze District establishments.
