**Interior**
- *Main area*: Low tables for tattooing, walls plastered with flash art, sketches, and examples of magical designs (like runes that glow faintly or animals that animate briefly).
- *Alchemy corner*: Shelves of pigments, infused inks, and enchanted powders that allow tattoos to carry minor effects: glowing in the dark, providing warmth, masking scents, or storing small enchantments.
- *Back room*: Reserved for the most intricate magical tattoos, often requiring several sessions and the assistance of a resident spellcaster.
- *Ambience*: Smells of ink, resin, and a faint electric hum. Brass lamps cast warm light, highlighting the intricate designs on clients’ skin.

**Owner & Staff**
- *Owner*: [[Kael “Needlehand” Varrick]], a grizzled human artist with a deft touch and a reputation for honesty. He’s got a few small magical talents himself, which he channels into tattoos, and a network of enchanters who consult on more complex jobs.
- *Apprentices*: A mix of humans, dwarves, and a few elves who handle standard tattoos, prep work, and help run errands for magical components.

**Services & Specialties**
- *Standard Tattoos*: Dragons, knots, family crests, marks of guilds, or decorative designs.
