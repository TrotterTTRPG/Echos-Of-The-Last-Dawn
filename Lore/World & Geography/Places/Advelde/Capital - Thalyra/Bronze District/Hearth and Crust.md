**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Bakery (Specializing in Bread)

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Modest shop with stone ovens visible from the front, their warm glow spilling into the street.
- Wooden shelves and counters display loaves of all shapes and sizes: sourdough, rye, oat, multigrain, and simple white loaves.
- Flour-dusted floors, the warm aroma of fresh-baked bread, and the rhythmic thumping of kneading dough create a cozy, homely atmosphere.
- A small bench outside allows patrons to enjoy fresh bread on the spot.

**Staff**
- Owner/Baker: [[Haldor Kneebread]], a Luxodon man who's always covered in flour and always wears a jovial smile.
- Assistants: 1–2 apprentices learning kneading, shaping, and baking techniques.
- Staff may occasionally teach locals how to maintain a good sourdough starter or bake flatbreads for festivals.

**Reputation & Role**
- Known as the best source for fresh, hearty bread in the Bronze District.
- Serves working-class families, guards, and adventurers who need filling, reliable food.
- Specializes in practical breads over pastries, though seasonal or festival breads are sometimes made.
- A gathering place for locals to chat, trade small news, or grab bread for journeys.
