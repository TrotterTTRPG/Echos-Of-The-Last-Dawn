AKA Drips (Dunkin Donuts)

**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]] 
**Reputation:** A little worn, a little loud, but absolutely beloved.  
**Description:**  
- The tables are mismatched
- The floors have seen better days — but the smell of fried dough and strong coffee hits you before you even turn the corner.
- Inside, the air is always thick with laughter, gossip, and the clatter of mugs.

The shop is run by [[Mara Tull]], a no-nonsense dwarf with flour permanently dusted into her hair and a knack for remembering everyone’s usual order. The pastries might come out uneven, but they’re fresh, hot, and served with a smile (or a side-eye, depending on your manners).

It’s the heart of the neighborhood — cheap, fast, familiar.

**Clientele:** 
- Guards on break
- Locals who refuse to get their morning coffee anywhere else. 

**Signature Item:** _The Glaze Bomb_ — a dense, sweet pastry fried in deep oil and dunked in syrup that drips onto your fingers no matter what enchantments they try.

**Vibe:** Grease, grit, and genuine warmth — the Bronze District in pastry form.
