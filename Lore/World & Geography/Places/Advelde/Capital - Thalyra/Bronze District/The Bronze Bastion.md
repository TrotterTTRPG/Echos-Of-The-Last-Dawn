Main headquarters of [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] in [[Thalyra]]'s Bronze District

**Function:** Command center, barracks, training grounds, and administrative offices.

**Description:**
- A broad, blocky fortress-like structure of bronze-hued stone and dark slate roofs.
- Modest in height but heavy in build — designed for defense over beauty.
- The front plaza holds banners of [[Advelde]] and [[Thalyra]].
- Behind it lies a courtyard for drills and parade formations.

**Staff:**
- Commanders, officers, administrative clerks, and trainees.
- Most rank-and-file guards live in adjacent housing in the Bronze District.

The Bronze Bastion is considered the “heart of discipline,” though in practice it’s more about paperwork and routine than warfare.
