**Location:** Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
**Type:** Cobblers / Working-Class Footwear

**Layout & Atmosphere**
- Small, sturdy shop with heavy wooden shelves lined with leather boots, simple shoes, and durable sandals.
- Workbench near the back covered in tools: hammers, awls, knives, and piles of leather scraps.
- The smell of tanned leather and polish permeates the air, mingling with faint smoke from a small leather-stamping stove.
- Functional, utilitarian design; little decoration beyond a mounted sign of a boot stepping on an anvil.

**Staff**
- Owner/Cobbler: [[Bram Coalheel]], very oldhuman man with strong hands, soot-streaked apron, and a reputation for endurance shoes.
- Assistants: 1–2 apprentices learning shoe repair and construction.

**Reputation & Role**
- Specializes in durable shoes and boots for laborers, guards, and adventurers.
- Known for quick, practical repairs and sturdy footwear designed for long days of walking or combat.
- Affordable and reliable; locals trust that shoes from Ironfoot will last for months of hard wear.
