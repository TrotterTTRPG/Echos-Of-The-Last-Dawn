There was a long span of time before [[The Gilded Age]] that may have been nearly as long that was defined by instability and war.

There are many theories about the reason for the war., what led up to it, and the context around all of the parties involved.

What is known for certain, is that most of the Kingdom was rallying against [[Dornthalm]], which is an extremely old and heavily fortified city.
- Some say that [[Dornthalm]] was acting imperiously, and bleeding the kingdom dry, hoarding it's wealth and resources.
- The people of [[Dornthalm]] tell another story, where it was being pillaged and sieged by the lesser cities, and had an obligation to protect it's archives. (Essentially this world's Library of Alexandria).
	- These archives hold such a vast amount of text, that most of what exists there remains uncatalogued.
- To this day, [[Dornthalm]] is a place that is heavily fortified and guarded by those who seek to protect the archives.
- In the end:
	- The general consensus aligns with [[Dornthalm]]'s depiction of of events, and is not seen in contemporary times as an imperial force in the realm.
	- [[Dornthalm]] stood largely unscathed, while the rest of the kingdom lay in relative ruin.
	- [[Velrosk]], [[Thalyra]] and [[Iridia]] have been built since the end of this war.
		- Places like [[Velrosk]] first formed adhoc as a place of trade, and grew organicall
		- [[Thelyra]] was a planned city state that would serve as a testament to the realms commitment of governance by law, and serves as a unifying capital for the city states of the realm. 

There was a lot of conflict within the realm beside just the warring against [[Dornthalm]]. Many other city states sided with [[Dornthalm]], and other were said to be trying to take advantage of the instability for their own gains.
