A quiet horror known to almost no one — not a teaching of any temple, not a chapter of any bestiary. Only the rare scholar of [[The Cycle]] suspects it, and the rarer creature it has touched never explains itself.

**The pattern:**
- During the height of [[The Umbral Soul]], the dead do not sleep. Spirits and remnants of forgotten ages drift freely, "drawn to what life remains," sustained by hunger, emotion, or memory.
- [[Verda]] is currently held unnaturally at 75% [[The Effulgent Soul]] / 25% [[The Umbral Soul]] (see [[The Cycle]]). That drift never fully stops in this age. It only thins.
- Of every wound a departing soul can carry, one calls to these wandering remnants louder than the rest: to be killed by the hand of someone who loved you exactly as much as you loved them. Not betrayal wearing love's face — a real, equal, devastating devotion, ended in the same breath that proves it.
- That paradox tears something in the soul that ordinary grief, ordinary murder, even ordinary heartbreak does not. A wandering Umbral remnant finds the tear and settles into it, wearing the body the way water settles into a wound.
- What rises after is not the person who died, and isn't quite a stranger either. It remembers the love. It remembers being killed by it. Both feel equally true, because they are the same truth.
- Its hunger always points back to the one who loved it and killed it in the same act. Feeding on them — only them — is the one thing that ever quiets it, even briefly.
- No scholar has named it properly. Too few have ever been seen and survived comparing notes.

**DM note:** This is the truth nobody in [[Thalyra]] — least of all [[Corwin Sallow]] — will ever piece together. See [[Mella]] for the case in question. The party never needs this to run the encounter; it exists for the DM, and as quiet foreshadowing if the larger Cycle plot ever surfaces: proof the Umbral Soul is bleeding through earlier, and more intimately, than the Gilded city wants to believe.
