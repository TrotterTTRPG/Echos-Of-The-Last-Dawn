**The Height of the Umbral Soul**
Entropy, Renewal, and Revelation
Entire Umbral Soul lasts ~2,000 years

When the Umbral Soul rises, it does not simply _influence_ the world — it _eclipses_ it. A second, half-seen realm overlays [[Verda]]: a reflection of the world’s soul turned inside out. The air grows heavy, the land darkens, and from the seams of this merging crawl beings not meant for light. They are not wholly evil — only _incompatible_ with the living world.

Resources wither and vanish; crops fail, and rivers run thin. The dead no longer sleep. Spirits and remnants of forgotten ages — wraiths, revenants, and memory-born beasts — drift freely, drawn to what life remains. Creatures of instinct and nightmare flourish: the soul-hungry and the soulless. Even beings that normally dwell deep within the underdark — darklings, skulks, shadar-kai — rise to claim the surface as their own.

Just as these creatures retreat into the earth during the reign of [[The Effulgent Soul]] Soul, so too do mortals in the age of the Umbral Soul. Whole empires collapse or withdraw behind the last fortified walls of [[Dornthalm]], where the living endure as the earth itself sheds its skin. Even there, whispers of the other world slip through stone and sanctum alike — dreams that bite, and shadows that think.

Yet for all its horror, the Umbral Soul is not chaos without purpose. It is the season of truth -  when masks of civility fall and those of strong spirit, courageous heart, clever mind are tested and reborn. Those who survive the Umbral Soul do not emerge unchanged; they become the seeds of the next Effulgent Soul, tempered and enduring.

*Environmental Signs*
- Forests shift and pulse with phosphorescent veins
- The dead rise — not mindless, but purposeful — as hunger, emotion, or memory sustain them.
- The stars warp and multiply, forming constellations never before seen.
- Magic grows primal, unpredictable, and tied to instinct or emotion; the gods fall silent.

*Cultural Tendencies*
- Civilizations collapse inward; survivors gather within [[Dornthalm]], the last bastion of the living.
- Truth is valued above comfort; art turns prophetic and grimly honest.
- Great heroes and terrible tyrants emerge — twin reflections of the same burning soul.
