**The Height of the Effulgent Soul**
Creation, Harmony, and Stagnation
Entire Effulgent Soul lasts ~2,000 years

When the Effulgent Soul ascends, [[Verda]] gleams like paradise. Rivers shimmer with mirrored skies, crops ripen without fail, and every wound — of body or heart — is healed. In this time, death itself ceases. Souls linger, sustained by the world’s overflowing vitality. None starve, none suffer, none truly end.

Civilizations flourish; cities of crystal and gold rise higher than ever before. Magic obeys will effortlessly. Empathy, logic, and art reach their apex. Yet beneath that brilliance lies stillness — the calm of a pond that no longer stirs.

Without death, passion fades. Innovation slows. Love becomes endless and therefore weightless. Even memory dulls, for there is no contrast to joy. The Effulgent Soul’s blessing becomes a slow suffocation — a peace so total it begins to erase the self.

*Environmental Signs*
- All life flourishes; even barren places teem with verdant growth.
- The sun lingers too long in the sky — day stretches, twilight endures, and true night vanishes.
- Spirits and undead dissipate instantly; nothing truly ends, only renews.
- The land itself hums with harmonious vibration, a song of abundance that never ceases.

*Cultural Tendencies*
- Vast cities and academies dominate
- Warrior cultures vanish; diplomacy and artistry govern society.
- A subtle melancholy pervades — the ache of souls that have forgotten how to yearn.
- Creation continues endlessly, but meaning drains from it.
