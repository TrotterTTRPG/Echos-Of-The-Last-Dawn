**Why don't the [[Gods Master List]] interfere?**

Mortal existence is meant to be shaped by mortal choices. If the gods intervened, the outcome would no longer be authentic — it would strip mortals of agency. Long ago, the gods discovered that the cycle of [[The Effulgent Soul]] and the [[The Umbral Soul]] within [[Verda]] is not theirs to control. It belongs to something deeper — the foundation of creation itself. If gods interfere, the outcome is corrupted. Mortals become puppets, and the cycle collapses into chaos.

---

**How does this affect the World?**

Clerics and paladins are taught that their power is a trust — mortals must act as hands of the divine where gods cannot tread.

Holy texts sometimes include cryptic lines like:
- _“The loom is mortal, the thread divine, but the weaving is ours to do.”_
- _“The dawn cannot break if no man wakes to see it.”_

Priests might argue heatedly about this: some think the gods are testing mortals, others that they are forbidden, others that they are simply unwilling.
