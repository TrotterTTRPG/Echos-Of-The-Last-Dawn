The world exists in a rare gilded age — centuries of peace after devastating wars, with populations and civilizations flourishing. But the world is inherently cyclical: ages rise and fall, die, and are reborn. The current gilded age is unnaturally prolonged; the natural process of decline has been suppressed.

A faction of influential, seemingly benevolent figures works to keep the age going. Their most powerful member knows that preventing the world’s natural decline requires a ritual fueled by the life force of exceptionally strong mortals — and they are subtly preparing the adventurers to become those participants.

Meanwhile, other factions, outwardly malicious or destructive, act to destabilize the world, accelerate the imbalance, or oppose those who prolong the age. The adventurers begin their journey unaware of this cycle. Over time, they encounter clues — increasing monsters, unnatural events, cryptic texts — and must decide where they stand: do they help maintain the gilded age at great personal cost, let the cycle proceed naturally, or find some alternative path?

The campaign is built around exploration, moral ambiguity, and the slow revelation of the world’s cyclical nature. The “villains” may be right, the “heroes” may be wrong, and the adventurers’ choices can shape the rebirth or the ruin of the world itself.

**The Cycle**

All living things in [[Verda]] — from the smallest blade of grass to the towering spires of the capital — bear within them two halves of the same essence: [[The Effulgent Soul]] and [[The Umbral Soul]]. These twin forces are reflections of the world’s own spirit, which shifts in long, slow ages like the turning of cosmic seasons. Every two millennia, Verda’s balance tilts — the Light Soul waxing to its zenith or waning as the Dark Soul ascends.
