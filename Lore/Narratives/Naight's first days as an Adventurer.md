[[Naieht]] was lucky by all accounts during the his [[Adventurer's Docket]] teams formation. He was placed in [[Darric Flamehand]]'s group.
[[Darric Flamehand]] was a well respected wizard, and knew a lot about magical spell components and crafting. The group had gone on a few excursions outside of the city on behalf of the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] to look for a variety of of items.

For the most part, there wasn't much interesting on these excursions. There were a few attacks on the caravan by roaming beasts and monsters, but nothing out of the ordinary. Some of the stronger monsters were easily subdued by [[Darric Flamehand]] and the powerful fire magics he wielded.

One thing that the group learned early on when working with [[Darric Flamehand]] was that it was usually best to not engage with him unless absolutely necessary, and when he gave an order it was not worth trying to argue. One of the original group members, a human, [[Brian Willow]] was promptly ejected from the group on the first day for correcting [[Darric Flamehand]] when he said that a full grown [[Sediment Snail]] shell could be harvested for a gram of [[Copper Rhudenem]]. Apparently you can only get 1/4 of a gram from a full grown [[Sediment Snail]], but [[Darric Flamehand]] got so angry, the group thought [[Brian Willow]] was going to end up a pile of ash. 

Thankfully, [[Darric Flamehand]] only ejected him from the group, and demanded that the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] permanently disbar him. After that everyone just gave [[Darric Flamehand]] space, which he seemed to prefer.

Besides [[Darric Flamehand]] who was in charge of the group, the rest of the group was made up of folks like yourself who had just recently joined the [[Adventurer's Docket]]. 
[[Alden Grutt]] was a dwarf from a mountain village north of [[Thalyra]]. He liked to talk - a lot 
[[Hesta Frough]] was a bookish elf, and aspiring apprentice. She was good friends with [[Brian Willow]], but did not seem too upset to be the only other wizard to be studying under [[Darric Flamehand]]. 
[[Sven Jehnn]] was a middle aged human ranger, and a good navigator. He seemed kind, but like to focus on the work or the road.

(4 days ago) On the 2rd day of Misuthar (2nd month of the year, but closer to early March season wise), [[Naieht]]'s group took a carriage to the woods northeast of [[Thalyra]]. It was about a days ride out. The group was looking for [[Blitz Lizards]] and scouting for [[Rockwood Trees]]. [[Darric Flamehand]] was quite good at finding spots for the hibernating [[Blitz Lizards]], which slept at the base of larger trees under the snow and leaves. The group met their quota quickly. [[Sven Jehnn]] had scouted for [[Rockwood Trees]] and found a small copse of them. This was not good enough for [[Darric Flamehand]] though and he wanted to continue gathering and scouting.

The problem started when [[Darric Flamehand]] had told the group that he had found a cave that would be full of [[Blitz Lizards]] that did not go into hibernation. Apparently they are much more valuable for alchemy when they are plump. 

The cave [[Darric Flamehand]] led you through was not full of [[Blitz Lizards]], but much larger lizards. [[Darric Flamehand]] claimed that the [[Blitz Lizards]] would be farther in. The larger lizards were very dangerous. Dangerous enough that even [[Darric Flamehand]] was weary of them. He called them baby [[Hookmaw]]. The cave was full of them, but [[Darric Flamehand]] and [[Sven Jehnn]] were taking the group through tunnels that were mostly free of them.

2 1/2 hours or so in, and [[Darric Flamehand]]'s real intention became apparent. There was a cavern filled with these [[Hookmaw]] eggs, and [[Darric Flamehand]] wanted one. Problem was, they were huge and he needed someone very strong to carry it out. The group naturally chose [[Naieht]] for the task.

Unfortunately on the way out, a few [[Baby Hookmaw]] became aggressive and the combat drew more and more. By the end of it, the group was rushing out of the cave - which was now full of frenzied baby [[Hookmaw]].

The group barely made it out of the cave, but was able to get to the carriage and loaded the egg before starting the journey back to [[Thalyra]].

When the group was about 1/2 way there [[Sven Jehnn]] noticed an extremely giant creature on the horizon, moving towards the carriage. It was one of the [[Hookmaw]] lizards, but grown up.

[[Darric Flamehand]] commanded that the horses be run at full gallop to the city. Even with the significant lead and moving at break necks speed - the [[Hookmaw]] caught up. It began flinging boulders as the carriage by raking its massive claws through the earth like it was water. 

Thankfully as it got close, so too did the city. The city guard started firing upon it with magical turrets, and opened the gates for the carriage to enter.

Upon entering [[Captain Seressa Varn]] disabled the carriage, and forced all to lay on the ground to await arrest.

Eventually, the [[Hookmaw]] was turned away.

[[Captain Seressa Varn]] was alarmed when an Eladrin Elf identified the creature, and they discovered the egg.

[[Captain Seressa Varn]] sent [[Darric Flamehand]] back out under the supervision of [[High Captain Durel]] to get the egg away from the city.

[[Captain Seressa Varn]] brought the group to the [[The Deephold]] jail where the group was separated and questioned.

It was a short stay however, as the group was eager to turn on [[Darric Flamehand]]. (You can choose how [[Naieht]] would have responded to the questioning)

As you were being released, [[Captain Seressa Varn]] told you that the group would be disbanded - but she knew of a group she could re-assign you to.
