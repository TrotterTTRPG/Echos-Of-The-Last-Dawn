**Narrator (Jon):**  The many snows of the winter have mostly melted as the season has started to wane into spring, but is last vestiges still rest upon the roof tops of the Gold District.
A frost forms on the window as [[Liora Vaelthir]] watches the road from high above in her office at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]]. 

The fine lines on her face hint only faintly at her true longevity.

Her office appears more modest than what would be expected from someone like Liora - someone of such prosperity and many storied years. Those without an eye for true luxury might even suspect that [[Liora Vaelthir]]'s office was normal, and drab. But those who know better, the ones with a sense of true quality and value would see that the seemingly plain desk was carved from Mirthwood, a timber thought extinct in most of [[Verda]], and the only timber that rivals Rockwood in strength. The drab quill in her inkpot is plucked from the wing of a [Coautl](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16832-couatl) and the small terrarium on her desk holds a single, perpetually flowering plant kept alive by the subtle enchantments rather than soil or water. The unassuming trappings of her workspace are countless small testaments to wealth so old it has long outgrown it garishness.

Liora wipes the frost from the glass and looks down upon the carriages bustling in the streets far below. The etched dome of the [[Hall of Accord]], which usually scatters sunlight in prismatic arcs, now lies buried beneath a thick quilt of snow. A flurry of guards and drivers move with quiet urgency, unloading and organizing a concord of ladies and gentlemen. Many brush away the snow to clear a path, leading their patrons through the drifts toward the Hall’s grand entry.

*Knocking*

**Assistant (Jon)**: "Lady Vaelthir, I received word that the last of the assembly has arrived and will be seated shortly. Do you need anything?"

**Liora (Bria):**  “No, thank you. You may tell them I will join them in less than a turn of the glass. Have them use this time for proper introductions - and to discuss their recent findings. I want any phenomena noted and compiled. I will require that list before the address. That is all.”

**Narrator (Jon)**: Liora leaves the window and opens a drawer in her desk, producing something like an hourglass. She turns it over and places it upon the desk, watching as grains of dreamsand shimmer through the glimmerglass, grain by grain vanishing in one bulb and reappearing in the other.

When just half the dreamsand had fallen, Liora entered the great Concord Chamber within the [[Hall of Accord]]. Although the chamber was filled to the brim with representatives from every major house, faction, and municipality, the vast room fell silent as Liora entered and took her place at the podium. The address was brief, but its impact would eventually echo through every corner of the realm.

She announced the formation of a common trust, funded by herself and several unnamed benefactors. Its purpose: to finance a series of stipends for organizations capable of responding to the rising tide of destabilizing events across [[Advelde]]. Any individual or institution summoned could apply—but only those who proved their effectiveness in addressing issues like those discussed that evening would see continued patronage.
