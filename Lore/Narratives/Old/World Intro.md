**Bria:**
The planet of [[Verda]] drifts quietly through space with it's two moons [[Selara]], and [[Umbrya]] in tow. 
It follows it's brothers and sisters around their sun, [[Cendralis]].

And on [[Verda]], we find the continent of [[Advelde]], and the canvas of our story.

We begin in a time known as [[The Gilded Age]] - which stands in stark contrast to the age it was born from, known as [[The Shattered Age]]. [[The Gilded Age]] is old, but there are a scant few individuals that remember it's beginnings.

[[The Shattered Age]] was the undoing of civilization, and the end of what was known as [[The Crystal Age]]. 

Constant and devastating conflicts destroyed the previous civilizations, and its survivors scattered throughout the realm.
[[The Shattered Age]] was long, and so thoroughly devastating that the collective knowledge from [[The Crystal Age]] was effectively lost.
 
 [[The Gilded Age]] that rose from its ashes is still a meager shadow of the civilizations that came before. 
 All pillars of the societies that came before - History, Technology, Agriculture and Arcana were almost completely lost in the Shattering Wars.
 
 And they would have been completely lost if it wasn't for the City of [[Dornthalm]], the only standing city from before the Shattering Wars.
  
**Jon:**
[[Dornthalm]] is the most northern of the major cities in [[Advelde]], and is so old, that it's origins and architects have long been forgotten. 
The materials and design that make up it's architecture are also unlike anything possible in contemporary times. The massive main structure in Dornthalm is a colosseum like tower with an obelisk at it's center. It's decorated with fortifications - and the stonelike material that comprises it all is so formidable it is said to be unbreakable. 

Within [[Dornthalm]]'s walls lies the [[Dornthalm Vaults]], or as it is more colloquially know - The Lexicon.
It is said that The Lexicon holds all knowledge. Not just the knowledge of the time, but a complete accountance of all that ever has been known.
Within the Lexicon is the [[Library of Luminence]], It makes contemporary tombs and scripts available to the masses.
However the Luminance holds only what has thus far been parsed from the [[Obsidian Stacks]] - which is relatively nothing.

The [[Obsidian Stacks]] is a enormous warehouse that contains the lost knowledge of [[The Crystal Age]]. It is so voluminous, that only a fraction of the content contained has been sorted through let alone deciphered. 

Unfortunately, most of the texts within the [[Obsidian Stacks]] are written in a seemingly endless amounts of lost languages. It falls upon the shoulders of the many linguists of [[Dornthalm]] and the surrounding areas to crack these languages before the contents of these tomes and scrolls can be transcribed, and sent to the Luminance to be studied. The known magics to comprehend languages proves ineffective for most of the vaults contents.

Even so, [[The Gilded Age]] has benefited greatly from the knowledge trickling out of [[Dornthalm]].
Material science, Medicine, Alchemy, Chemistry, Hard Religion and most schools of the Arcane were born from the Obsidian Stacks, and makes it the most precious resource of [[The Gilded Age]].

Many different groups and societies have formed around these areas of knowledge, and the economy of [[Dornthalm]] and the surrounding areas are generally in support of these academics.

Where [[Dornthalm]]s history is shrouded in mystery, the three other major cities have risen from the ashes and are themselves the greatest achievements of the Gilded age. 

**Bria:**
The sister cities of [[Iridia]] and [[Velrosk]] were founded after the shattering wars.

[[Iridia]] lays upon the the [[Horn Islands]] off of the East Coast. It is known by most as a place of luxury, with white sand beaches, tranquil hot springs and a vibrant artsy culture. It is known as a playground for the fortunate, a place that the common and wealthy alike travel to and many dream of it in great fondness.

What most also know, is that [[Iridia]] sits within the largest and most known sources of artifacts, some perhaps even predating [[The Crystal Age]]. Ruins of some ancient time lay around [[Iridia]], and many adventurers have made a living hunting and gathering these artifacts, and selling them mostly to buyers in [[Dornthalm]].

These artifacts were the original draw to [[Iridia]]. Adventurers were flocking there trying to take advantage of the high bounties on artifacts. By a large amounts more artifacts have come out of [[Iridia]] then anywhere else. It is said that there are more artifacts under the [[Horn Islands]] than the whole of [[Dornthalm]].
Eventually though, all the easy to find artifacts had been found and sold, leaving only the very difficult and dangerous to reach artifacts in and beneath the ruins. The vast majority of adventurers packed up, leaving Aridia and only the most hardened and motivated adventurers.

This created vacancy in almost all of the housing that had been built in what seemed like an endless gold rush at the height of this time.
 
This is when [[Iridia]] created what has become the largest tourism destination in the realm, and the culture around it as it now dominates it's economy.

**Jon:**
[[Velrosk]] is the older of the sister cities, and was the first major city to have been founded in [[The Gilded Age]]. The vast forests of Rockwood that once stood there were perfect for the construction of fortifications, ships, land vehicles and housing. 

The biggest hurdle modern civilization had to overcome was the hostility of the wilds. Towns and Settlements become targets for roaming monsters, so it was a great feat when [[Velrosk]] created the first successful system outside of [[Dornthalm]] to house a large population of peoples in safety. Though their ingenuity and ability to plan for tomorrow, [[Velrosk]]ans tend to be a people with a live for today mentality. Some of the largest musical, food and trade festivals in the realm happen here, and the dock yard districts have some of the best bars and entertainment to be found. The shipyards of [[Velrosk]] remain the largest in [[Advelde]], and they remain the only city capable of creating Rockwood vessels. 

Over the ages, [[Velrosk]] grew into a merchant economy due to their ability to create crafts capable or braving the wilds of [[Advelde]]. They in most part created the highway system of the realm, and continue to maintain it. They supply transportation to the realm and are very important in securing the trade routes. There is a saying that if you can't find it in [[Velrosk]], it isn't for sale.
It was the lucrative artifact trade that grew [[Velrosk]]'s economy to what it is now. As artifacts became more and more valuable, [[Velrosk]] was eager to establish a system for moving goods between [[Iridia]] and the rest of the realm, which they did with great success. 

The success and cooperation between the three major cities created what we now call [[The Gilded Age]]. It is an age that is defined by overcoming hardship, and putting in place examples of humanity at it's best. [[The Gilded Age]] is called such because it predates what is thought to be an inevitable Golden age.
