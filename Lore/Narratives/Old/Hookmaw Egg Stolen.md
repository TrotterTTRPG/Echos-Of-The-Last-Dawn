In the forest North East of [[Thalyra]], the pyromancer [[Darric Flamehand]] kneels down and runs his hands along the edge of an enormous footprint large enough that he could take a bath in it with a few friends if it were filled.  

Even though the print was old - maybe a few months or so by his estimation, he felt a cold sweat on his brow and under his robes. As quickly as the sweat would collect, it would just as quickly evaporate. The heat of his skin which was always hot to the touch was even more ablaze right now. He pulled at his robes, and a thick steam steadily drifted upward.

He was equal parts excited and scared. A combination of emotions he lived for and thrived in.

*This could be big* he thought to himself as he stood up - but he was considering not the size of the creature, but what it could mean for him specifically.  

He composed himself and glanced through the thin gap between the young damaged trees where he stood. He saw bits of movement in the distance where the rest of his company was doing as he ordered - brushing aside cold wet leaves at the base of trees in search of hibernating [[Blitz Lizards]]. They had already collected far more that what was requested, and had even found a copes of [[Rockwood Trees]] on top of that, but [[Darric Flamehand]] never did the bare minimum. Nor was he one to let an opportunity for extra praise and reward go to waste.

He was careful to move away without being noticed by the others. If any of them were to come talk to him or follow him, they would see the tracks for themselves, and would surely want to leave immediately.  But the survival instincts of these craven would only ruin this for him, so he followed the tracks and broken trees quietly as possible.

He traveled about two hundred yards or so, and came upon a sight he hoped that he might. It was a large cave - and went inside.

A short time after [[Darric Flamehand]] returned to the group, taking a path that swung wide of any tracks - while plotting the return journey.

When he arrived back, they were still searching for lizards.  "Group!", he yelled, getting all of their attention. "Come hither. Whilst you were poking around here - I've explored and identified an area quite fertile with [[Blitz Lizards]], and quite possibly a cave where they are currently active and plump due to the consistent temperatures deep within."

The group became excited. Perhaps they would finally be done. A few [[Blitz Lizards]] of in season quality were worth at least a dozen or so of these skinny hibernators. 

They congratulated [[Darric Flamehand]] and praised him as any good squire might witnessing the excellence of a master.  

[[Darric Flamehand]] led them towards the cave, being sure to avoid any sign of what they were walking towards.
