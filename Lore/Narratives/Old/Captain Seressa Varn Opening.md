Dawn comes to [[Thalyra]] like an order already given.

The bells of [[The Bronze Bastion]] mark the changing of the watch with mechanical precision, their sound carrying through stone corridors already beginning to smooth with the constant passage of boots. Pale morning light creeps through the narrow windows, catching on steel, leather, and banners rolled tight against the walls.

[[Captain Seressa Varn]] stands alone in the armory.

Her armor waits for her on its stand. Every piece has been polished, repaired, and maintained beyond what regulation requires. The steel bears a series of engraved hearts that have been scarred, repaired, and engraved again. Each one is a mark of devotion, not to glory, but to duty, to the city, and to the lives beyond these walls.

Seressa moves with practiced economy as she fastens the final strap of her cuirass. No flourish. No hesitation. Every buckle knows her hands as well as her hands know it.

As she secures the final clasp, her eyes catch on a shallow dent along the pauldron. Barely visible. Easily overlooked. One she refuses to repair.

Her fingers still.

The armory does not fade away so much as it gives way as the memory floods back to her.


An eastern alarm light in [[the Vigil Network]] had flared out of sequence - too bright, too fast. Not the steady pulse of an incursion alarm, but a sharp spike that suggested interference. Reports followed in fragments. Heat signatures where none should be. A pressure drop in an old service corridor beneath the inner wards. A breach, perhaps? Or maybe a fault in the alarm light itself?

The sector in question bordered sealed maintenance routes and decommissioned supply tunnels - places no civilian had cause to be, and no enemy should have reached without triggering half a dozen safeguards. Seressa chose containment over spectacle and dispatched a small squad instead of sounding a full city alarm.

6 Wardens - 4 Veterans and 2 recent transfers. Shields up. Spacing Tight. Orders clear.

They advanced into the lower corridors, where the construction was far less polished and the air smelled faintly of soot and damp iron. Emergency runes flickered along the walls, inconsistent but intact. No signs of forced entry. No movement ahead.

It was quiet. Too quiet.

No rats skittered along the edges of the passage.
No dripping water echoed from cracked stone.

The corridor felt emptied - as if the space itself were holding its breath. 

The first explosion shattered the corridor and dropped the ceiling behind them. The second collapsed the escape route. Seressa had seconds to choose: press forward toward the source, or pull back through falling stone.

She chose forward.

By the time reinforcements cut through the rubble, the flames were dying. The threat was gone. So were five of her soldiers. No witnesses. No enemy bodies. Only scorched stone, twisted metal, and the unmistakable geometry of a trap designed for disciplined responders.

The Captain's report out of [[The Golden Hall of Command]] would say her decision was sound given the information available.  
An official communication from the [[Hall of Seasons]] would later say it was unnecessarily decisive.  
The families of the fallen would say nothing at all.

Seressa exhales - slow, controlled - and the weight of her armor settles fully against her shoulders. The armory returns.

She turns to the narrow desk against the wall. Letters lie there in a neat stack, each sealed, each bearing official sigils. Each contains commendations folded around condolences and praise threaded carefully through concern. Words like "service", "legacy", and "earned rest" appear again and again. She knows what they're all pushing for - retirement, written politely enough to resemble gratitude.

She does not open them.

Instead, she sets her helm beneath her arm and steps into the corridor beyond. The fortress reacts to her presence as it always has. Guards straighten. Movements sharpen. Salutes rise unprompted. 

She descends toward the training yards, where new recruits wait to be taught what discipline costs and what hesitation buys. She no longer leads charges, but within the ranks of [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]], the Heart of the Watch still beats - steady, scarred, and unyielding.

And though unseen fractures spread through [[Thalyra]]’s foundations, it will not be Seressa Varn who turns away from them first.

Not while she still stands the watch.
