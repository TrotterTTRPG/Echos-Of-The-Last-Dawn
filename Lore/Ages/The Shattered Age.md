*The undoing of civilization and the end of [[The Crystal Age]]*

**Overview**
- The Shattered Age followed the collapse of [[The Crystal Age]], marking a long period of ruin and chaos.
- It was defined by constant and devastating conflicts — [[The Shattering Wars]].
- These wars destroyed nearly all organized civilization across [[Verda]], leaving only fragments of memory and ruin.
- It represents the darkest chapter in known history — the great reset of knowledge, power, and stability.

**Causes and Nature of the Conflict**
- The wars appear to have been civilizational, not external invasions — nations and factions turning upon one another.
- Originally sparked by struggles over dwindling resources and the desire for control as [[the Umbral Soul]] (dark era) consumed the world.
- Later, as resources became scarce, the conflict turned into a war for dominance and survival, driven by greed and fear rather than necessity.
- Magical warfare and divine interventions likely escalated the devastation, rendering entire regions uninhabitable.

**Consequences and Impact**
- The undoing of civilization — cities fell, kingdoms vanished, and knowledge was lost.
- All major pillars of society collapsed:
- History was erased or fragmented.
- Technology and engineering knowledge vanished.
- Agriculture systems failed, returning most survivors to a hunter-gatherer state.
- Arcane study and magical practice were nearly extinguished.
- Most of the world’s population perished or became nomadic refugees.
- The survivors scattered across [[Advelde]], forming small, isolated communities without shared culture or literacy.
- The environment itself was scarred — once lush lands became barren, rivers ran dry, and unnatural creatures began to roam freely.

**Legacy and Recovery**
- The City of [[Dornthalm]] was the only known city to survive the Shattering Wars intact.
- Its mysterious construction materials and architecture made it impenetrable.
- It became the repository of lost knowledge — home to the [[Dornthalm Vaults]] (The Lexicon).
- What remains of [[The Crystal Age]] lies buried in the [[Obsidian Stacks]], vast archives of indecipherable language and forgotten lore.
- The gradual recovery of knowledge from these archives ushered in [[The Gilded Age]], restoring fragments of civilization.

**Cultural and Historical Memory**
- Few beings alive in [[The Gilded Age]] have any direct memory of the Shattered Age; it lies over 600 years in the past.
- To most, it’s a mythic cautionary tale — a reminder of what unchecked ambition and greed can cause.
- Scholars debate whether it was the result of mortal folly, divine retribution, or cosmic imbalance.
- The lingering scars of the Shattered Age — both physical and spiritual — still echo across [[Verda]], visible in ruins, monsters, and lost relics.
