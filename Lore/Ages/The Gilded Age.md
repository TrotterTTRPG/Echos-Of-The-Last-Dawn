- *Parent Age*: [[The Effulgent Soul]]
- *Duration*: ~600+ years
- *Defining Traits*: Prosperity, harmony, open trade, and abundance of nature
- *Societal Mood*: Peaceful, complacent, trusting
- *Mystical Underpinning*: A world seemingly self-sustaining — untouched by scarcity or decay

**Overview**
- The Gilded Age marks the current era of the world — a time of unprecedented peace, prosperity, and abundance.
- It has lasted for at least six centuries, long enough that most living beings know only comfort and stability.
- The era falls within [[the Effulgent Soul]], a larger age of radiant harmony and spiritual illumination that has defined the last millennium of mortal history.

**The Nature of Prosperity**
- The world thrives under an almost unnatural equilibrium. Crops grow freely, beasts are plentiful, and sickness is rare.
- Cities such as [[Thalyra]] and [[Velrosk]] sustain themselves not through industry or toil but through the land’s effortless bounty.
- Even sprawling metropolises can subsist on hunting and gathering, as if nature itself bends to the will of civilization.
- Farms still exist, but more as a matter of comfort and preference than survival:
	- Horses are bred for travel and companionship, not necessity.
	- Gardens grow medicinal herbs, spices, and fruits rather than staple crops.
	- Livestock are rare; cows and pigs are seen as curiosities of a bygone age. Some are kept for ease rather than necessity.
- Trade and craftsmanship are driven by luxury and artistry rather than scarcity. Goods are made to beautify, not merely to sustain.

**Social Conditions**
- Poverty, while still present, is relative and gentle. Even the poorest live in safety and health, lacking little beyond luxury or status.
- Security is lax across the realm. The long peace has made most cities open and trusting:
- [[Thalyra]]’s great walls stand open, their gates rarely closed to travelers.
- Guards patrol more for show than necessity, a symbol of stability rather than vigilance.
- Violence and hunger are almost mythical. Bandits are rare, monsters rarer still.

**Cultural Effects**
- Faiths and philosophies emphasize gratitude, serenity, and perfection of the self rather than struggle or penance.
- Art, architecture, and magic flourish. Cities gleam with artistry and enchantment — marble streets that stay clean, lanterns that never dim, and fountains that never run dry.
- Yet beneath the surface, some whisper that this prosperity feels too perfect — that the world’s balance is artificial, or perhaps borrowed.
