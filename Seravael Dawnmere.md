**Title:** Senior Desk Officer at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra ]] 
**Race:** High Elf  
**Gender**: Female
**Age:** 187  

**Appearance:**
- Tall, poised, and deliberately still — she does not fidget
- Silver-blonde hair worn in a tight, architectural knot
- Sharp grey eyes that make sustained eye contact feel like an assessment
- Impeccably pressed deep charcoal uniform with brass buttons
- No jewelry except a single thin gold band on her right hand — institutional, not personal

**Personality:**
- Composed under any circumstance
- Precise in speech; does not use two words where one will do
- Reads people quickly and conceals that she is doing so
- Holds herself to a standard of professionalism that some find cold and others find reassuring
- Has never raised her voice in a professional setting

Seravael Dawnmere has worked the desks of the Crown Bank for sixty years and has no intention of leaving. She is not ambitious in the conventional sense. She wants her desk, her ledger, and the quiet authority of being the person a client must persuade. She is exceptionally good at this, and she knows it.
