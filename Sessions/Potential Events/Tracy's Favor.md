**Location:** Thalyra — Gold District ([[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] → [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]])

**Level/Tone:** Low-combat, social/stealth investigation. Optional tension spike if the party blows their cover.

**Involved NPCs:** [[Tracy]], [[Chad Braxton]], [[Bolo]]
## 1. THE HOOK — Tracy's Request

Tracy normally wouldn't bring this to strangers. She'd go to [[Aranemin Zylris]], [[Ereykos Phirona]], or [[Velagrys Faro]] since they know Chad. But Aranemin and Velagrys are mid-shift at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]], and Ereykos just got roped into covering *Chad's* shift tonight, which is the entire problem. She's rattled enough that she's willing to pay outsiders instead of sitting with the not-knowing.

She finds the party somewhere plausible fidgeting with her jewelry before she even opens her mouth.

**Read or paraphrase:**

> "Okay, so — hi, sorry, this is going to sound insane, but I need, like, a favor? A paid favor. I'd ask Ereykos but Chad just dumped his whole shift on him tonight, which — see, that's the thing. Chad *never* actually works his shifts. He always finds a way to blow them off and come see me. Always. So when he tells me he's 'busy' tonight? That's not busy. That's a lie. He's done this before. I just — I need someone to follow him. Not, like, confront him. Just *see*. I'll pay you. I just need to know."

If asked why she doesn't do it herself: she'll admit, a little sheepishly for once, that Chad knows her face, her walk, her perfume — she's "not exactly subtle" — and that showing up herself always turns into a screaming match before she learns anything useful.

**Payment:** 20 gp up front, 30 gp on completion (adjust to your table's economy). She'll pay it "with anything" if the news is bad — she's not haggling, she's coping.

**What she wants specifically:** confirmation of whether Chad is meeting someone tonight, and if the party can manage it, *who* and *why*.

## 2. THE TAIL — Following Chad

Chad leaves the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] near dusk, ditching Ereykos with the intake log exactly like everyone predicted. He is not being careful, because it has never once occurred to him that he needs to be. He's walking fast, sunburnt and irritated rather than furtive.

- A single group Stealth check (or Stealth vs. his passive Perception, ~10) to stay unnoticed through the Gold District streets.
- If someone rolls badly, have a market cart or street performer create a near-miss instead of blowing the whole thing immediately; save the real tension for the wine bar.

He heads straight for the [[Clandestine Vineyards & Cellar]] and is shown, by name, to a secluded table in a corner of the patio.

## 3. THE MEETING — Fully Scripted Scene

The private room is stone and velvet, low light, one bottle already breathing on the table. Chad is visibly the more nervous of the two people in the room, which is unusual for him.

[[Bolo]] is already seated when he arrives. She doesn't stand.

Read the party the option:
- They can eavesdrop from just outside the door/curtain (requires a Stealth or Perception-adjacent check to position and hear without being seen) - DC 15
- They can simply observe from a distance that Chad is meeting a woman without hearing a word - No check needed
- They can try to get a table (requires a Persuasion check) - DC 17
### — SCRIPT —

**CHAD:** _(dropping into the seat across from her, too loud for the room)_ Bolo! Hey. Sorry, sorry, had to shake a guy at the Hall, you would not believe the week I've had —

**BOLO:** Chad Braxton ... You're late.

**CHAD:** I'm — okay, I'm _a little_ late. Did you order? Please tell me you ordered the '19.

**BOLO:** _(doesn't answer that)_ The Gentleman sent me.

**CHAD:** _(the smile drops half a notch)_ Oh. Oh, great. Fantastic. Love a surprise Gentleman update.

**BOLO:** You still owe him.

**CHAD:** I _know_ I still owe him, Bolo, I don't need a courier for that, I've got it handled —

**BOLO:** You don't.

**CHAD:** _(laughs, too fast)_ Okay, "handled" is a strong word, but — listen, I'm _good_ for it, obviously, my family's got more gold than got sense, I just — the thing is, I can't exactly walk up to my dad and go "hey, quick loan, no questions," because my dad _is_ the questions. He's literally made of questions. He's the Head of Accounting. Asking him for money without an explanation is like — asking a dragon to not notice you took a coin.

**BOLO:** _(a long pause, studying him)_ My employer isn't The Gentleman. But he's willing to pay The Gentleman off. All of it. Quietly, it matters.

**CHAD:** _(visibly relieved)_ Okay. Okay, great, see, this is why I like you, you're a _problem-solver_ —

**BOLO:** In exchange for access.

**CHAD:** Access to — access to what?

**BOLO:** Your father's records. The bank's. Funding requests, ledgers, whatever crosses his desk.

**CHAD:** _(the smile falters for the first time)_ Whoa, whoa. That's — okay, that's not "collaborative problem," that's "get me disowned and also probably arrested."

**BOLO:** No one has to know it was you.

**CHAD:** Everyone will know it was me. I'm the only person alive who's _in_ that house. He doesn't leave paperwork lying around, he doesn't talk in his sleep, he _files things._ This is not a guy who loses track of a page.

**BOLO:** Then don't lose one. Copy it. Return it.

**CHAD:** _(rubbing his face)_ Who even is this for? Who's asking?

**BOLO:** You wouldn't know him.

**CHAD:** I feel like I'm owed a name.

**BOLO:** I owe you nothing *(a pause)* But, if you insist, he goes by Aelan.

**CHAD:** Never heard of him.

**BOLO:** I told you that you wouldn't.

**CHAD:** _(quiet, for once)_ Bolo. This is my dad.

**BOLO:** _(unmoved)_ And this is your debt. Three days.

**CHAD:** Three — that's not — Bolo, come on, three days isn't even enough time to figure out _how,_ let alone —

**BOLO:** Three days. After that, my employer stops offering, and The Gentleman is left to collect however The Gentleman collects.

**CHAD:** _(swallows)_ What does that mean?

**BOLO:** You don't want to find out.

_(She stands. She does not finish her wine. She leaves a coin on the table and walks out without another word, leaving Chad alone, staring at the glass.)_

**CHAD:** _(to no one, several seconds later)_ ...Great meeting. Love that for me.

---
### Eavesdropping check

Suggest a DC scaled to your table (12–15 is reasonable) to hear clearly through the door/curtain without being made — Bolo is quiet by nature and half-listening for exactly this kind of intrusion the whole time. On a failed check, the party catches only fragments: "*three days*," "*your father's records*," a name that might be "Aelan" or might not have been heard right at all. Let a bad roll produce an incomplete picture rather than nothing — it keeps the branch interesting instead of just failing them.

## 4. IF THE PARTY INTERRUPTS

If anyone makes noise or is spotted mid-scene:

- **Bolo** doesn't flinch, doesn't argue, doesn't threaten. She simply stands, says nothing, and leaves — out a back route she clearly already scouted before Chad ever arrived. She is not the kind of person who gets caught flat-footed twice.
- **Chad** is furious in the loud, wounded, deeply personal way of someone who has just had the one adult conversation of his month ruined. He'll recognize the party. He will **not** explain what the meeting was about — he's scared and embarrassed, and Chad handles both by getting louder and less helpful, not more.
- The party gets nothing new from him tonight. Any further information has to come from Tracy's reaction to "he was meeting a woman and we startled them" (see below) or from independent digging into Bolo/Aelan later.
- Bolo now knows someone was tailing Chad. If you want this quest to have legs, this is your seed — Aelan's people quietly clock the party's faces going forward.

## 5. REPORTING BACK TO TRACY

- The party only reveals that he was with a woman and either doesn't reveal or doesn't know any the gambling debt part
	- They tell Tracy: Chad met a woman, alone, in a private room, and left without them being able to overhear anything.
	- Tracy goes very quiet, then says something like:
		- *"That's it. That's — okay. That's proof. That's basically proof."* She doesn't actually ask what would count as evidence — she's already decided.
	- She pays the party in full, thanks them in a small, deflated voice completely unlike her usual performative brightness, and leaves without her usual dramatics.
	- This is the version of Tracy that's actually hurt, not performing hurt.
- The party heard the conversation and tells her everything
	- Once she understands it's a gambling debt and not another woman, the shift is immediate and almost comic — relief hits her like a wave.
		- "Wait — *that's* it? He owes someone money? Oh my god. Oh my GOD. I thought — I really thought — okay. Okay! That's fine, that's fixable, that's not even that weird for him, honestly."
	- She'll want every detail: how much (the party may not know — Bolo never named a figure, just implied it was substantial), who Bolo was, who Aelan is, and the three-day deadline. She pays the party in full and, depending on how you want to play her, may immediately pivot to planning how *she's* going to fix Chad's problem for him — whether he wants her to or not.

Whether she then goes and confronts Chad about the debt, tries to intervene with [[Aelan]]'s people herself, or does something reckless is a great follow-up hook if you want to keep pulling this thread.
