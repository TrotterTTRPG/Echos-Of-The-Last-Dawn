[[Captain Seressa Varn]] is generally very tight-lipped but will loosen up and reveal more information when she has some alcohol in her.\

"You handled yourselves well. The Watch keeps record of that sort of thing. This evening, [[The Iron Tankard]], Bronze District, my treat"

She drinks something strong and plain, no frills.

**After One Drink:**
- She never wanted to be High Captain
	- "Command from a desk rots the spine."
	- "I'm more useful in the field."

**After Two Drinks:**
- The government of [[Thalyra]] is not as incorruptible as it appears.
	- "Coin certainly lubricates."
	- "[[Thalyra]] likes to pretend corruption doesn't live here."
- She despises [[The Adventurer's Docket]] system. 
	- "We already have the Watch. We don't need a second army with better branding."
	- "They answer to patrons, not the people."
	- "They're dispatched where the wealthy benefactors have 'interests' - trade routes, noble estates

**After 3 Drinks:**
- She acknowledges the truth about her demotion:
	- "They say I stepped down ... I was stepped down"
- She knows [[High Captain Durel]] was installed for political reliability.
	- "He doesn't argue."
	- "He doesn't question."
	- "He was sending wardens towards the Gold District when the threat was just outside the Bronze District."

**After 4 Drinks:**
- She admits she's tired.
	- "You can't win against rot. You can only cut it back."
- She sometimes thinks about retirement: not because she wants to, but because they want her gone.
- She worries that the Wardens are being quietly weakened.
	- Resources have been diverted to this adventurer;s Docket
	- Prestige is handed to Docket Adventurers.
	- Political oversight is increasing.


- "If someone offered you coin to look the other way ... would you?"
