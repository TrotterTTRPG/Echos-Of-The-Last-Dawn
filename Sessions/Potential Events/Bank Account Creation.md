[[Thalyric Crown Bank]]

**Durel**
- "You all went above and beyond what was expected"
- "Give me a couple hours to get word to the bank. Head there, let them know I sent you, and they'll open an account for you with your payment in it."

**Outside the Bank**
- Description
	- They approach the tallest building in [[Thalyra]] that overtops every government building, every noble estate, every civic landmark in the city
	- Built from pale, heavily veined stone with a severe, vertical design: clean lines, minimal ornamentation, the aesthetic of something that has no interest in being charming
	- The walls are visibly thick, especially on the lower floors
	- Narrow, deeply-set windows line the lower floors; the upper floors have larger windows
	- The main entrance consists of large, massive iron-banded doors, currently open during banking hours and guarded by guards distinctly not of [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]]

**Inside the Bank**
- Description
	- Upon entry, the party notices sound-dampening. Voices carry only so far, footsteps muffle without explanation
	- The vast main hall features high-arched ceilings that make them feel small
	- The interior is completely austere: pale grey and cream marble floors, no tapestries, no softness, no ornament
	- A long walk leads to a raised dais at the far end, which holds a semicircle of desks in burnished brass and dark walnut
	- Guards are stationed at appropriate intervals throughout the hall
- The party approaches one of the desks and is greeted by [[Brek Ironmantle]] - **Jon**
- After the initial conversation, the Senior Desk Officer [[Seravael Dawnmere]] appears and personally escorts the party upstairs to the second floor - **Bria**
	- The hall is lined with thick, dark walnut doors with brass handles and no nameplates on any doors
- The party is brought into the last office. This is [[Edran Braxton]]'s office, the largest on the second floor - **Jon**

**Account Opening Options**
- The party is presented with the opportunity to open bank accounts, either:
	- Individual accounts for each party member, or
	- A shared group account to pool resources
- Edran explains the account protections and benefits per the [[Thalyran Banking Rules]]
