## The Referral — Aelar's Friends Hand It Off

Pick up wherever the party last left the sample caps with the Eladrin. [[Aranemin Zylris]] (excitable, warm) does most of the talking; [[Ereykos Phirona]] (colder, practical) delivers the caveat; [[Velagrys Faro]] (calm) hands back a neatly written slip of findings.

What their testing at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] established — and all they'll commit to:
- The glow **isn't the mushroom.** It's *residual arcane saturation* — the caps have been drinking loose ambient magic wherever they grew.
- The **large and small are the same organism** at different maturities, not two species.
- The big mature caps hold that charge like a battery; the small ones are volatile and *spore-heavy.*

> [[Aranemin Zylris]], delighted: *"They're wonderful. They're basically little arcane sponges. We could write a paper — we're* going *to write a paper."*
> [[Ereykos Phirona]], flatly: *"What we can't tell you is what to* do *with them. We study why magic behaves. We do not brew. If you want potions, you want the Alchemical Arts — ask for Flintflower. Tell her Metamagic sent the sample ahead."*

*(If Aelar is present, lean into the friendly-rivalry warmth — they're still jealous he fought the hookmaw. The referral is genuine, not a brush-off.)*

## Arrival — The Hall of Alchemical Arts

A refined storefront in the Gold District that stops pretending to be refined the moment you're through the door. Bubbling glass, brass piping, glass skylights over the main labs — and, somewhere upstairs, a *faint muffled explosion* that nobody reacts to. The air is herbs, hot metal, and brewing reagents.

Apprentices in stained aprons produce basic potions at long benches. When the party mentions Violuma — or "the purple mushrooms Metamagic sent over" — an apprentice fetches the Head Alchemist rather than handling it herself.

**[[Belvyre Flintflower]]** comes down already talking. A halfling woman, brown curls in space-buns, enormous goggles shoved up on her head, a few teeth missing, and a fresh dusting of *three different colors* of powder on her sleeves. From [[Dornthalm]], and renowned for it.

> *"Purple mushrooms, purple mushrooms — where—* there *you are. Metamagic sent a note ahead, which means they've had a poke and given up, which means it's* interesting. *Right. Show me. Both sizes. Don't be shy, I've had worse than a mushroom explode in my face — twice." (a gap-toothed grin) "Once on purpose."*

## Belvyre's Verdict — What the Mushrooms Are

Belvyre confirms and *extends* the Metamagic Hall's theory — she's the one who turns "arcane sponge" into "here is what that's good for." Let her think out loud; she's fast and generous with information because she's excited, not because she's careless.

Her findings, given as a giddy running monologue:
- She names the fungus **Violuma** on the spot ("*violet* plus *lumen*, don't overthink it"). The big ones she dubs **"Crowns,"** the small ones **"Sparks."**
- The **Crowns** hold that arcane charge in dense flesh — a natural *stabilizer.* "Anything you brew that has to *last,* this is the thing that makes it last."
- The **Sparks** are packed with volatile, light-reactive spores. "These want to *reveal* things. Also they want to go off in your hand, so, respect."

**The demonstration (run it).** To prove the Sparks are light-reactive, Belvyre holds one up to an arcane lamp — and it **pops**, dusting the nearest party member (or her own goggles) in a cloud of glowing violet spore that clings and gently shines for a minute.

> *"—and* that, *my friends, is a Glowspore Draught trying to be born early. Harmless. Mostly. You'll glow in the dark tonight; enjoy it." (already scribbling) "The little ones mark things. The big ones steady things. Do you understand what you've been carting around in your pockets? This is a* menu."*

## What You Can Make

This is the heart of the scene: Belvyre lays out the options. **Hand the players the full detail from [[Custom Items]]** — recipes, gold values, and the crafting-budget tables — but here's her pitch, so you can run it live:

| What | From | Belvyre's one-liner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draught of Lucid Casting** *(common)* | 7 Crowns | "For your spellcasters. Keeps a spell from slipping when someone hits them. Cheap. Apprentice work." |
| **Glowspore Draught** *(common)* | 7 Sparks | "You throw it. Coats the target, everyone hits it easier until someone lands one. Good for the slippery ones." |
| **Crowncap Resin** *(reagent)* | 18 Crowns | "The good stuff, distilled. This is what makes the *big* potions possible." |
| **Sparkcap Dust** *(reagent)* | 18 Sparks | "Ground-up seeing-powder. Feeds anything to do with sight and scrying." |
| **Potion of Mind Reading / Clairvoyance** *(rare)* | 1 reagent unit | "Standard rares — I can do these in my sleep once you've got me the resin and dust." |
| **Potion of Sustained Sorcery** *(rare, custom)* | 2 vials Crowncap Resin | "Concentrate on *two* spells at once. Metamagic's little darlings would sell a kidney for this. **This is the one they want.**" |
| **Potion of Truesight** *(rare, custom)* | 2 pouches Sparkcap Dust | "See everything. Through the dark, through invisibility, through a liar's face. Expensive to make. Worth it." |

She's blunt about the trade-off, and she'll spell it out so the players feel the choice:

> *"Here's your problem, and it's a nice problem. You've got — what, fifty-odd of each? Refine nearly the whole lot into one of the fancy rares and you've spent your entire haul on a single bottle. Or spread it thin and get a fistful of common drafts. Or sell the raw caps and walk out with coin today. There's no wrong answer. There's just the answer you'll* wish *you'd picked. Take a day. Actually — take two, that's how long the rares take me anyway."*

*(Full numbers: [[Custom Items#Crafting Budget]]. Short version — the whole stock is worth roughly **510 gp of Crowns / 424 gp of Sparks** sold raw at Hall bounty, or a couple of Rare custom potions if fully refined, or ~7 common drafts of each if spread thin.)*

## Who Else Wants Them — The Politics

The reason this becomes a *recurring* thread and not a one-off shopping trip: three institutions want the same mushrooms, and the party is holding all of them.

- **The [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] (Belvyre).** Wants to *brew* — and, quietly, wants the party to bring Violuma to **her** first. Her standing grievance: the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] gets first pick of every exotic material in [[Thalyra]] and hands the Alchemists the "dregs." A novel reagent that came in through *her* door, not theirs, is a small, delicious victory.
- **The [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] (Aelar's friends, and above them [[Ilyana Corvess]]).** Wants to *study* the held arcane charge — and it's their research that makes the **Potion of Sustained Sorcery** possible. They'll pay a **bounty** for raw Crowns for their own experiments.
- **The [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]].** Doesn't know yet. If they catch wind of a new arcane-saturated organism, they may try to assert their usual first-claim on "exotic materials" — which is exactly what Belvyre is racing to get ahead of.

> Belvyre, lowering her voice for the first time: *"If Acuity hears the word 'novel reagent,' they'll have a form for it by lunch and a claim on it by supper. So. Do we understand each other? You found these. You brought them to* me. *Let's keep it that way, and I'll make it worth your while."*

## The Decision — Terms & Time

Belvyre's offer, all negotiable — she respects a sharp haggler:
- **She'll brew anything** on the menu for the labor fees in [[Custom Items]]; the party supplies the caps. Common drafts, same day. Rares, ~2 days each.
- **She'll buy raw** at (or a hair above) the Metamagic Hall's bounty rate if they'd rather have coin now.
- **Two small asks** (drop, keep, or trade them for a discount):
    1. She wants to keep **a few caps of each** for her own research and to try to *culture* them.
    2. She wants **first refusal** on any future Violuma the party finds — her edge over Arcane Acuity.
- **The cultivation hook:** she *really* wants to know **where they grew.** Violuma only takes root where raw magic bleeds into the ground — "a spell-scar, a ley-seep, a magitech leak, something. Find me the patch and I'll never have to ration this again." *(Callback: the party first found them where Aelar sketched them in Session 005.)*

Let the party split the haul however they like — sell some, brew some, hold some, hand a bounty of raw Crowns to the Metamagic friends. **Record what they keep vs. spend against the stock in [[Custom Items]].**

## DM Notes & Levers

- **This is a "reward the hoard" scene.** They've carried these caps for three sessions; the payoff is agency, not a fight. The fun is the *choice* — one flashy Rare vs. a spread of utility vs. fast coin — so make the trade-offs vivid and let them stew.
- **Quick resolution table for what the 51/53 stock becomes** is in [[Custom Items#Crafting Budget]]. Don't re-derive it at the table; open that page.
- **The three-way politics is the recurring engine.** Whichever Hall the party favors, the other two notice. Favoring Belvyre needles Arcane Acuity; selling raw to Metamagic warms that friendship (and Aelar's); if Arcane Acuity ever learns of Violuma, you have a jurisdiction squabble ready to drop.
- **The cultivation thread** turns a finite haul into a renewable one — and a reason to go back to where they found them. If you ever want Violuma to *mean* something larger, "grows only where magic bleeds through" is a quiet thread you can tie to bigger arcane-saturation lore later.
- **Keep Belvyre generous with knowledge.** She over-shares because she's thrilled, not sloppy. The information is free; the *labor* and the *caps* are the cost.
