**Type:** Personal thread / investigation follow-up (low-combat, social)

**Trigger:** [[Aulberos Lightmind]] pursues the name "Aelan" surfaced during [[Tracy's Favor]] — the fixer [[Bolo]] named **Aelan** as the party behind the Braxton ledger scheme.

**Purpose:** Give Aulberos something real to find when he asks after Aelan, without spending the in-person confrontation prematurely. This note meters the reveal and keeps Aelan remote for now.

---

## DM orientation — what's established vs. open

**Established (do not contradict):**
- [[Aelan]] is Aulberos's old friend — a fellow Firbolg druid he trained and worked beside for many years at [[The Cradle of Thorns]]. Aulberos urged him to leave when the order began selling its services; Aelan stayed and now sits on the ruling council of [[The Stonebloom Enclave]]. They have not seen each other in a very long time (centuries).
- In [[Tracy's Favor]], [[Bolo]] acts "for Aelan," offering to clear Chad's debt to [[The Gentleman]] in exchange for access to [[Edran Braxton]]'s bank ledgers, on a three-day deadline.

**Deliberately open (see [[Open Questions and Continuity]] — preserve these):**
- Whether Bolo's "Aelan" is *the same* Firbolg druid Aulberos knew is **not confirmed in play**. It is the intended intersection, but let the party *earn* the confirmation rather than handing it over.
- Bolo's employer's relationship to [[The Gentleman]] is unresolved. Nothing establishes that Aelan *is* the Gentleman, or that Bolo works for the Gentleman in this transaction.

**Design stance:** Keep Aelan **far, operating through proxies, this session.** The in-person reunion is a centerpiece of Aulberos's arc and should land as a deliberate future set-piece (ideally tied to travel toward the Cradle of Thorns), not a side-quest beat. Reward the *asking* with concrete direction, not a doorstep.

---

## THE TIERED REVEAL — what asking after it yields

Scale checks to your table. Let effort and good roleplay move him up the tiers; a single lucky roll shouldn't hand over everything.

**Tier 1 — Confirm the name & the connection** *([Savone] potentially??)*
- The name "Aelan" is tied to [[The Stonebloom Enclave]] / [[The Cradle of Thorns]]. Enough for Aulberos to be certain: this is *his* Aelan.
- The enclave still exists, and it still trades in influence and services — the rot he left over never healed.

**Tier 2 — Locate the operation, not the man** *(digging into [[Bolo]], following money, watching for her)*
- Bolo is a professional cut-out. She reports *outward* — toward the enclave's old territory, not to any Thalyra address.
- Direction, not a doorstep. Aulberos learns Aelan is behind the scheme but is nowhere near the city.
- **If they push Bolo directly:** she vanishes rather than fights (per [[Bolo]]), and now Aelan's people quietly clock the party's faces.

**Tier 3 — Who Aelan has become** *(old contacts — [[Sadiya]], caravan guides, nomads at [[Kareth's Crossing]]; or word sent toward the enclave)*
- Aelan is alive, senior, and *changed*: cooler, more mercenary than the friend Aulberos remembers.
- Seed one specific detail that stings — see options below. Give exactly one; save the rest.

**Stinger options:**
- Aelan has spoken of Aulberos by name, dismissively — "the one who ran off to play hermit."
- The ledger scheme isn't Aelan's first reach into Thalyra; the enclave has been quietly buying leverage in the capital for years.

---

## AFTERMATH & HOOKS

- However far Aulberos gets, aim to leave him **certain it's Aelan, pointed toward [[The Cradle of Thorns]], and unable to reach him yet.** A hook that pulls, not a wall.
- If the party antagonized Bolo, the enclave now watches them — a slow-burn complication independent of Aulberos's feelings.
- **Confrontation trigger for later:** hold the in-person Aelan for when the party has reason to travel toward the Cradle of Thorns, or when Aelan chooses to surface because Aulberos has become inconvenient. Aulberos himself is "not particularly driven to make good with the enclave, but if thrown into a situation with them would have strong conflicting feelings" (per [[Aulberos Lightmind]]) — let that ambivalence, not a revenge drive, carry the eventual scene.
