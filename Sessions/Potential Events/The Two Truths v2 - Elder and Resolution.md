### What you need to remember

- Sugarbend collects and extracts an addictive product from the pond. Reserves are already depleted. The equipment has been running less for ordinary reasons unrelated to the fairies, while the number of dependent fairies is growing.
- **Umbral secrecy:** the fairies disclose information only under extreme duress. Bringing the party across is illegal; they are already breaking the law. The elder expects useful results to make the people waiting there overlook the violation. Give only what the job requires; exact laws and prior offenses remain undefined.
- **Town mood:** desperate and barely functioning. Everyone’s Sweetpitch ration has been cut to supply the Umbral buyers; even these cuts have not cleared the backlog.
- The elder's first priority is the Umbral buyers. He also needs his working community to function. Fairy bands have attacked the factory exactly twice, during extended vacations.
- The fairies know little about this world. They recently connected their supply to the woman's operation. Raw cane does not produce the same effect.
- The woman squeezes out and discards sugarwater to make the cane burn very hot for her steam engine. She does not know the sugars collect downstream or become addictive.
- **DM truth:** a pond organism biologically/alchemically transforms ordinary sugars. Whether the woman's soul theory is correct remains unresolved. The sugar's established transformation does not settle that question.
- Waycaps carry fairies bodily to the Umbral. The elder sincerely expects them to work for the party. Instead, material creatures' souls detach into purgatory while their bodies remain.
- After the collapse, the elder and doctor assume accidental poisoning. They do not know the party traveled.
