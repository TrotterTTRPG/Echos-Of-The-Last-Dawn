The three Eladrin that [[Aelar Aurelius]] originally traveled to [[Thalyra]] with arrive at [[The Bronze Lantern]] once again.

There’s still that underlying jealousy when they speak to Aelar, but now it’s wrapped in admiration, curiosity, and just a hint of calculated friendliness.

---

[[Velagrys Faro]]
- Appearance
	- Her skin has a faint warm undertone
	- Her hair falls in loose waves and is the color of pale rose petals
	- Her eyes are a vivid emerald
- Personality
	- Calm, cool, collected
[[Aranemin Zylris]]
- Appearance
	- His skin is a warm copper color
	- His hair is short, and curly, and a dark auburn color
	- His eyes are a deep gold
- Personality
	- Excitable, warm
[[Ereykos Phirona]]
- Appearance
	- His skin is a cool bronze color
	- His hair is long, straight, and stunning crimson red
	- His eyes are a deep, dark brown
- Personality
	- Colder, more practical

---

[[Velagrys Faro]] subtly steers the conversation toward the party's future plans, asking thoughtful almost academic questions:
- What kinds of threats they expect next
- Whether they anticipate needing magical support
- If they've considered bringing along "arcane specialists"

[[Aranemin Zylris]] is less subtle. He leans in and tries to ingratiate himself with the group.
- Eagerly asks for details of their latest mission
- He reacts dramatically to every injury or close call
- He laughs too hard at the party's joke.

[[Ereykos Phirona]] is the least overt but also the most transparent in intent. He studies Aelar more than he speaks:
- He asks precise, technical questions
- He's particularly interested in *what went wrong* during the fight
- He frames everything in terms of "applied arcane theory"

---

They offer a discount at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]]:
- [[Velagrys Faro]] - "If you find yourselves in need of scrollwork ... I could ensure you receive and *employee rate*. Purely professional, of course."
- [[Aranemin Zylris]] - "Seriously, though ... anything from the Hall? I can *absolutely* make that cheaper for you. One hundred percent above-board ... mostly"

They're still doing the same work at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]]
- [[Velagrys Faro]]
	- Still handling scroll copying and ink testing
	- Now frames it as "necessary but underutilized expertise"
- [[Aranemin Zylris]]
	- Still logging cursed artifacts and sampling residue
	- "I do all the dangerous parts but none of the interesting ones"
- [[Ereykos Phirona]]
	- Still reviewing spell failures
	- "I am, effectively, employed to read about other people's mistakes"

[[Chad Braxton]] & [[Tracy]]

[[Aranemin Zylris]] - "Of course, some people at the Hall seem to have ... *different* responsibilities

[[Chad Braxton]] is a human wizard (Order of the Scribes). He has red hair and is perpetually sunburnt from his latest vacation. He is loud, overconfident, and deeply unserious. He has powerful, wealthy parents in the city.

They describe Chad with a mix of disbelief and resentment:
- Does minimal actual work
- Passes off tasks whenever possible
- Somehow never gets reprimanded
- [[Ereykos Phirona]] - "His primary arcane specialization appears to be delegation"
- [[Aranemin Zylris]] - "Last week, he 'supervised' me doing his entire intake log"

[[Tracy]] is also human. She's from a moderately wealthy family in the Silver District in [[Thalyra]]. She is constantly hovering around [[Chad Braxton]] at the shop front.
- [[Velagrys Faro]] - "She attends nearly every one of his shifts."
- [[Aranemin Zylris]] - "She watches *everyone* like they're about to steal him. Which - honestly - no one is trying to do."
- [[Ereykos Phirona]] - "It creates a ... distracting work environment."
