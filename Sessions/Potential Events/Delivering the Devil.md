Location: [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]], Silver District, [[Thalyra]]
Central NPCs: [[Sabine Corvane]] (Senior Curator of Necrotic Studies — **runs the scene**), [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill]] (Master Docent & Civic Liaison — greets, then hands off), [[Caelith Verion]] (Senior Administrator — signs off on the arrangement), [[Corwin Sallow]]
Type: Roleplay + investigation + moral-horror. No forced combat. Picks up **after the battle with [[Mella]]** in [[Conditions Of His Parole]].
Connects: the "what do they do with the undead?" fork in the Session 008 prep — the *Bring to Hall of Arcane Acuity* branch (investigate-undead mission + Aelar's plaque). Seeds: [[The Loveslain]] / the Cycle drift; a dormant specimen the Hall itself cannot confirm is dead.

> *Corry called her the devil and swore he killed her twice. The party has now killed her a third time — or thinks they have. The question the session opens on isn't "did we win." It's "what do we do with the body," and the Hall is the one place in [[Thalyra]] built to take it off their hands. The horror is that the Hall is genuinely delighted to.*

## The Body Problem — Where We Open

However the fight ended, the party is standing over **[[Mella]]'s remains** with a logistics problem and a moral one.

- **If they dropped her by any other means** — the far likelier outcome at night — they are carrying an **intact corpse in exactly the state [[Corwin Sallow]]'s crossbow bolts always left her in.** Stilled. Never confirmed *killed.* She does not decay. She does not cool. A body that refuses to behave like a body is the whole reason the rest of this scene has teeth.

**The candidates for what to do with her** (the fork from prep):
- **Leave her / bury her / dump her.** She's off the party's hands — and free to be the DM's problem later (see *The Reactivation Lever*).
- **Bring her to the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]].** The party already knows the Hall (the hookmaw hand-off, [[Tour of the Hall of Arcane Acuity|the tour]], [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill]], [[Caelith Verion]]). It's the obvious, and correct, instinct. This document assumes they take it.

---

## Getting Her There

Transporting a body across the Silver District in daylight is not free.

- **DC 13 to move discreetly** (a hand-cart, a wrapped tarp, a story, a *Disguise Self*, whatever they improvise). Success: they reach the Hall without incident.
- **Failure / no attempt:** a [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra|Warden]] patrol takes an interest in the shape under the tarp. This is *not* a trap — it's a chance to lean on the goodwill they earned in *Conditions Of His Parole* (the Wardens quietly owe them for the Bronze District blood-killings). A calm word, or invoking the Hall, gets them waved through with a look that says *we don't want to know.*
- The Hall is **"by appointment only," with no posted way to get one** — but the party has a prior relationship. A runner sent ahead, or simply presenting themselves at the recessed archway with an undead specimen, gets them admitted. Word of *what* they're carrying travels fast once inside.

**Do they bring [[Corwin Sallow]]?** This is the single choice that decides how dark the scene goes. He is alive. He may cling to the party (he wants sanctuary, warded and watched), or he may not want to go anywhere near the place that studies monsters. Track it — the Hall's offer below hinges entirely on whether Corry, or his story, comes through the door.

## Reception — Torren's Quick Hand-Off to Sabine

The two-foot doors open halfway. [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill]] is his usual warm, unflappable self — right up until the tarp is folded back.

> *"Good afternoon! Wonderful to see you again — I trust the goodie bags were— oh."*

A beat. The Master Docent's cheer doesn't crack so much as *professionalize.* This is above a tour, and it is above his desk.

> *"Yes. Yes — this is a matter for the Curator. Please: the coat check, as before, and then I'll take you straight down to her."*

(Same peace-binding / weapon-surrender ask as the tour. The silver verification circle hums as they enter.)

Torren walks them down himself and makes the hand-off quickly. The party knows him, and they know [[Caelith Verion]] (the tall high-elf Senior Administrator who took [[Aelar Aurielius|Aelar]]'s hookmaw deposition) — but the person the Hall sends for *undead* is someone they haven't met: **[[Sabine Corvane]]**, Senior Curator of Necrotic Studies. She is waiting at the Level 2 chambers before they arrive, already gloved, already reaching for the tarp. Caelith is "off signing something" — he re-enters only at the *end,* to authorize what she proposes.

> *"You brought this to us. Directly — before the Wardens, before a magistrate, before you tried to sell it." (a thin, genuine smile, her eyes already on the shape under the cloth) "That is either very good judgment or very good luck. Either way: thank you. Now hush a moment and let me look at her — then you'll tell me everything."*

**From here, Sabine runs the scene.** She is careful, unhurried, and genuinely *delighted* in a way that slowly curdles — the Hall's motto *"Knowledge before Fear"* made flesh. She talks softly to the body as she works.

## The Examination — She Fits Nothing

Sabine works over her at a **Level 2 dissection/observation chamber** (see the Hall's [[Hall of Arcane Acuity|subterranean levels]]). Let the party watch, or wait — but if they watch, the professional confidence slowly drains out of the room.

Every check the Hall runs is one they expect to close the case, and each one instead opens it wider — mirror the "tells" from *Conditions Of His Parole*, now under a microscope:

- **The wound isn't a bite.** The feeding scar is a **ring-shaped welt**, not a fang-mark. No cataloged blood-drinker in the Hall's records leaves that. *(This is why no examiner ever flagged Corry — and why their whole undead taxonomy is useless here.)*
- **She won't decay, and she won't cool.** Hours in, the body holds an unnatural cold patch and shows no rot where rot should be. A junior arcanist says the quiet part out loud: *"Curator… I can't tell you this specimen is dead. I can only tell you it isn't currently moving."*
- **The old rules don't apply.** If they test her the way you'd test a vampire — stake, running water, invitation — nothing resolves. The one thing that *does* provoke a reaction is **light.** Direct sunlight (or a *Daylight* effect) makes the husk **hiss and blacken.** The Hall notes it instantly: *this* is the lever. Containment is written around it.
- **Provisional classification:** Sabine refuses to file her as any known undead. She's logged as an **anomalous specimen** and slated for the **Level 4 Deep Vault** — the undead wing Sabine herself keeps: long-term containment for dangerous beings, warded, lightless-but-for-suppression-glyphs, sealed behind lockdown corridors.

> *Sabine, to no one in particular — or perhaps to the body: "In nineteen years I have not failed to name a thing on this table." (a pause, gloved hand still resting on the husk) "Log it open. We do not pretend to knowledge we don't have."*

**Even the Hall can't be sure she's dead.** They will contain her *as if she is not.* That is the truth the whole future rests on.

## What the Hall Wants — The Larder Arrangement

This is the dark heart of the scene, and it only fires **if the party tells the story** — Corry, the invitation, the attic, the crate to [[Velrosk]], and above all the detail that *she only ever fed on one man.* (Corry present makes it land harder; Corry absent, the story alone is enough for Sabine to start asking where he is.)

Sabine connects it faster than the party may like. If the specimen only ever fed on **[[Corwin Sallow]] and no one else** — and if the Hall cannot confirm she's truly dead — then the single most valuable thing about this acquisition isn't the body.

It's the man who feeds it.

> *"You've handed me something I can't kill and can't currently classify. That is, professionally, the best day I've had in a year. But a dormant specimen teaches us a fraction of what a* studied *one would. And if I understand your account, there is exactly one key that turns this lock." (she looks at Corry, or asks where he is — gently, the way she'd ask after a patient) "I'd like us to retain Mister Sallow. Room and board. Wards on every wall. Watched around the clock. Never, ever in the dark. Everything he begged the Wardens for — and in return, on a schedule* we *control, under conditions* I *make safe, he lets us learn what she is."*

The obscenity of it is that it's dressed in the Hall's own mottos — *"Knowledge before Fear. Containment before Catastrophe. Understanding before Eradication"* — and that **it is, technically, exactly the sanctuary Corry has been screaming for.** A warded cell, forever, safe from the dark. The Hall is simply also proposing to feed the dark, on him, on purpose.

**Run Corry honestly.** He is a ruined, terrified man being offered the one thing he wants by the one door he'd never have to leave. He might *take it.* He might weep with relief and horror at the same time. He might beg the party to tell him it's alright. Don't play him as obviously refusing — the trap is that it's tempting.

**Levers for the table:**
- **DC 14 Insight on Sabine:** she is not cruel and not lying. She genuinely believes a controlled arrangement is more humane than turning a hunted man loose to die in a gutter — which, per *Conditions Of His Parole*, is exactly what happens if no one takes Corry in. She has simply stopped hearing how the sentence sounds.
- **Caelith closes the door on it.** This is where the Senior Administrator re-enters: Sabine needs [[Caelith Verion|his]] authorization to retain a living person, and he gives it — briskly, as routine procurement. The **Public Ethics Board** signs off without blinking. That's the horror: two decent officials, a plaque out front, and a man handed to the dark by paperwork.
- The party can **broker, refuse, negotiate terms** (limits on feeding, an exit clause for Corry, oversight, a flat sale of the body with Corry kept *out* of it), or **walk Corry out the door** and take responsibility for him themselves — which puts them back on the hook as the thing standing between one drunk and the dark.

## What the Party Gets

Regardless of how the Corry question lands, delivering the specimen earns them standing and two concrete threads from prep:

- **The Investigate-Undead Commission.** Sabine wants to know **where a thing like this comes from.** She commissions the party — discreetly, off the [[The Adventurer's Docket|Docket]] or quietly through it — to trace her origin. This is the Hall handing them the leads they may not have pulled in *Conditions*: the [[Velrosk]] freight chit, the crate that came back **empty** with its lashings *burst from the inside*, the bloodless ship's crew, the unrented attic. *"Find me the next one before it finds a city."* A clean, ongoing job with the Hall as patron.
- **Aelar's Plaque.** Callback reward: since they're back at the Hall, [[Aelar Aurielius|Aelar]] can be shown that **[[Jeremy]]'s enclosure now bears a small brass plaque** crediting him as the one who brought the hookmaw in — a quiet, sincere honor from an institution that doesn't hand them out lightly. Let him have the moment.
- **Coin & favor.** A specimen acquisition fee (~150–250 gp, matching the *Conditions* bounty range, negotiable up if they're sharp), plus real reputation with [[Sabine Corvane]] and [[Caelith Verion]] and the Hall — a standing arcane resource, a place that owes them, and a name that opens the by-appointment-only door.

## The Reactivation Lever (DM Only)

Because **even the Hall can't confirm she's dead,** [[Mella]] is now a **dormant time-bomb in the Level 4 Deep Vault** — and you hold the fuse. Nothing forces it; pull it when the campaign wants a set-piece, or never.

Plausible triggers, all consistent with what she is:
- **Proximity to her anchor.** If Corry is retained on-site, she is *closer to the one thing she exists to reach* than she has been since the crate. Her whole nature ([[Mella|Her Devotion]], Siphon) points at him. A single feeding "session" that goes wrong, a wardsmith's lapse, a lightless corridor — and the Deep Vault has a breach with a target already inside the building.
- **The Umbral drift.** Per [[The Loveslain]], the dead don't fully sleep even in this age of [[The Effulgent Soul|75% Effulgent]] / [[The Umbral Soul|25% Umbral]] — the drift only *thins.* She doesn't need a reason to stir. She needs an opportunity.
- **The Hall's own hubris.** They'll test the sunlight weakness, the feeding, the regeneration. Experiments have accidents. *"Containment before Catastrophe"* is a motto, not a guarantee.

A future **containment-breach at the Hall of Arcane Acuity** — the party's own delivery, loose in a facility full of *other* things in cages — is the payoff this scene quietly loads.

## DM Eyes Only — What the Hall Can Never Learn

[[Mella]] is [[The Loveslain]] — an Umbral remnant settled into a soul killed by a hand that loved it exactly as much as it loved back. **No one in this scene gets to find that out**, and per her file you shouldn't resolve, even privately, whose hand actually made her.

What the Hall *can* do is be the first institution in [[Thalyra]] to bump into the edge of it. Sabine is a careful, honest scholar who has never failed to name a thing on her table — and she cannot name her. Play that as the quiet Cycle seed: not a revelation, just a competent woman logging a case *open* because the truth is bigger than her bestiary.

**The omen she's chasing without knowing it.** Per [[The Loveslain]], one of her kind can *only* form where the undead presence is already extreme — the Umbral drift locally thick enough that the possessing spirit was present and waiting when she died. So the investigate-undead commission Sabine hands the party (below) is, unknown to her, an investigation into **where the veil has worn dangerously thin.** The honest answer to "where does a thing like this come from" is not a place on a map — it's *a place the Effulgent age isn't supposed to allow.* Whatever the [[Velrosk]] thread leads to, let the trail run colder, older, and more wrong than a single haunted attic can explain. If the wider Cycle plot ever surfaces, this delivery is retroactive proof the Umbral Soul is bleeding through earlier, and more intimately, than the Gilded city wants to believe — and the party carried the evidence in themselves, under a tarp, and *thanked the Hall for taking it.*

## If They Don't Bring Her Here

- **Leave / bury / dump her:** she becomes an unanchored (or Corry-seeking) menace at large — reintroduce per the "*If the Party Walks Away*" section of [[Conditions Of His Parole]]. Bloodless bodies begin turning up.
- **Try to destroy her themselves without sunlight/fire:** they'll find, as Corry did, that she only ever goes *still.* The lesson lands the hard way later.
- **Sell her elsewhere** (a private collector, the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]], a fence): possible, but no one else has the containment — or the ethics board to make the Corry arrangement "legitimate." Riskier, and it cuts them out of the Hall's patronage and the investigate-undead thread.
