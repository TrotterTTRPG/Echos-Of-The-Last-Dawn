[Mage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Mage#content) - Rodrick Harroway

A Mage paces outside of a house, mumbling to himself.

If the party approaches and inquires about them, the Mage will describe that they're after a family heirloom that has now fallen into the "wrong hands". The house's resident is not home, but the heirloom is.

They then request help in two forms: 
1. Go in and steal the heirloom discreetly
2. Buy a magic item (Circlet of Blasting) for an exorbitant 750gp so the Mage can buy the heirloom

---

The Mage has 36gp on them and will pay them that amount for stealing the item.
The Mage also has the Circlet of Blasting.

The heirloom is a decorative Ring of Protection that the owner of the house legally inherited.

---

The house has a few different rooms:
1. Front entry/sitting room
2. Dining Room
3. Study
4. Bedroom
5. Kitchen and Back Door
6. Small Garden

The Heirloom is located in the Bedroom, inside a locked jewelry box on a vanity.

Security and Obstacles
1. Locked doors & Windows
	- Front door: sturdy, average lock - DC 15
		- Failure by 5+ - noticeable noise - +1 suspicion
	- Back door: simpler lock - DC 12
		- Failure by 5+ - minor noise - +1 suspicion
		- Failure by less than 5 - advantage on next attempt
	- Windows: latched, one slightly loose in the kitchen
		- Kitchen window - DC 10
			- Failure - cat notices their presence right away
		- Other windows - DC 13
			- Failure - audible noise - +1 suspicion
2. The "Guard" - a very old, very grumpy housecat.
	- Hisses at strangers
	- Loud, dramatic meowing if startled
	- Can be calmed with:
		- Food
		- Animal Handling - DC 12
			- Success: cat tolerate presence
			- Failure: suspicious (soft meowing begins)
				- First failure: disadvantage on future checks with cat
				- Second failure: +1 suspicion
		- Speak with Animals - DC 11
			- Failure: "I don't like you. You're loud" (loud, dramatic meowing) - +1 suspicion
3. The Study
	- The study contains:
		- Letters hinting the homeowner recently acquired the ring legally - DC 13 investigation
4. Minor Magical Protection n the Bedroom
	- The jewelry box is locked and lightly warded
		- Noticing the ward - DC 13 Arcana or Investigation
		- Unlocking - DC 14 Normally, DC 17 to try to unlocking while bypassing ward
			- 2 tries
				- Failure on first try - no issue
				- Failure on second try - lock breaks and cannot be repaired (magical lock)
					- Home owner will know something happened when they return rather than when they notice the ring is missing
		- By passing/removing the ward
			- DC 13 Arcana (carefully suppress it)
			- Dispel Magic (auto success if cast)
			- DC 14 
	- If opened incorrectly - faint chime (audible in nearby houses at night) - +1 suspicion
	- If dispelled or carefully handled - no issue

How to decrease suspicion:
- If the cat has contributed to suspicion, feeding it eliminates that increase
- Believable distraction (noise outside, illusion, etc)
- Successfully bluffing the neighbor

Suspicion Level
- Starts at 0
- 1 - "Something's off"
	- Cat is alert, following them more
	- Floor creaks feel louder
	- A dog barks in the distance
- 2 - "Eye on the House"
	- A neighbor looks out the window
	- Footsteps pass outside
	- Someone mutters: "Thought I heard something ..."
- 3 - "Interruption"
	- Knock at the door: "Hello? Everything alright in there?"
	- Neighbor lingers outside
- 4 - "Consequences"
	- Neighbor lets themselves in
		- Do they hide, bluff, hurry, escape?
