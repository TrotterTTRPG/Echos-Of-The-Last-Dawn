[Bandit](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Bandit#content) - Markus Crow

A hooded Drow appears from an alleyway, asking one of the PC's if they wish to make a little money.

They have to successfully pass a DC 15 Persuasion check to convince the Drow they are capable

The Drow has a small package hidden in their robe. The package is sealed, and they refuse to describe what is inside, insisting that they'll earn enough to satisfy their curiosity instead.

The Drow does not make the delivery themselves because thieving bandits.

---

If rejected, the Drow will leave the spot.
If accepted, they will disclose the drop location as a Bandit in TBD

They offer 5gp upfront and 15gp from the Bandit on delivery.
The box contains (roll):
1. 3x Potions of Poison
2. Necklace with Azurite gem
3. Ring with Blue Quartz
4. Malachite bracelet

If the package is not sealed on delivery, they will only receive 5gp on delivery.
