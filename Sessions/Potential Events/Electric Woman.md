**Name:** Hedra Brasswick. Her daughters call her Ma; she asks strangers to address her as Mistress Brasswick.

An Old Woman and Daughters run a generator for the town. They burn sugar cane to power something they call lightning energy.

> One of the daughters is named Ally
> The other daughter is named Mary

They think it is the souls of the cane (sugar cane) are released during burning, and those souls are transferred to the water, then to the air. Then from air, the souls move to earth. From the earth, it moves through to lightning, where it can be trapped in a loop. because the are so fast,  they can jump to other things.

They have a contraption that is essentially a furnace that powers a steam engine that goes to an air pump. the air pump blows through a tube. air at the other end is blowing out the tube lifting a heavy stone in some water.  They can drop the stone to spin a rock very fast. While its spinning, the souls get very fast, and can jump through earth to various things. They say only certain things can be a conduit. Some things the souls prefer more than others.

The old woman is originally from [[Dornthalm]], where she sound a book that had diagrams.
They diagrams were how to build a sugar cane factory, but doesn't understand that. She thinks the lightning energy is the entire point.

The book she has are stolen from [[Dornthalm]]. [[Aelar Aurelius]] might remember her name as he was one of the individuals that were suspected of taking them.
 
She can't read the books really, but has used diagrams to recreate the process. She is very intelligent.

They squeeze the sugarwater out of the cane solely to prepare it as fuel: the squeezed cane burns very hot, which is excellent for their steam engine. They discard the expressed sugarwater into the river. She does not know that sugars collect downstream, that the pond transforms them, or that the resulting product is addictive. Sugar production is not her goal.

Down river - a village of Fairey's thrive. They are from [[The Umbral Soul]]'s last generation. They are far removed from that event, and are more tame than their ancestors. They are aware of other umbral creatures, but they would not give that information to non-umbrals.

These fairies are generally nomadic, but have become addicted to the sugars that have collected in a pond that they have inhabited. The fairies are pacified by the sugar. if they are taken away from the sugar for too long, they become aggressive - as they are normally.

> The fairies have attacked exactly twice, both times during extended vacations when the woman and her daughters had stopped working.
> They have a boat to cross the river

The fae village downriver is named **[[Sugarbend]]**.
