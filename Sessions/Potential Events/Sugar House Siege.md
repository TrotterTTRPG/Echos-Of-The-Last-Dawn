# Sugar House Siege

**Built for:** five level-4 PCs (Aelar, Aulberos, Chein Po + Kai, Naieht, Vivatrix), 2014 rules.
**Difficulty:** each wave alone is around or above the Deadly threshold (2,500 adjusted XP for this party). Together the three waves total about **10,700 adjusted XP**, roughly 85% of the party's whole adventuring-day budget, with no rest in between. Expect at least one PC to go down. Use the dials at the bottom to scale it.
**Lightning:** none of these creatures resists or is immune to lightning.

## Why they come, and what they want

The women's break has stopped the runoff. Sugarbend can't fill its orders, and the buyers are done waiting. They are not here to wreck the sugar house. **They want the machinery intact and someone to run it**, the same logic as [[NPCs/Groups/Sugarbend Fairies#Rusk Thornwake — the dissenter|Rusk's plan]] on a bigger scale. That gives the fight its shape:

- They try to **capture** Hedra, Ally, and Mary, not kill them.
- They avoid smashing the furnace, steam engine, or coil, which lets the party fight near the equipment with some cover.
- A downed PC may be **dragged off as labor** instead of finished. The fight feels lethal, and losing can turn into a rescue rather than a TPK.

Each wave is what the buyers *think* is enough. When it fails, they escalate.

## Wave 1: The Snatchers

*They think the sugar house is empty, or held by an old woman and two girls. This is a grab-and-hold crew.*

| Creature | Source | CR | # | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quickling | Volo's Guide to Monsters | 1 | 3 | 600 |
| Darkling | Volo's Guide to Monsters | 1/2 | 4 | 400 |

**Adjusted:** 1,000 × 2.5 = **2,500** (Deadly threshold).

**Tactics**
- **Quicklings** go straight for Hedra and the daughters. They try to pin them inside or drag one toward the river. They also cut lines, steal tools, and open valves to draw PCs away from the coil. With Evasion they shrug off area spells that allow a Dex save, so a lightning *attack* or a soaked floor that doesn't allow a save is what kills them.
- **Darklings** come in out of the dark through windows and the furnace room. Their death flash blinds anyone next to them. They cluster around the workers they're restraining, which makes them easy targets for area lightning.
- **What this wave teaches the players:** the fey are protecting the machinery, and lightning hurts them.

**Wave 2 is sent** when Wave 1 is down to two creatures or at the top of round 4, whichever comes first. A surviving quickling flees toward the river to report. If the party chases it, they may spot Wave 2 coming.

## Wave 2: The Breakers

*The snatchers failed, so someone is defending the place. Send the ones who break defenders.*

| Creature | Source | CR | # | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Redcap | Volo's Guide to Monsters | 3 | 2 | 1,400 |
| Satyr | Monster Manual | 1/2 | 2 | 200 |

**Adjusted:** 1,600 × 2 = **3,200** (well above Deadly).

**Tactics**
- **Redcaps** stomp in at initiative 20, wading up from the river or kicking through the door. Each targets the PC who did the most damage in Wave 1. They use Ironbound Pursuit to stick to casters (Aelar, Vivatrix, Aulberos). Three sickle attacks a turn can drop a level-4 caster in a single round. This is where the fight starts to feel deadly.
- **Satyrs** stay back and play their panpipes: Charming Melody on Naieht and Kai to tie up the frontline, Frightening Strain on whoever is working the coil. Their Magic Resistance makes them the obvious "shoot them with lightning instead" targets.
- The redcaps' iron boots in a wet room are a gift to a clever party. Let soaked floors, metal pipes, and the river edge pay off.

**Wave 3 is sent** when one redcap drops or the second falls below half HP. The Duchess doesn't wait for Wave 2 to finish. Run its stragglers alongside her.

## Wave 3: The Buyer

*She comes herself, furious and shaking. She hasn't had a proper dose in weeks.*

| Creature | Source | CR | # | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duchess Morrowmire | Homebrew (below) | 6 | 1 | 2,300 |
| Sprite | Monster Manual | 1/4 | 4 | 200 |

**Adjusted:** 2,500 × 2 = **5,000**, plus any Wave 2 survivors. The 2× multiplier overstates how dangerous the sprites are. The real threat is the Duchess's action economy.

**Tactics**
- The **sprites** are her retinue. They hang back, shoot sleep-poison arrows at anyone who reaches her, and turn invisible to reposition. If they bunch up around her, area lightning can catch her too.
- **The Duchess** opens with a demand, not an attack: *"Give me the old woman and the works, and the rest of you may crawl home."* Then she fights. She uses Whispered Fix each turn on whatever ally is left, Unmaking Breath when three or more PCs cluster at the coil, and Glamour Swap to push hits onto her sprites.
- **Aelar is an eladrin.** She recognizes him as fey and may offer him a place at her side, or call him a traitor to his blood. This beat is optional.
- **Off-ramps:** she retreats at 30 HP or fewer, or if she's offered enough Sweetpitch or a credible supply deal. Hurt pride is acceptable to her. Losing her supply is not. Whatever deal or grudge she leaves with becomes a future thread, so record it if it happens.

### Who the Duchess is

*DM decision, 2026-11-07.*

- **Drug lord, not a noble.** She belongs to no Umbral court. Her power comes from controlling supply, not from rank.
- **Self-titled.** "Duchess" is a title she gave herself. No court granted it.
- **One buyer among several, but the main distributor.** Oren sells to more than one Umbral buyer. She is the largest, and most of the Sweetpitch reaching the Umbral's addicted fairies passes through her network. That is why the shortage hurts her most and why she comes herself.

Still open: who the other buyers are, how her network is organized, and whether any court tolerates or profits from her trade.

### Duchess Morrowmire *(homebrew, proposed)*

*Medium fey, neutral evil*

A self-made Umbral power whose shape won't hold still: her gown drifts between moth-wing, bark, and smoke, and her features slide when no one is looking straight at her. Black tar stains her lips and fingertips, and the veins at her wrists have gone dark with it.

- **Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
- **Speed** 30 ft., fly 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 20 (+5) |

- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +4, Cha +8
- **Skills** Deception +8, Intimidation +8, Perception +4
- **Condition Immunities** charmed
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan
- **Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP), proficiency bonus +3

***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If she fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

***Withdrawal Spite.*** The first time she is reduced below 60 HP, Unmaking Breath recharges and she immediately uses Whispered Fix without spending her bonus action.

**Actions**

***Multiattack.*** She makes two Thorned Scepter attacks.

***Thorned Scepter.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

***Unmaking Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** She exhales sweet black smoke in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area makes a DC 16 Constitution save. On a failure it takes 27 (6d8) necrotic damage and can't regain hit points until the start of her next turn. On a success it takes half damage and has no other effect. This is the Umbral as change and entropy: wood greys, metal spots with rust, and flesh feels briefly *wrong*.

**Bonus Action**

***Whispered Fix.*** One ally within 60 feet that can hear her uses its reaction to move up to half its speed and make one weapon attack. She promises it a dose.

**Reaction**

***Glamour Swap.*** When she is hit by an attack roll, she swaps places with a willing ally within 30 feet that she can see. The ally becomes the target of the attack instead.

**Legendary Actions (2/round)**, used at the end of another creature's turn:

- **Flit.** She flies up to half her fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Scepter.** She makes one Thorned Scepter attack.
- **Dread Sweetness (Costs 2).** One creature within 30 feet that can see her makes a DC 16 Wisdom save or is frightened of her until the end of its next turn.

## Rewards

**The Dornthalm book.** Hedra gives the party the book of diagrams she used to build the works. It is **one of the missing [[Dornthalm]] books that [[Aelar Aurelius]] was accused of stealing**. The suspicion was false ([[Campaign Reference/Party and Personal Threads|Party and Personal Threads]]). Hedra brought it from Dornthalm and can't really read it. She worked from the diagrams alone, which show how to build a sugar cane factory, though she believes the lightning is the point ([[Sessions/Potential Events/Electric Woman|Electric Woman]]).
- Having the book doesn't prove who took it or clear Aelar's name automatically. How Hedra came by it, whether she remembers Aelar from Dornthalm, and what returning it would mean all stay to be decided in play.
- What the text says beyond the diagrams is also undecided.

**Moth-Shadow Mantle** *(wondrous item, cloak, uncommon, requires attunement; homebrew)*. Taken from the Duchess, or given up by her as part of a bargain. The cloak drifts between moth-wing, bark, and smoke, like her gown.
- Once per long rest, when you are hit by an attack roll, you can use your reaction to swap places with a willing ally within 30 feet that you can see. The ally becomes the target of the attack instead.

## Dials

**Easier**
- Wave 1: drop one quickling (adjusted 2,000).
- Wave 2: the second redcap arrives a round late.
- Wave 3: the Duchess starts at 90 HP (hurt crossing over) or loses Legendary Resistance.
- Give the party a short breather (one round of no enemies) between waves.

**Harder**
- Add a third satyr or a fifth darkling.
- Wave 3 also brings a Yeth Hound (Volo's Guide to Monsters). It shrugs off nonmagical, nonsilvered weapons but not lightning, which makes the coil essential.
- Let the Duchess use Whispered Fix on two allies when she is bloodied.
