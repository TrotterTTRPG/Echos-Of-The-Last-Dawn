**The Job (What Reggie Tells Them)**
- [[Reginald Tharvayne]] has a new diplomatic mission for them
- **The cargo:** 6 horses and 3 carts, being sent out as a diplomatic gesture to another city. Reggie is vague on why 
	- It's "above his pay grade, just needs to happen."
- **The passengers:** a woman and her infant child will be riding along. Reggie frames this as incidental: she just needs transport in the same direction and [[Thalyra]] is doing her a kindness.
- Normally this kind of job would go through [[Caleb Whitmere]] at [[The Thalyran Stud]], but his arm is broken (see [[Livestock Disappearing]]) and he can't make the ride.
- **The meet:** the party is to gather everyone at the South Gate the next morning.
- **The route:** no more than half a day's travel to the meeting spot Southwest of the City. He can mark it on their map.
- **The handoff:** the party camps overnight at the meeting spot, where another group will arrive to take over the escort. The party's job ends there.
- **Payment:** Reggie pays out 300gp total once the handoff is confirmed complete.

**The Woman and Child**
- **[[Sela Brindle]]**:
	- Thin
	- Hollow-eyed
	- Somewhere in her late twenties
	- Looks like she hasn't slept properly in weeks
	- Quick to startle, but she hides it from her child
	- Polite to the party but guarded
	- Has her meager possessions, Badge #907, and the letter from her husband
		- _My dearest Sela,_
		- _I hope this letter finds you and the babe well. I know the silence has been hard on you both, and harder still without explanation — for that I am sorry, though circumstances made it impossible to write sooner._
		- _The truth is, I left [[Thalyra]] in a rush for a contract in [[Iridia]] — good coin, steady work, the kind of opportunity a man doesn't get twice. I should have told you myself, in person, but the offer came suddenly and the timing was not mine to choose. I have thought of you and the child every day since._
		- _I am settled now. I have lodgings, steady wages, and room enough for a family. It is time you and our son joined me here._
		- _I have arranged transport for you — a shipment of horses and carts already bound in this direction, with a party of capable escorts seeing it through. It costs you nothing, and you need only travel with them as far as they go before the next leg carries you on. I would not trust you to just anyone, Sela. These arrangements were made with care._
		- _Gather only what you need. Meet the party at the South Gate in the morning. Show them this letter, and the badge enclosed with it, so they know it comes from me and that you travel under my word._
		- _Soon enough, this will all be behind us, and we will be a family again, together and far from anything that troubled us in Thalyra._
		- _With all my love, [[Tomas Brindle]]_
	- **Her child (Jen Brindle):**
		- An infant
		- Only a few months old
		- Bundled tightly against her regardless of the weather

**The Horses**
Six horses make up the transferred stock. Five are unremarkable; one is not.
- **A dapple-grey gelding** — calm, well-mannered, clearly the lead horse of the group. Responds easily to rein and voice.
- **A bay stallion** — high-strung, prone to shying at loud noises; needs a steady hand.
- **A black mare with a white sock on her left foreleg** — heavily pregnant, moves slower than the others, needs extra care on the road.
- **A stallion with a white star mark on his chest** — calm, well-trained. **This is [[Henry the Horse]]**, the prize stallion missing for days from [[The Thalyran Stud]], stolen and folded into this shipment to be quietly sold off in [[Velrosk]].
	- **DC 15 Animal Handling or Investigation** — this horse responds to commands with a precision and bearing that doesn't match a common stock animal; he's clearly had expensive training.
	- A party member familiar with [[The Thalyran Stud]] or [[Livestock Disappearing]] may recognize the star marking outright.
- **A dun-colored gelding** — older, placid, the kind of horse used to long hauls.
- **A spotted piebald mare** — young, skittish, clearly green-broke and not fully trained for travel yet.

**What's Really Going On**
- The woman is the wife of one of the sewer workers killed during [[Sewer Cleaner Gone Mad]]. Her newborn is that man's child.
- To cover up his role in the deaths, [[Reginald Tharvayne]] told the city that the missing sewer workers simply left Thalyra unexpectedly. Most of the other victims had no strong ties to the city, so the story stuck — except with her. She never believed her husband would abandon them, and she's spent weeks putting up missing posters and making noise in her neighborhood about it.
- To quiet her down, Reggie sent her a forged letter — sealed with **Badge #907** (the badge the party recovered) — claiming her husband is alive and settled in work in [[Iridia]], and that cheap transport has been arranged for her to join him by riding along with a shipment of horses and carts already heading that way.
- **The catch:** [[Iridia]] is northeast of [[Thalyra]]. This shipment is headed southwest, toward [[Velrosk]]. She has no way of knowing the direction is wrong.
- Reggie has arranged several handoffs along the route specifically so no single group — including this one — knows her final destination. The party's leg is just one link in a chain meant to keep the truth, and the body count, buried.
- If the party investigates closely (the badge, her story, the route, Henry's bearing), the pieces line up to expose Reggie's cover-up — but nothing forces the reveal; it's there to be found.

**If the Party Refuses the Job**
- [[Captain Seressa Varn]], now operating as a vigilante, approaches the party directly.
- She doesn't order them to take it — she pushes them toward it as a way to learn more, hinting she already has doubts about [[Reginald Tharvayne]] and the missing sewer workers.

**If the Party Takes the Job but Refuses the Handoff**
- Refusing to hand over the woman, child, and horses at the meeting point doesn't end the job quietly — the group sent to take over the escort won't let it go.
- This escalates into a fight against the relief group, who were simply told to collect the shipment and won't have answers for the party beyond their orders.

**If the Party Completes the Job**
- The handoff goes through as planned, and [[Reginald Tharvayne]] pays the party in full once it's confirmed.
- The woman and child continue on toward [[Velrosk]], unaware of where they're really headed — leaving the cover-up intact unless the party acts on what they've pieced together.
