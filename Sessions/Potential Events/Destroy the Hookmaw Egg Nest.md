**The Chamber**
- One massive entrance that is 60ft diameter. This is the Hookmaw Jrs primary entry/exit
- 4 narrower tunnels that serve as entrances to adjoining chambers
- 1 5-10ft diameter tunnel entrance for the group.
- There are rock column/stalagmites. These count as half or 3/4 cover

**The Objective:**
- There are 5 nests - each within an adjoining chamber.
- The group was given 10 alchemical demolition charges from the city
- Each charge requires:
	- An action to plant
	- A DC 13 Arcana or Investigation (Intelligence) check to secure it quietly
		- Failure: **1 chamber noise token**
		- Failure by 5 or more: **1 Noise token**
- Once the first charge is planted, they have 90 seconds () to escape.

![[HighCaptainDurel.png]]

- [[High Captain Durel]] gives them one half of a sending stone and he keeps the other half
	- He will whisper to them updates on the hookmaw
	- He has an old injury that makes him useless for stealthy missions
	- Mechanically, he can gives one player advantage by signaling when would be best for them to move throughout the antechamber.

**Main Chamber:**
- There is a sleeping Hookmaw in the middle of the chamber.
	- When guarding eggs, Hookmaws enter a semi-dormant state unless they sense danger directly near the eggs.
	- They rely mostly on tremorsense.
	- Small noises deeper in the cave don't matter much, because there is still activity within the cave system that doesn't concern the Hookmaw.
- Gaining Noise Tokens within the main chamber:
	- Failing stealth by 5+
	- Loud spellcasting
	- Dropping debris
	- Critical failure on a check

**Nest A - Fragile Egg Cluster**
- Eggs are stacked precariously
- Planting explosives requires an additional DC 13 Acrobatics
- Failure means an egg-shifts and there's an **automatic 1 Noise Token**

**Nest B - Bone Pit**
- The floor is covered in crunching bones
- Moving at more than half-speed means there is noise
- Players can: move slowly, levitate, or clear bones quietly.
	- Moving at half-speed, there is no check
	- Moving at normal speed: DC 12 Stealth or Acrobatics check
	- Moving fast is automatic **chamber noise token**

**Nest C - Acid Seep**
- A mineral pool leaks an acidic fluid
- Players must cross narrow stone ridges to get across the chamber
	- DC 13 Acrobatics
	- Failure means falling in the pool - 1d4 acid per round and made a splash **1 chamber noise token**

**Nest D - Territorial Parasites**
- Small scavenger creatures are feeding on egg residue from some hatched eggs
- They attack silently unless killed quickly. Every round: 1 chamber noise token.
- **Chamber noise tokens** accumulate as well if there is:
	- Thunder damage
	- Explosions
	- Screaming
- Fight is 5x [Giant Centipede](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16877-giant-centipede)s and 1x [Thri-Kreen](https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=thri-kreen)

**Nest E - The Largest Egg**
- There is a larger egg here (male hookmaw).
- Planting a charge here risks alerting the Hookmaw even inside the chamber.
	- On a failure by more than 5, the Hookmaw is alerted.
	- On a success, roll a d6. On a 1, gain **1 Noise Token**

**Does the Hookmaw wake up?**
- 3x Chamber Noise Tokens = 1 Noise Token
- Hookmaw wakes at 4 noise tokens
	- 1 - Hookmaw stirs in its sleep
	- 2 - Hookmaw rolls over
	- 3 - Hookmaw further shifts position and sniffs
	- 4 - It wakes and patrol begins
- If the Hookmaw is alerted and woken up, it starts patrolling

**Hookmaw Jr Stealth System** - Instead of standard initiative, this is run in Stealth Rounds.
*Structure of a Stealth Round:*
- Hookmaw Jr moves along its circular patrol path and stops every 60 ft or so
- It pauses at a marked "checkpoint"
- At each checkpoint it sweeps its 60ft vision cone and makes a Perception check
- Then players take turns (in any order)

**Vision Cone Rules**
- This is a 60ft cone that will be visible to players
- If a character enters the cone:
	- They must make a Stealth check vs the Hookmaw Jrs Passive Perception (no roll needed if behind total cover, advantage if behind partial cover, disadvantage if in open ground)
	- If they fail by 5 or more, The encounter is over and the objective becomes to escape
- If the cone sweeps over a character:
	- They must make a Stealth check vs the Hookmaw Jrs Perception check (no roll needed if behind total cover, advantage if behind partial cover, disadvantage if in open ground)
	- If they fail by 5 or more, The encounter is over and the objective becomes to escape

**Escalation Skill Challenge Escape Thingie**
- 3 successes as a group before 3 failures
- Possible checks:
	- Athletics to climb a ledge
	- Stealth to vanish behind stalagmites
	- Deception to throw a rock and convince it the noise came from somewhere else
	- Survival to predict its movement
	- Spell use or feature use creatively
- Failure consequence
	- A PC is grabbed
	- They must be freed or take massive damage before escape.

**[[High Captain Durel]]'s sacrifice**
- If it looks dire or they're about to TPK:
	- Durel regain consciousness briefly.
	- He yells and draws the creature's attention
	- He shoots an explosive round to collapse part of the cave. 
	- He dies, kills/traps the Hookmaw Jr, and the party can escape.
