# Room 4 — The Unfinished Constellations

Replacement for Room 4 in [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths|The Two Truths]]. Both groups hear all narration. The solution cards, not private spoken information, provide the difference between what each group knows.

## Puzzle at a glance

**Premise:** one group studies a completed constellation and verbally guides the other group in drawing it on a matching dot grid. Each drawing becomes a constellation overhead and builds part of the path onward.

**Procedure:** Effulgent guides while Umbral draws two pictures. Then Umbral guides while Effulgent draws two. Four completed pictures finish the crossing. Rotate the person holding the pencil between pictures so both groups share the work.

**Rules to tell players:** connect the required dots in one continuous path matching the solution. Once a dot has been visited, the path cannot enter it again, including the starting dot. Describe the solution card without showing it, tracing on the other group's sheet, or pointing out the route for them. All spoken narration and conversation may be heard by everyone. No extra ban on coordinates or directional vocabulary is imposed.

**Mistakes:** wipe or erase the whole current attempt and retry. Earlier completed pictures remain complete. No damage, resource cost, or hidden time limit. Crossing lines between dots is not prohibited; revisiting a dot is.

**Win:** four correctly reproduced constellations open the path through the doorway. No constellation-name guess, offering, or extra riddle is required. The destination is Room 5 in the five-room scenario; this scene does not itself return them to Sugarbend.

**Physical preparation still needed:** four solution cards and their matching blank dot grids, pencils, and erasers. This note supplies the approved narrative, not finished puzzle handouts. Before play, check that each intended route can be drawn continuously without revisiting any dot. Give Effulgent the solutions for pictures 1–2 and Umbral their blank grids; reverse for pictures 3–4. Orient each matching pair consistently.

## Arrival — Effulgent

> The path ends at a circular terrace of pale stone. Above you, stars fill a clear sky. None flicker. Each shines with the same steady light, and between certain stars run lines so fine they might have been scratched into glass.
>
> Two constellations stand directly overhead, complete in every detail.
>
> At the terrace’s center is a shallow stone table. Two thin crystal plates rest upon it. Each holds a perfect image of one of the shapes above you. Beside them lies a silver stylus.
>
> Across the terrace, a doorway opens onto darkness. Its threshold is clearly visible, but there is no ground beyond it.

Give this group the first two solution cards.

## Arrival — Umbral

> The path ends at a circular terrace whose outer stones are slowly breaking away. Their fragments rise instead of falling, disappearing into the sky.
>
> Above you, stars brighten, dim, and trail loose threads of light. Clouds unwind between them. Yet a handful of stars keep their places, scattered across two dark stretches of sky.
>
> At the terrace’s center stands a stone table. Two black plates lie upon it, their surfaces marked with those same scattered points. Beside them rests a stylus, its tip glowing like an ember.
>
> Across the terrace, a doorway opens onto darkness. Dust drifts through it and vanishes.

Give this group the first two blank grids. The changing sky is atmosphere: the puzzle dots keep their positions throughout an attempt.

## First two pictures — Umbral draws

**When an Umbral character touches the stylus to a dot:**

> The point beneath your hand brightens. Overhead, one star burns suddenly clear.
>
> As you move the stylus toward another point, a thread of light follows it across the sky. The two stars join. Behind the moving tip, the first star closes into a dark bead, still held within the shining line.

**Effulgent sees:**

> A thread brightens across the sky, following the line your companions have just drawn. On the plate before you, the intended constellation remains complete and unchanged.

The drawing appears as actually drawn; it does not automatically correct itself. Tell players the rules plainly before the first attempt. In particular, a used dot cannot be visited again. A wrong line requires resetting that picture, not retroactively revising the sky.

**If a player tries to revisit a dot:** the stylus stops at its edge. The point has already become part of the line and will not accept it again. Offer the reset without imposing another penalty.

**Reset narration:** Umbral watches the attempted threads unravel; Effulgent sees those same lines disappear, leaving the intended pattern on its plate untouched. The blank grid is ready for a fresh attempt.

## Completing either of the first two pictures

**Umbral:**

> The final star catches the line. For a moment, the entire figure burns above you.
>
> Then its light loosens. Threads descend through the dark, gathering beyond the empty doorway. They twist together into another section of a narrow path.

**Effulgent:**

> The constellation above you remains perfectly still, but its image fades from the crystal plate. Beyond the doorway, a section of pale path is now present—complete, sharp-edged, and waiting.

Mark the picture complete. After picture 1, repeat with picture 2. After picture 2, reverse the roles below. Do not reveal the other group's solution card while checking an attempt.

## Reverse roles — Umbral now guides

**Effulgent:**

> The lines disappear from the two constellations overhead. The stars themselves remain, bright and separate.
>
> On the table, the crystal plates are clear except for scattered points. The silver stylus grows warm beneath your hand.

Give Effulgent the blank grids for pictures 3–4.

**Umbral:**

> The drifting threads overhead draw together. Two figures emerge from the restless sky, each holding its shape long enough to be followed from beginning to end.
>
> Their paths appear on the black plates before you. Around the marks, the surface continues to ripple, but the lines themselves remain clear.

Give Umbral the solutions for pictures 3–4. These remain legible and stable for as long as the players need; the narration does not establish a timer.

## Last two pictures — Effulgent draws

> Your stylus moves across the plate. Above you, a straight thread appears between two stars.
>
> The star you leave behind becomes fixed within the figure, its place settled. When you try to bring the stylus near it again, the tip stops against a resistance as smooth and firm as glass.

Read the last sentence only if someone attempts a revisit. Umbral sees each attempted line reflected in its changing sky and compares it with the stable solution on its plate. Use the same rules and whole-picture reset as before.

**After picture 3:** in Effulgent, another finished stretch of path stands beyond the doorway. In Umbral, threads detach from the completed figure and weave another length into the crossing. One stretch remains unfinished. Move to picture 4.

## All four pictures complete

**Effulgent:**

> Beyond the doorway, the last missing section of the path is present. Its edges meet without a seam. The stars above it stand in four finished constellations.
>
> You can see the far end now.

**Umbral:**

> The last threads descend from the sky and weave themselves into the crossing. Light travels along it in slow currents. Behind you, the constellations begin to loosen, their stars drifting apart.
>
> The path holds beneath your first step.

Read the final sentence when someone steps onto it. Everyone can cross; no one must keep drawing or stay behind. Continue to Room 5. The two aspects offer different experiences of one completed route: a finished structure in Effulgent and change making a way forward in Umbral.
