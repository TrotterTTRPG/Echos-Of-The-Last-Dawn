Miresen, Misuthar 9

To the adventurers lately contracted to my command: [[Aelar Aurelius]], [[Aulberos Lightmind]], [[Chein Po]], [[Naieht]], and [[Vivatrix]] 

I gave you a lawful order in the field, plainly and twice spoken: surrender the creature, or see it put down. You answered with vines and veils and conjurer's tricks, turned upon the very officer whose charge you had taken. Call it what you like - in my ledger it is *insubordination*, and men have been stripped of contract and held behind stone for far less than laying hands upon the High Captain of [[Thalyra]] at his duty. If I am grateful for anything, it is only that you relented before I was forced to cut you down where you stood. That you will answer for none of it is not mercy but arithmetic: the creature's worth is greater than any satisfaction I would take in your ruin.

So hear it plainly. From this day you are struck from the roll of parties I will set to any work of standing. You will receive no further charge from my hand - not through [[The Adventurer's Docket]] nor by any other road that runs through my office; the clerks are already instructed. The Department of Public Works remains open to you - that being the lowest station this city keeps, and the whole of what I judge you fit for. Look no higher.

You proved yourselves capable. That is the whole of what galls me - it was obedience I required, and obedience you would not give. Do not present yourselves to my command again.

By my hand and seal,

[[High Captain Durel]]
High Captain of [[Thalyra]]
