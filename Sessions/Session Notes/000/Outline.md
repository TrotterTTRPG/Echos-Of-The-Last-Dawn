Truths and Lies
- As part of character creation, everyone is going to write 3 truths and 3 lies about their character in the style of rumors that someone may have heard about them.
- These will be handed out to other players and NPCs you interact with as it makes sense. For example, for a character with a Folk Hero or Noble background, these might be handed out to the other players immediately. For a character with a criminal background, these might not be handed out until the character's backstory is interacted with (the other players meet members of the criminal organization that character is a part of)
- I encourage everyone to think about and write down the circumstances surrounding these rumors - both true and false.
- As the story progresses, I may create and hand out additional rumors about you to other PCs or NPCs. There may also be rumors about the party that circulate as you continue to affect the world.
