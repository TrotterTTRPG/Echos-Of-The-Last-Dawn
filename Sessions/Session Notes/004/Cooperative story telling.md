Talk about Player agency
1. Don't need to wait for us to guide you towards your characters interests.
	1. If you think your characters have interests they want to explore - seek opportunities to do so.
		1. Use downtime
		2. Use NPC interactions
		3. Flavor your characters interactions with the environment. Ex. Aelar drawing the Hookmaw Jr.
	2. Talk to DMs about intended character actions that may touch upon larger threads, or have plot/lore consequences.
		   1.  My character was upset about NPC X doing this thing, and wants to trick the party into going along with a revenge plot.
2. License to participate in world building
	1. Look for ways to contribute to the fullness of the world.
	2. If your character loves tea, and is on a crusade to find the best tea in the world, don't rely on us to make up a location that has tea. You might:
		1. Make up types of tea
		2. Places that have tea
		3. People in the tea trade
		That gives us a thread to pull on. And a basis for content that would be fun to engage with.
	3. Limitations to the license
		1. Always be sure that it is reasonable, adheres to existing lore and tone, and doesn't give an advantage
			1. In a town were where weapons are outlawed, making up a weapon shop on the main road wouldn't make sense, and we would help work it into something that works (like a blackmarket dealer or something like that)
			2. Making up a weapon shop that sells items to Elves for free
		 2. If it is something that would obviously exist, even if we have not established it - you can assume it exists, and is interactable.
3. Pitch additions to backstory and lore
	1. Until it happens at the table - any PC lore is malleable. Feel free to pitch modifications.
	2. Feel free to pitch larger things, we will work with you to make it a thing
		1. Ex. Aloria
			1. As Part of her character creation, Donna created a pretty ambitious setting that she was sure we would say no to. But we worked it into the world and eventually we will get to interact with it, and I am certain it will be a setting everyone is going to enjoy.
