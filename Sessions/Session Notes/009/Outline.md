## Starting point

The party is on Sugarbend’s outskirts beside a pond bank, among scattered mushroom homes. The elder’s nectar “banquet” amounted to roughly a thimble-sized serving. They have not entered the town proper.

## Prepared sequence

Use [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths v2 - Elder and Resolution|the table guide]] for narration, dialogue, choices, and transitions.

- Oren invites the party onward and walks them around the kidney-shaped pond’s inward bend, revealing the main village; town description and guarded local history.
- Discuss the shortage; Rusk Thornwake challenges Oren and advocates seizing the hut.
- Negotiate Oren’s proposed buyer visit.
- Offer **10 Pondstrider potions for the party**; settle delivery terms. Waycaps are only for Oren’s guided crossing, not part of the reward.
- The party accepts, changes, or refuses the proposed crossing.
- If they take Waycaps: failed crossing and the other fairy’s call for the doctor.
- Purgatory encounter — still being developed separately in [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths v2|the design note]].
- If they return: wake in the clinic and deal with the aftermath.
- Pursue investigation, negotiation with the electric woman, or another chosen resolution.

These are possible scenes, not predetermined outcomes.

## Before play

- Finish the purgatory encounter and return mechanism before running that branch.
- The opening reward includes 10 Pondstrider potions; settle remaining mechanics. Each potion is one dose, lasting until the drinker’s next long rest.
- Negotiate when the 10 Pondstrider potions are handed over.
