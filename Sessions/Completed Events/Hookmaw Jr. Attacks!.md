## Core Mechanic: Collapse Track

A visible pressure meter that advances due to time _and_ mistakes.

**Track Range:** 0 → 10  
**Starting Value:** 2

### Collapse Track States

- **0–3:** Cracks, dust, ominous noises
    
- **4–6:** Falling debris, spreading fire, blocked paths
    
- **7–9:** Severe instability, dangerous terrain, limited access
    
- **10:** Total collapse — anyone inside is buried
   


---

## Typical Rescue Actions

Nearly every skill can contribute. Use the examples below as guidance.

|Skill|Example Use|Typical DC|Result|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Athletics**|Lift rubble, brace beams, force doors|12–14|+1 Progress|
|**Acrobatics**|Navigate unstable floors, falling debris|12|+1 Progress|
|**Stealth**|Move quietly to avoid collapse or panic|12|+1 Progress or avoid a complication|
|**Sleight of Hand**|Free trapped limbs, delicate debris removal|12|+1 Progress|
|**Perception**|Hear cries, spot weak points, find survivors|12|+1 Progress|
|**Investigation**|Identify load-bearing structures, plan routes|12|+1 Progress or advantage on next roll|
|**Survival**|Find safe paths, assess fire spread|12|+1 Progress|
|**Medicine**|Stabilize injured, treat smoke inhalation|10–12|+1 Progress or pause a failure timer|
|**Arcana**|Assess magical fires, unstable enchantments|12|+1 Progress or negate a magical complication|
|**Nature**|Predict fire behavior, manage smoke or water|12|+1 Progress|
|**Religion**|Call for divine focus, bolster resolve|12|+1 Progress or grant advantage to an ally|
|**History**|Recall building methods or past disasters|12|+1 Progress|
|**Animal Handling**|Calm beasts or mounts trapped inside|11|+1 Progress|
|**Deception**|Reassure civilians with false confidence|12|+1 Progress (risk on failure)|
|**Intimidation**|Force panicked civilians to move|12|+1 Progress|
|**Performance**|Keep civilians calm, direct crowds|11|+1 Progress|
|**Persuasion**|Convince civilians to trust and follow|11|+1 Progress|

**Tools & Kits:** Carpenter’s tools, mason’s tools, healer’s kit, crowbars, ropes, and similar equipment can grant advantage or convert a success into +2 Progress.

Spells, class features, or especially clever plans may auto-succeed or grant +2 Progress.

## Complications

Triggered when:

- A round ends
    
- A player fails a check
    
- The Collapse Track crosses 4 or 7
    

**General Complications (roll or choose):**

1. **Falling Debris** — Dex save DC 12 or 1d6 bludgeoning
    
2. **Fire / Smoke** — Con save DC 11 or disadvantage on next physical check
    
3. **Blocked Passage** — One route becomes impassable
    
4. **Structural Shift** — Collapse Track +1 unless stabilized
    
5. **Reduced Visibility** — Disadvantage on Perception checks
    

Civilian-specific complications override these when applicable.

---

## Preventing or Reducing Complications

Players can actively fight the collapse.

### 🛠 Stabilize Structure (Action)

- **Athletics or relevant tools** (mason, carpenter)
    
- DC 12–14
    
- On success, choose one:
    
    - Reduce Collapse Track by 1
        
    - Negate the next end-of-round +1
        

### 🧠 Assess Weak Points (Action)

- **Investigation or Perception**, DC 12
    
- Success:
    
    - Advantage on next stabilization attempt
        
    - OR choose the next complication instead of rolling
        

### 🔥 Control Fire / Smoke (Action)

- **Survival, Nature, or spells**
    
- Success:
    
    - Remove fire-based complications
        
    - Prevent smoke penalties this round
        

### 🗣 Calm Civilians (Action or Bonus Action)

- **Persuasion, Intimidation, or Performance**
    
- Success:
    
    - Remove panicking complication
        
    - Next rescue action grants +1 Progress

## Spell & Feature Highlights

Reward creativity.

- _Mold Earth / Shape Water:_ Negate a complication
    
- _Create Water:_ Remove fire; reduce Collapse Track by 1
    
- _Feather Fall:_ Ignore falling debris
    
- _Enhance Ability:_ Advantage on key checks
    
- _Shield / Uncanny Dodge:_ Negate debris damage

## Optional Combat Pressure (Light)

Add minor threats without overshadowing the rescue.

- 2–3 creatures total (rats, mephits, animated debris)
    
- Retreat after 1–2 rounds
    
- Their presence exists to disrupt, not dominate

## GM Tips

- Track failures **per civilian**, not globally
    
- Announce visible countdowns to heighten tension
    
- Let players choose who to save first
    
- Losing civilians should hurt emotionally, not mechanically
    

_This encounter is about impossible choices under pressure._

- Narrate the building constantly — sound, dust, shifting walls
    
- Keep the Collapse Track visible
    
- Let clever ideas reduce pressure
    
- Never stall momentum with hard stops
    

_This encounter is about urgency, teamwork, and heroism under pressure._
