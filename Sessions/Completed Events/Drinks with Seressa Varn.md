[[Captain Seressa Varn]] is generally very tight-lipped but will loosen up and reveal more information when she has some alcohol in her.

She drinks something strong and plain, no frills.

**When the party arrives, she's already three drinks in. She didn't wait for company:**
- She acknowledges the truth about her demotion flatly, like she's said it enough times to stop flinching:
	- "They say I stepped down ... I was stepped down."
- **She's being forced into retirement.** Thalyra's upper echelon had been quietly pushing for it for some time — ever since she made the wrong call during an earlier incident — but she refused to go.
	- After [[Hookmaw Jr. Attacks!]], she openly challenged [[High Captain Durel]] in public — in front of citizens, in front of his own men — over his decision to pull the gate contingent away right as the beast arrived.
	- That was the last straw. Whatever ground she had to stand on to keep refusing retirement is gone now. Insubordination in public, witnessed by half the Bronze District, gave them all the justification they needed.
	- "I was right. I am still right. It didn't matter."
- She knows [[High Captain Durel]] was installed for political reliability, not competence.
	- "He doesn't argue."
	- "He doesn't question."
	- "He was sending wardens towards the Gold District when the threat was just outside the Bronze District."

**After Four Drinks:**
- She's open now, and bitter.
- She's open about her disdain for [[The Adventurer's Docket]], for how [[Thalyra]]'s government has shifted, for all of it.
	- "We already have the Watch. We don't need a second army with better branding."
	- "They answer to patrons, not the people."
	- "They're dispatched where the wealthy benefactors have 'interests' — trade routes, noble estates."
	- "Coin certainly lubricates."
	- "[[Thalyra]] likes to pretend corruption doesn't live here."
- But underneath the disdain is something rawer: she loves this city. She loves the rank and file — the [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra|Wardens]] who actually walk the wall and answer the alarms, not the ones who sit behind desks and make the calls.
	- "I gave that city everything. Every alarm, every fire, every damn thing that came over that wall — I was there."
	- "And now what am I? Tell me what I am without the badge."
- Her entire identity has been built around being the city's protector, and that's being taken from her. She's not just losing a job, she's losing the only thing she's ever been.

**Once She's Had Enough — The Turn:**
- She brings up [[Reginald Tharvayne]] unprompted, and the warmth drains out of her fast.
- She remembers the document the party handed her — everything they'd gathered on Reggie. She wanted to dig into it herself, pull the thread, find out what he was really hiding.
- It was taken from her before she could. Pulled out of her hands by people above her, no explanation given.
- She's convinced — not suspects, *convinced* — that it was a cover-up, and that it reaches higher than Reggie alone.
	- "I had it. I had him. And it just — disappeared."
	- "You don't take a file like that away from someone unless there's something in it worth burying."
- **If the party doesn't talk her down here, it turns on them.** She starts connecting the party themselves to it — they're the ones who handed the file over, they're the ones still working with Reggie, they're the ones who let it get buried.
	- "You knew. You had to have known something."
	- She gets up in someone's face, accusing them of helping cover for him, of being complicit, of being just like the rest of them.
	- If no one manages to calm her down (Persuasion/Insight to read what she actually needs, or simply giving her something real — an admission, a promise to keep digging, proof they're not on Reggie's side), she takes a swing. **She will try to fight the party.**
	- She's drunk, not incapable — treat her as dangerous but not lethal-intent. She wants to hurt something, not kill anyone. The Wardens called to the scene (if it escalates that far) will not want to arrest their own captain and will look to the party to defuse it instead.

**Calming Her Down:**
- She doesn't want to be managed or placated — she wants someone to actually agree that something is wrong.
- Confirming her suspicion about Reggie, offering to keep pulling the thread, or simply sitting with her anger instead of arguing it away will bring her back down.
- Once calm, she'll apologize, gruffly, and go quiet again — back to the tight-lipped Captain the party first met, just for a little longer.
