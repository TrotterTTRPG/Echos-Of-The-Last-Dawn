At the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] Invited by the [[Letter Back to Aelar]]

**Level 0**

At the designated time, the two-foot thick doors open inwards only halfway.

Standing in the doorway is a well-dressed dwarven man. He is broad-shouldered, compact, and solid. His hair and beard is deep auburn and streaked with silver. His beard is meticulously braided into two braids that are capped with polished silver clasps. His hair is pulled back and tied neatly, low at the back. His eyes are a warm brown. He's wearing a tailored charcoal-grey longcoat cut in the Thalyric elven fashion but adjusted to dwarven proportions. Underneath is a deep blue waistcoat.

"Good Afternoon! My name is [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill]]. I'm the Master Docent and Civic Liasion here at the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]]. Welcome! Please come on in!"

He directs the party inside.

The sound of the doors closing behind them is LOUD and final. The moment the doors shut, a faint silver circle inlaid on the floor hums softly. The air presses very slightly against your ears - like a pressure change.

"This is just standard verification. Nothing intrusive!"

Inside is a stone antechamber that is intentionally narrow and tall rather than wide. It's about two stories high, 20ft across, and 40ft long. The air is cool and very dry, and it's very quiet. You can hear every breathe of your companions and the click of the dwarves' boots. To the left side of the chamber is a small desk in front of a closed door. At the end of the hall is a large archway framing a wide staircase leading down. To the right is a large brightly lit chamber.

"Here is our coat check, if you will" He gestures toward the small desk. "We ask that you surrender any weapons you have on you. If you absolutely cannot do that, we ask that you allow us to undergo the process of peace binding them."

PLAYERS CHOOSE

He leads the party to the brightly-lit open chamber. The placard next to the chamber reads "The Public Ethics Board Hall". This is a very polished chamber similar to the Concord Chamber where the Adventurer's Docket took place - minus the enormous stained glass dome ceiling. There are many plaques listing sponsored noble houses, oversight council members, and research milestones.

There are statements etched in the stone across the 3 walls:
"Knowledge before Fear."
"Containment before Catastrophe."
"Understanding before Eradication."

Torren gives a little spiel: "The creation of the Hall of Arcane Acuity was no small feat. For many years, the leadership here in [[Thalyra]] discussed the need for a specific governmental branch to study monsters and their arcane nature. Due to the rise in monster population and aggression, this became a necessity sooner than anticipated. Thanks to generous benefactors who also believed in the good that this department could do for [[Thalyra]] and [[Advelde]] at large, the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] was funded and built in record time due to innovations in [[Thalyran Magitech]], derived by the [[Department of Arcane Mechanisms (DAM)]]. As you'll see from the rest of the tour, almost the entirety of this hall exists below ground. This is for several reasons. The first is that space in [[Thalyra]] is limited. Where other governmental halls were created as part of the original city planning, this had to be added afterwards. Moving underground was deemed more possible than building narrow but high. The second is containment. It is far easier to contain the creatures we study here underground. This facility can be completely locked down in a matter of seconds ensuring the safety of [[Thalyra]]n citizens."

As he leads the party through the archway at the end of the antechamber, subtle glyphs flare as they pass through the arch. 

---

**Level 1**

The staircase is enormous. Each step takes 3-4 actual steps to cross. As you descend the staircase behind Torren, it slowly curves until it's facing the opposite direction. The walls, ceiling, and floor are all stone and bare of decorations.

The staircase ends at the large hallway that's two stories tall, 40ft wide and incredibly long. The lighting here is given by long thin rocks that glow unnaturally white and cast a very bright light.

To the left and right are a large open areas with lots of desks and people in similar coats to Torren writing, reading, researching, and collaborating. Small placards beside the chambers label these as "The Open Offices".

Once you pass the two large open areas, there are several closed doors along the walls. They are unlabeled.

He leads you to the left to a large room/cave that's labelled "The Goodberry Conservatory". This is an underground greenhouse chamber that's illuminated instead by gentle daylight magic. There are tiered stone beds each with climbing vines that are heavy with bright, red berries. The air smells clean and slightly sweet.

Torren turns to the group. "First stop: The Goodberry Conservatory. This is a fundamental for the sustainability of the research that is conducted here. Containment requires sustainability. Who here is familiar with goodberries?"

*Waits for response*

"Goodberries are an amazing plant that can sustain a creature of humanoid size for an entire day - on a single berry! No other food or water is needed. They also have some healing properties as well. This makes them a very attractive source of sustenance for us"

"Here, through selective druidic crossbreeding and arcane stabilization, these berries retain nutritional potency sufficient to sustain large creatures efficiently."

The party can see junior arcanists tending to the vines. One prunes a vine. The severed stem bleeds faintly silver sap before sealing itself closed. Irrigation channels snake between the vines and are full of slow moving water. 

He brings the party over to two glass containers - both contain small plinths. The one on the left appears to have nothing in it but some rotting, sticky juice. The one on the right contains dehydrated fruit.

"This is one thing that makes these Goodberries more special than the common variety produced by druids. Typical goodberries lose their potency after 24 hours. They can still be eaten, but they will sustain you as much as a single raspberry might. Over time, they will also rot and disintegrate like most berries. The ones we cultivate here can be dried and preserved and still maintain their potency"

He leads the party further down the hallway. They pass more greenhouses. All are growing goodberries but each is slightly different than the last. 

*If asked, he'll let them know that they experiment with conditions and different variable to see if it makes differences with the berries. *

"Now, you may be asking yourselves, why would a monster eat a dried up berry? They are blood thirsty critters. And you would be absolutely correct in your line of reasoning, and one of the first facts that this laboratory was able to confirm. Monsters do not require food. They will change with their hunger. Some become skinny - some transform, but they do not die. And this brings us into one of the larger questions that we are trying to unravel the answers to. What is a monster? How are they derived? And if they do not require food, what drives their need for blood and flesh?"

"The good berries you see here are the first step in the food chain of beasts we raise for the specific purpose of feeding the monsters under our care. Rather than relying on costly livestock or imported feed, we cultivate these magically enhanced goodberries."

"Ah" he says seeming to hear something you don't. "In a moment you will see such a creature that we are cultivating." 

As you go down the hallway, the lighting in the hallways gets dimmer and dimmer making each greenhouse stand out more and more.
 
 At the end, he leads you to the right side. The placard alongside this space reads "The Cavern Equine Stables and Beast Husbandry".
 
 This is a massive subterranean hall that is incredibly dark. 
 
 You hear the clopping of some hooved creature moving closer - but you cannot see them in the vast dark hall.
 
 As they step into the feint light - you see approaching you are two massive horses pulling an open-topped cart. They are enormous. Their coats are pale similar to a polar bears where their skin is black and their fur is almost clear. They are wide-chested and their eyes are a pale blue and reflect no light.
 
 "The Umbral Draught lineage. They have been one of the most important discoveries here at the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]]. What would have taken decades of selective breeding for strength, temperament, and subterranean navigation became available through controlled adaptation research.
 
 They are trained from both transport and for relocation of specimens"

**Level 2**

Torren directs the party to get in the cart and they continue down to level 2. Instead of a staircase here, there is a similarly wide and curving ramp that allows the cart to move smoothly down to the next level. It is incredibly dark except for a few dancing lights that the driver attaches to each corner of the cart.

This ramp opens up to an enormous chamber where constructed rooms divide the chamber into multiple large hallways. There is no light in the greater chamber but each of the rooms glows with it's own light. The driver of the carriage turns the horses down the second of these hallways.

Each of the rooms is a viewing chamber. He stops in front of one marked specimen 22-A - Elemental, Lesser (Air) where there is a restrained air elemental. It's docile and glowing. There are two scholars taking notes. Occasionally, one will use a special ink and mark the walls. All are filled with tactical diagrams. A chart at the back is labelled "Countermeasures Developed - 47 Confirmed Threat Neutralizations."

"As you can see - we study not only monsters, but all manner of magical beings. Though monsters are considered the primary threat to civilization - understanding all magical creatures, is incredibly useful. There are some links between monsters, and what you see here. Elementals are like monsters in the sense that they are - from what we can tell - derived from the arcane and have needs that are not grounded in the requirements of food and shelter like ourselves and natural beasts.  Uncovering the fundamental natures of these creatures will provide the frameworks of being able create ways to reduce the threats they pose."

"Research conducted here has helped [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] maintain the safety and security of the city. It has helped fine tune [[The Vigil Network]] so it can properly identify the type of threats that [[Thalyra]] faces. Also, once thoroughly studied, some of the materials collected as part of our research are sent to the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] for the development of new or heightened potions. Certain specimens that meet strict safety parameters have been utilized in controlled combat simulations within [[The Crucible of Crowns]]."

"Thank you all for attending this tour! I hope that you learned more about the type of work we do here and walk away with an appreciation for the importance of this branch of the [[Thalyra]]n government.
