To the Interested Party,

Your request for an appointment with the Hall of Arcane Acuity has been received and duly recorded.

Please understand that the Hall is not a public institution. Access to our laboratories, containment levels, and research divisions is restricted to sponsored scholars, contracted hunters, or officials recognized by the administrative council of [[Thalyra]] and most recently the members of [[The Adventurer's Docket]] assigned to this hall.

Unaccompanied and privileged visitation cannot be granted.

However, in the interest of fostering transparency and civic trust, we are pleased to extend an invitation to attend a guided institutional tour of approved upper-level facilities that we normally extend to the general public.

You may form a group of other interested companions, and present yourselves at the Hall’s entrance on:

Folsen, Misuthar 6
At the tenth bell past midday

You will be received by a docent and escorted through designated areas. Portions of the facility remain restricted for safety and containment reasons.

We ask that you arrive unarmed, and prepared to submit to standard ward verification. Weapons may be peace-bound if required to remain on person.

We look forward to your visit.

In Service to Knowledge and Safeguarded Progress,

[[Caelith Verion]]
Senior Administrator
