Harpy Songs – DC 11 – Range 60 ft. - A harpy may sing one song on its turn. If there are multiple harpies singing, each must sing a different song from each other. Harpies can stop singing at anytime. A creature can only be affected by one harpy song at a time. While affected by a harpy song, it ignores the effects of all other harpy songs. A target can also repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns. A target that successfully saves is immune to that particular song for the next 24 hours. The song ends if the harpy is incapacitated.  
  
orange Luring Song - *Melodic & Beautiful* - The harpy sings a magical melody. Every humanoid and giant within 60 ft. of the harpy that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed until the song ends. If the charmed target is more than 5 ft. away from the harpy, it must move on its turn toward the harpy by the most direct route. It doesn't avoid opportunity attacks, but before moving into damaging terrain, such as lava or a pit, and whenever it takes damage from a source other than the harpy, a target can repeat the saving throw.

gray Dissonant Song - *Shrill & Painful* – The harpy sings a piercing, discordant melody that rattles the body. Every humanoid and giant within 60 ft. of the harpy that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or become disoriented until the song ends. While disoriented:
- The creature’s speed is halved
- The creature has disadvantage on the first attack roll it makes on its turn
- The creature cannot take reactions

yellow Dirge of Despair – *Low & Echoing* - The harpy sings a low, sorrowful dirge that fills listeners with dread. Every humanoid and giant within 60 ft. of the harpy that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of the harpy until the song ends. While frightened:
- The creature has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of its fear is within line of sight
- The creature can’t willingly move closer to the source of its fear.

blue Siren’s Lament – *Almost silent, vibrating in the chest* - The harpy unleashes a haunting, soul-rending aria that gnaws at the will. Every humanoid and giant within 60 ft. of the harpy that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 11 Charisma saving throw or become overwhelmed until the song ends. While overwhelmed:
- At the start of each of its turns, the creature takes 1d6 psychic damage
- The creature can take either an action or a bonus action on its turn, not both
- The creature has disadvantage on saving throws against harpy songs
