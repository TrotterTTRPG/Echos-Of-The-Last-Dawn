Rising from the heart of [[Thalyra]]’s Gold Ring, the [[Hall of Accord]] dominates the square. Its pale marble tiers stretch upward, accented with gilded veins that catch the weak sunlight of this late winter morning and glimmer like threads of gold. Carved reliefs of clasped hands, quills, and scales of balance decorate the façade, speaking to the careful measure of ambition, coin, and oath within. The courtyard outside the Hall is lined rope guides indicating that this short line was once much longer and snaked to the entrance. You take your place at the end and shortly make your way within the [[Hall of Accord]].

 Just inside the grand double doors, several clerks sit behind polished oak desks. Their quills move in steady rhythm, scratching against parchment as they take names, examine vouchers, and ask short, pointed questions of each applicant.

Beyond the desks, a small team of assistants works feverishly at a long marble table, sorting freshly filled cards into neat rows and rearranging them like pieces on a game board. Every few moments, one of them mutters something—“too many casters,” “short on scouts,” “swap group five with seven”—and shuffles the cards again, trying to balance skill, experience, and luck. The tension in their voices suggests that they’ve been at this for hours.
