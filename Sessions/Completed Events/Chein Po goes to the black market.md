In the open markets of [[Thalyra]]'s bronze district many things can be found.

For those with discerning interests, this is a much better place to do business than indoor the malls or stores.

As you walk through the market, signs of criminal organizations are out in the open for all to see, but only those who know the thieves cants.
Chien is familiar with these, and has been taught to recognize them. 

Some symbolize a type of grift or scam that they are providing as a service, or providing a front for.
Some of the symbols denote the type of stolen goods that can be found "off menu".
There are also the symbols of the organizations as well. They signal out points of contact to that organization.

As Chein walk around, getting a read on the different underground activities on display, she notices something that fills her with as much of a sense of striking gold as her could hope. 
A carpet vendor's sign has a horizontal notch in the bottom righthand side of his signage. "Vibrant Velrosk floor weavings". This meant that this was a place of contact. On a tankard directly below the signage, a familiar symbol is painted.

It's a symbol you've seen on [[Faris Harith]]. it looks like a family crest. On the left hand side there is a Knight standing guard in front of a golden daylight backdrop, on the right side a moon pale blue backdrop frames an empty knights helm with a sapling sprouting from the face mask.

A somewhat portly man is finalizing the sale of a carpet, and stores his coin before sitting back down and opening up a book.
