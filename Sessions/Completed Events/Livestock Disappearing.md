In [[Thalyra]] at [[The Thalyran Stud]]

You leave the city through the western gates, and follow the highway until you see a sign for The Thalyran Stud pointing south-southwest down a much smaller, but well maintained road.

It winds through the now thawing fields. The road is soft from the now melted snows of winter, and the grasses are just starting to wake, leaving the fields mostly brown straw. The smell of spring is carried through a gentle breeze that occasionally becomes windy.

Along the road you see a few different groups of farmers starting to prepare their fields for the coming seasons.
Agriculture is fairly rare in [[Advelde]], this may be the first time you've seen fields this large being worked.
Some of the largest are two or three acres in size. Overlooking many of these fields are small watchtowers, some up to twenty feet high. 
You see individuals atop, lone guards keeping an eye in all directions.

Further down the road from the farms paddock fencing and a large stone archway comes into sight.

As you get closer, you notice the archway has a sun, and a horses head carved into it. And a large sign reads [[The Thalyran Stud]]

Beyond the gate is a large horse farm. Horses run in the paddocks, workers move in and out of barns moving horses here and there. Farriers clean hooves and and shoe them. 

In some of the pens, horses are being broken and trained.

There are a mix of buildings away from the barns that look like housing.

guard towers are set up around the edges of the paddocks.

situation upon arrival -  
- 0 All live stock besides the few reported missing. Given a horse. upon completion.
- 1 A many horses were killed last night. Cost of horses dramatically increases. Farmer was wounded.
- 2+ No horses left. Farmers all dead

Generally there are workers of all kind tending to this farm. It is the largest horse farm outside of [[Velrosk]].

The are stable men and woman, and a large amount of guards protecting the horses.

[[Caleb Whitmere]] the proprietor and his daughter [[Lydia Whitmere]] live on the farm, and there is temporary housing for workers - though most of them venture back and forth to the city.

They've been complaining about their livestock being killed. It started with some of the smaller livestock going missing at night. Then some were found mostly or partially eaten in the paddock. Now, they are attacking some of the larger animals. Some horses were killed a few days ago, and [[Henrey]],  Caleb's prized stud, went missing in the night.  

They are somehow getting by the watchmen and guards, and into the paddocks. 
Large animals have been seen fleeing after an attack, but no one has gotten a good look at them.

[[Caleb Whitmere]] is convinced it's wolves, as he was on watch during one of the attacks, and swears they moved, and acted like dire wolves. 

[[Lydia Whitmere]] claims to have seen bears, but no one believes her, as bears don't normally act that way.
