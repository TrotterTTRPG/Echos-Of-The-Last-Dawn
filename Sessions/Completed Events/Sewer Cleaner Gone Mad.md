**Narrator: (Jon)**
You are brought by [[Reginald Tharvayne]] to the Bronze District. During your walk he talks of his various, important exploits as Head of Public Works, and how the city would likely crumble in weeks if it was not for his exceptional leadership and guidance. He nary leaves room for anyone to interject with comment the entire walk.

He leads you to a large building surrounded by a smattering of sheds, some with their contents on display.
Piles of sand, rocks, and stone pour from some of the three sided sheds, and a variety of carts, construction and cleaning equipment can be seen in some of the larger sheds as workers move about them.
Reginald leads you inside - the interior has a very practical design with a garage on the floor level. You see artificers specializing in mechanics working on all sorts of various diggers, lifters and sweepers.
Reginald leads you to a stairway left of the garage and you move up to what looks like an office. 
You walk by several decent sized ones, some occupied, some empty. You make your way to the end where Reginald starts shuffling through his keys.

The office door looks like any of the others, but from the way Reginald was speaking, this is probably the corner office where he would orchestrate all of the goings on of the public works department.
He opens the door and a small windowless office that was clearly a former closet.. 
It's small, cramped, and it and can barely fit the 5 of in it, but you seem to manage cramming in.

[[Reginald Tharvayne]] Bria: (Explain the mission)

He has their first mission ready for them and would like them to complete it as soon as possible:
- He lays out that people sent to clean the sewers by the public works department have been going missing. This is news to people as they haven't seen missing posters or anything.

		- DC 13 - He's being dodgy about something
	- Persuasion/Intimidation
		- DC 15 - "It's dangerous, yes, but I assure you it isn't my fault"
		- DC 20 - Full confession - he secretly commission and released a prototype sewer cleaner construct hoping to get recognition for solving waste management problems, but it couldn't possibly cause that many issues 

He would give them a sewer map from the manhole they will descend - [[Reginald Tharvayne]] directs them to a manhole - a sewer entrance in the Bronze District.

**Evidence**

There are parts of the sewer that are unnaturally spotless. Algae and muck has been stripped away in harsh patches. However, these patches have some weird large scratch marks on the walls. They're carved evenly into the brickwork of the tunnels - clearly artificial.

After the other two encounters, they party finds partial victim remains - boots, scraps or uniform scraps.
- DC 12 Investigation - Uniform from sewer workers
- DC 12 Medicine - These bodies were torn apart, not eaten. This is strange for rats or crocodiles.
