Required event from [[The Adventurer's Docket]]

**Bria:**
	The party approaches a few clerks behind makeshift tables. 
	"Can I get your names and the department you are working under?"
	...
	"Oh yes! We hadn't heard from you. We weren't sure if you were gonna make it. What you're gonna do is go down the hallway behind me, keep going straight, and you'll enter the arena proper. There are already adventurers there."
	You squint against the bright light as you enter into the center of the arena proper and join the group of adventurers. You recognize faces from [[The Adventurer's Docket]], but now everyone stands mostly with their groups. The stands are filled to the brim with [[Thalyra]]n citizens. The enormous crowd is quietly murmuring, but due to the size, it still produces quite a roar.
	Three figures stand in the with you arena facing the group of adventurers.
		One you recognize as [[Elowen Maristad]], the human man who ran [[The Adventurer's Docket]].
		One is a human man in his mid twenties who hovers a half-step behind the others, pale against the dust and iron of the arena. His blonde curls refuse to stay neatly tied, and his blue eyes never stop moving - flicking from adventurer to adventurer - settling on their hands, their footing, the way they breathe. He shifts his weight, adjusts his sleeves, then stills, only to fidget again moments later.
		The other is a woman. She does not wave, and she does not acknowledge the group before her. She is fully clad in steel with her sword sheathed at her side and her shield clasped in her hand. The heart motif on her armor stands out against the morning light. This woman stands utterly still. Her helmet remains on with the visor lowered, so you cannot determine her race or age. If she hears the roar of the stands, she gives no sign of it.

**Jon** as Elowen: 
	"Thank you all for coming. I can say that we have been most impressed with the success you have all found in your first few days as adventuring groups, and we appreciate you taking time to come back for this evaluation. Given the importance of your tasks within [[Thalyra]] and throughout [[Advelde]], the benefactors of [[The Adventurer's Docket]] deemed it necessary to test the abilities of all unknown or lesser known adventurers who were selected."
	*Elowen gets serious here*
	"I want to be abundantly clear about the expectations here. To remain in [[The Adventurer's Docket]], you will be required to participate in 2 fights today. These may be with monsters or against humanoid combatants. You will be in no danger of dying within these fights. In fact any injuries sustained during the fight including but not limited to physical injuries, poisoning, burns, frostbite, necrosis, radiation, and even unconsciousness will be healed completely by [[Maren Fallow]] here."

**Bria**:
	The man who was standing half a step behind the others moves forward. He gives a warm smile and wave before stepping back again and resuming his fidgeting.

**Jon** as Elowen:
	"However, restoring anything consumed during the fighting is beyond his - and frankly any cleric's - capabilities. Be aware that arcane energy used to cast spells and any consumable items cannot be restored to you. That's not to say that they cannot be used, but please keep their consumable nature in mind when deciding to use them. You will have at least an hour to rest in between each fight to strategize and prepare for the next one. Lastly, and this should be obvious, we want you to strive to win these fights. We want to see you at your full capabilities and putting in your best effort. Defeat in these fights does not mean failure. [[Captain Seressa Varn]] here ..."

**Bria:**
	The stoic woman turns her head slightly towards Elowen and nods before returning to face the adventurers.

**Jon** as Elowen:
	" ... will be the one evaluating your performance throughout the fights and - win or lose - these evaluations will help [[The Adventurer's Docket]] in determining the type of assignments your adventuring group will be assigned going forward. If needed, she may call you back tomorrow for further evaluation, but that will be determined after the fights before you leave the arena today. Does everyone understand? Are there any questions?"
	 *Elowen pauses for people to answer*
	 "Now, you'll led to a room with food and drink while you await your turn. Once it is your turn, you'll be led back to the arena and you'll have 30 seconds to prepare before the combatant or combatants are released. Thank you."

**Bria**:
	With that, Elowen, Maren, and Seressa turn and make their way towards the northern edge of the arena while many assistants approach each adventuring group from the southern edge. A halfling named [[Kaylie]] approaches you.
	"Hello! I have you assigned to Room 6 - if you'll follow me."
	She leads the party through the hallway and to a door with a large silver 6 on the door. The room is small, but it has comfortable chairs, room to stretch, and a table with a number of different edible goodies on it. Each has a small handwritten placard thanking the business that donated - plates and cups from the [[Silver Ember Forge]], whiskey from the [[Emberglass Distillery]], and coffee water and pastries from [[Fragrant Jones]].
