[Gladiator](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16903-gladiator) AKA [[Captain Seressa Varn]]

The gate opens and steel boots press into the sand as [[Captain Seressa Varn]] steps into the arena - bare-headed this time. No visor. No barrier. Just the woman herself. Her face is marred by scars that are faint but visible. Her expression is one of strict discipline.

The crowd reacts immediately and quiets.

Only certainty.

She walks until she is within speaking distance of the party and stops. She studies each of you slowly and deliberately - assessing weapons and stances

When she speaks, her voice is low and meant only for you four..

“You’ve done well to reach this point. You adapted. You survived. That’s more than most, especially those so inexperienced.”

She reaches up and takes the helmet from under her arm, turning it once in her hands to stare at its face.

“But survival isn’t victory.”

Her eyes meet theirs one last time - cool, unflinching. She slides the helmet on. The visor comes down with a clean, final click. She raises her shield and draws her sword.

[[Captain Seressa Varn]] will yield when at 1/2 hp, or when 2 combatants are down.
