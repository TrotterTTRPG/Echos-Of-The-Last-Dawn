# NPC Creation Notes — Vessin Thorne

## Assumptions Made

**Race:** Half-elf. Not specified by the user. Silver District reads as cosmopolitan and aspirational — home to mid-tier merchants, junior bureaucrats, and students (see Fragrant Jones lore). Half-elf fits this social register: not the High Elf prestige of the Hall of Arcane Acuity crowd, not as common as human. A sensible middle-ground for someone running a respectable but not elite shop.

**CR:** 1/4. Not specified. A shopkeeper with herbalist training is not a combatant. CR 1/4 maps to the Acolyte baseline (which was used as the reskin reference — 9 HP, 11 AC, simple weapon attack, one minor ability). Adjusted to drop the Spellcasting in favor of a practical Herbalist's Eye trait and a Healing Draught action, which better reflects the NPC's role without granting cleric spell slots to a secular shopkeeper.

**Name:** "Vessin Thorne." Generated from scratch after checking all existing NPCs/ and NPCs/Up For Grabs/ files for collision — no match found. "Vessin" follows the Iridian-Latinate naming flavor present in the campaign (Seressa, Elowen, Liora, Caelith, Selene). "Thorne" carries a botanical echo fitting an herbalist, is easy to pronounce, and does not duplicate any existing NPC.

**Gender:** Female. Not specified; chosen to balance the short NPC list for the Silver District (Junis Vale at Fragrant Jones is male/half-elf; Seraphina Windstrum at The Forthright Remedy is female — chosen female here for a more natural-feeling Silver District roster, but this is an arbitrary pick).

**Shop name:** "Thorne & Petal" — used as plain text, not a [[wiki-link]], because the apothecary does not yet exist as a campaign file. Per SKILL.md rule 3 on wiki-links: do not link to invented things unless also creating that file.

**Save location:** The task instructions directed saving to the eval outputs folder. The SKILL.md workflow would normally ask the user to choose between `NPCs/<Name>.md` (committed) or `NPCs/Up For Grabs/<Name>.md` (pool). Since this is an unattended eval and no session direction was given, the NPC was written only to the specified eval output path — nothing was written to the real campaign NPCs/ folders.

**Adventure hook:** The Silver District index's "Healing" section is blank — no apothecary exists there. Vessin fills a real gap. The implied hook: she sources rare reagents and operates in the shadow of the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] in the Gold District, which controls access to unusual ingredients and treats independent apothecaries as second-class suppliers. This gives the DM a ready tension to exploit if needed.
