**Skills** Medicine +4, Nature +3, Perception +4
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Elvish
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  **Proficiency Bonus** +2

***Herbalist's Eye.*** Vessin can identify any common herb, plant, or natural reagent by sight or smell. She has advantage on Wisdom (Medicine) checks made to diagnose illness or identify poison.

***Fey Ancestry.*** Vessin has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

### Actions

***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage.

***Healing Draught (3/Day).*** Vessin produces a vial of herbal tincture and administers it to a creature within reach. The creature regains 4 (1d4 + 2) hit points.
