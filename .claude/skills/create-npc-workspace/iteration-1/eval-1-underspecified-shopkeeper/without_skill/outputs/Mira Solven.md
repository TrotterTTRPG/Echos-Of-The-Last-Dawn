# Mira Solven
**Role:** Apothecary Shopkeeper — *The Verdant Measure*, Silver District, Thalyra
**Race:** Half-elf
**Age:** Mid-40s
**Pronouns:** She/her

---

## Description

Mira Solven runs a tidy, mid-range apothecary called **The Verdant Measure** wedged between a tailor and a cartographer's shop on one of the Silver District's quieter side streets. The shop smells of dried lavender, camphor, and something faintly sweet — likely the honey-based tinctures Mira is known for.

She is a compact woman with chestnut hair threaded with grey, sharp amber eyes, and ink-stained fingers. She wears a practical apron over sensible clothes and keeps a small magnifying lens on a chain around her neck for reading ingredient labels. She is pleasant but no-nonsense — she'll chat about a remedy but won't be drawn into gossip about other customers.

The shop stocks standard remedies: healing salves, antitoxins, mild sleeping draughts, smelling salts, and a rotating selection of common herbs. She does not sell potions of healing herself (she lacks the arcane training) but will refer adventurers to the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] in the Gold District for anything more powerful.

---

## Personality

- **Even-keeled and practical.** She's seen enough ailments to not be squeamish and enough scammers to not be naive.
- **Quietly proud.** The Silver District is a step up from where she started (the Bronze), and she keeps her shop immaculate as a point of pride.
- **Dry humor.** She'll deadpan something sharp if a customer is being dramatic about a minor cut.

---

## Roleplaying Notes

- She is a reliable, neutral source of information about the Silver District's daily life — who's been sick, what's been going around, which noble recently sent a servant to buy something suspicious.
- She is not an adventurer and has no combat role. She is simply competent at her trade.
- If the party needs common alchemical supplies, she is the go-to in the Silver District.
- She has a mild rivalry with the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] — she thinks they're pretentious and overpriced for anything basic.
