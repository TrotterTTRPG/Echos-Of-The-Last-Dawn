# Notes — Lieutenant Halric Vesh

## Files Consulted

- `NPCs/Groups/The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra.md` — Established that the Wardens are Thalyra's peacekeeping force, that rank-and-file report to the Bronze Bastion, and that the organization has little real combat history ("few have ever seen true battle").
- `Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Capital - Thalyra/Thalyra.md` — City layout (three concentric districts), notable NPCs, and placement of the Bronze Bastion in the Bronze District.
- `Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Capital - Thalyra/Bronze District/The Bronze Bastion.md` — Physical description of the Bastion (bronze-hued stone, courtyard, barracks, administrative character), confirmed as headquarters.
- `Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Capital - Thalyra/Bronze District/The Bronze Bastion Academy.md` — Training culture; used as backstory element for Halric's upbringing and entry into the Watch.
- `NPCs/Captain Seressa Varn.md` — Established Seressa as Halric's commanding officer, her demotion backstory, and her reputation as "Heart of the Watch." Used to inform Halric's relationship with his superior.

## Key Assumptions Made

1. **"Lieutenant" as a mid-tier rank.** The Wardens file does not enumerate ranks. Lieutenant was assumed to sit between Sergeant and Captain, consistent with real-world military/guard structures.

2. **CR 3 framing.** A lieutenant in a largely ceremonial, peacekeeping force with limited real combat experience. Mechanically built around leadership traits, defensive fighting, and crossbow support — consistent with a trained officer rather than a seasoned warrior. HP (52), AC (16), and multiattack achieve CR 3 by standard DMG benchmarks.

3. **Bronze District origin.** Thalyra has strong class stratification implied by its district system. A Warden lieutenant born in the Bronze District — the working-class outer ring — felt consistent with the Academy's noted pipeline from the Starlight Haven orphanage and working families.

4. **No magical abilities.** The Wardens are described as a traditional peacekeeping force. No spellcasting was assigned; the "Steady Command" trait is flavor-adjacent to the Inspiring Leader feat rather than magic.

5. **Name.** "Halric Vesh" was invented. No existing NPC with this name was found in the campaign files.

6. **Two plot hooks invented from whole cloth.** The corruption-in-supply-requisitions hook and the missing merchant Davan Crestfall were created to give the NPC immediate utility at the table. Neither conflicts with existing lore.
