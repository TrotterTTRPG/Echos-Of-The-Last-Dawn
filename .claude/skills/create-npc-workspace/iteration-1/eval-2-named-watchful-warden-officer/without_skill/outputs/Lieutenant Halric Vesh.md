# Lieutenant Halric Vesh

**Role:** Officer, Watchful Wardens of Thalyra
**Station:** The Bronze Bastion, Bronze District, Thalyra
**Race/Gender:** Human Male
**Age:** 34
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral

---

## Appearance

Halric Vesh is a lean, angular man with close-cropped dark hair going gray at the temples ahead of his years. He has a strong jaw, sharp brown eyes that miss little, and a jaw-length scar along his left cheek — a reminder of a bar fight turned ugly during his third year on patrol. He carries himself with the precise posture of a man who has internalized discipline rather than merely performed it.

His Warden uniform is always immaculate: bronze-trimmed leather armor over a dark slate-gray gambeson, bearing the rank insignia of a lieutenant — two bronze pips on each pauldron. He wears a short sword on his left hip and a hand crossbow at the small of his back.

---

## Personality

Halric is methodical, dutiful, and quietly ambitious. He believes in the Watch with an almost reverent sincerity that some of his peers find naive. He rose through the ranks by being the officer who always filed paperwork on time, always showed up for drills early, and never looked the other way when a guardsman cut corners.

He is not cruel, but he is firm — and he has a particular dislike for corruption or sloth within the Wardens' own ranks. He views his post at the Bronze Bastion as a proving ground: he intends to earn a transfer to the Golden Hall of Command eventually, though he is careful not to say so aloud.

He respects Captain Seressa Varn deeply, perhaps more than anyone else in Thalyra. He has studied her tactics during training exercises and privately suspects her demotion was politically motivated — a suspicion he keeps to himself, because loyalty to the institution comes before loyalty to individuals.

**Quirks and Habits:**
- Drums his fingers against his thigh when thinking.
- Keeps a small brass pocket watch and checks it compulsively before briefings.
- Softens measurably around civilians who are genuinely frightened — the armor of authority slips for a moment, revealing something almost paternal.

---

## Background

Halric was born in Thalyra's Bronze District to a cobbler father and a laundress mother. He enrolled in the Bronze Bastion Academy in his teens — one of the handful of students who genuinely wanted to be there rather than falling into it by circumstance. He graduated in the top quarter of his cohort and was assigned to foot patrol in the outer Bronze District.

He earned his sergeant's stripes at 26 after subduing an extortion ring that had been operating out of the Bronzewalk Bazaar, and was promoted to lieutenant at 31 following a methodical, months-long investigation into a smuggling route that had been funneling stolen city supplies out through the western docks. Both commendations sit in a neat folder in his desk drawer. He does not display them.

He has no wife or family of his own, though he visits his parents regularly on the first Tenday of each month.

---

## Roleplaying Notes

- **Tone:** Measured, professional. Does not raise his voice. When he does, something has gone wrong.
- **Hook — Corruption in the Ranks:** Halric has quietly started documenting irregularities in supply requisitions at the Bastion. He doesn't know yet whether it's petty theft or something worse — but he needs discreet outside help to pursue it without tipping off whoever is involved.
- **Hook — An Old Case:** He was the lead officer on a case two years ago that he was forced to close without resolution. A merchant named Davan Crestfall disappeared after reporting threats from an unknown party. Halric never bought the official conclusion of "voluntary departure." Anyone with information about Crestfall's fate has his full, guarded attention.
- **Attitude Toward PCs:** Professionally skeptical at first. Warms to those who demonstrate competence and don't make his job harder. Will cooperate with adventurers if they work within (or at least near) the law.

### Traits

**Coordinated Tactics.** When Halric hits a creature with a melee attack, he can use a bonus action to grant advantage on the next attack roll made against that creature by an ally before the start of Halric's next turn.

**Tactical Acuity.** Halric has advantage on initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.

**Steady Command.** Allies within 30 feet of Halric who can see and hear him may add a d4 to any Wisdom saving throw they make. (This trait functions once per short or long rest per ally.)

---

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Halric makes two melee attacks, or one melee attack and one hand crossbow attack.

**Short Sword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Hand Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

**Suppressing Order (Recharge 5–6).** Halric commands those around him to hold a line. Each creature of his choice within 30 feet that can hear him must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of Halric until the end of its next turn. Allies within the same range can use their reaction to make one melee weapon attack.

---

### Reactions

**Shield Block.** When Halric or an ally within 5 feet is targeted by a melee attack that Halric can see, he can impose disadvantage on the attack roll. He must be wielding a shield to use this reaction.

---

*Halric fights defensively, positioning himself between threats and anyone he is protecting. He uses Suppressing Order early in a fight to break a charge or disrupt a caster, then closes to melee. He is methodical — he looks for exits, counts opponents, and takes no unnecessary risks.*
