## Assumptions Made

1. **Alignment — Lawful Neutral.** The user gave no alignment. For a career bureaucrat-officer who respects procedure over outcomes and has quiet personal ambitions, LN fits cleanly. LG was considered but Halric is too self-interested and rule-bound.

2. **CR 3 base — Veteran reskin.** The 5e 2014 Veteran (CR 3) was the natural anchor: Multiattack, longsword, heavy crossbow, chain mail AC 16, same HP band. The stat block was lightly adapted — swapped the Veteran's two-attack Multiattack and Parry reaction intact, replaced the generic off-hand unarmed with a Command recharge ability to differentiate an officer's role and add a meaningful encounter dynamic without inventing mechanics (Command is narratively "shout an order," a clean in-fiction action).

3. **Age — 34.** The user gave no age. 34 is old enough to have earned a lieutenancy after years at the Academy and several years of service, young enough to plausibly still be ambitious rather than settled.

4. **Bronze District origin.** The user gave no backstory origin. Placing him as a Bronze District native (cobbler's son) grounds him in the district where he now serves, gives him working-class roots that contrast with the Gold District's polish, and makes his loyalty to the Bastion feel earned rather than assigned.

5. **The Deephold hook.** The user gave no adventure hook. The Deephold (Bronze District jail) appears in canonical files as a real location. Using it as the source of Halric's "small irregularities" gives the DM a local, verifiable thread to pull without inventing an unlinked place.

6. **No spellcasting.** Nothing in the prompt or faction file suggests Wardens are casters. The Bronze Bastion Academy notes "minor magical tactics if available" but treats them as exceptional. Halric was written as a straight martial officer.

7. **Save location — NPCs/.** Per task instructions, the NPC was treated as committed (named, with specific role), so `NPCs/` is the appropriate location. The file itself was written to the specified eval output path rather than the live vault.
