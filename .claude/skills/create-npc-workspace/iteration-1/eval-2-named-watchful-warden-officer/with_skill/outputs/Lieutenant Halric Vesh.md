**Role:** Lieutenant within [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]], currently stationed at [[The Bronze Bastion]] in the Bronze District of [[Thalyra]]
**Race:** Human (male)
**Age:** 34
**Background:** Career Warden who rose through the ranks of [[The Bronze Bastion Academy]] and earned his lieutenant's commission on the strength of meticulous performance reviews rather than battlefield valor

**Appearance:**
- Lean and square-jawed, with close-cropped dark hair and a neat soldier's beard trimmed to regulation length
- Armor is kept to a mirror polish; a small bronze sun-cross pin on his left pauldron marks his officer rank
- Carries himself with precise, almost exaggerated uprightness — back straight, chin level, every stride measured
- A faint scar runs along his right jawline from a training accident he will not discuss
- Eyes are pale grey and restless, always tracking the room

**Personality:**
- Punctual to the point of anxiety — he times patrol changeovers by the Bell of the Bronze District and visibly tightens when they run late
- Respectful of the chain of command with one notable exception: he holds [[Captain Seressa Varn]] in something between admiration and wariness, never sure whether her unorthodox assignments are tests or punishments
- Quietly ambitious; he believes the lieutenant's post is a steppingstone to captain, and he keeps a mental ledger of everything he's done right
- Has a dry, understated sense of humor that surfaces only around junior Wardens he trusts
- Privately uncomfortable with the Gilded Age's pervasive ease — a man trained for emergencies who never gets them

**Role at The Bronze Bastion:**
- Oversees the day and early evening patrol rotations across the Bronze District, coordinating schedules and logging incident reports
- Serves as the liaison between the Bastion's administrative clerks and the officers at [[The Golden Hall of Command]], routing paperwork that Wardens would rather ignore
- Assists [[Captain Seressa Varn]] in evaluating advanced recruits from [[The Bronze Bastion Academy]], occasionally running his own practical drills in the courtyard
- His door is theoretically open to citizens with complaints, though most route those to the Hall of Inquiry at [[The Golden Hall of Command]] instead

Halric Vesh grew up in the Bronze District, the son of a cobbler who kept a stall near [[The Bronzewalk Bazaar]]. He enrolled in [[The Bronze Bastion Academy]] at fifteen and graduated near the top of his cohort, known less for brilliance than for a relentless attention to procedure. He earned his lieutenant's pin at thirty-one, the youngest in his intake year to do so. The promotion felt like a triumph. Three years later it is beginning to feel like a ceiling.

He has spent most of his tenure keeping the Bastion's patrol logs clean and its junior Wardens disciplined — useful work in a city where the long peace means crime runs to petty theft and noise complaints. He is good at this. He is perhaps too good at it, which is the kind of problem no one in [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] wants to name aloud. The Bastion runs smoothly. Nothing happens. He is very well-positioned to ensure nothing keeps not happening.

The one wrinkle is [[Captain Seressa Varn]]. Her demotion brought her back to the Bastion just as Halric's career was finding its groove, and the two have developed a cautious working relationship that neither would call warm. Seressa trusts his logistics. He trusts her read of recruits. Beyond that, they orbit each other carefully. He has heard enough about the lower-district incident that cost five Wardens their lives to know she carries something she hasn't put down, and he is not sure what he would do with that knowledge if she ever asked him to.

What Halric actually wants — though he wouldn't phrase it this way — is a crisis he can handle cleanly. Something real. A theft ring, a genuine threat to the district, a situation that would let him prove the gap between himself and the rank and file. The Gilded Age has denied him this so far. Lately, small irregularities in the patrol logs around [[The Deephold]] have begun snagging his attention: nothing that rises to a report, but the kind of nothing that accumulates.
