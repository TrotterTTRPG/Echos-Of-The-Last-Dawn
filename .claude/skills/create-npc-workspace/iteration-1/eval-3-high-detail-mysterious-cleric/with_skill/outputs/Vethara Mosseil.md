Half-elf woman
Age: 54
Keeper of the Shrine of the Woven Light, on the eastern outskirts of [[Dornthalm]]
Known as "the Thread-Keeper"

**Appearance:**
- Slight and angular, built more like a scholar than a priestess — wrists thin, posture bent from decades over writing desks
- Hair a deep ash-grey, worn loose to the shoulders with two narrow braids at the temples, threaded through with small silver rings stamped with nine-pointed stars — the symbol of [[Mystra]]
- Eyes pale green, calm, and rarely blink enough; people report feeling studied
- Robes are simple undyed wool, travel-worn, but over them she wears a stole embroidered in faded blue and silver arcane sigils — one panel notably older than the rest, clearly salvaged from an earlier garment
- Left hand bears an old burn scar across the back, fingers intact but permanently stiff; she never explains it

**Demeanor:**
- Unhurried in speech and movement, as if she has calculated exactly how much time everything deserves
- Answers questions with questions — not evasively, but as though she genuinely believes the question itself is more valuable than any answer she could give
- Warm to those who show curiosity; cold to those who show arrogance about learning or magic
- Keeps the shrine meticulously clean and lit, but deflects any suggestion that she is lonely there
- Laughs quietly at things other people don't find funny, usually moments later — as if she's still processing

Vethara was born in [[Dornthalm]] to a human ironsmith and an elven woman who left before Vethara could form memories of her. She grew up in the mountain city's shadow, apprenticed briefly to a scribe attached to the [[Cloister of the Sharpened Quill]] before magic made itself known in her at an unusually late age — seventeen. A small accident: she reached for a candle across a desk and the flame moved to meet her. She spent the next decade in quiet study, never formally joining the [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]] (she lacked, she says, the bladesinger's temperament) but working alongside them as a translator and archivist.

She took her holy vows to [[Mystra]] in her late thirties, an act some found eccentric — [[Mystra]] is openly worshipped on [[Advelde]], but rarely by someone who had already spent twenty years as a secular archivist. Vethara didn't see a contradiction. Magic was already her life. Making it her faith simply gave it a shape. She established the Shrine of the Woven Light on the city's eastern fringe not long after — a small, stone-carved structure built into a natural alcove of the mountain, maintained by donations and by Vethara's own modest means.

During her archival years, Vethara catalogued material from the deep holdings of the [[Library of Luminence]] — specifically a sub-level archive that predates the city's current administrative records. That work was suspended, officially because the section was structurally unstable. Vethara believes the real reason was that someone in the city's administration grew uncomfortable with what she was finding. What she found — what she recorded in a private cipher journal and never formally filed — was evidence that the [[Dornthalm Vaults]], known to most only as a secure municipal treasury, hold a second function entirely. The Vaults, which the city calls "the Lexicon" in older documents, were originally constructed as a containment structure. Not for gold or weapons. For something that was *written* — a text, or possibly a collection of texts, so magically volatile that housing them required stonework laced with permanent suppression enchantments. What the texts are, Vethara does not know. Whether they are still there, she suspects — but cannot confirm.

She has not told the [[Cloister of the Sharpened Quill]]. She has not told the city. She has told no one. In [[The Gilded Age]], the question of whether to surface old dangers is complicated by the era's studied refusal to look at them. Vethara keeps the shrine, keeps the candles lit, and waits for someone she believes [[Mystra]] will eventually send.

***Fey Ancestry.*** Vethara has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

***Spellcasting.*** Vethara is an 9th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following cleric spells prepared:

- Cantrips (at will): *guidance*, *mending*, *sacred flame*, *thaumaturgy*
- 1st level (4 slots): *detect magic*, *identify*, *sanctuary*, *shield of faith*
- 2nd level (3 slots): *augury*, *lesser restoration*, *silence*, *suggestion*
- 3rd level (3 slots): *dispel magic*, *nondetection*, *speak with dead*
- 4th level (3 slots): *arcane eye*, *divination*
- 5th level (1 slot): *legend lore*

*(Vethara uses the Knowledge domain. Domain spells are always prepared and marked above with italics where overlapping; she does not expend slots for domain-granted spells at the appropriate spell level per 5e 2014 rules.)*

### Actions

***Mace.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage.

***Sacred Flame (Cantrip).*** One creature Vethara can see within 60 ft. must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 14 (4d6) radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw.

***Channel Divinity: Knowledge of the Ages (Recharge after Short or Long Rest).*** Vethara uses her Channel Divinity. She chooses one skill or tool. For 10 minutes, she has proficiency with the chosen skill or tool.

***Channel Divinity: Read Thoughts (Recharge after Short or Long Rest).*** Vethara uses her Channel Divinity to read the surface thoughts of one creature she can see within 60 ft. The creature makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, Vethara learns the creature's surface thoughts for 1 minute, and during that time she can cast *suggestion* on it without expending a spell slot (the creature makes the saving throw normally). On a success, Vethara learns nothing and cannot use this feature on that creature again for 24 hours.

### Reactions

***Shield of Faith (Prepared).*** Vethara can cast *shield of faith* as a reaction when she or an ally she can see is targeted by an attack, if she has not already cast it this encounter and has a 1st-level slot remaining.
