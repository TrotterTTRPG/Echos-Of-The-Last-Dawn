## Assumptions Made

**Name:** "Thessaly Vareth" — invented. No existing Mystra cleric or shrine-keeper existed in the campaign files.

**Shrine location:** The prompt specifies "outskirts of Dornthalm." Placed on the eastern lip of the mountain pass approaching the city gates, giving it a liminal quality that suits a keeper of hidden things.

**The Secret:** The Dornthalm Vaults file contained only the text "AKA The Lexicon." All other vault lore was invented to fit the campaign's central metaphysic (the Cycle, Effulgent/Umbral balance, Crystal Age origins). The core idea — that the Vaults are a containment structure for a fragment of the Cycle itself, not merely a library — was designed to be campaign-relevant, morally weighty, and something a lone cleric could realistically sit on for decades.

**Stat Block approach:** CR 5 for a Knowledge Domain cleric at level 8 is consistent with a prepared caster whose combat capability is real but secondary. WIS 20 is her primary stat. The mace does bonus radiant damage as a flavor choice (faith-as-force rather than enchanted weapon) to avoid adding a magic item that the DM might not want in circulation.

**Mystra domain:** Confirmed as Knowledge (not Arcane — that is a 5e 2024 addition; this is 5e 2014 rules as specified).

**Institutional positioning:** Deliberately placed outside the Library of Luminence and the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes to give her independence and plausible deniability. Her relationship with Elyndra Sunweaver uses an existing named NPC from the campaign files to create a grounded connection without forcing the DM's hand.

**The iron key:** A concrete physical object representing her secret. Small, carriable, deniable. The DM can use it as a plot device or ignore it.

**The blue lantern:** Added as an unexplained phenomenon that functions as an in-world signal of something unusual — useful for player investigation and atmospheric flavor.
