# Thessaly Vareth
**Role:** Keeper of the Shrine of the Starweave — Outskirts of Dornthalm
**Race:** Half-elf
**Age:** 54
**Class:** Cleric (Knowledge Domain) 8
**Deity:** Mystra
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Pronouns:** She/her

---

## Appearance

Thessaly is a lean woman who carries her fifty-four years in the particular way of half-elves — no wrinkles yet, but the silver in her black hair is real, and the stillness behind her grey-green eyes is earned. She stands slightly shorter than average, moves with deliberate economy, and smells faintly of incense ash and mountain cold.

She wears plain grey robes hemmed with silver thread worked into a constellation pattern — nine outer stars circling one central star, Mystra's symbol rendered small enough to be mistaken for decoration by the inattentive. A polished mithral disc hangs at her throat: her holy symbol, worn inside the collar where strangers rarely see it. Her hands are permanently ink-stained at the fingertips, and her right forearm is covered in a dense sleeve of tiny arcane notation she has tattooed onto herself over decades — a habit she dismisses as "journaling."

She does not look like a guardian of secrets. That is, she will tell you, entirely intentional.

---

## The Shrine

The Shrine of the Starweave sits at the eastern lip of Dornthalm's mountain pass, where the road narrows before the city gates. From outside it looks unremarkable: a low stone building no larger than a modest workshop, its roof shingled with dark slate, a single lantern burning blue-white above the door regardless of weather or hour. Inside it holds a circular altar of dark granite inlaid with Mystra's symbol in pale silver, a half-dozen narrow shelves of votive candles, and a small library alcove holding maybe two hundred volumes — a fraction of what Thessaly actually owns.

The remaining collection is elsewhere.

Thessaly serves anyone who comes seeking knowledge freely given: translations, identification of old texts, guidance on arcane matters. She charges no coin. She has never explained publicly why she chose the outskirts over a proper district within Dornthalm. Locals assume modesty, eccentricity, or both. They are wrong.

---

## Personality

- **Precise and unhurried.** She gives answers in full sentences with specific words. She does not use "sort of" or "kind of." She finds verbal sloppiness mildly offensive and will quietly supply the correct term if a speaker fumbles it.
- **Genuinely warm, not performatively so.** She makes tea without asking whether you want it. She remembers your name from a single meeting six months prior. She asks follow-up questions about things you mentioned in passing.
- **Uncomfortable with flattery.** Compliments about her knowledge make her visibly uneasy. She deflects to Mystra every time. Compliments about her tea she accepts graciously.
- **Has a hard line she does not cross.** If a question touches on what she knows about the Vaults — its actual purpose, its oldest contents, what lies beneath the catalogued levels — she changes the subject with practiced smoothness. If pressed, she becomes utterly still and says, "That knowledge is not mine to give." She will not elaborate. She will not apologize.

---

## History

Thessaly grew up in a mixed-heritage settlement in the foothills south of Dornthalm, the daughter of a human scrivener and an elven wanderer who left before she was three. Her father taught her to read in four languages before she was ten. By fourteen she had copied the entire contents of his modest library into notebooks she bound herself. By twenty she had exhausted every text within two days' travel and begun walking.

She came to Mystra not through a temple but through a vision she will not describe in detail — only that she was standing in a rainstorm in a ruined archive somewhere in the eastern reaches of Advelde, and that Mystra's light came through a gap in the broken roof and she understood, without words, that the preservation of knowledge was a sacred act, not merely a scholarly one.

She spent her twenties and thirties in wandering study. She translated texts for anyone who would give her access to collections. She copied fragments of Shattered Age script that no one else could read. She sat for two years in the Obsidian Stacks beneath Dornthalm's Library of Luminence, cross-referencing what the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes had catalogued against her own field notes.

It was in the Obsidian Stacks — specifically in a sub-chamber that the official catalogue marks as "collapsed and inaccessible" — that she found the passage.

She has kept its existence to herself for nineteen years. She established the Shrine of the Starweave on the city's outskirts six months after that discovery. She has not left Dornthalm since.

---

## The Secret

The Dornthalm Vaults — called "The Lexicon" in official records — are understood to be a repository of texts, artifacts, and preserved knowledge from before the Shattering Wars. The Protectors of the Ancient Tomes, the Cloister of the Sharpened Quill, and the Library of Luminence all operate under this understanding. It is largely correct.

What Thessaly knows, and no one else does, is this:

**The Vaults were not originally built to store knowledge. They were built to contain it.**

In the sub-chamber the official record calls collapsed, she found a sealed antechamber. Inside it: a single text written in pre-Shattering script on material that is neither parchment nor stone, recording the original purpose of the structure. The Vaults were engineered as a binding vessel during the Crystal Age — not for books, but for something that was described in the text as *a condensed record of the Cycle itself.* The text implies that a portion of the knowledge encoded into the Cycle's structure — the mechanism by which the Effulgent and Umbral Souls maintain their balance — was deliberately extracted and imprisoned within the Vaults' foundation stones during the Shattering Wars, by parties the text calls only "the Architects."

The practical implication, as Thessaly has spent nineteen years working out, is that the Dornthalm Vaults are not merely a library. They are a lock. The knowledge within the foundation is not just historical — it is functional. It is, in some sense she does not yet fully understand, *load-bearing.* What it holds is not information about the Cycle. It is a piece of the Cycle itself.

She does not know what would happen if the foundation were breached. She suspects it would be severe. She suspects someone, somewhere, either already knows this or is moving toward finding it out. She has not told the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes. She has not told Elyndra Sunweaver, Dornthalm's Head Archivist. She does not trust that institutional knowledge of this fact would remain contained, and she does not know whose hands she would be placing it in.

She has, however, been watching the city's new arrivals very carefully for the past six months. Something has shifted. She can feel it in the way Mystra's presence comes to her in prayer — sharper, more insistent, more like a warning than a blessing.

## Roleplaying Notes

- She will not volunteer the secret. She will not hint at it until she trusts the party deeply and has reason to believe they are already involved in something larger than they understand.
- She is a potential **major exposition source** for campaign revelations about the Cycle, but only at the right moment. She is not a quest-giver by default — she prefers to be found, not sought.
- She can identify arcane texts, translate Shattered Age script (she is one of maybe four living people who can do this reliably), and provide Knowledge Domain cleric spells as services for trusted contacts.
- She dislikes the Cloister of the Sharpened Quill's institutional approach and has a quietly frosty relationship with its leadership — she thinks they are better at cataloguing than understanding.
- She will help the party freely with almost anything except questions that touch on the Vaults' deeper nature. If the party investigates the Vaults themselves, she will eventually reveal herself as an ally — but only once she is certain they cannot be easily co-opted or destroyed.
- **If she is killed,** the location of the antechamber dies with her unless the party has already been told. She has written nothing down. She has made one backup: a sealed letter entrusted to Mystra in prayer, which she believes the goddess holds in some form she cannot fully explain. This is intentionally left ambiguous for the DM.

---

## Connections

- **Elyndra Sunweaver (Head Archivist, Library of Luminence):** Professional respect, surface cordiality. Thessaly deliberately keeps her at arm's length. Elyndra thinks Thessaly is eccentric and slightly antisocial; she has no idea how much Thessaly knows.
- **Protectors of the Ancient Tomes:** She respects their dedication and thinks their founding oath is genuine. She would not trust the order as an institution with what she knows, but she might trust an individual member of exceptional character.
- **Mystra:** Her relationship with her goddess is unusual — less devotional warmth, more a sense of being under a very attentive eye. She speaks of Mystra the way some people speak of a mentor who has given them a responsibility they did not ask for and cannot put down.

### Special Traits

**Fey Ancestry.** Thessaly has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

**Spellcasting.** Thessaly is an 8th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following cleric spells prepared:

- *Cantrips (at will):* guidance, mending, sacred flame, thaumaturgy
- *1st level (4 slots):* bane, command, detect magic, identify\*, sanctuary
- *2nd level (3 slots):* augury\*, hold person, silence, spiritual weapon
- *3rd level (3 slots):* bestow curse, clairvoyance\*, dispel magic, tongues\*
- *4th level (2 slots):* arcane eye\*, banishment, divination\*

*\* Knowledge Domain bonus spells, always prepared*

**Knowledge Domain: Blessings of Knowledge.** Thessaly has proficiency in Arcana and History. Her proficiency bonus is doubled for these skills (already included above).

**Knowledge Domain: Read Thoughts (Recharge after Short or Long Rest).** Thessaly can use her Channel Divinity to read a creature's surface thoughts. She must concentrate on this effect (as concentrating on a spell) for up to 1 minute. The target makes a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw; on a failure, Thessaly learns the surface thoughts (current preoccupations, not deep secrets) of that creature for 1 minute. She can then ask up to 6 questions; the target is compelled to answer truthfully, though evasively. On a success, the target is unaware of the attempt.

**Knowledge Domain: Visions of the Past (1/day, 10 minutes).** Thessaly can call up visions of recent events in an area or connected to an object. She meditates for 1 minute and receives a brief, impressionistic vision (DM-described) of events from the last 24 hours in the immediate vicinity.

**Channel Divinity: Turn Undead (Recharge after Short or Long Rest).** As an action, Thessaly presents her holy symbol. Each undead within 30 ft. that can see or hear her must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the undead is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage. A turned undead must spend its turns moving away from Thessaly by the fastest available means, and cannot willingly move within 30 ft. of her.

**Destroy Undead (CR 1/2 or lower).** When Thessaly uses Turn Undead and an undead of CR 1/2 or lower fails its saving throw, the creature is instantly destroyed.

---

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Thessaly makes two attacks: one with her Mace of the Unbroken Seal and one with Spiritual Weapon (if active).

**Mace of the Unbroken Seal.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6 − 1) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) radiant damage. The mace is a plain iron weapon with Mystra's symbol etched into the head; the radiant damage is a property of the wielder's faith, not a magical enchantment on the weapon itself.

**Sacred Flame (Cantrip).** One creature Thessaly can see within 60 ft. must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 13 (3d8) radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw.

**Spiritual Weapon (2nd-level spell, concentration not required).** As a bonus action, Thessaly conjures a spectral mace-shaped construct of pale silver light within 60 ft. The construct lasts 1 minute. On each of her turns, as a bonus action, she can move it up to 20 ft. and make a melee spell attack (+8 to hit) against one creature within 5 ft. of it. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 5) force damage.

---

### Bonus Actions

**Healing Word (1st-level spell slot).** Thessaly targets one creature she can see within 60 ft. That creature regains 1d4 + 5 hit points.

---

### Reactions

**Shield of Faith (reaction, 1st-level spell slot, concentration).** When a creature Thessaly can see is targeted by an attack, she may cast shield of faith as a reaction, granting that creature +2 AC until the start of her next turn. *(Note: This is a creative use of reaction timing — standard shield of faith is a bonus action; treat this as a DM option for dramatic moments, or replace with a standard readied action.)*

### Legendary / Lair Actions
None. Thessaly is a cleric, not a monster. If the party is fighting her, something has gone very wrong.

## Adventure Hooks

1. **The Tattoo.** A character with high Arcana or History who examines Thessaly's forearm tattoo closely (she would need to allow this) recognizes fragments of Shattered Age script — the same type found in the Obsidian Stacks. The phrases visible include "the weight of binding" and "what was placed will hold only while the stone holds." She will not answer questions about it directly. She will change the subject. She will be visibly unsettled.

2. **The Blue Lantern.** The lantern above the shrine door burns blue-white without oil or flame source. It has done so for nineteen years. It began burning the week she discovered the antechamber. She has never explained it. She does not know, herself, whether it is Mystra's doing or something bleeding through from below.

3. **She Finds the Party First.** If the party has been inside the Vaults or is visibly involved in something touching on the Cycle, Thessaly may appear where they are least expecting her — at their inn, at the city gate as they're leaving — and say only: "You've been somewhere you don't fully understand yet. When you're ready to understand it, you know where I am." Then she leaves.

4. **A Request for Translation.** The party finds a fragment of pre-Shattering text and brings it to the Library of Luminence. They are redirected to a woman at a small shrine on the east road. Thessaly can translate it. Depending on what it says, her reaction to the contents may tell the party more than her words do.
