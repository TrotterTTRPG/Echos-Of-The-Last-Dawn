**Title:** Desk Officer at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Half-Orc 
**Gender**: Male
**Age:** 31  

**Appearance:**
- Broad-shouldered and powerfully built in a way that reads as incongruous behind a banker's desk
- Deep olive-grey skin, short dark hair cropped close
- Small tusks, kept filed to a professionally acceptable length — a concession he makes without comment
- Uniform always perfectly fitted, which took several rounds of bespoke tailoring to achieve
- Handshake that he has learned to moderate

**Personality:**
- Straightforward and literal — he means what he says and finds subtext exhausting
- Genuinely good with numbers; got the job on merit in an institution that did not initially want to hire him
- Patient with confused clients, less patient with condescending ones
- Quietly proud, in a way he does not perform

Dorak Ashfen was the first half-orc hired to work the public desks of the Crown Bank. He is aware of this. He does not bring it up, but he does not forget it either. He has been at the bank for six years and has had no formal complaints filed against him, which he considers an insufficient measure of success and the only one available to him.
