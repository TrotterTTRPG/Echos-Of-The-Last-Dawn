## Electric Woman → Sugarbend → The Two Truths

**Recorded endpoint, clarified by the DM on 2026-09-20:** arrival at the fairy village and meeting its elder. Player recollections are being gathered. The description below remains scenario preparation beyond what this clarification establishes.

**Prepared situation:** An ingenious old woman and her daughters use sugar cane, steam, compressed air, lifted weight, and a generator to produce electricity. Their explanation invokes souls passing between materials. Her stolen Dornthalm diagrams may connect to Aelar's past suspicion. Downstream fae have become dependent on the sugar discharge; their apparently tame behavior conceals their Umbral heritage and what deprivation can bring out.

[[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths|The Two Truths]] prepares Tansy Bellwether's feast and Twinsight Caps to detain the adventurers in the Hollow Bloom while fae move on the mill. The recommended split is Aelar/Aulberos on the Effulgent side and Vivatrix/Naieht/Po with Kai on the Umbral side. Two physically separated tables communicate by voice, each holding information the other needs. Vivatrix's quiet comfort is foreshadowing only.

The dream has no combat attrition; the paired gate, tapestry/door, herd, bridge, and simultaneous shrine offerings are collaborative puzzles. Use the main scenario for answers, timing, and resource rules; the two scripts supply each table's narration. About six hours pass, and the prepared wake-up finds the real conflict upriver. Neither that outcome nor the dream's events is established by session 008.

Before running it, clarify the intended sugar cutoff and the party's willingness/means of entering the dream. The main scene refers back to an Electric Woman cutoff that the shorter source does not explicitly establish. Do not turn that gap into an event that already happened.

## Mella, Corwin, and the Hall

Mella is a [[Lore/The Cycle/The Loveslain|Loveslain]], a custom undead connected to extreme local Umbral drift. Her feeding, bond, and recurrent stillness are not generic vampire assumptions. Corwin's account of her origin is deliberately unresolvable; the source explicitly forbids choosing a secret definitive answer. The Hall must not correctly recognize the larger Umbral cause.

[[Sessions/Completed Events/Delivering the Devil|Delivering the Devil]] prepares Sabine's clinical rationale for keeping Corwin as a controlled food source while sheltering him. That contract, any investigation commission, payment, and later containment failure remain conditional unless the DMs confirm they occurred. Delivery alone is recorded. Separate the Hall's claims of humane treatment from objective endorsement.

There are competing feeding and daylight details across the Loveslain, Mella, and earlier scenario notes. Consult [[Campaign Reference/Open Questions and Continuity|Open Questions and Continuity]] before the next encounter involving her.
