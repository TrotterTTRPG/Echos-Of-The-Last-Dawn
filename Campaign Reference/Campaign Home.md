## The campaign in one paragraph

Five adventurers in prosperous Thalyra have been drawn from ordinary civic work into dangerous institutional compromises. Their Public Works patron Reggie concealed deaths caused by a sewer construct; the Hookmaw emergency exposed failures in city leadership; and their compassion for Jeremy put them at odds with High Captain Durel. Meanwhile, apparently isolated changes in monsters and undead point toward a much larger imbalance between Effulgent and Umbral souls. The Gilded Age is being artificially prolonged at an increasingly terrible cost. Following the Electric Woman lead, the party was brought to the fairy village and met its elder. The Sugarbend dream adventure remains prepared material, not a recorded outcome.

## How to read these references

- **Recorded:** stated in actual session notes. This can still omit details or contain a typo.
- **Established lore / DM secret:** stated in a setting or character source; not necessarily known to any PC.
- **Prepared:** an outline, scenario, possible branch, or random encounter. Its existence is not proof that it happened.
- **Unresolved:** the sources conflict or do not settle the question. Do not fill the gap by inventing an answer.

Use actual session outcomes over an older description of a location or an unused scenario branch. A file in `Completed Events` can still contain conditional dialogue and alternative endings. `NPCs/Up For Grabs` also contains established characters; the folder name alone does not determine canon status.

## Keeping this useful

After each game, record the ending location, in-world date, party level, choices, payments, inventory changes, promises, and secrets actually learned in that session's Notes. Then update the current state and active threads with a source link. Keep planned scenes in preparation until played. The original notes remain the detailed source of truth; this folder is their navigation and continuity layer.
