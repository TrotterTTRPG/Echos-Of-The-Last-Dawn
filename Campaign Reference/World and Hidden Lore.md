# World and Hidden Lore

> [!warning] Major DM spoilers. Keep these truths separate from what each character has discovered.

## The world people inhabit

The world is [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Verda|Verda]], with the campaign centered on Advelde. [[Lore/Celestial Bodies/Cendralis|Cendralis]] is the sun; [[Lore/Celestial Bodies/Selara|Selara]] is the bright moon with a 33-day cycle, and [[Lore/Celestial Bodies/Umbrya|Umbrya]] is the darker purple moon with a 328-day cycle. Calendar month/day names have their own notes; no reliable current adventuring date is reconstructed here from Obsidian's selected calendar day.

[[Lore/Ages/The Gilded Age|The Gilded Age]] has lasted over six centuries. Abundance, civic sophistication, artistic life, and relative safety are real features of the setting. Status inequality and concealed exploitation exist within that prosperity. Ordinary scenes should not assume a continent of starving peasants or ubiquitous bandit armies. Increased monster trouble and unusual undead are meaningful changes against that baseline.

The older [[Lore/Ages/The Crystal Age|Crystal Age]], [[Lore/Ages/The Shattered Age|Shattered Age]], and [[Lore/War/The Great War|Great War]] provide the deep history. Dornthalm preserves knowledge across catastrophe. The relationship between the Great War and the currently empty Shattering Wars entry remains unsettled.

## The Cycle

### DM clarification for the v2 encounter

The pure Effulgent is like an ever-growing fractal: no shortage, extreme order, perfect geometries everywhere, and no motion—everything simply is. The Umbral is change and entropy. Distinguish these definitions from the older descriptions of their influence on Verda during the Cycle.

The party's purgatory is neither pure state. It manifests aspects closer to one or the other. Effulgent descriptions should approach ordered completeness and stillness; Umbral descriptions should convey ongoing transformation rather than only static ruins. Proposed sensory examples and the distinction between confirmed lore and portrayal choices are in [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths v2|The Two Truths v2]].

[[Lore/The Cycle/The Cycle|The Cycle]] describes roughly 4,000 years, divided into 2,000-year Effulgent and Umbral phases with gradual transitions. Every living soul participates in this balance.

[[Lore/The Cycle/The Effulgent Soul|Effulgence]] supports growth, abundance, empathy, and life. At its extreme, the absence of death and loss becomes stagnation: endless flourishing can drain purpose and change. [[Lore/The Cycle/The Umbral Soul|Umbral influence]] brings entropy, hunger, death, undead, and overlap with a harsher realm, but also the necessary clearing away that allows renewal. This is not a simple moral equation in which one half is good and the other is evil.

**The concealed crisis:** the world is being held at approximately **75% Effulgent / 25% Umbral**. An influential faction with benevolent intentions uses a ritual fueled by exceptionally powerful mortal lives to prolong the desirable age. Its need for power helps explain the cultivation of heroes. The longer the balance is held, the greater the cost and the danger of a violent correction.

The sources do **not** explicitly identify the ritual's leader. Londomere's power and Liora's Docket role are not sufficient evidence to declare either the caster or every member of the Crown complicit. Keep motive, knowledge, and responsibility distinct.

[[Lore/The Cycle/Why Don't the Gods Interfere|Why Don't the Gods Interfere]] places the Cycle deeper than ordinary divine management and gives mortal decisions real consequence. Some older historical prose speculates about divine intervention; avoid treating that as a precise mechanism overriding this cosmology.

## The Umbral Dragon and the Undying

[[NPCs/The Umbral Dragon|The Umbral Dragon]] is an ancient undead dragon, more than three millennia old, conceived under Umbral conditions and born into Effulgence. Hidden away by its mother and held in a world wrong for its nature, it seeks restoration of the natural turn of the Cycle. Its agenda is terrifying without being merely “destroy everything.” It influences intermediaries, seekers, cults, and creatures; counter-ritual and soul themes belong to this concealed conflict.

[[NPCs/The Undying|The Undying]] is explicitly this dragon. [[NPCs/Legion|Legion]] serves as the mortal intermediary in Vivatrix's story. Her pact, soul jar, dreams, and the dream adventure's feelings of familiarity can point toward the truth, but neither the patron's name nor the DM's knowledge means she already understands it.

Shun's hidden anti-Umbral mission and relationship with Liora intersect the same broad danger from another direction. Her exact organization should not acquire a fabricated name, membership list, or omniscient reach.

## The Loveslain is an omen, not an answer key

[[Lore/The Cycle/The Loveslain|The Loveslain]] describes an uncommon manifestation associated with intense Umbral drift. Mella's presence is evidence of a changing world even though Arcane Acuity lacks the correct explanation. Her relationship with Corwin is intentionally resistant to certainty: truthful account, misremembered mundane killing, and prior manipulation remain possibilities. **Do not secretly choose which happened.** This ambiguity is a design instruction, unlike accidental contradictions that need a DM decision.

## Religion and public knowledge

[[Lore/Gods/Gods Master List|Gods Master List]] separates publicly worshipped Gond, Ilmater, Lliira, Mystra, Silvanus, Torm, Tymora, and Tyr from known but concealed worship of Bane, Bhaal, Leira, and Myrkul. Other deity notes are categorized as unknown in this setting. Generic D&D familiarity does not make them common local knowledge.

The calendar nonetheless names some festivals after gods filed as unknown. Flag that inconsistency before using those festivals to deliver a public religious revelation. Do not resolve it by inventing a new theological explanation.

## Content implications

Give institutional antagonists credible reasons to believe they protect the public. Let prosperity feel worth protecting before exposing its cost. Give Umbral material unsettling physical and emotional specificity without making every related creature mindlessly malicious. Preserve a difference between clues, NPC explanations, and ultimate truth. The party's decisions about Jeremy, Corwin, Shun, and Vivatrix's pact can carry these tensions without prematurely explaining the whole setting.
