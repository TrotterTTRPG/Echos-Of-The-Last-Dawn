# Places and Factions

> [!warning] DM-only. Relationships here include institutional and personal secrets.

## Geographic orientation

[[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Capital - Thalyra/Thalyra|Thalyra]] is the southeastern capital, about 38,000 people, divided into Bronze, Silver, and Gold rings. [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Dornthalm/Dornthalm|Dornthalm]] lies toward the northern/central mountains and preserves prewar knowledge. [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Velrosk/Velrosk|Velrosk]] is the western mercantile city of waterways, trade, and transport. [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Iridia/Iridia|Iridia]] is an island destination: the labeled maps place it east/northeast of Thalyra, while its prose note says southern islands. Confirm the intended geography before calculating routes.

The labeled Advelde maps agree on those broad relative positions. `Greater Thalyra.png` places Aloria World and Devil's Gate north/northeast of the city. None of these observations supplies an approved hex scale. [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Solstice/Solstice|Solstice]], [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Equinox/Equinox|Equinox]], [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Helmut/Helmut|Helmut]], and [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/The Cradle of Thorns|The Cradle of Thorns]] carry important PC connections; many smaller regions are names or empty placeholders rather than developed settlements.

The Crown's upper five are Ardda Calhoune (Thalyra), Dorrik RockBarron (Velrosk), Zarielle Vex (Iridia), and Liora Vaelthir plus Londomere Vayne (Dornthalm). The lower body has thirty elected representatives, ten per district. Wealth, patronage, secrecy, and the bank shape governance; do not assume the public knows how every upper-level decision is made. ## Personal routes into the world

Aelar's trail runs through Solstice's Monastery of the Sacred Scroll, Dornthalm's Cloister of the Sharpened Quill, and his colleagues in Thalyra. Aulberos's runs through the Cradle of Thorns and Stonebloom, then the desert and Sadiya. Po's runs from Helmut to Faris and Savone, with Shun's hidden route beyond. Vivatrix's runs through Aloria World, Astra, Legion, and the patron she does not fully understand. Naieht's destroyed home and inherited pendant remain less geographically and historically resolved.
