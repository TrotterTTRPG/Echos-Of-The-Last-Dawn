# Party and Personal Threads

> [!warning] DM-only. Backstories and patron identities below are not shared party knowledge by default.

## Aelar Aurelius

[[Player Characters/Aelar Aurelius|Aelar Aurelius]] is an Eladrin Bladesinger wizard and scholar shaped by Solstice, the Protectors of Ancient Tomes, and service at Dornthalm's Cloister of the Sharpened Quill. His mentors include winter elf [[NPCs/Caelen Quickfrost|Caelen Quickfrost]] and summer elf [[NPCs/Elyndra Sunweaver|Elyndra Sunweaver]]. Preservation, curiosity, and the proper use of knowledge are stronger anchors than generic wizard ambition.

His backstory distinguishes rumor from truth: the tavern-fire brawl happened; suspicion that he stole the missing books is false. The Electric Woman's stolen diagrams are a prepared connection to that suspicion, not a theft he secretly committed. His absent autumn aspect and pilgrimage rumor should retain the source's uncertainty.

[[NPCs/Velagrys Faro|Velagrys Faro]], [[NPCs/Aranemin Zylris|Aranemin Zylris]], and [[NPCs/Ereykos Phirona|Ereykos Phirona]] are his friends now working at the Hall of Metamagic and Theory. Their mixture of affection, professional rivalry, research interest, and access to spells provides recurring social ties. The party gave them mushroom samples. Latest personal developments: learned Blur, sought potion-making training, and was disappointed by Durel's letter.

## Aulberos Lightmind

[[Player Characters/Aulberos Lightmind|Aulberos Lightmind]] is a Firbolg Circle of the Land (Desert) druid, more than six centuries old and a veteran of the old war. He once burned a city to halt a spreading rot plague. His concern for living things coexists with a capacity for devastating decisions made to protect the larger balance.

He left the Stonebloom druids when their relationship with wealthy patrons became exploitative. [[NPCs/Aelan|Aelan]], who stayed and rose within the circle, is an old friend. [[NPCs/Sadiya|Sadiya]] is a human friend near Velrosk/Kareth's Crossing whom he saved from sand fever. Institutions selling or exploiting life are a personal fault line, making Jeremy and the Hall's treatment of monsters particularly relevant.

The Bolo/Aelan bank-ledger scheme is a possible convergence with this backstory. Do not assume Aulberos already knows the scheme, or that the entire Stonebloom circle shares responsibility.

Sources: [[Player Characters/Aulberos Lightmind|Aulberos Lightmind]], [[NPCs/Aelan|Aelan]], [[NPCs/Sadiya|Sadiya]], [[Sessions/Completed Events/Tracy's Favor|Tracy's Favor]], [[Sessions/Session Notes/007/Notes|007 notes]].

## Chein Po and Kai

[[Player Characters/Chein Po|Chein Po]], formerly Chein Yang, is a wood-elf Drakewarden ranger, roughly 120 years old. Her former criminal identity, the Robbing Shadow, conflicts with her present wish to live honestly and honor her father. Her mother [[NPCs/Shun Yang|Shun]] disappeared when she was very young. As a child she stole the egg that became her drake; the mother drake later burned her home and killed her father, Kai Yang. Po and the young drake killed the mother drake in a landslide. This guilt belongs to her personal history, not automatic public knowledge.

[[Player Characters/Kai|Kai]] is the drake companion, originally called Peng and later named for Po's father. Do not confuse these two Kais. [[NPCs/Faris Harith|Faris Harith]], a courier in Velrosk, is a mentor and father figure; [[NPCs/Savone|Savone]] is their Thalyra contact. Po's latest letter says she is trying honest work, Kai likes the group, and she is not ready for an easy reconciliation with Shun.

**DM secret:** Shun's absence is bound to dangerous anti-Umbral work reporting to Liora. She eventually learned of her husband's death and believed Chein dead. Her mission and the presence of Po's drake create tension, but the notes do not prescribe their reunion or its resolution. The latest lead points northeast; it does not give a confirmed destination.

Sources: [[Player Characters/Chein Po|Chein Po]], [[Player Characters/Kai|Kai]], [[NPCs/Shun Yang|Shun]], [[NPCs/Faris Harith|Faris Harith]], [[Sessions/Completed Events/Savone gets information to Chein Po|Savone gets information to Chein Po]], [[Sessions/Session Notes/007/Notes|007 notes]].

## Naieht

[[Player Characters/Naieht|Naieht]] (pronounced “Night”) is an orc Path of the Giant barbarian from destroyed Gugragh. Human thief [[NPCs/Garrick|Garrick]] raised him and passed on a pendant bearing an unknown sigil, found in ruins. Revenge, belonging, and the pendant's origin remain open. Do not identify the attackers or explain the sigil without a source or a DM decision.

His earlier placement with Darric's group pulled him into the stolen Hookmaw egg disaster; Seressa's reassignment connects him to the current party. His latest concrete equipment development is the Balanced hammer. Garrick's NPC note says “Dead?” while Naieht's story describes a deathbed handover; the discrepancy should remain visible until clarified.

Sources: [[Player Characters/Naieht|Naieht]], [[NPCs/Garrick|Garrick]], [[Lore/Narratives/Naight's first days as an Adventurer|Naight's first days as an Adventurer]], [[Sessions/Session Notes/008/Notes|008 notes]].

## Vivatrix and Edgar

[[Player Characters/Vivatrix|Vivatrix]] is an Undying warlock, mid-twenties, whose prose describes a half-elf while the stored PDF says High Elf. Her childhood friend [[NPCs/Astra|Astra]] drowned and was never recovered; dreams calling “find me” remain an active personal mystery. She performed tarot at [[Lore/World & Geography/Places/Advelde/Aloria World|Aloria World]], but cannot genuinely predict the future merely because she performs divination theatrically. [[NPCs/Brumble|Brumble]] is the park's exploitative current owner; its original founder disappeared.

[[NPCs/Legion|Legion]] led her toward her pact. **The Undying is the Umbral Dragon**, an explicit DM identity that she and the other PCs must not automatically know. The soul jar and promised life are story commitments, not a license to invent a current soul total or new immortality mechanics. Session 003 explicitly says she did not take souls at the ruined tavern.

[[Player Characters/Edgar|Edgar]] is her raven in the fiction, with imp familiar statistics in the practical data; the personal reflavor has him fly away when reduced to zero HP. Preserve that presentation rather than replacing him with a visibly generic imp. Chad's sending stone now belongs to Vivatrix. The prepared Umbral dream gives her a quiet sense of familiarity, not an explicit patron reveal.

Sources: [[Player Characters/Vivatrix|Vivatrix]], [[Player Characters/Edgar|Edgar]], [[NPCs/Astra|Astra]], [[NPCs/Legion|Legion]], [[NPCs/The Undying|The Undying]], [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths|The Two Truths]], [[Sessions/Session Notes/003/Notes|003 notes]], [[Sessions/Session Notes/008/Notes|008 notes]].
