**Non-Lethal / Lethal Damage**
- RAW - When a melee attack reduces a creature to 0 hit points, the attacker can choose to knock them unconscious instead of killing them. The creature falls unconscious and is stable. This can be applies to either a melee weapon attack or melee spell attack, but not a ranged attack.
- Specify Lethal - All damage is non-lethal unless otherwise specified
- Enemies will roll death saves - If you want the enemy to be guaranteed a death, spend resources attacking it so it definitely dies. If you want the enemy to stabilize, spend resources stabilizing it. If you don't care either way, let the dice decide.

**Healing Potions**
- You can drink it as a bonus action, but you must roll for healing
- You can drink it as an action to get the maximum healing.  

**Standard munitions** like arrows, bolts, daggers, and javelins don’t need to be tracked.
- If it’s special in any way, you’ll need to retrieve it.
- You cannot sell an unlimited amount of daggers or javelins, etc to make money. Don’t abuse the system.

**Death saves** will be rolled in front of the board

**Weapon Draw/Stow** is a free action once per turn.
- You cannot draw a greatsword (putting away your shield in the process), hit a creature, then draw your shield (putting away your greatsword in the process)

**Bard, Sorcerers, Warlocks** (or any class with static spells) can change out one spell per Long Rest.

| Class                       | How to change Spells                               | New Rule                                          |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Bard, Sorcerer, and Warlock | Can replace one spell when the gain a level        | Can replace one spell when you finish a long rest |
| Paladin, Ranger             | Can replace one spell when you finish a long rest  |                                                   |
| Cleric, Druid, Wizard       | Can replace any spells when you finish a long rest |                                                   |
**Standing from prone** takes 15ft of movement or half your movement, whichever is less.

**When rolling for healing**, you can reroll 1s.

**Inner Monologue Tokens**
- Each session, you get 1 inner monologue token.
- They can be played on another character or NPC to hear their inner monologue.
- It can be in or out of character, but it should include what the character is thinking or feeling in this moment. What are they excited about? What are they worried about?
- This all happens in the character's head and is known to the players, but not the characters.
- Monologue tokens do not carry over to the next session.
- What this isn’t:
    - A free Detect Thoughts – other character’s won’t know the information
    - A free way to learn a character’s secrets – no secrets need to be revealed
    - A free insight check – this doesn’t necessarily reveal motivations or whether or not someone is lying
- What this is: a way to provide more role playing opportunities
