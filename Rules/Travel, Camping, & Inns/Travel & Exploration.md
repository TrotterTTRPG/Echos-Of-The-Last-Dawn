This ruleset assumes a 24hr day and breaks down activities into 4hr increments

**Travel**
- A travel day consists of 8 hours of travel. 
- There are 2 travel speeds for wilderness travel:
	- Normal pace - 3 mph or 24 mpd
	- Slow pace: 2 mph or 16 mpd
- While traveling, adventurers can select from Travel Activities for every 4 hours of travel. which can be at advantage, disadvantage or a normal roll depending on the pace. 

**Skill Trounces Luck**
- Travel and exploration are lengthy activities, thus success in the wilds is more often attributable to skill and training than to luck. 
- When making ability check for any Travel Activity or Camp Activity, you can choose to instead use your passive ability score (10 + all modifiers that normally apply. +5 for advantage or -5 for disadvantage)

**Getting Lost**
- The DM can select a random direction for the party to travel or can roll a d6 to make this decision. On a 6, the party may veer only slightly off course. On a 1, the party may make no progress or travel even farther from their destination

**Travel Activities**
- **Navigate** (adv at slow pace)
	- Intelligence - Navigators Tools
	- Wisdom - Survival
	- Unless you are trekking the well-signed highways between cities, someone will need to take point and direct the party during their travels.
		- DC None - Terrain has clear roads
		- DC 5 - Terrain has winding paths or is in flat, open landscape
		- DC 10 - Terrain lacks paths and is in rolling, open landscape
		- DC 15 - Terrain lacks paths and is in dense foliage
		- DC 20 - Terrain is obfuscated using illusions or other magic
		- DC 25 - Terrain is obfuscated using powerful magic
	- If the navigator fails, the party gets lost
- **Track** (adv at slow pace)
	- Wisdom - Survival
	- Instead of traveling towards a known destination, sometimes adventurers need to track down another creature. 
	- The tracking difficulty is the higher or the Sneak DC of the tracked creature or the Navigation DC of the terrain
	- The speed at which the tracker catches up or loses the creature depends on the roll.
		- Beats DC by 5 or more - Makes correct assumptions about the behavior of the creature and gains on it (moves at double speed toward it)
		- Beats DC - Moves in the direction of the tracked creature at normal speed
		- Fails DC by less than 5 - Moves in the direction of the tracked creature at half speed
		- Fails DC by more than 5 - The party is lost
-  **Keep Watch** (adv at slow pace)
	- Wisdom - Perception
	- When traveling in dangerous territory, the prudent adventuring party will have at least one person dedicated to Keeping Watch for threats.
	- Characters engaged in other actions may use their passive perception scores to Keep Watch
- **Sneak** (dis at normal pace)
	- Dexterity - Stealth
	- Tables can turn without warning, and the hunter can find themselves the hunted. When this happens, travelers can attempt to cover their tracks and lose their pursuers
- **Hunt & Forage** (adv at slow pace)
	- Wisdom - Survival
	- Shelter, good, and water are the 3 vital resources needed to survive.
	- The ease of hunting an foraging depends on the abundance of resources in the surrounding terrain. See table below
	- On rations is equivalent to one pound of food and one gallons of water 0 enough to sustain a small or medium creature for 1 day.
	- Travelers find greater success hunting and foraging if they do so while camping
