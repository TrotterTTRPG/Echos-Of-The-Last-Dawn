This ruleset assumes a 24hr day and breaks down activities into 4hr increments

Staying at an Inn
- An Inn stay generally occupies 12 hours of the day, including meals, sleep, and downtime.
- During an Inn stay, each character may choose one Inn Activity.
- Characters who only require 4 hours of sleep may take two Inn Activities instead.

**Skill Trounces Luck**
- Cities, inns, and settlements reward experience, reputation, and physical presence over chance.
- When making an ability check for any Inn Activity, you can choose to instead use your passive ability score (10 + all modifiers that normally apply. +5 for advantage or -5 for disadvantage)

Inn Activities
- Gather Rumors
	- Charisma - Persuasion or Deception
	- Inns are crossroads of stories, secrets, and half-truths. A skilled conversationalist knows when the buy a round, when to listen, and when to steer the tale
	- On a success, the character uncovers local rumor, which may include political tensions, criminal activity, local dangers or opportunities, or information about specific NPCs or factions
	- DC Guidelines
		- DC 5 - Common gossip, well-known events
		- DC 10 - Useful rumors with some truth
		- DC 15 - Closely held or sensitive information
		- DC 20 - Dangerous or valuable secrets
		- DC 25+ - Information someone actively wants suppressed
- Establish Presence
	- Strength - Athletics
	- Charisma - Intimidation
	- Sometimes reputation is built by feats of strength, dominance, or sheer confidence: arm-wrestling contests, breaking up a brawl, or making it clear you're not to be trifled with.
	- On a success, the character gains advantage on one social check made in this settlement within the next 24 hours or causes hostile NPCs to hesitate before escalating conflicts
	- Failure may result in unwanted attention, rivals, or challenges
- Carouse
	- Constitution check
	- Charisma - Performance
	- Drinking, gambling, singing, and celebrating loosens tongues and bonds
	- On a success, gain advantage on the next gather rumors or request favor check in this inn
	- On a failure, suffer disadvantage on your first ability check the next day due to excess (drink? embarrassment?) 
	- A character proficient with brewer's supplies or gaming sets gain advantage
- Request Favor
	- Charisma - Persuasion
	- The character leverage reputation, coin, or goodwill to secure assistance. Examples include: free or upgraded lodging, introductions or local figures, use of restricted spaces, discreet services.
	- DC depends on the favor requested and the character's standing.
	- On a failure, the favor is refused and word may spread.
- Work the Crowd
	- Wisdom - Insight
	- Instead of talking, the character watches: noticing alliances, tensions, and who holds real power.
	- On a success, the character leans: who influences the inn, which NPCs are lying/desperate/dangerous, and social dynamics they can exploit later. It grants advantage on one social check targeting an observed NPC.
- Perform
	- Charisma - Performance
	- The character entertains patrons with music, storytelling, poetry, or feats.
	- On a success, they can earn modest coin, improve their reputation, and gain advantage on one social interaction with common folk in the settlement.
	- Exceptional success may attract patrons, rivals, or invitations.
