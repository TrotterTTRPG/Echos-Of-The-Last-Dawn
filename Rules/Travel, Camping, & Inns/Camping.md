This ruleset assumes a 24hr day and breaks down activities into 4hr increments

**Camping**
- Camp generally takes 16 hours of the day - 4 hours of activity and 8 hours of rest. Those that only need to sleep for 4 hours may do an additional 4 hour activity
- While camping, adventurers can select from several Camp Activities

**Skill Trounces Luck**
- Travel and exploration are lengthy activities, thus success in the wilds is more often attributable to skill and training than to luck. 
- When making ability check for any Travel Activity or Camp Activity, you can choose to instead use your passive ability score (10 + all modifiers that normally apply. +5 for advantage or -5 for disadvantage)

**Camp Activities**
- **Explore**
	- Intelligence - Investigation
	- Travelers simply passing through a region may encounter superficial landmarks, but discovering landmarks off the beaten path requires time dedicate to methodical exploration
	- Those who take time to explore can search for landmarks within a 1-mi radius
	- Some landmarks can be easily found while others are hidden. The difficulty depends on the landmark
		- DC None - Landmark is unmistakable
		- DC 5 - Landmark has a winding path
		- DC 10 - Landmark lacks path and is in rolling, open terrain
		- DC 15 - Landmark lacks path and is in dense terrain
		- DC 20 - Landmark is hidden, with active efforts to conceal existence through mundane means
		- DC 25 - Landmark is hidden using illusions or other magic
		- DC 30 - Landmark is hidden using powerful magic
- **Keep Watch**
	- Wisdom - Perception
	- Characters engaged in other actions make use their passive perception scores to Keep Watch. If the watchperson spots a threat, they may alert others even those away from camp
- **Camouflage**
	- Dexterity - Stealth
	- Hiding ones tracks can be an effective method of ensuring an uneventful night stays exactly that - uneventful. Characters may spend their time camouflaging their camp and creating false tracks while covering their own to throw pursuers off their scent.
- **Hunt & Forage**
	- Dexterity - Survival
	- Skilled survivalist may be able to hunt on the move, but many find it easier to keep rations stocked by dedicating a camp activity to gathering food and water. 
	- When hunting and foraging from camp, make the check at advantage and add 5 to the check when determining the rations found

Adventurers who are at home in the wilds are granted advantages above and beyond their proficiency in survival.
- Rangers
	- Rangers with the Natural Explorer feature take Navigate, Track, and Sneak travel activities with advantage.
	- They can Keep Watch while simultaneously performing other Activities
	- The number of rations received from Hunt & Forage activities is doubled
