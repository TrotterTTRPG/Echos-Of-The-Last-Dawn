# Pondstrider

**Established fairy name: Pondstrider.** Drinkable magical consumable used by Sugarbend’s collectors and offered as a possible reward.

## Established effect

**Duration:** until the drinker’s next long rest. **Dose:** each potion is one dose for one drinker.

- The drinker can walk and breathe underwater as though on land.
- They fight without the disadvantages normally imposed by underwater fighting.
- **They cannot swim while the potion is active.**

Workers use it to reach the pond-bottom [[Rules/Custom Items and Spells/Items/Sweetwaters|sweetwaters]]. Exact collecting vessels are still proposed. The potion is distinct from Sweetpitch, Waycaps, and the nectar banquet.

## Prepared reward

Oren’s opening offer includes **10 potions for the party**, totaling 10 doses. Delivery timing remains to be agreed. This is prepared compensation, not stock already received by the party or the village’s entire inventory.

## Still to decide

Activation timing; available quantities; whether it can end early; falling or losing contact with the bottom; currents, visibility, pressure, and other hazards. No concentration rule, rarity, or price has been selected. It does not establish walking on the water’s surface, wall climbing, or underwater flight.

## Superseded idea

This replaces the proposed Clinging Salve/Spider Climb reward. Do not apply the salve’s proposed mechanics.
