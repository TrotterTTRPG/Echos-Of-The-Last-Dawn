# Sweetpitch

**Established fairy name.** An addictive black, tar-like molasses consumed locally and exported to Umbral buyers.

## Production

Discarded cane sugarwater feeds a biological/alchemical pond process. Fairies first made repeated visits to drink ordinary pond water. Later they discovered the richer bottom layer called [[Rules/Custom Items and Spells/Items/Sweetwaters|sweetwaters]]. The method of producing Sweetpitch was discovered later still. Sugarbend has been established for many years. In the current process, they settle sweetwaters further in large barrels, draw the high-sugar liquid, and use fire magic to flash off the water. The residue is Sweetpitch.

## Established consequences

The fairy population is dependent on it. Depleted reserves and growing demand are already causing severe problems in Sugarbend and among Umbral buyers. Exactly two attacks on the electric factory have occurred during extended vacations.
