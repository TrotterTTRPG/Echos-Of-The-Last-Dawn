# Sweetwaters

**Collection material, not the finished drug.** The rich layer along the pond bottom where the transformed sugars settle. The first fairies repeatedly returned for ordinary pond water before discovering this layer. Turning sweetwaters into Sweetpitch was a later discovery again. Ordinary pond water still contains some sugars, but drinking it is no longer the usual form of consumption.

Collectors drink [[Rules/Custom Items and Spells/Items/Pondstrider|the Pondstrider]] to walk down to the layer. Collected sweetwaters separate further in barrels before the rich liquid is finished with fire magic into [[Rules/Custom Items and Spells/Items/Sweetpitch|Sweetpitch]].

Layer behavior is established fictional biology/alchemy. The stoppered collection pots, barrel taps, precise layer arrangement, times, and yields remain proposals or unresolved details.
