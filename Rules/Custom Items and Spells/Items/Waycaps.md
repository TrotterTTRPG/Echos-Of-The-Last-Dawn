# Waycaps

**Fairy name:** Waycaps; singular, waycap. Magical mushrooms used for literal travel between planes under fairy control. They are not offered as bargaining goods or rewards to non-Umbral creatures. Oren’s exceptional guided crossing does not include spare Waycaps for the party to keep.

## Established effect

For fairies, eating them carries body and soul to the Umbral. For material-plane creatures, the crossing only partly works: the soul separates from the body and enters one shared purgatory with Umbral/Effulgent aspects. The body remains in the material world.

The elder advises travelers to eat together and follow him, and discourages traveling alone. He has only heard of fairy users but sincerely expects them to work for the party. The illegal nature of bringing outsiders is a separate issue from his ignorance of the partial crossing.

## Current party preparation

Aulberos and Vivatrix enter the Umbral aspect; Aelar and Chein the Effulgent aspect. Naieht is fully present in one aspect, chosen before play; the later design direction replaces his former ghost-between-both role. This split is encounter preparation, not a universal alignment rule or recorded event.

The [[Sessions/Potential Events/The Two Truths v2 - Trip DM Run Guide|trip run guide]] supplies the selected three-part encounter and a proposed return through the seasonal garden. Its puzzle effects are encounter-specific preparation, not universal Waycap powers.

## Still to decide
