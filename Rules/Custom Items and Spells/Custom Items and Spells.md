## Alchemy and recipes

[[Rules/Custom Items and Spells/Alchemy/Violuma Alchemy|Violuma Alchemy]] contains the complete original catalogue: **Violuma Crowns, Violuma Sparks, Crowncap Resin, Sparkcap Dust**, potion recipes, supporting ingredients, costs, and historical crafting budgets. It also contains campaign recipes for the **standard** Potions of Mind Reading and Clairvoyance; those base items are not new homebrew.

The catalogue’s stock figures predate later spending. Do not treat this reorganization as a reset of party inventory; see [[Campaign Reference/Open Questions and Continuity|continuity questions]].

## Using this section

Item notes are the lookup point for current effects. Setting and scene notes retain history, production details, and NPC knowledge. Explicitly mark unanswered mechanics; do not fill them with assumed standard-item rules. Existing catalogues remain authoritative for their exact written effects and recipes.

General [[Rules/Homebrew Rules|house rules]], monsters, character abilities, and setting descriptions remain in their own sections; this section collects custom items, substances, enhancements, and spells.
