During combat, you can attempt to recall information about creatures using knowledge skills. This helps the party understand what they’re up against without stepping on the toes of class features.

Once per combat, on your turn, you may use a Bonus Action to make a Knowledge Check. I will let you know which skill - Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion - depending on the type of creature.
- Arcana: Aberrations, Constructs, Dragons, Oozes
- History: Humanoids, Giants, Monstrosities
- Nature: Beasts, Fey, Plants
- Religion: Celestials, Fiends, Undead

Info Gained: One piece of information per DC tier (DM’s choice). A creature never gives away its entire stat block.

| DC  | Info Learned                                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 13  | Basic Identification (creature type, reputation, origin)  |
| 17  | Combat Habits/Traits (one notable attack form or ability) |
| 21  | Defensive Clues (one resistance, immunity, or defense)    |
| 25  | Rare Lore/Social Hooks (esoteric facts, lair actions)     |

Some classes and subclasses have features that already grant monster knowledge. These override or expand Knowledge Checks:
- Ranger - All Subclasses - Favored Enemy (1st Level)
	- "You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track your favored enemies, as well as on Intelligence checks to recall information about them."
	- Advantage on the check for your favored enemy
	- Give you options on the information you get
- Ranger - Monster Slayer - Hunter’s Sense (3rd Level)
	- "As an action, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet of you. You immediately learn whether the creature has any damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities and what they are. If the creature is hidden from divination magic, you sense that it has no damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities."
	- Action - you can automatically know all this information
	- Bonus Action - attempt the roll as normal like anyone else to learn 1 thing
- Fighter - Battlemaster - Know Your Enemy (7th Level)
	- "If you spend at least 1 minute observing or interacting with another creature outside combat, you can learn certain information about its capabilities compared to your own. The DM tells you if the creature is your equal, superior, or inferior in regard to two of the following characteristics of your choice: Strength score, Dexterity score, Constitution score, Armor Class, Current hit points, Total class levels, if any, Fighter class levels, if any"
	- Different information
	- If you Know Your Enemy before the fight, you have advantage on the roll
- Monk - Cobalt Soul - Extract Aspects (3rd Level)
	- “You can strike pressure points to intuit crucial information about a foe. When you hit a creature with one of the attacks granted by your Flurry of Blows, you can analyze it ... when you analyze a creature, you learn all of its damage vulnerabilities, damage resistances, damage immunities, and condition immunities."
	- Guaranteed to get all this information if you hit
	- Can also use bonus action to attempt roll to learn one piece of higher tier information
- Rogue - Inquisitive - Insightful Fighting (3rd Level)
	- "At 3rd level, you gain the ability to decipher an opponent's tactics and develop a counter to them. As a bonus action, you make a Wisdom (Insight) check against a creature you can see that isn't incapacitated, contested by the target's Charisma (Deception) check. If you succeed, you can use your Sneak Attack against that target even if you don't have advantage on the attack roll, but not if you have disadvantage on it. This benefit lasts for 1 minute or until you successfully use this feature against a different target."
	- Different information
- Spell - Legend Lore (5th Level Spell)
	- "Name or describe a person, place, or object. The spell brings to your mind a brief summary of the significant lore about the thing you named. The lore might consist of current tales, forgotten stories, or even secret lore that has never been widely known. If the thing you named isn’t of legendary importance, you gain no information. The more information you already have about the thing, the more precise and detailed the information you receive is. The information you learn is accurate but might be couched in figurative language. For example, if you have a mysterious magic axe on hand, the spell might yield this information: 'Woe to the evildoer whose hand touches the axe, for even the haft slices the hand of the evil ones. Only a true Child of Stone, lover and beloved of Moradin, may awaken the true powers of the axe, and only with the sacred word Rudnogg on the lips.'"
	- Successful checks increase your knowledge and therefore increase the legend lore information you have
