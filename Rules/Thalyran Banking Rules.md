[[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]

**Account Safety**
Any gold in an account is guaranteed safe. It cannot be stolen, lost, or spent by anyone but the account holder.

**One-Way Coin Purse (2,000gp+)**
When an account reaches 2,000gp or more, the account holder receives a One-Way Coin Purse - This magical item allows the account holder to withdraw gold from the bank. However, gold withdrawn cannot be redeposited without returning in person to the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] to conduct the transaction.
Imprintable Item

**Interest (5,000gp+)**
When an account reaches 5,000gp or more, the account holder begins earning interest.
- **Rate:** 5% monthly
- **Calculation:** Interest is calculated on the minimum balance held in the account over the month
- **Condition:** Interest is only paid if the minimum balance for that month remains at 5,000gp or above
