Upgrading armor is much simpler and follows these guidelines:

- All prerequisite conditions must be satisfied to apply a new tag, as shown in the Armor Upgrades table.
- The cost is the flat cost listed in the cost column.
- Typically, it takes a blacksmith a full day of work (minimum 8 hours) to upgrade a piece of armor with a new tag.
- Once added, a tag can’t be removed. 

Note: You can replace the properties of the 3 armor proofing tags with a bonus to Armor Class. In this case, each tier of proofing provides a cumulative +1 bonus to AC and the cost of each is tripled.
