Short or long rests will trigger the chance of a random encounter.

The chances of a random encounter will be determined by how dangerous the environment is.

This level of danger, will set a probability and DC, that will be known to the DM's.

For short rests, the a DC  may be modified by taking precautions such as locking the room or otherwise taking some reasonable precaution.
If a random encounter occurs, it interrupts the rest. 
Another short rest can be attempted after combat, but is also subject to the possibility of a random encounter.

For long rests, a probability and DC will also be set by the DMs. 
Like short rests, if a random encounter occurs, the rest is interrupted and another rest can be attempted.
A set of  actions can be taken during the setting up of the camp.
These actions may modify the chances of a random encounter, or provide a boon for the following day.

Each player picks an action that their player will be the leader of, and will be the one making the skill check roll.
The player must describe how the group is performing the action, and can RP if they like.

ACTIONS:  
1. Create Shelter:
   In extreme weather conditions, more attention to creating a shelter will be required. 
   DMs will let you know if extra shelter is required.
   Roll a survival check
     01 - 05 : Inadequate shelter. 1pt exhaustion. 2d4 damage. 
	  06 - 10 : 2d4 damage
	  11+ 15 : 2 d4
	  16 - 20 : 3 d4
	  20 - 25 : 4 d4

3. Plan :  
	  Roll a d20 and add your int modifier. 
      The dice can be applied to any skill check performed by a member of the party when performing a skill check related to executing an aspect of the plan specified the next day. 
	  Once a dice is used, it is gone. Can apply the dice after learning of a failure once per skill check. 
	  01 - 05 : No boon  
	  06 - 10 : 1 d4
	  11 - 15 : 2 d4
	  16 - 20 : 3 d4
	  20 - 25 : 4 d4

4. Train Skill:   
     Pick a skill and make a skill check with it. You help train that skill for yourself and the group.
     You can distribute up to once dice per character for use of that skill the next day.
     The dice can be used any time the character uses that skill for the day.
      01 - 05 : No boon  
	  06 - 10 : 1 d4
	  11 - 15 : 2 d4
	  16 - 20 : 3 d4
	  20 - 25 : 4 d4

 5.  Train Combat:
	    Make an attack with the ability of your choice. (Roll a 1d20 + Ability modifier)
	    You can distribute the dice however you like.
	    The dice can be added to any attack roll, but before knowing the result.
	    Once a die is used, it is gone
	    01 - 05 : No boon  
	    06 - 10 : 1 d4
	    11 - 15 : 2 d4
	    16 - 20 : 3 d4
	    20 - 25 : 4 d4

6.   Cook:
       Put some extra effort into your meal, and prepare something that makes you heartier.
       Must have sufficient supply of food.
       Roll a 1d20 + Wis Modifier. If proficient with Cooks's Utensils, add proficiency bonus.
       Distribute the dice. They can be added to any saving throw, but before knowing the result. 
       Can combine with Chef's feat.       
	    01 - 05 : No boon  
	    06 - 10 : 1 d4
	    11 - 15 : 2 d4
	    16 - 20 : 3 d4
	    20 - 25 : 4 d4

7.   Watch: 
	   You are in charge of organizing the watch. Use ability of your choice for planning it.
	    Make a Survival Check.
	   	01 - 05 : No boon 
	    06 - 10 : 1 d4 to Watches perception checks
	    11 - 15 : 2 d4 to Watches perception checks
	    16 - 20 : 3 d4 to Watches perception checks, reduces chances of encounter
	    20 - 25 : 4 d4 to Watches perception checks, reduces chances of encounter, 50% chance to set up ambush
 
8.   Fortify:
	    You are in charge of setting up defenses for the camp.
	    Make a Survival Check.  
        01 - 05 : No boon 
	    06 - 10 : 2 d4 damage to enemies at start of encounter 
	    11 - 15 : 3 d4 damage to enemies at start of encounter
	    16 - 20 : 4 d4 damage to enemies at start of encounter
	    20 - 25 : 5 d4 damage to enemies at start of encounter

9.    Hide:
		You are in charge of Hiding the camp from being noticed
        Make a Survival Check. 
		01 - 05 : No boon 
	    06 - 10 : 1 d4 reduction of encounter probability
	    11 - 15 : 2 d4 reduction of encounter probability
	    16 - 20 : 3 d4 reduction of encounter probability
	    20 - 25 : 6 d4 reduction of encounter probability

10.    Hunt/Forage: 
        Make a Survival Check.
        01 - 05 : No boon 
	    06 - 10 : 1 d4 rations
	    11 - 15 : 2 d4 rations
	    16 - 20 : 3 d4 rations
	    20 - 25 : 4 d4 rations 

11. Improvise:
	    Suggest an activity to perform, and how you intend to perform it. 
	    DMs will determine what skills are applicable, what the outcomes are.
	     Ex. I want to study this book we found. Or I want to craft an item.
