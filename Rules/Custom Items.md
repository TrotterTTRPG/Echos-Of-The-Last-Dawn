# Violuma Fungus

A purple, faintly glowing fungus identified by the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] from a batch of specimens the party collected. The Hall's finding: the large and small caps are **the same organism at different maturities**, not two species. The glow is not a property of the fungus itself — it is *residual arcane saturation*. Violuma grows only where raw magic bleeds into soil or stone (ley seepage, old spell-scars, [[Thalyran Magitech]] runoff), and it slowly drinks that ambient charge. This is exactly why the Hall covets it: the caps store loose spell energy that alchemy can coax back out.

| Form                      | Common Name | Description                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Violuma Crown** (large) | "Crown"     | Mature cap. Dense flesh saturated with held arcane charge. Steadies magic. |
| **Violuma Spark** (small) | "Spark"     | Juvenile cap. High spore content, volatile, brightly luminescent.          |

# Low-Level Potions

Apprentices at the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]] can brew these reliably.

### Draught of Lucid Casting

- **Rarity:** Common — **Value:** 60 gp — **Craft Cost:** 25 gp + 7 Crowns
- **Recipe:** 7 Violuma Crowns + 1 dose Alchemist's Base (5 gp) + a pinch of Powdered Silver (10 gp) + 10 gp labor
- **Effect:** A calm, warm violet tonic. **Drink as a bonus action.** Until the start of your next turn, you have **advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration** on a spell.
- *A panic button, not a buff — meant to be drunk the same turn you cast something you can't afford to drop.*

### Glowspore Draught

- **Rarity:** Common — **Value:** 50 gp — **Craft Cost:** 15 gp + 7 Sparks
- **Recipe:** 7 Violuma Sparks + 1 flask Lamp Oil (1 gp) + 1 dose Alchemist's Base (5 gp) + 9 gp labor
- **Effect:** A cloudy vial of suspended, drifting spores. **As an action, throw it** at a creature within 20 feet, making a ranged attack roll against its AC (or shatter it on a surface within 20 ft, coating the nearest creature). On a hit, the target is coated in glowing violet spores and sheds dim light in a 5-ft radius. **Attack rolls against the coated creature have a +2 bonus.** The spores are shaken loose — and the effect ends — the first time the creature is **hit by an attack**.
- *Helps the party land the first solid blow on a slippery or heavily-armored target; the mark is spent the moment it lands.*

# Refined Reagents

Bulk caps are distilled into stable reagents. This is the "expensive" path — a single unit eats a big share of the stock, but unlocks the higher-tier brews below. Refining is done by the [[Hall of Alchemical Arts]].

| Reagent            | From      | Yield / Cost          | Value        | Craft Cost         |
| ------------------ | --------- | --------------------- | ------------ | ------------------ |
| **Crowncap Resin** | 18 Crowns | 1 vial per 18 Crowns  | 150 gp/vial  | 30 gp + 18 Crowns  |
| **Sparkcap Dust**  | 18 Sparks | 1 pouch per 18 Sparks | 150 gp/pouch | 30 gp + 18 Sparks  |

- **Crowncap Resin** — Crowns slow-simmered over a low arcane flame into a thick, glowing violet resin that holds a "charge" of unspent spell energy. A stabilizer/potency reagent for effects that must *last*.
- **Sparkcap Dust** — Mature Sparks dried and milled into a fine, volatile luminescent powder that reacts to arcane light and makes hidden things visible. A reagent for sight, scrying, and detection brews.

# Higher-Level Potions

These require a refined reagent plus supporting ingredients. Only the Head Alchemist, [[Belvyre Flintflower]], brews the Rare items (the custom recipes were supplied by the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]]).

### Potion of Mind Reading  *(standard)*

- **Rarity:** Rare — **Value:** 500 gp — **Craft Cost:** 130 gp + 1 vial Crowncap Resin
- **Recipe:** 1 vial Crowncap Resin + 1 Spellbound Crystal Shard (25 gp) + 1 dose Alchemist's Base (5 gp) + 100 gp labor
- **Effect:** When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the *detect thoughts* spell (save DC 13). The resin sustains the mental link for the full 10-minute duration.

### Potion of Sustained Sorcery  *(CUSTOM — Hall of Metamagic & Theory flagship)*

- **Rarity:** Rare — **Value:** 800 gp — **Craft Cost:** 150 gp + 2 vials Crowncap Resin
- **Recipe:** **2 vials Crowncap Resin** + 1 Spellbound Crystal Shard (25 gp) + 1 dose Alchemist's Base (5 gp) + 120 gp labor
- **Effect:** When you drink this potion (bonus action), a surge of *ordered* arcane energy steadies your mind for **10 minutes**. For the duration:
    - You have **advantage on Constitution saving throws** to maintain concentration.
    - You can **concentrate on two spells at once**, provided the second spell is **2nd level or lower**. Casting a third concentration spell ends the oldest.
    - If you fail a concentration save or are otherwise forced to end concentration, you lose concentration on **both** spells.
    - When the duration ends, you may keep concentration on **only one** spell of your choice; the other ends.
- *The Hall's showpiece and the reason they want more Crowns than anyone. A crude, bottled taste of a discipline they claim only true metamages can wield.*

### Potion of Clairvoyance  *(standard)*

- **Rarity:** Rare — **Value:** 500 gp — **Craft Cost:** 200 gp + 1 pouch Sparkcap Dust
- **Recipe:** 1 pouch Sparkcap Dust + 1 Powdered Gemstone Focus (100 gp) + 100 gp labor
- **Effect:** When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the *clairvoyance* spell.

### Potion of Truesight  *(CUSTOM)*

- **Rarity:** Rare — **Value:** 800 gp — **Craft Cost:** 210 gp + 2 pouches Sparkcap Dust
- **Recipe:** **2 pouches Sparkcap Dust** + 1 Gorgon-glass Lens (50 gp) + 1 Wisp of Ectoplasm (40 gp) + 120 gp labor
- **Effect:** When you drink this potion, you gain **truesight out to 30 feet for 1 hour**. For the duration you can see in normal and magical darkness, see invisible creatures and objects, automatically detect visual illusions (and succeed on saving throws against them), perceive the true form of a shapechanger or a creature transformed by magic, and see into the Ethereal Plane.
