 "On the Tenth Age of Twilight" - A book written by a half-mad scholar describing that every age ends in a Great Sleep, where the world itself withers before being "sun awake again." The book is considered heretical nonsense. ([[The Cycle]])

"The Litany of Seasons" - A religious text that describes the world as a garden that must be pruned, burned, and reseeded. Most priests take this metaphorically, but it's actually literal. ([[The Cycle]])

"Conflict and Sexuality" - A collection of journal entries from religious warriors abstaining from pleasures of the flesh. One outlines imagining that the armored figure he fights against is a woman. One admits to having sexual fantasies about his god. One admits to holding his own hand over a candle flame to prevent sexual thoughts about his female commander.

Book of Children's Stories:
	There was a man who lived by a forest. 
	As he grew older and older, eh started losing his hair, until one day, on his deathbed, he was completely bald. That day, he called his children to a meeting.
	He said, "Look at my hair. If used to be so magnificent, but it's completely gone now. My hair can't be saved. But look outside at the forest. It's such a lovely forest with so many trees, but sooner or later, they'll all be cut down and this forest will look as bald as my hair."
	"What I was you to do" the man continued, "is every time a tree is cut down or dies, plant a new one in my memory. Tell your descendants to do the same. It shall be our family's duty to keep this forest strong."
	So they did. Each time the forest lost a tree, the children replanted one, and so did their children, and their children after them. And for centuries, the forest remained as lush and pretty as it once was, all because of one man and his receding hairline.

"Little Magics" - A book where a not very successful wizard explains that he believes that everyone possesses little magics. 
	My ankles never twist. I've always been rather active. One time, I was running and stepped in a hole. I lost my shoe and landed sprawling about 5 ft away. I pulled my show on and kept running. I have a friend who somehow makes better tea and everyone else even though she only buys the ones that come in bags. All you have to do is put the bag in hot water. My brother can copy complex paper folds just by looking at the finished product. My mother can do the same thing with knots. I knew a guy who never made an error while transcribing scrolls.
	Maybe we all have little magics, the kind that don't realize you have. Just tiny things that make your life slightly better but are completely unnoticed on the outside.
