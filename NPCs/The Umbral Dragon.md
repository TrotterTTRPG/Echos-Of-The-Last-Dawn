
**Facts**:
- Age - Estimated over 3k years - ancient even by draconic measure
- Type - Undead Dragon
- Cycle Origin - Conceived in [[The Umbral Soul]], Born in the [[The Effulgent Soul]]
- Location - Hidden deep beneath [[Verda]]

**Appearance**
- Ashen silver scales - dull until they're in the phosphorescent light of the Umbral Soul
- Eyes are like starlight [Like This](https://www.reddit.com/media?url=https%3A%2F%2Fpreview.redd.it%2Fblind-owl-with-galaxy-eyes-v0-ff8428agdma81.jpg%3Fwidth%3D640%26crop%3Dsmart%26auto%3Dwebp%26s%3D29bace2fd4b8b5b8a5f40d675782068acc71349e)
- Wings are torn and translucent
- Voice is barely a whisper, but still loud due to its size

**Personality and Motivations**
- Swings from patient, calculating, and eerily calm to enraged at the injustice it is facing and must constantly rail against
- Obsessed with balance - believes [[The Cycle]] must flow as intended and must not be tampered with
- Haunted by memory - it dreams of its mother's voice and how she would describe the Umbral Soul. Having never experience it, it can only dream of the paradise that will come

**Background Story**
- Born from an egg laid during the Umbral Soul but hatched into the light of the new Effulgent soul
- Its mother, who was able to exist in both the Umbral and Effulgent ages, saw the newborn's nature as dangerous in the new cycle. She commanded it to hide beneath the world, promising the Umbral Soul would return in 2k years when it would finally belong
- Obedient, it buried itself deep beneath mountains and forgotten ruins.
- When the promised Umbral Soul never came, it emerged to a world alien and blinding. Everything that should have perished had prospered. The Effulgent Soul's dominance was unnatural and unending. It was forced to retreat back to its hiding place
- Over centuries it studied mortals, their faiths, and their magic. Seeking the reason. It learned of a elven ritual - a manipulation of the planet's soul itself, and act of hubris that stalled the cosmic balance. 
- Unable to confront the elf directly, it began to influence others: cults, secret orders, scholars - planting the idea that the world was sick and something ancient must return
- It sees itself as a herald of restoration, not destruction.
