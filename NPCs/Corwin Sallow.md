Corwin "Corry" Sallow
Human male
Age: 41 (looks a decade older)
A chronic petty drunk and day-laborer of the Bronze District, [[Thalyra]]
Most recently a "guest" of [[The Deephold]] — and desperate to stay one
Known around the cells as "the Devil-Killer" — always with a laugh

**Appearance:**
- Lean to the point of gaunt, with the loose, sallow skin of a man bled thin over months, not days
- Keeps his left sleeve buttoned to the wrist; beneath it, a *ladder* of ring-shaped welts climbs his forearm — some old and silvered, some fresh and weeping, each where something latched and pulled. They look like lamprey sores or infected leech-bites, nothing fanged
- Watery, red-rimmed eyes ringed with sleeplessness; they never stop flicking to doorways, windows, reflections, and the angle of the sun
- Knuckles split and splintered — the hands of a man who drove nails in a hurry, twice over a grave and once into a crate lid
- Clutches a cheap tin token of [[Tyr]], the balance-scale worn smooth from thumbing
- Smells of cheap gin, tar, and the brine of the [[Merchant's Landing - Western Docks|Western Docks]]

**Demeanor:**
- Starts every account loud, slurring, and grinning — the practiced patter of a man who's told this story to every guard who'd listen
- The grin slips the moment anyone actually believes him; sober, sleepless terror underneath
- Speaks of [[Mella]] with a sick, lingering fondness even now — half the time he sounds like a man describing a lover, not a monster
- Clings to the cell bars; the closer he gets to the door, the more he comes apart — begging, bargaining, swearing oaths on the Scales of [[Tyr]]
- Would rather be beaten and dragged back inside than walk out a free man

Nobody remembers when Corry Sallow wasn't half in the bottle. He hauled crates at the [[Merchant's Landing - Western Docks|Western Docks]], drank his wages, and collected a tidy ledger of petty offenses — drunk and disorderly, public urination off the Bronze District piers, one memorable night singing to a Warden's horse. He was already on parole when it happened, which is the only part of his record anyone in [[The Deephold]] takes seriously.

By his telling — and he will tell it — there was a woman named *[[Mella]]*. It started warm: more than friendly, the best thing that had happened to a man like him in years. She asked, sweet as anything, if she could come in and stay — and so she did, up in the attic-corner, and after that she was *insatiable*. He says it with a wretched pride. He didn't understand what that hunger really was until the night she fastened onto his arm in the dark and something in her mouth latched and *pulled*, and he woke gutted of strength with his sleeve glued to the sore.

So he shot her through the heart with an arrow and buried her. She came back. She wasn't angry — that's the part that breaks him to say — she came back *gentle*, still wanting him. And after that she never truly left. Night after night, week after week, she returned: feeding from his arm, whispering that she loved him, that they'd never be parted. He stopped sleeping. He stopped eating. He saw her in the black of windows and the corner of his eye, waiting. He killed her a second time and buried her deeper; she came back again. Running didn't work — she always found the dark he was hiding in. Drink didn't work. Prayer didn't work. The man simply wasted, night by night, into the husk now clinging to the bars.

The crate was his last desperate idea: with a crossbow bolt already lodged through her heart and her body lying still as stone, he nailed her into a freight box, bribed a manifest clerk two silver and a bottle, and shipped her to [[Velrosk]] on the morning tide. *"You don't bury the devil,"* he says. *"You send her somewhere she can't walk back from."* It bought him three quiet nights before the dread came crawling back — because he has put [[Mella]] in the ground twice and across the sea once, and she has never *once* failed to return. He insists she is far too strong for the likes of him; only her love keeps leaving him openings, and those openings are the sole reason he still breathes.

So Corwin Sallow did the cleverest thing a desperate drunk ever did: he got himself locked up. The escalating benders, the parole violation — those weren't bad luck, they were a *plan*. Because a cell in [[The Deephold]] is the one place she has never reached him. It is not his home for him to invite her into; it is stone, and torchlight, and guards who never leave, and it is never, ever dark. For the first time in months, here, he slept.

Here is the part that damns him: there is no [[Mella]]. No lease in the district rolls bears the name. The lodging-house keeper swears no such woman ever held a room. The attic-corner is empty and always was, by every account but his. The Wardens found a bloodstained floor and a raving drunk and nothing — *nobody* — to say a murder ever happened. With no victim, no body, and no proof the woman existed at all, the magistrate could only hang the small stuff on him: public intoxication, public urination, parole violation. A few short months. Served, now — which is the cruelest sentence of all, because they are about to put him back out into the dark, and the next ship from [[Velrosk]] is already on the water.

The cruel joke is that every word is true. The party will meet a man the whole city has written off as a lunatic, and the only question that matters is whether anyone listens before that ship comes in.

**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)  **Proficiency Bonus** +2

***Marked Prey.*** Corry has been fed upon many times and never fully healed. He has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice undead, and he wakes at the faintest sound after dark. The DM may rule he simply *feels* [[Mella]] drawing near before anyone else does.

***Drunkard's Tenacity.*** The first time Corry would drop to 0 hit points but isn't killed outright, he makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw, dropping to 1 hit point instead on a success. He recovers this use after a long rest.

***Reek of Gin.*** Any creature that relies on scent has disadvantage on checks to track Corry by smell — he reeks of cheap spirits and tar.

### Actions

***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) piercing damage. This is the same crossbow he's put a bolt through [[Mella]]'s heart with — twice; he never lets it stray far.

***Desperate Grasp.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* The target is grappled (escape DC 9) as Corry clings to a sleeve, a belt, a cell bar — whatever keeps him from being put out the door. He cannot grapple a creature more than one size larger than himself.

**Gear.** A worn hand crossbow with a half-empty case of bolts; a creased [[Velrosk]] freight chit (crate manifest stub, the clerk's mark smudged); a tin token of [[Tyr]]; an empty gin bottle he won't part with.
