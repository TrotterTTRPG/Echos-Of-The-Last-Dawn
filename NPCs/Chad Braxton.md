**Basic Information**
- **Name:** Chad Braxton
- **Race:** Human
- **Class:** Wizard (Order of the Scribes)
- **Age:** Mid-20s
- **Occupation:** Staff member at [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] (Gold District, [[Thalyra]])
- **Family:** Powerful, wealthy parents in [[Thalyra]]; family has significant political/financial influence; Son of [[Edran Braxton]]

**Appearance**
- Red hair; perpetually sunburnt (always just back from vacation)
- Well-dressed in expensive clothes (paid for by parents)
- Physically fit despite doing minimal work

**Personality**
- Loud, overconfident, deeply unserious
- Charming in a shallow way; uses humor to deflect from lack of substance
- Lacks any sense of consequence or responsibility
- Takes credit for others' work without shame

**Work Ethic (or Lack Thereof)**
- Does minimal actual work at the Hall
- Passes off tasks to colleagues whenever possible
- Never gets reprimanded despite his behavior (protected by family connections)
- [[Ereykos Phirona]] describes his "primary arcane specialization as delegation"
- [[Aranemin Zylris]] reports Chad "supervised" him doing Chad's entire intake log
- Whenever work gets difficult or tedious, Chad finds an excuse to leave it for someone else

**Social Dynamics at the Hall**
- Resented by his colleagues (especially [[Ereykos Phirona]] and [[Aranemin Zylris]])
- Protected from consequences by his family's wealth and influence
- Represents everything wrong with nepotism in the institution

**Relationship with Tracy**
- [[Tracy]] (from a moderately wealthy Silver District family) is obsessed with Chad
- She hovers around him constantly at the shop front
- [[Velagrys Faro]] notes she "attends nearly every one of his shifts"
- [[Aranemin Zylris]] sarcastically observes she "watches *everyone* like they're about to steal him"
- [[Ereykos Phirona]] finds the situation "distracting to the work environment"
- Chad seems largely oblivious to or indifferent toward her attention
