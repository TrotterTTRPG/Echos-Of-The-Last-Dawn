Located in [[The Cradle of Thorns]]

A druidic order dedicated to healing the scars of the war and preserving the wilds

Seeking purpose, [[Aulberos Lightmind]] joined [[The Stonebloom Enclave]]
- Found solace in their teachings for many decades and rose to some prominence within the order.
- Over time, a faction within the Enclave began selling their powers and services to nobles and warlords, abandoning their neutrality and ideals.
- Aulberos openly opposed them, clashing with the new leadership - particularly the ruling council that included his old friend [[Aelan]].
- Disillusioned, he left the Enclave rather than watch it decay from within.
