The guards of [[Thalyra]]
Commonly called "the Wardens" or "the Watch"

[[Thalyra]]'s standing peacekeeping force - the city’s guards, investigators, and ceremonial protectors.

They maintain a strict schedule of drills, patrols, and ceremony, yet few have ever seen true battle.

The rank and file report to [[The Bronze Bastion]] while the captain's office is in [[The Golden Hall of Command]]
