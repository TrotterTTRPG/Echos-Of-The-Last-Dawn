Located in the [[Monastery of the Sacred Scroll]]
A bladesinger faction dedicated to the protection of knowledge, with the library of [[Dornthalm]] being the greatest repository of knowledge they vow to protect.
[[Aelar Aurelius]] is a member
The Protectors of the Ancient Tomes plays a major role within [[Dornthalm]] and its government, currently the Head Archivist of the library is a member of this order

**History**
- In the age of great strife, every person, every race and every town throughout the continent of [[Advelde]] had a role to play during the great war.
- An order was formed here in [[Solstice]] called the [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]], answering the call for the sake of [[Dornthalm]]
- Uniting under a prominent Eladrin in the town of [[Solstice]], the townsfolk stepped forwards, all of which great scholars, and were taught in the arts of the blade under this prominent Eladrin.
- Paired with their magical abilities, and their vast knowledge, they answered the call during the Siege of [[Dornthalm]] to both protect its people, and more importantly, the great library that resided there.
- Each of these scholars swore an oath, founding the [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]]. This oath was then written down on a scroll by this prominent Eladrin and currently still resides within the prominent Eladrin’s home to this day, which has been since named to the [[Monastery of the Sacred Scroll]].
- Every initiate would recite this oath as part of their final act before being inducted officially into the order as a full member.
