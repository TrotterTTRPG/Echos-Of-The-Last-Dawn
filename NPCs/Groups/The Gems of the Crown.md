The 5 officials holding the highest seats of governmental power
Upper house of the [[Conclave of the Crown]]

These are officials representing the interests of not just [[Thalyra]], but the other major cities as well.

There officials are 
[[Ardda Calhoune]] - Human Woman from [[Thalyra]]. This seat is traditionally held by this family.
[[Dorrik RockBarron]] - Male Dwarf from [[Velrosk]]
[[Zarielle Vex]] - Tiefling Woman from [[Iridia]]
[[Liora Vaelthir]] - Elf Woman from [[Dornthalm]]

[[Londomere Vayne]] - High Elf man from [[Dornthalm]] - Hold the most power in crown.

The Gems of the crown are the ones with the levers of power in the world.
For the creation of this centralized government within [[Thalyra]], [[Dornthalm]] required it hold two seats to the one set of the other cities, including [[Thalyra]] itself.

Though these individuals hold all the power in [[Thalyra]] - It is the money of the crown bank they derive their power from. They are free to distribute funds to the city, but only as much as is agreed upon from the bank, who is controlled by the wealthy and ultra-wealthy.

In many ways, the Gems of the crown are beholden to the money that flows in from the other parts of the world, and as such are beholden to the richest of the rich in the other cities.

Each member holds responsibility for different aspects of the cities affairs, but they all must vote on the appropriation of the large funds.
It is not public knowledge as to who does what or controls what within the upper house.
