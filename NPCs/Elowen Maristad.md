
**Role:** Chief Protocol Officer, [[Hall of Accord]] in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Age:** 38  
**Background:** Former diplomat / scribe from [[Iridia]]

**Appearance:**
- Always immaculately dressed in tailored robes of pale gray and silver, accented with subtle embroidery depicting scales of balance and quills.
- Hair pulled back into a tight braid, streaked with silver threads from stress and meticulous late nights.
- Carries a slender staff topped with a silver orb that doubles as a wand for magically marking contracts or highlighting critical clauses.
- Eyes are sharp and observant, constantly scanning documents or the room for disruptions.

**Personality:**
- **Meticulous and exacting:** Elowen keeps every treaty, contract, and oath in perfect order. Misspellings, missing seals, or even a slightly crooked signature draw her sharp notice.
- **Highly disciplined:** He arrives before sunrise and leaves after everyone else, personally overseeing every stage of negotiations or registrations.
- **Unflappable:** Even in heated disputes, He remains calm, precise, and authoritative, making her a linchpin of the Hall’s operations.
- **Slightly intimidating:** Many adventurers and emissaries respect her, but a few fear her quiet, piercing gaze and reputation for catching even the smallest procedural error.

**Role in the Hall:**
- Supervises registration clerks, scribes, and minor mediators.
- Ensures that every accord, treaty, or contract is legally and magically binding, preventing errors that could undo decades of careful diplomacy.
- Sometimes called upon to mediate disputes, especially when both parties respect procedure more than power.
- Known to leave small, discreet notes on draft contracts for diplomats or adventurers who attempt shortcuts, showing her insistence on proper form.
