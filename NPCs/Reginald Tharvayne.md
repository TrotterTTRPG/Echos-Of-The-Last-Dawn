AKA "Reggie"

**Role:** Head of Public Works, [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Age:** 42  
**Background:** Minor noble / unsuccessful merchant

**Appearance:**
- Slightly pudgy, with a perpetually flushed face from overexertion or embarrassment.
- Always impeccably dressed in a slightly outdated, gilded coat—more about appearances than practicality.
- Hair is thinning at the crown but combed meticulously; he sports a waxed mustache that he believes commands respect.

**Personality / Motivations:**
- **Ambitious but cowardly:** Reggie is desperate for recognition and social advancement, but unwilling to take responsibility for failure.
- **Secretive and cautious:** He commissioned the “Sewer Cleaner” construct in private, hoping it would appear as a brilliant innovation under his leadership—but he will not admit it unless cornered.
- **Delegates responsibility ruthlessly:** He sent the standard sewer cleaning crew down the tunnels, but they never returned. He is more embarrassed than concerned, hoping the party will “handle” the mess so he can claim credit.
- **Facade of competence:** Outwardly jovial and charming, but constantly deflects questions about the sewer disaster. Will speak in vague platitudes about “innovative solutions” and “city efficiency.”
- **Oblivious to danger:** He believes the construct is working brilliantly and cannot imagine it could be causing real harm—he assumes it’s just “performing poorly” rather than actively threatening anyone.

**Background Story:**
- Born to a minor noble house that had more ambition than wealth, Reggie attempted a career as a merchant but failed spectacularly, mostly due to naïveté and terrible negotiation skills.
- Rather than be disgraced entirely, he was offered a minor government position. Through a mix of luck, family influence, and a desperate city council’s tolerance, he ended up as the head of Public Works - a single man department created to placate the Tharvaynes.

He has one job - schedule a once monthly cleaning of the city's expansive sewer system.
