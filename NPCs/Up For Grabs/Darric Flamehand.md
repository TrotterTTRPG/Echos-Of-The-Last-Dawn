Adventurer introduced in [[The Adventurer's Docket]]
Assigned to the Department of Arcane Acuity

Human - curley Red hair - clean shaven - open chested red robe

A popular human pyromancer whose daring exploits in the northern mountains have become the stuff of tavern stories. His robes shimmer with faint embers, and a faint heat radiates from him at all times.

He tends to be bold, and self assured in the wisdom of his actions.
He leads and hates being led.
The only reason he doesn't rule all, is merely due to the failing of idiots to see his superiority.

He had been issued a team to scout the forests outside the [[Thalyra]] in search of [[Rockwood Trees]] and [[Blitz Lizards]] when he came across [[Hookmaw]] tracks.
