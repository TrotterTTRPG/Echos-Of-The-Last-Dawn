**Race/Build:** Human, tall, broad-shouldered, moves like she's never once been in a hurry and never needed to be.
**Age:** Late 30s
**Occupation:** Fixer/collector for [[Aelan]]
**Appearance**
- Close-cropped blonde hair, no jewelry, nothing that jingles or catches light
- Plain, well-made clothes — expensive but deliberately unremarkable, built for movement rather than show
- A single old scar through one eyebrow; otherwise unmarked, deliberately so
- Sits and stands with total stillness — no fidgeting, no wasted motion
- Doesn't raise her voice. Ever. Doesn't need to.

**Personality**
- Economical with words to the point of unsettling — says exactly what's necessary and not one syllable more
- Not cruel, not warm — simply unmoved. Threats aren't performed; they're just facts she states once
- Absolute professional. Doesn't get drawn into Chad's jokes, doesn't rise to bait, doesn't negotiate past her mandate
- Competent in a way that reads as dangerous precisely because nothing about her is flashy

**Voice & Performance**
- Flat, low, unhurried. Full stops, not trailing sentences
- Never repeats herself, never raises volume to be heard — makes people lean in instead
- Silence is a tool she uses constantly and deliberately; she'll let a pause sit until the other person fills it
- Doesn't banter, doesn't insult, doesn't explain herself twice

**In a Fight (if it comes to that)**
Bolo is built as a last-resort combat encounter, not a social one — she would much rather vanish than fight, and will do so if given any opening. If cornered, she's efficient, not showy: no monologuing, no taunts, just the fastest route out or through.

![[bolo.png]]

**Bolo's Role at the Table**

She's not meant to be beaten — she's meant to be *memorable.* If the party is smart enough to just listen instead of confront, she never even knows they were there. If they blow it, she gives them a taste of just how far outmatched they'd be if this ever became personal, and then she's gone. She should feel like the first sign that this quest connects to something bigger tha`n a wizard's gambling problem.
