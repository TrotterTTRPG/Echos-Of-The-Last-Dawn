*Curator Sabine Corvane*

**Role:** Senior Curator of Necrotic Studies at the [[Hall of Arcane Acuity]] in [[Thalyra]] (Silver District)
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 51
**Background:** A Thalyran mortician's daughter who turned an unsettling childhood ease around the dead into the Hall's foundational undead-research program; she has been below ground, cataloguing the dead that don't stay dead, for nineteen years.

**Appearance:**
- Tall and spare, with the permanent underground pallor of someone who measures daylight in *Daylight* spells. Dark hair gone iron-grey at the temples, scraped back and pinned tight so it never falls across a specimen.
- Wears the Hall's charcoal-grey longcoat like [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill|Torren]] and [[Caelith Verion|Caelith]], but hers is scuffed at the cuffs and faintly stained, worn over an apron of warded leather. Thin silver-rimmed lenses she flips down to examine tissue.
- Ink-stained fingers, always gloved when working. Carries a warded cane she uses as much for pointing and prodding as for walking.
- She smells faintly of myrrh and cold stone — the residue of the *gentle repose* she casts on half the things she touches.

**Personality:**
- Precise, unhurried, and genuinely *curious* where others are afraid — the Hall's motto "Knowledge before Fear" isn't a slogan to her, it's temperament. A husk on her table is a question, not a horror.
- Honest to a fault. She will tell you exactly what she does and doesn't know, and she refuses to file a specimen she can't name. In nineteen years she has never had to leave a case open.
- Treats the dead with a clinical tenderness that reads as either deeply respectful or deeply wrong, depending on who's watching. She talks *to* her specimens while she works.
- Her blind spot: she has spent so long inside the Hall's logic that she has stopped hearing how her proposals sound to people who don't live underground. She can pitch something monstrous in the gentle tone of a physician recommending rest.

**Role in the Hall:**
- She is the Hall's foremost authority on undeath — the one [[Torren Feldrik Stonequill|Torren]] fetches and [[Caelith Verion|Caelith]] defers to the instant anything dead walks through the door. She keeps the undead wing of the **Level 4 Deep Vault** and runs the undead dissections on **Level 2**.
- **In [[Delivering the Devil]]:** after Torren's quick hand-off, *she* runs the scene. She examines [[Mella]], finds the ring-welt that's no bite, the body that won't cool or rot, the old vampire-remedies that do nothing — and, for the first time in her career, **logs the case open.** She's the one who spots that **light** is the only lever, writes containment around it, and — because she cannot bear an unstudied specimen — makes the quiet, obscene **Larder Arrangement** pitch: retain [[Corwin Sallow]], warded and watched and never in the dark, as the one key that turns the lock. She commissions the party to find where a thing like this *comes from.*
- **Hook:** she is a good, careful woman one small step from an atrocity, and she'll never see it as one. The party can befriend her, use her as their standing arcane resource — or be the only people in the room who tell her *no.*
- **DM only:** Sabine is the first competent mind in [[Thalyra]] to bump against the edge of [[The Loveslain]] and the Cycle's drift, without any idea that's what she's doing. The one thing she prides herself on — naming what's on her table — is the exact thing this specimen denies her. She must never learn what Mella truly is.

## Stat Block

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 12 (15 with *mage armor*)
**Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
**Speed** 30 ft.

**Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +4
**Skills** Arcana +7, Medicine +6, History +5, Investigation +5, Insight +4
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft. (warded lenses), passive Perception 12
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Dwarvish
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  **Proficiency Bonus** +2

***Expert Necrologist.*** Sabine has advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) and Wisdom (Medicine) checks made to identify undead, necrotic effects, or the cause of a creature's death or animation. Given ten minutes with a body or specimen, she learns whether it is truly dead, and what — if anything — is keeping it moving. *(Even she cannot resolve what [[Mella]] is.)*

***Composure of the Vault.*** Sabine is immune to the frightened condition from undead, and has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

### Actions

***Warded Cane.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

***Chill Touch (Cantrip).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d8) necrotic damage, the target can't regain hit points until the start of Sabine's next turn, and if it is undead it also has disadvantage on attack rolls against her until then.

***Spellcasting.*** Sabine casts one of her prepared spells (see below).

### Spellcasting

Sabine is a 5th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following wizard spells prepared:

- Cantrips (at will): *chill touch*, *light*, *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*
- 1st level (4 slots): *detect magic*, *false life*, *mage armor*, *protection from evil and good*
- 2nd level (3 slots): *gentle repose*, *magic weapon*, *see invisibility*
- 3rd level (2 slots): *dispel magic*, *magic circle*
