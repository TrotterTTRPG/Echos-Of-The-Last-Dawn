Title: Arch-Mechanist of the [[Department of Arcane Mechanisms (DAM)]] in the Bronze District in [[Thalyra]]
Race: Rock Gnome
Age: 143

"Magic is simply a system. Once you understand the rules, you build with it."

Appearance:
- Very small, even for a gnome
- Shock of silver hair always escaping from a leather cap
- Thick magnifying goggles worn on his forehead
- Grease stains on robes
- Numerous mechanical tools strapped to a belt

Personality:
- Brilliant
- Blunt
- Hyper-focused
- Impatient with bureaucracy
- Ruthlessly practical

Within [[Thalyra]], he is considered one of the most brilliant artificers alive. He pioneered the process of stabilizing beast essences into arcane power cells, making large-scale arcane mechanisms possible.

Moral Philosophy of [[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]]:
- He believes beasts are part of a natural arcane ecosystem and their essences can be used responsibly.
	- "An ankheg heart rotting in the wilderness serves no one. The same heart digging tunnels for new departments or study can save lives."
- He does have limits:
	- He refuses to experiment on sentient creatures
	- He rejects necromantic energy sources
	- He dislikes war machines even when they're explicitly commissioned for defense
