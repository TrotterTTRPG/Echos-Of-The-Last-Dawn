**Basic Information**
- Full Name: Faris Harith
	- Faris (Arabic): “Horseman” or “knight”
	- Harith (Arabic): “Plowman” or “cultivator”
- Race: Likely Human (not explicitly stated)
- Occupation: Courier, smuggler, and information broker
- Primary Location: [[Velrosk]], where he and [[Chein Po]] first met
- Age: Older than Chein by several decades; worldly, experienced, and shrewd
- Status: Alive — still operating as a courier and contact in [[Velrosk]]

**Personality and Demeanor**
- Calm, patient, and observant, with an old-world charm and quiet authority.
- Possesses a natural paternal instinct, though he hides it beneath a pragmatic exterior.
- Nonjudgmental — accepts people as they are rather than who they should be.
- Sharp-minded and well-connected, but not power-hungry; prefers subtle influence.
- Sees through deceit easily but rarely calls it out — he values silence as a form of honesty.

**Role in Chein’s Life**
- Met Chein during her years in [[Velrosk]], while she operated as a criminal, thief, and spy.
- Functioned as her primary contact and supplier, arranging goods, information, and safehouses.
- Unlike others in the underworld, he respected her privacy and silence, never prying into her past.
- Became the only person Chein ever confided in about her childhood, the drake, and her father’s death.
- His empathy and discretion led him to become a mentor and father figure, quietly filling the void left by [[Kai Yang]]’s death.

**Relationship with Chein**
- Treats her like a wayward daughter — not indulgent, but protective.
- Offers guidance without judgment, believing everyone deserves a chance to change course.
- Encouraged her to leave the criminal life and pursue a more honest path.
- Recognized her guilt and self-loathing and helped her see that atonement can take many forms.
- Before parting, he reassured her that she could always find him again if she needed counsel or help.

**Catalyst for Change**
- Told Chein about [[The Adventurer's Docket]] in [[Thalyra]], where she could:
	- Start fresh with legitimate work
	- Meet new allies outside the underworld
	- Rebuild her life without lies or theft
- Also revealed that a woman named Shun had been sighted in or near [[Thalyra]] — sparking Chein’s decision to go.
- His conversation with her marks a turning point — the moment Chein chooses to honor her father’s memory and seek redemption.

**Personality Traits**
- Empathic Realist: Understands the necessity of crime in harsh worlds but believes in redemption.
- Stoic and Grounded: Rarely shows fear or anger; uses humor and patience to defuse tension.
- Worldly and Wise: Experienced in both legitimate and illicit dealings.
- Protective Mentor: Subtly looks out for Chein’s safety without smothering her independence.

**Symbolic and Narrative Role**
- Represents redemption, moral grounding, and paternal warmth.
- Acts as a bridge between Chein’s criminal past and her hopeful future.
- Symbolizes the idea of found family — love and mentorship outside blood ties.
- His dual meaning name (“knight” and “cultivator”) reflects his nature:
	- “Knight” — defender and guide
	- “Cultivator” — nurturer of second chances and new beginnings

**Current Status and Hooks**
- Still operates in [[Velrosk]] as a courier and broker; a reliable contact for adventurers.
- May send or receive letters to Chein if she ever writes back.
- Possible campaign uses:
	- Provides information or side quests via courier messages.
	- Could become a plot link if [[Velrosk]] comes under threat or political intrigue.
	- Might uncover new truths about [[Shun Yang]] or Chein’s past through his contacts.

**Key Quotes (Possible Roleplay Tone)**
- “I don’t need your history, girl — I see who you are right now. That’s enough.”
- Honesty isn’t about telling the truth. It’s about living it.”
- “You’ve run long enough, Chein. Maybe it’s time to start walking toward something instead.”
