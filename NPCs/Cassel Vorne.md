**Title:** Desk Officer at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Tiefling  
**Gender**: Male
**Age:** 27  

**Appearance:**
- Slim, with deep burgundy skin and short black horns that curve back close to his skull
- Long-fingered hands that handle documents and coin with precise, economical movements
- Yellow eyes that are calm to the point of flatness
- Uniform worn correctly but not enthusiastically — the one area where his professionalism shows a hairline crack

**Personality:**
- Dry and observational — his humour is invisible to about half the people he uses it on
- Excellent under pressure; does not visibly stress, which unnerves some colleagues
- Holds grudges with perfect patience and expresses them through bureaucratic exactness
- Is building toward something, though he has told no one what

Cassel Vorne applied to the Crown Bank three times before being hired. He keeps the rejection letters in his desk drawer. He is not certain why — possibly as a record, possibly as motivation, possibly because he enjoys the irony of their current location. He is the youngest officer on the public floor and the most likely, in [[Seravael Dawnmere]]'s private assessment, to eventually move upstairs.
