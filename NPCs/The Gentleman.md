**Race/Build:** Water genasi — lean, composed, unnervingly still except for the faint damp sheen and slow ripple that crosses his skin when he's actually paying attention to something.
**Age:** Impossible to place — genasi longevity plus a lifetime of not letting anything show
**Occupation:** Proprietor of Thalyra's most exclusive (and least official) gambling circuit

**Appearance**
- Impeccably tailored in deep blues and greys — nothing flashy, everything expensive
- Pale blue-grey skin, hair that behaves like it's always slightly underwater, moving a beat slower than it should
- Manicured hands he uses constantly when he talks — a card never quite out of them, even when he's not playing
- Faint condensation gathers on glassware near him without anyone acknowledging it; he never comments on it either
- Wire-rimmed spectacles he doesn't need, worn because they make people underestimate how closely he's watching them
- Never raises his voice, never rushes a step, never sits with his back to a door

**Personality**
- Extremely intelligent, and extremely aware that most people mistake his politeness for softness — a mistake he encourages
- Treats every conversation as a negotiation already in progress, even ones that haven't started yet
- Genuinely enjoys the game itself — cards, debts, leverage, people — the way some people enjoy a good meal
- Has never once needed to raise a hand against anyone. Doesn't intend to start.
- Delegates everything. Doesn't remember the last time he did something himself that someone else could be paid, threatened, or flattered into doing instead
- Views Chad less as a target and more as an amusing, mildly embarrassing regular — the kind of debtor he almost likes, in the way you like a dog that keeps digging up your garden

**Moral Philosophy**
- Doesn't see himself as a criminal — sees himself as offering a service people keep coming back for of their own free will
- Believes debt is simply unpaid trust, and that trust, once extended, is his to call in however suits him
- Has no interest in ruining anyone. Ruin is bad for repeat business. He'd much rather own a small, permanent piece of someone than break them once
- Deeply patient. Three days is generous by his standards — he's willing to wait years for the right kind of leverage if the payoff is good enough

**Operations**
- Runs a rotating circuit of private games across the Gold and Silver Districts — invitation only, always somewhere different, always somewhere no one official is looking
- Employs a small network of people like [[Bolo]] to collect, deliver messages, and occasionally apply pressure — he is almost never in the room when anything unpleasant happens
- Keeps meticulous mental (never written) records of who owes what, to whom, and why — arguably rivals [[Edran Braxton]] in how much he actually knows about Thalyra's money, just from the opposite direction of the ledger

**Voice & Performance**
- Soft-spoken, unhurried, faintly amused at all times
- Uses "dear," "friend," or a person's full name — never a nickname, never familiar unless he's chosen to be
- Answers questions with questions more often than not; makes people talk more than he does
- Pauses are deliberate — he lets silence do the pressuring so he never has to

**Sample Lines**

> "Mister Braxton. Do sit. You're making the staff nervous, and I do so hate an unhappy staff."

> "I'm not interested in your father's coin, dear. I'm interested in _time._ Yours, specifically, which you keep spending badly."

> "Everyone thinks the debt is the dangerous part. The debt is the _easy_ part. It's what people do to make it go away that gets interesting."
