**Basic Information**
- Name: Kai Yang
	- Kai (Chinese) means triumph, victory, or music
- Race: Wood Elf
- Occupation: Farmer / resident of [[Helmut]], a remote village near the [[Gray Mountains]]
- Family:
	- Wife: [[Shun Yang]] (left when Chein was 3)
	- Daughter: Chein Yang (later renamed [[Chein Po]])
- Status: Deceased — killed during the mother drake’s attack on their home

**Personality and Character**
- Loving and patient father, deeply devoted to his daughter after his wife’s departure.
- Tried to shield Chein from bitterness, always speaking kindly of her absent mother.
- Honest, gentle, and principled — instilled moral strength and compassion in Chein.
- Though strong in character, he was emotionally scarred by his wife’s abandonment.
- Believed in hard work, personal integrity, and forgiveness.
- His quiet grief and avoidance of the past left Chein to fill in emotional gaps with guilt and self-blame.

**Relationship with Chein**
- Raised Chein alone from age 3 onward.
- Tried to nurture her curiosity and independence but also to protect her from danger.
- His refusal to discuss her mother’s reasons for leaving led to Chein’s internalized resentment and feelings of inadequacy.
- When Chein wanted to explore the wilderness at 13, he forbade it out of fear for her safety.
- Despite their close bond, this conflict indirectly set the tragedy of Helmut in motion.

**The Tragedy at [[Helmut]]**
- When the mother drake attacked searching for her stolen egg, she set their home ablaze.
- Kai became trapped under fallen debris as he and Chein tried to escape.
- In his final moments, he ordered her to “GO!”, sacrificing himself to save her.
- His death profoundly shaped Chein, instilling both guilt and determination to survive.
- The townspeople later found only his remains and assumed Chein had been eaten by the drake.

**Legacy and Influence**
- Kai Yang’s values continue to define Chein’s moral compass:
- Her desire to live honestly despite her criminal years stems from his teachings.
- Her renaming of her drake Peng → [[Kai]] was a gesture of remembrance and love.
- His last words — “Go” — became both her burden and her drive to survive and change.
- His memory acts as a moral anchor for Chein, particularly when she feels lost or self-destructive.
- She keeps his lessons buried beneath layers of guilt and secrecy, but they guide her every attempt at redemption.

**Symbolic Role**
- Represents honor, self-sacrifice, and unconditional love in Chein’s story.
- His death marks the end of her innocence and the beginning of her exile.
- By naming her drake “[[Kai,]]” Chein symbolically merges her two protectors — her father and her drake — into one living embodiment of her past and her promise to do better.

**How He Lives On**
- Spiritually present through Chein’s actions and moral struggles.
- Mentioned by Chein only rarely — she avoids speaking of her family to others.
- The renaming of her drake and her pursuit of honest adventuring are acts of penance and attempts to honor him.
