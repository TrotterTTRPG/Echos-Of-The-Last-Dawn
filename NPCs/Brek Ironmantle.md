**Title:** Desk Officer at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Dwarf  
**Gender**: Male
**Age:** 89  

**Appearance:**
- Stout, grey-bearded, and radiates the specific immovability of someone who has sat in the same chair for decades
- Deep-set brown eyes under heavy brows that do most of his communicating for him
- Braided beard with small brass rings — the only personal ornamentation he permits at work
- Sleeves always rolled to the elbow, which is technically against dress code and which no one has ever successfully enforced

**Personality:**
- Blunt without being rude, though the line is occasionally close
- Has a complete and detailed internal ranking of every type of client, which he shares with no one
- Deeply fair in a way that surprises people who mistake his manner for hostility
- Finds the younger officers endearing in the way one finds very determined children endearing

Brek Ironmantle has worked the desk for fifty-one years. He was there before [[Seravael Dawnmere]]. He was there before [[Edran Braxton]]. He has outlasted four directors, two financial crises, and one attempted robbery that he does not discuss in any detail. He will retire when he decides to and not a moment before. Nobody has asked when that will be, because the answer is obvious.
