## QUICK REFERENCE

**The Vibe:** Paranoid, manipulative, jealous girlfriend constantly accusing [[Chad Braxton]] of cheating while flirting with other men to make him jealous.

**Key Traits:**
- ✓ Constantly suspicious [[Chad Braxton]] is cheating
- ✓ Brings up infidelity unprompted in every conversation
- ✓ Flirts openly with other men (no actual interest) to make [[Chad Braxton]] jealous
- ✓ Watches [[Chad Braxton]] obsessively; present at all his shifts
- ✓ Makes accusations about every woman he talks to
- ✓ Toxic, exhausting, creates workplace chaos

**How to Play Her:**
1. Start bubbly/friendly, pivot to accusing [[Chad Braxton]] of cheating
2. If [[Chad Braxton]] ignores her → immediately flirt with nearest man
3. Interrupt conversations to make snippy comments about other women
4. Use exaggerated body language: loud laugh, arm touches, sustained eye contact
5. Oscillate between "everything is fine" and "he's definitely cheating"

**Voice:** Sharp, gossipy, paranoid undertone. Valley-girl-adjacent. Quick talker.

**The Irony:** [[Chad Braxton]] doesn't care about her suspicions OR her jealousy tactics. She's exhausting him, not manipulating him.

## TRACY'S WEEKLY ROUTINE

**Monday:** [[Tracy]] gets her nails done at a Silver District salon in the morning, then spends the afternoon preparing outfits for the week. She knows [[Chad Braxton]] works the evening shift, so she shows up around sunset to "casually" chat with him and observe who else is at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]].

**Tuesday:** She takes a leisurely morning at home, spends an hour on her hair and makeup, then arrives at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] mid-afternoon. If [[Chad Braxton]] is friendly, she clings to him; if he seems distant, she positions herself near male customers and turns up her flirtatiousness volume.

**Wednesday:** Typically [[Chad Braxton]]'s busiest day, so [[Tracy]] comes by to "keep him company" (surveil him). She brings him food or coffee as an excuse to stay longer. If she sees [[Chad Braxton]] being helpful or charming to female customers, she'll immediately start flirting with a male patron to "remind him what he has."

**Thursday:** [[Tracy]] meets girlfriends for brunch in the Silver District, gossiping about [[Chad Braxton]] and his coworkers, then visits the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] later in the day. She's usually in a better mood after friend time, but it fades quickly if [[Chad Braxton]] isn't excited to see her.

**Friday:** She spends the day getting ready for the weekend, planning outfit changes and potential locations where she might "coincidentally" run into [[Chad Braxton]] outside work. She shows up at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] with the expectation that [[Chad Braxton]] will have evening plans with her — if he doesn't, she becomes noticeably upset and dramatic.

**Saturday:** Depends entirely on [[Chad Braxton]]'s availability. If they have plans, she's anxious and controlling throughout, watching him for signs of infidelity. If [[Chad Braxton]] is working or unavailable, she sulks at home or aggressively shows up at the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]] to remind him she exists (often flirting with other men to make him jealous).

**Sunday:** [[Tracy]] uses this day to reset emotionally and strategically. She plans her weekly "[[Chad Braxton]] monitoring" schedule, updates her appearance routine, and mentally prepares for the week ahead. By evening, her paranoia has built back up and she's anxious about Monday, worried about what [[Chad Braxton]] did/will do while she wasn't watching.
