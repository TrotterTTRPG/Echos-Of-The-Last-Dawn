Female tabaxi druid from [[The Cradle of Thorns]]
Healer and custodian within [[The Stonebloom Enclave]]
Marked by silvery ritual scars along her forearms; her eyes reflect starlight even in dim chambers

---

## Stat Block

***Lilac of the Valley***
*Medium humanoid (tabaxi), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (leather armor)
**Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +6, Con +5
**Skills** Insight +6, Medicine +6, Nature +4, Perception +6
**Condition Immunities** charmed
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
**Languages** Common, Sylvan
**Challenge** 15 (13,000 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +4

***Druidic.*** Lilac knows Druidic and uses it to leave hidden messages among plants and natural markers.

***Lunar Affinity.*** Lilac has advantage on Wisdom checks related to celestial navigation, moon phases, and natural divination. Once per long rest, she can cast *moonbeam* without using a spell slot.

***Spellcasting.*** Lilac is a 15th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +6 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following druid spells prepared:

- Cantrips (at will): druidcraft, guidance, shillelagh
- 1st level (5 slots): cure wounds, goodberry, healing word
- 2nd level (4 slots): moonbeam, pass without trace, spike growth
- 3rd level (3 slots): call lightning, create food and water, plant growth
- 4th level (3 slots): conjure woodland beings, polymorph
- 5th level (2 slots): commune with nature, tree stride
- 6th level (1 slot): sunbeam

### Actions

***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage, or 5 (1d10) bludgeoning damage if used with two hands.

***Healing Word (Bonus Action).*** Lilac casts *healing word* as a bonus action, targeting a creature within 60 ft.

### Reactions

***Moonlit Ward.*** When a creature within 30 ft. that she can see takes damage, Lilac can use her reaction to grant that creature resistance to the damage. She can do this once per short rest.
