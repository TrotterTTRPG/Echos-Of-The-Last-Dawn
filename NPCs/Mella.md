*Mella*
The "devil" of [[Corwin Sallow]]'s tale — the woman he invited in, who fed on him for months, whom he killed twice and shipped to [[Velrosk]], and who has never once failed to come back.

She is **not a standard vampire** — most of the folklore checklist (running water, a wooden stake, an open invitation) doesn't reliably stop her. She drinks through a long, leech-like tongue that fastens onto exposed skin, leaving a ring-shaped welt that heals to a faint circular scar — never a fang-mark. This is why no examiner ever flagged Corry as a vampire's victim, and why the things that are "supposed" to kill her monster might not.

She has not fed since the last time Corry put a crossbow bolt through her heart — days ago now. She is starving, and it shows: sunken eyes, skin sloughing at the joints, lips cracked back from her teeth. She is not the seductress of the old stories tonight. She is desperate, and the only thing in the world she wants is Corwin Sallow.

For the encounter she anchors, see [[Conditions Of His Parole]].

> Custom undead, not a reskinned Vampire Spawn. CR 6 (estimate) for 5 level-4 PCs — already a heavy fight before the party brings the climax-tilting tricks (Corry himself, sunlight, etc.). Tune live as needed.

## DM Eyes Only — What She Actually Is (And What Even You Don't Get to Know)

**The party should never learn this. Corry doesn't know it either. Neither, fully, do you.**

Mella is [[The Loveslain]] — what the world's Umbral remnants make of a soul killed by a hand that loved it exactly as much as it loved that hand back. That much is true regardless of how she got here. Her hunger for Corry specifically (**Siphon**, **Her Devotion**) isn't a personality quirk bolted onto a generic monster — it's mechanically and literally the entire reason she exists.

What is *not* settled — and should stay that way, even in your own head — is whether Corry's hand is the one that made her. Hold all three of these loosely; the encounter works no matter which is true:
- He killed her exactly as he tells it — bolt through the heart, in a moment of real, mutual love — and his hand is what tore the wound a remnant could pour itself into.
- He killed *something*, but not that. A woman named Mella existed, and he ended her life in some far more mundane, far less monstrous way than the story he tells — a fight, an accident, a moment he's buried under a kinder version of events. What answers to her name now grew from that wound, and Corry's mind rebuilt the only version of the story he could survive telling.
- He never killed her at all. She was already [[The Loveslain]] before she ever found him — drawn to a man capable of loving that hard, hunting for exactly the kind of devotion that could feed her — and everything he remembers "doing" to her is a story she let him believe, because a guilty man convinced he's responsible is so much easier to keep close.

Don't resolve this — not at the table, not in private notes. The horror holds best for as long as even you don't know whose hand actually did it.

If the campaign's wider Cycle plot ever surfaces, any of the three still works as quiet, retroactive proof that the Umbral Soul is already bleeding through more than anyone in the Gilded city wants to admit.

## Stat Block

***Mella***
*Medium undead, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 97 (13d8 + 39)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 17 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +4, Cha +5
**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +6
**Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP)  **Proficiency Bonus** +3

***Regeneration.*** Mella regains 15 hit points at the start of her turn if she has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight. Only regenerates after using syphon on [[Corwin Sallow]]

***Spider Climb.*** Mella can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** Mella takes 20 radiant damage when she starts her turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, she has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. *(This is her one true weakness — the old hunter's tricks of running water and a stake don't reliably work on her, and she answers to no rule about invitations.)*

***Starving.*** Mella hasn't fed in days and it shows — she is gaunt, frantic, and far from the composed seductress of legend. She has disadvantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks that rely on charm or beauty, but her hunger has sharpened her violence: when her Siphon hits, she can make one additional Siphon attack as a bonus action. This disadvantage vanishes for as long as she holds temporary hit points from feeding (see Siphon) — fed and sated, even for a moment, she looks fully human again.

***Her Devotion.*** Mella will not willingly reduce **Corwin Sallow** to 0 hit points, and would sooner be destroyed than truly lose him. She always moves toward him and prioritizes reaching him over fighting others. If Corry speaks to her, pleads, or steps into her path, she hesitates: the next attack roll against her before her next turn has advantage. If he is threatened or used as a shield, she falters and may break off to parley — she wants him *fed upon and kept*, not killed.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Mella makes two attacks, only one of which can be a Siphon.

***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage. Instead of dealing damage, she can grapple the target (escape DC 14).

***Siphon.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., **Corwin Sallow only** — no one else will do. *Hit:* 1 (1d4) piercing damage, and Mella regains hit points equal to ten times the damage dealt, even if this raises her above her hit point maximum (the excess becomes temporary hit points). Her Devotion still prevents this from dropping Corry to 0 hit points — he is left at 1 instead, gasping and gutted, no matter how much she drains. The moment her tongue draws blood from him, the gauntness and decay sloughs away: for as long as she holds temporary hit points from this feeding, she looks fully, terribly human again — the woman in his stories, not the thing clawing at the door.

***Hungering Gaze (Recharge 5–6).*** Mella locks eyes with one creature she can see within 30 feet (never Corwin Sallow — her hunger for him answers only to Siphon). The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become **enthralled** for 1 minute. An enthralled creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success; taking damage doesn't grant an early save. At the start of each of its turns while enthralled, roll a d6 to determine its behavior for that turn:

| d6  | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :-: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2 | **Pulled Under.** Overcome with longing, the creature uses its full movement to walk toward Mella by the safest available path and can't take an action or reaction this turn, other than using their reaction to use their body to block an attack on Mella - as long as they are within 5ft of the attacker, or 5 ft of Mella for ranged attacks |
|  3  | **Lost in Her.** The creature stands transfixed and can't move, take an action, or take a reaction this turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 4–5 | **Lashing Out.** Something in the creature snaps. It moves toward the nearest creature other than Mella and makes one melee attack against it if able.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  6  | **Shaken Free.** The creature resists and acts normally this turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

The effect also ends early if Mella dies or is incapacitated.
