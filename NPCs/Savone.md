Savone is an old friend of [[Faris Harith]] and will be willing to speak with [[Chein Po]] openly once he knows who she is.

He will tell her that [[Faris Harith]] loved her greatly, and wanted to protect her from the thieves life.

## Established relationships and discretion

Po asked Savone for information about the Gentleman; he told her to stay away. He genuinely likes Po and remains loyal to Faris. He is a powerful man, cautious about giving an untested party information or access that could lead back to him. His exact rank and whether his influence rivals the Gentleman’s remain open.

His carpet stall is the contact point described in [[Sessions/Completed Events/Chein Po goes to the black market|Po’s introduction]]: the sign advertises “Vibrant Velrosk floor weavings,” and the nearby tankard bears the crest Po recognizes from Faris. [[The Moonhelm Society]] provides the existing context of criminal connections and mutual aid.

## Planned vetting mission

Savone will present a completely staged job as ordinary work that came across his desk. Every arranged participant belongs to his organization and assesses the party’s conduct. He wants to know whether Po’s companions are safe to trust, not merely whether they can win a fight.

[[Sessions/Potential Events/Savones Test - The Couriers Account|The Courier’s Account]] is the proposed playable implementation: recover an illicit ledger taken by a former bookkeeper claiming unpaid wages. His attempt to sell it has apparently gone wrong, leaving him barricaded inside a parcel depot against a rival crew. The party must investigate, infiltrate or confront the opposition, and extract the records; combat, stealth, negotiation, and alternate escape routes are all viable. The operation takes place away from Savone’s carpet business. Neither apparent disputant can safely appeal to the Watch. The depot, rival crew, reward, and extraction complications remain preparation, not recorded play. Savone’s people can surrender and abort the test, but real violence remains a risk he is responsible for creating. This does not reveal his whole organization or establish him as its formal leader.
