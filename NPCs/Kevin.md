Human man
Age: 34
Independent artificer and "spell re-tuner" in the Bronze District of [[Thalyra]]
Works out of *The Barrel*, a converted warehouse wedged beside [[The Bronzewalk Bazaar]]
Answers to "Kevin." Just Kevin. He had a last name once. It was, like, a long one.

Built as a PC-style **Level 10 Artificer (Artillerist)**. See Stat Block below.

**Appearance:**
- Tall and lanky, with a permanent sun-brown that Thalyra's weather cannot explain
- Shoulder-length blond hair, stiff with salt, tied back with a twist of copper wire and topped with brass goggles he never pulls down
- A fresh stripe of white zinc paste down the nose, always. It is a failed prototype that he decided to keep.
- Open-collared shirt under a patched, fin-shaped hooded cloak that flaps even indoors (a Cloak of the Manta Ray)
- Board shorts with "like, forty-one pockets, dude, I counted once." Clockwork-sprung sandals that click.
- A shell-shaped collar at his throat hums faintly. It's the Micro Hut.
- Smells of salt, ozone, and sunscreen

**Demeanor:**
- Unhurried. Slow, easy smile. Has never been seen to raise his voice, including mid-explosion.
- Speaks plainly in surfer slang, even when explaining high arcana. His metaphors are accidentally precise: "A spell's a wave, right? I just moved where it breaks."
- Calls everyone "dude," "bro," or "brah." Has done so to a Warden captain, at least once.
- Deeply, sincerely kind. Wants everyone to be "chill." Genuinely believes he is "just kinda good at fixing stuff."
- **His tic:** when something lands (an insult, a plan, an accusation, a truly terrible piece of news) Kevin says "Hold on..." and goes quiet for a beat or two, eyes unfocused, while the words filter in. Then he says "...keep going," with total serenity, no matter what he was just told.

Kevin doesn't invent magic so much as *re-tune* it. He takes a spell that already exists, flattens it, shrinks it, re-aims it, or makes it more polite, then traps the result in a trinket that anyone can use. He has no formal training that he can name. When asked how he does it, he says "vibes." He is not lying. Somewhere in that sun-bleached head is a mind that can see the structure of a spell the way most people see the shape of a wave, and he simply doesn't have the vocabulary for what he sees.

Unlike [[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]] and the [[Department of Arcane Mechanisms (DAM)]], Kevin uses no beast essences and no living subjects. His raw material is spells and nothing else. He considers Brikk "the smartest dude in the city" and Brikk's ankheg-heart approach "a total bummer for the ankheg." Brikk, for his part, regards Kevin as "a hazard with a tan." Brikk has tried to hire him twice. Both times Kevin said "Hold on... keep going," and then forgot the offer within the hour.

In [[The Gilded Age]], nobody polices unlicensed enchanting. Nothing has gone wrong in years, and the city has forgotten how to worry about it. Kevin sells from a stall at [[The Bronzewalk Bazaar]] and from the front of *The Barrel*. His prices are set by vibes: sometimes free, sometimes triple what a thing is worth, and often paid in favors, stories, rare shells, or a really good sandwich. [[Ilyana Corvess]], Head Magister of the [[Hall of Metamagic and Theory]], has spent two years trying to extract a "proper methodology" from him. Kevin's answer is "it's just, like, vibes, dude," which she finds professionally offensive. [[Chad Braxton]] is a regular customer and believes Kevin is his best friend. Kevin says "Hold on... keep going" to Chad so often that Chad has decided it's a compliment.

**The forgotten surname:**
Kevin had a last name. He is sure of this. During a memory-related experiment (a bottled *Modify Memory* he calls "the Rinse Cycle") he "put it somewhere safe," and now cannot recall where, or what it was. He is not bothered. He is the only one who isn't. DM's choice of truth:
- It is in a jar somewhere in *The Barrel*, labeled in his own handwriting: "KEVIN'S LAST NAME (PROBABLY COOL)."
- A noble family, guild, or debt-holder is looking for a missing heir, apprentice, or contract-signer, and Kevin's name is the one thing they can't find.
- It is a very old, very important name, and whoever holds it holds a claim over him.

**Adventure Hooks:**
- **The Big One.** Kevin is building a tsunami in a bottle so he can finally "ride a proper barrel." He needs a large body of water and a lot of ambient magic to fill it. If the party is anywhere near [[Emerald Quay - Eastern Docks]] when he tests it, they are in the splash zone.
- **The Storm Conch.** Kevin has accidentally built a shell that stores ambient magic without any beast essence. [[Dr. Brikk Thricewright]]'s secret prototype needs exactly that kind of power. Kevin would give it away for free if someone asked nicely, and he'd never realize what he'd handed over.
- **Nose Thief.** A burglar keeps robbing Silver District homes, and the only witnesses report a floating white stripe about the size of a thumb. Somebody is using Kevin's Zinc Sticks, and Kevin has no idea who bought them.
- **Wardens' Little Secret.** Off-duty Wardens from [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]] quietly buy Chill Pills by the dozen. [[Captain Seressa Varn]] does not know. Kevin does not understand why this is a secret.
- **The Rinse Cycle.** One of Kevin's memory bottles has gone missing from *The Barrel*. Someone, somewhere, has forgotten something they shouldn't have.

**Voice & Roleplay:**
- Slow, warm, and relaxed. Trails off. Never hurries. Vocabulary: *dude, bro, brah, gnarly, stoked, rad, sick, epic, bummer, wipeout, bail, shred, barreled, mellow, chill, no worries, for sure, totally, cowabunga.*
- Explains complex magic in the simplest, most literal way. Never uses a word longer than three syllables if he can help it, and never says "abjuration."
- When the pause comes, actually pause. The whole table should feel it. Kevin's "...keep going" is never sarcastic and never worried. Often, right after the pause, he has just realized the answer to a problem and will say it as though it were obvious.
- Sample lines:
  - "Whoa, hey! Welcome to The Barrel. Careful, that's a... yeah, don't touch that. It's cool. It's probably cool."
  - "So a hut is, like, a bubble, right? And I was like, why's the bubble gotta be so big? Bubbles should fit whatever needs bubbling. So, head bubble."
  - Player: "The Wardens think you're a fugitive." Kevin: "Hold on..." *(two-beat pause)* "...keep going."
  - "Yeah, no, that's a bummer. Like, a *big* bummer. Gary, get the door."

**In a Fight:**
- Kevin doesn't want to hurt anyone. He leads with Web, Grease, Wind Wall, and Gary in Protector mode.
- If someone is going to die, he'll get loud for the first time in his life. Once, and briefly.
- Gary, his Eldritch Cannon, is a small brass hermit crab wearing a conch shell that swivels and fires. Kevin talks to it. It has never once been wrong.

## Kevin's Creations

Kevin's specialty is taking a spell that was made for wizards and making it work for anyone. Each item below lists the original spell and what Kevin changed.

**Pricing:** Kevin sets prices on vibes. The gp value listed is what a fair shop would charge. Kevin will trade for: a good story, a rare shell, a really good sandwich, help "testing" a prototype (dangerous), or fixing something he's broken (also dangerous).

### Signature Item

#### Leomund's Micro Hut
*Wondrous item (collar or amulet), uncommon, requires attunement. ~400 gp*
**Source:** *Leomund's Tiny Hut*
**Kevin's alteration:** Shrunk the 10-ft-radius dome to a 1-ft radius and centered it on the wearer's head. "It's the same hut, but only for your dome."
- **Activation.** Once per day, as an action, speak the command word (*"Cowabunga"*). A transparent dome of force appears, centered on your head, radius 1 foot. It lasts 8 hours or until you dismiss it as a bonus action. You can make it opaque or clear at will.
- **Head-only shelter.** Any object or creature already inside the dome when it forms (hat, helmet, earrings, a stubborn moth) stays inside and can move freely. Nothing else can pass through the dome, including gags, hoods, blindfolds, water, smoke, and anything trying to strangle you.
- **Air pocket.** You can breathe normally inside the dome in any environment (underwater, in smoke, in poison gas) for up to 10 minutes per activation. After that the air inside is used up.
- **Gas guard.** You have advantage on saving throws against gas, vapor, and cloud effects (*stinking cloud*, *cloudkill*, spore clouds, etc.) while the dome is active.
- **Comfort.** The air inside is always dry and comfortable, whatever the weather outside.
- **Catch.** It only protects your head. Kevin insists this is a feature. If you activate it after putting on a hat, the hat is inside; if you put on a hat after activating it, the hat cannot pass through. ("Pro tip: hat first, dude.")

### Everyday Gear

#### Boogie-Board Buckler
*Shield, uncommon. ~300 gp*
**Source:** *Shield*
**Kevin's alteration:** Shield spell packed into a foam-lined buckler. "You gotta ride it, bro."
- **3 charges**, regained at dawn (1d3).
- **Reaction:** When you are hit by an attack, expend 1 charge. You gain +5 AC against that attack and until the start of your next turn. After the attack resolves, you can move up to 10 feet in a direction of your choice without provoking opportunity attacks.

#### Bail Sandals
*Wondrous item, uncommon, requires attunement. ~350 gp*
**Source:** *Feather Fall*, *Jump*
**Kevin's alteration:** Clockwork spring soles that "stick the landing."
- **3 charges**, regained at dawn (1d3).
- **Reaction, 1 charge:** When you fall, as *feather fall* on yourself. You may also slide up to 15 feet in any direction on landing.
- **Bonus action, 1 charge:** Triple your jump distance until the end of your turn (as *jump*).
- **Catch.** If you roll a natural 1 on an attack roll or Dexterity saving throw while the sandals have charges, they spend one and jump you 10 feet in a random direction. ("Involuntary bail-out. It's a feature.")

#### Chill Pill
*Potion (consumable), uncommon. ~50 gp*
**Source:** *Calm Emotions*
**Kevin's alteration:** No concentration, no area, just one very relaxed person. "Off-duty Wardens are my best customers, and I don't even know why."
- A creature that swallows a Chill Pill must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw (a willing creature may choose to fail) or be **calmed** for 10 minutes: any effect making it charmed or frightened is suppressed, and it becomes indifferent to creatures it was hostile toward. It will not willingly take hostile actions unless attacked.
- A creature that fails the save is not aware it's been affected until the pill wears off.
- Can be slipped into a drink. Kevin does not encourage this.

#### Zinc Stick, SPF Invisible
*Wondrous item (consumable), uncommon. ~150 gp*
**Source:** *Invisibility*
**Kevin's alteration:** Invisibility as sunscreen. "Slap it on and you're, like, gone. Mostly."
- Apply to yourself or a willing creature (action). The creature turns invisible as the *invisibility* spell for 10 minutes or until it attacks, deals damage, or casts a spell.
- **The stripe.** A 2-inch stripe of white zinc paste on the nose remains visible and floating. A creature within 30 feet that succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the stripe and learns the invisible creature's location (though attacks against it are still made at disadvantage as if against an unseen creature).
- **Clear Coat (+100 gp, rare).** Kevin can mix a stripe-free version. He refuses to. "The zinc is the whole point, dude."

#### Polarized Truth Shades
*Wondrous item (glasses), uncommon, requires attunement. ~400 gp*
**Source:** *See Invisibility*
**Kevin's alteration:** Sunglasses that polarize away the lie of the world.
- You cannot be blinded by bright light, sun glare, or radiant flashes.
- Once per dawn, as an action, you can see invisible creatures and objects for 1 hour (as *see invisibility*). They appear as faint glare-outlines.
- **Catch.** They're sunglasses. You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in dim light or darkness.

#### Vibe Check Bracelet
*Wondrous item (bracelet), uncommon, requires attunement. ~350 gp*
**Source:** *Detect Thoughts* (heavily stripped)
**Kevin's alteration:** Kevin removed the thoughts and kept the feelings. "I don't need to know what they're thinking. I just need to know if they're *cool*."
- **3 charges**, regained at dawn.
- **Action, 1 charge:** Read the room. The bracelet's shell stones glow **green** (nobody within 30 feet intends harm to you or your allies in the next 10 minutes), **yellow** (someone is wary, hiding something, or considering it), or **red** (someone is actively hostile). It doesn't say who or why.
- It doesn't work on creatures with Intelligence 3 or lower or on undead and constructs.

#### Message-in-a-Bottle (Pair)
*Wondrous item (two corked bottles), uncommon. ~400 gp per pair*
**Source:** *Sending*
**Kevin's alteration:** Sending stones that only work through liquid. "It's, like, a *bottle*. In the *water*. It's a whole thing."
- Write a message of up to 25 words on any scrap and drop it into the bottle. Then submerge the bottle in any liquid (a drink, a rain barrel, the sea).
- The message instantly appears in the paired bottle, anywhere on the same plane, **if** the paired bottle is also immersed in liquid. If it isn't, the message waits inside until it is.
- Each bottle can send one message per day.

#### Instant Hammock Coil
*Wondrous item, uncommon. ~200 gp*
**Source:** *Web*
**Kevin's alteration:** Web, but not sticky. Unless you flip the switch.
- **3 charges**, regained at dawn (1d3).
- **Action, 1 charge:** Tie the free ends to two solid anchors 5 to 30 feet apart. The coil becomes a hammock that holds up to 1,000 pounds and lasts 8 hours. You can take a long rest on it, even over a pit, a cliff face, or a room full of angry rats.
- **Web mode (1 charge, action):** Flip the switch and the hammock becomes a 20-foot cube of *web* (DC 15) centered on the coil. It lasts 1 hour.

#### Conch of Tunable Thunder
*Wondrous item (conch shell), uncommon. ~300 gp*
**Source:** *Thunderwave*
**Kevin's alteration:** A volume knob. "Volume one is a *very* polite hello."
- **4 charges**, regained at dawn (1d4).
- **Action:** Set the dial (1 to 5) and blow.
  - **Dial 1:** No damage. A gentle 5-foot shove on creatures in a 15-foot cube. Sound carries 100 yards ("Marco... Polo").
  - **Dial 3 (1 charge):** As *thunderwave* (2d8 thunder, DC 13 Constitution saving throw, pushed 10 feet on a failure). Audible for 300 feet.
  - **Dial 5 (2 charges):** As *thunderwave* cast at 2nd level (3d8, DC 15). Audible for 1 mile.
- **Catch.** Everyone hears it. Everyone.

#### Tide-Pool Tonic
*Potion, uncommon. ~75 gp*
**Source:** *Cure Wounds*
**Kevin's alteration:** Slow release. "Like a wave rolling in."
- On drinking, you regain **1d4 hit points at the start of each of your turns for 5 rounds** (average 12 total).
- Also cures sunburn. "It's really more of a sunburn cure, if I'm honest."

### Adventuring Kit

#### Chill Bonfire Bead
*Wondrous item (consumable bead), rare. ~800 gp*
**Source:** *Fireball*
**Kevin's alteration:** Fire that has learned to be a friend.
- **Bonfire mode (default).** Throw the bead at a point within 60 feet. A gentle 10-foot-diameter bonfire appears and burns for 8 hours without consuming fuel. It gives bright light 30 feet, dim light 30 feet beyond, and burns only what you want it to. Creatures within 20 feet have advantage on saving throws against cold and against exhaustion from cold. Creatures who take a short rest within 20 feet regain an extra 1d6 hit points.
- **Boom mode.** If crushed in hand as an action instead of thrown, it bursts as *fireball* (DC 15, 8d6 fire, 20-foot radius, centered on the bead). Kevin will not admit this mode exists.

#### Ground-Swell Board
*Wondrous item (surfboard), rare, requires attunement. ~3,000 gp*
**Source:** *Water Walk*, *Expeditious Retreat*, *Fly*
**Kevin's alteration:** The board rides anything wave-like. This is his masterpiece and the one item he won't sell.
- While standing on the board, you can travel across water, mud, sand, snow, ice, and rubble as if it were solid ground, ignoring difficult terrain from those surfaces. Your speed is 40 feet if it was lower.
- **Wave Dash.** Three times per dawn, as a bonus action, take the Dash action.
- **Big Air.** Once per dawn, as an action, launch skyward and fly up to 60 feet in an arc. You must land by the end of your turn or fall (there's a nice fall-slowing effect on the board itself, as *feather fall*).
- The board carries only you. Kevin has tried to fix that. "It's a solo ride, bro."

### The Big One

#### The Big One (Tsunami in a Bottle)
*Wondrous item (consumable), very rare. Not for sale.*
**Source:** *Control Water*
**Kevin's alteration:** He wanted to ride a real barrel.
- **Action:** Uncork and throw the bottle up to 60 feet. A wave 60 feet wide, 20 feet tall, and 120 feet long rolls forward in a straight line from the impact point. Each creature in its path must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw. On a failure, it takes 6d10 bludgeoning damage and is carried 60 feet along the wave's path; on a success, it takes half damage and is not moved.
- **The Barrel.** The wave has a hollow tube down its center, 10 feet wide, sheltered from all damage and forced movement. Kevin can designate up to 8 creatures at the time of casting; they can ride the barrel unharmed, and on the last step of the wave they are gently deposited.
- The water evaporates over the next minute. Anything inside it (fish, debris, small boats) does not.
- Kevin has built exactly one. He would like to test it.

## Stat Block

***Kevin (Level 10 Artificer, Artillerist)***
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic good*

**Armor Class** 19 (studded leather with Enhanced Defense +2, Boogie-Board Shield with Repulsion Shield +1)
**Hit Points** 73 (10d8 + 20; maximum at 1st level)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 60 ft. (Cloak of the Manta Ray)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 9 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +9
**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Arcana +9, Investigation +9, Perception +4, Sleight of Hand +6
**Tools** Tinker's tools +13 (expertise), thieves' tools +9, woodcarver's tools +9, smith's tools +9
**Senses** passive Perception 14 (9 in dim light or darkness; see Polarized Truth Shades)
**Languages** Common, Gnomish, Primordial
**Level** 10 (Artificer)  **Proficiency Bonus** +4

***Spellcasting.*** Kevin is a 10th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks; +11 when casting through his Enhanced Arcane Focus). He has the following artificer spells prepared:

- Cantrips (at will): *fire bolt*, *mending*, *prestidigitation*
- 1st level (4 slots): *absorb elements*, *cure wounds*, *grease*, *identify* (ritual), *shield**, *thunderwave**
- 2nd level (3 slots): *invisibility*, *levitate*, *rope trick*, *web*, *scorching ray**, *shatter**
- 3rd level (2 slots): *dispel magic*, *water walk*, *fireball**, *wind wall**

\* Always prepared (Artillerist).

***Magical Tinkering.*** Kevin can touch a Tiny nonmagical object and give it a minor effect (light, recorded message, odor or sound, or a visual effect). The effect lasts indefinitely.

***Infuse Item.*** Kevin has four infused items: his Studded Leather (*Enhanced Defense*, +2 AC), his Driftwood Wand (*Enhanced Arcane Focus*, +2 to spell attacks and ignores half cover), his Boogie-Board Shield (a *Repulsion Shield*, +1 AC; a bigger cousin of the Buckler above), and his Cloak of the Manta Ray (*Replicate Magic Item*).

***Magic Item Adept.*** Kevin can attune to up to four magic items. He is currently attuned to: the Boogie-Board Shield, the Driftwood Wand, the Micro Hut, and the Polarized Truth Shades.

***Tool Expertise.*** Kevin's proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check that uses his tinker's tools.

***Flash of Genius (5/Long Rest).*** When Kevin or a creature he can see within 30 feet makes an ability check or saving throw, he can use his reaction to add +5 to the roll. He usually says "Hold on..." right before doing this.

***Arcane Firearm.*** When Kevin casts an artificer spell through his Driftwood Wand, he can roll 1d8 and add it to one of the spell's damage rolls.

***Explosive Cannon.*** His Eldritch Cannon's damage increases by 1d8 (already included below). As an action, he can command the cannon to detonate if it is within 60 feet; each creature within 5 feet of it must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) force damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. The cannon is destroyed.

***Polarized Truth Shades.*** Kevin can't be blinded by bright light. Once per dawn, as an action, he can cast *see invisibility* on himself (1 hour, no slot). He has disadvantage on sight-based Wisdom (Perception) checks in dim light or darkness (passive Perception 9 there).

***Micro Hut Collar.*** Once per day, as an action, Kevin wraps his head in a 1-foot-radius dome of force for 8 hours (see Kevin's Creations). It grants a 10-minute air pocket and advantage on saving throws against gas and cloud effects.

### Actions

***Fire Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +11 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 1d8 Arcane Firearm) fire damage. Flammable objects hit ignite.

***Scorching Ray (2nd level).*** Three rays. *Ranged Spell Attack:* +11 to hit each, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) fire damage per ray, +4 (1d8) once from Arcane Firearm.

***Fireball (3rd level).*** 20-ft. radius, range 150 ft. DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. 28 (8d6) fire damage, plus 4 (1d8) from Arcane Firearm; half on a successful save.

***Light Crossbow ("Harpoon Gun").*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

***Eldritch Cannon ("Gary").*** Once per long rest (or by expending a spell slot), Kevin uses an action to create a Small or Tiny cannon in an unoccupied space within 5 feet. Gary has AC 18, 50 hit points, and is immune to poison and psychic damage. He lasts 1 hour, or until destroyed. Kevin can command him as a bonus action (once per turn) to move up to 15 feet and to activate one of the following:
- *Flamethrower.* 15-ft. cone, DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, 13 (3d8) fire damage on a failed save, half on a success. Unattended flammable objects ignite.
- *Force Ballista.* *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (3d8) force damage, and the target is pushed 5 feet away from Gary.
- *Protector.* Emits a burst of temporary hit points: 1d8 + 5 (avg 9) temporary hit points to Gary and each creature of Kevin's choice within 10 feet.

### Reactions

***Flash of Genius.*** See trait above.

***Shield (1st-level slot).*** +5 AC until the start of his next turn, including against the triggering attack.

***Absorb Elements (1st-level slot).*** Resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage from the triggering effect until the start of his next turn; his next melee attack deals an extra 1d6 of that type.

***Repulsion Shield (4 charges/dawn).*** When a creature within 15 feet hits Kevin with a melee attack, he can push it up to 15 feet away in a straight line.

### Equipment
Studded leather (Enhanced Defense +2), Driftwood Wand (Enhanced Arcane Focus +2), Boogie-Board Shield (Repulsion Shield), Cloak of the Manta Ray, Micro Hut collar, Polarized Truth Shades, light crossbow, thieves' tools, tinker's tools, woodcarver's tools, smith's tools, 1d4 Chill Pills, 1 Zinc Stick, 1 Tide-Pool Tonic, a really good sandwich (usually).
