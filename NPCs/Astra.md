Childhood friend of [[Vivatrix]]

Grew up near a quiet riverside forest where she spent her days playing with her best friend [[Vivatrix]]
During one of their adventures, Astra slipped into the river and disappeared beneath the current.
[[Vivatrix]] dove in after her but couldn’t save her - Astra’s body was never recovered.

Since then, [[Vivatrix]] has been having dreams of her whispering "find me" and has become obsessed with the idea that Astra might be alive.
