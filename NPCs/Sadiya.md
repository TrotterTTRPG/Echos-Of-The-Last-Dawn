- 40 year old human trader
- Member of a nomadic tribe that lives in the desert and trades at [[Kareth's Crossing]]

She once found [[Aulberos Lightmind]] almost dead in the desert suffering from a sand fever and nursed him back to health. She is one of the only mortals Aulberos feels comfortable with. Having grown up there, she understands the desert the way he does and is quick to defend him from others who call him a mad hermit.
