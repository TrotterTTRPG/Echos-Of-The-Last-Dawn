A living legend among [[The Watchful Wardens of Thalyra]], Captain Seressa Varn is known throughout the ranks as “The Heart of the Watch.” The title once carried the weight of admiration. Now it carries something closer to defiance.

She once held the rank of High Captain, overseeing operations across  [[Thalyra]] from her post at [[The Golden Hall of Command]]. That ended after a mysterious incident in the lower districts cost five Wardens their lives. Though her official report cleared her of misconduct, the political cost was considerable. Quietly and without spectacle, Seressa was demoted back to Captain.

Officially, she requested to return to field command.
Unofficially, she was too difficult to control.

Now she commands training operations at [[The Bronze Bastion]] and occasionally serves as a combat examiner in [[The Adventurer's Docket]] Trials. Many believe the assignment is meant to keep her visible—but contained.

To the recruits she trains, however, Seressa remains what she has always been: the standard by which Wardens measure themselves.

Several drinks in (see [[Drinks with Seressa Varn]]), the discipline slips. She's loud, she's got something to prove, and she's looking for a fight — no armor, no weapon, just fists, furniture, and whatever's left of her training. ***Brave.*** Seressa has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

***Hardened Brawler.*** Even bare-knuckled, years on the Watch keep her guard up: her Armor Class above already includes her Constitution modifier, as if she were trained to fight unarmored.

***Inebriated.*** Seressa has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on Dexterity saving throws. Her passive Perception above already reflects this.

***Brute.*** A melee attack deals one extra die of its damage when Seressa hits with it (already included below).

***Reckless Brawler.*** At the start of each of her turns, Seressa can decide to fight recklessly. If she does, she has advantage on melee attack rolls until the start of her next turn, but attack rolls against her have advantage during that time as well.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Seressa makes three melee attacks, or two ranged attacks with a thrown bottle.

***Haymaker*** *Melee Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, or 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage if thrown and the bottle shatters.

***Tackle.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) bludgeoning damage. A Medium or smaller target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Reactions

***Drunken Parry.*** Seressa adds 3 to her AC against one melee attack that would otherwise hit her, as long as she can see the attacker. Bare hands or not, the reflex is hard to drink away.
