**Basic Information**
- *Name*: Shun Yang
	- Shun (Chinese) means obey or submit
- *Race:* Wood Elf
- *Origin:* [[Helmut]], a remote mountain village northeast of [[Dornthalm]]
- *Family:*
	- *Husband:* [[Kai Yang]]
	- *Daughter:* Chein Yang (later [[Chein Po]])
- Status: Alive (believed) — rumored sightings near [[Thalyra]]
- Current whereabouts: Unknown — possibly somewhere in or around [[Thalyra]]

**Departure from [[Helmut]]**
- Left suddenly in the middle of the night when Chein was only three years old.
- Offered no explanation or destination; took only what she could carry.
- Her disappearance devastated [[Kai Yang]], who nonetheless refused to speak ill of her.
- Told Chein she “had somewhere more important to be,” leaving the girl to fill in the emotional void herself.
- Her abandonment planted in Chein:
	- A buried resentment toward women and complete families
	- A lifelong feeling of inadequacy and guilt, believing she was the reason Shun left
	- A driving need to understand why

**What was Shun Yang doing?**

Shun was always part of a long, quiet campaign to track the movements and servant of [[The Umbral Dragon]]. The dragon itself has never appeared. Instead, it acts through cults, corrupted beasts, and agents seeking to end the unnaturally long Effulgent age ([[The Effulgent Soul]]) and bring it to its natural conclusion. This would also bring in an Umbral Age ([[The Umbral Soul]]). This is the natural progression of this world, but the ushering in of an umbral age would certainly kill off most if not all of humanity. It's not difficult to see why Shun is actively trying to work against this and why this work must remain quiet to most of the world. An impending apocalypse would only incite panic.

Shun was one of the few field operatives who understood what was really happening. 

**125 - 117 years ago:**
- She was dispatched to [[Helmut]] - a small remote town in the North East of the [[Verda]], because dragon activity had been reported there.
- She was there to find out more about the dragon activity and see if there was any connection with [[The Umbral Dragon]].
- She would use the guise of hunting to explore the surrounding wilderness and look for signs of cult activity, dragon related activity, etc.
- While there, she met and fell in love with [[Kai Yang]]. This was not part of her assignment, but she was told to do whatever necessary to blend in. The heart wants what the heart wants, so she dated, fell in love with, and eventually married Kai.
- They eventually had a child together Chein Yang ([[Chein Po]])
- All 3 lived together peacefully with Shun continuing her exploration and writing letters back to ????? with her findings. Most of her findings indicated that the reported dragon activity in the area was actually likely drake activity and not tied at all to the [[The Umbral Dragon]]
- After 8 years in [[Helmut]], she finally got the letter she was beginning to dread. Since no sign of [[The Umbral Dragon]] has been found, she was being sent somewhere else.
	- Shun had developed a reputation among the secretive faction she was working for: extremely patient, extremely quiet, and willing to disappear into assignment for years at a time. Because of this, they had a new type of assignment for her.
- She loved Kai more than anything, but was going to need to abandon him for a unknown (but likely long) length of time. She explained the entire situation to him, laying all of her cards on the table. She tried her best to explain the situation the world was in and how important to work she was doing was. She was ensuring the world continued one for Kai, for Chein, and for all of humanity.  It was a lot of information to process, so Kai didn't understand at first. He was initially incredibly hurt that she had lied to him for all these years. (This is the arguind that Chein overheard).  Ultimately though, Shun was leaving regardless of whether or not he fully understood the why. 
	- In time, Kai came to understand why it was necessary, but he couldn't reveal too many details about it, especially to a young child who may talk to others.
	- Kai Yang told Chein simply that her mother had somewhere more important that she needed to be, and that her absence was wholly justified.

**117 - 116 years ago:**
- Shun was recalled to [[Thalyra]]
- She was expecting another similar assignment, but learned that there had not been another sighting of [[The Umbral Dragon]]. Instead omens were increasing across the continent. 
- She was assigned to investigate omens, cult movements, and magical disturbances across the continent, but on her own intuition rather than assignment. Her focus was shifting from investigating things tied directly to dragons and rather investigate things tied directly to the Umbral.

**116 - 106 years ago**:
- During her time on the road, Shun investigated a number of incidents across [[Thalyra]].
	- She investigated rumors of shadow-touched beats
	- She tracked down small cults
	- Recovered or destroyed artifacts tied to ancient dragon worship
	- Intercepting scholars or mages who had discovered or began experimenting with umbral energies
- Most incidents were minor.
- For these 10 years, she would occasionally (every few years) write to Kai and receive a reply back not too long after. She cherished these letters and tried to pour as much of what she was feeling into her letters as possible. She never wrote to Chein directly for fear of her reaction. She missed her family terribly, and the thought of Chein lashing out or shutting down completely filled her with a chest-tightening sense of dread.
- The last letter sent never received a reply, so Shun went to investigate herself.

**106 years ago**:
- She learned that the village was attacked only a few months ago by a drake. Bizarrely, only the Yang residence was attacked. Kai's remains were found and Chein's remains were missing. The villagers presumed she was taken or eaten by the drake. 
- Rumors swirled about why only the Yang residence was attacked, nut no one could pinpoint the exact reason.
- Shun spent a year or so investigating the area for any information she might have missed initially. She was hoping against hope that her daughter was alive. If Shun was being honest with herself, she knew that 6 months was too long, and she was actually out for revenge more than anything. Following any trace of the drake only lead her to Drake body buried under a rockslide. If Chein had been eaten or taken by the drake, there was definitely no way she survived that.
	- *Lore Note*: The drake followed Chein and Peng (now Kai) for several months afterwards away from [[Helmut]]. Chein eventually defeated her using an avalanche in a valley some distance from Helmut.

**105 years ago to a few months ago**:
- To escape her guilt, Shun throws herself into her work. 
- She continued with work that was very similar to what she was doing prior. 	
	- She investigated rumors of shadow-touched beats
	- She tracked down small cults
	- Recovered or destroyed artifacts tied to ancient dragon worship
	- Intercepting scholars or mages who had discovered or began experimenting with umbral energies
- The work did not change much, but the frequency with which the incidents would pop up did increase. It became harder and harder to thoroughly investigate, because her time was often cut short to investigate and pull another thread.
- Shun was repeatedly frustrated with the fact that she could never track down [[The Umbral Dragon]]. There were many things connected to it, but she was never able to ascertain any information about it never mind a location. It was incredibly frustrating for her to not only continually fail at her true mission but also need to continually write back to [[Thalyra]] with more and more disappointing news.
