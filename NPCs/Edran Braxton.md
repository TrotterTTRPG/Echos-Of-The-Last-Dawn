**Title:** Head of Accounting at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Age:** 54  

Father of [[Chad Braxton]]

**Appearance:**
- Tall - 6'
- Dark Brown hair always kept neatly combed
- Always dressed in a dark, well-maintained suit
- Always appears serious

**Personality:**
- Methodical and unhurried
- Quietly observant
- Polite to everyone, warm to almost no one
- Deeply uncomfortable with waste, inefficiency, and vagueness
- Has a long memory for numbers, names, and the people behind both

Edran Braxton has never been wealthy. He lives in a modest home on the edge of the Gold District — close enough to walk to work, far enough that no one would mistake him for a man of means. He does not mind this. Wealth, in his view, is a variable. Information is the constant.

Every funding request that enters the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] passes across his desk. He reads them all. He approves very few without amendment, denies more than the applicants expect, and remembers every single one. Over thirty years in the position, this has made him arguably the most comprehensively informed person in [[Thalyra]] — not because anyone tells him things, but because money tells him everything. He knows which noble houses are quietly hemorrhaging funds, which city projects are padding their budgets, and which businesses are growing faster than their public profile suggests. He has never sold this information. He has never needed to.

**Moral Philosophy:**
- Believes the bank's stability is a civic good, regardless of who benefits from it. A collapsed financial system, in his view, hurts the poor far more than the rich
- Deeply suspicious of anyone who wants money quickly and explains it poorly
