[[Naight's first days as an Adventurer]]

Naieht was too young to remember the faces of his parents, only the fire. He recalls the smell of smoke, the clash of steel, and a massive shape falling in front of him before everything went dark. When he awoke, the world he knew was ash. The clan was gone, his mother and father among the dead, cut down by hands he never saw. He would surely have perished where he lay if not for a passing human thief named [[Garrick]], who had come to the ruins hoping to scavenge and instead found a furious, half-starved orc child trying to bite anyone who came close. For reasons Naieht never fully understood, [[Garrick]] took him in. He fed him, hid him, and raised him in the gutters and back alleys, teaching him how to cut purses, slip through shadows, and listen at doors without being heard.

The lessons never quite took. Naieht grew into a broad-shouldered, towering hulk, his size and temper making subtlety a constant struggle. He tried to step lightly as [[Garrick]] showed him, but floorboards creaked under his weight and windows shattered in his grip. When frightened, he did not skulk, he roared. Still, Garrick taught him patience and gave shape to the rage that simmered in his blood.

On the night [[Garrick]] lay dying from a wound earned on a job gone wrong, he pressed a stolen pendant, taken long ago from the ashes of Naieht’s village, into his adopted son’s hand. It bore a sigil Naieht did not recognize. In that moment, the orc swore an oath: he would uncover who destroyed his clan, track them across kingdoms if he must, and answer blood with blood. So he set out towards [[Thalyra]], hoping to find clues about his past. Subtlety may not be his gift, and stealth may fail him, but his fury will not.
