**Character Overview**
- *Full Name*: Aulberos Lightmind
- *Race/Class*: Firbolg Druid (Circle of the Land – Desert)
- *Hometown*: Unknown (likely western [[Advelde]], far from the desert)
- *Age*: Over 600 years

**Current Goals**:
- Understand what is disturbing the natural balance of the desert
- Restore harmony to the land before his life ends
- Find peace with his past — both the war and the Enclave he left behind

**Early Life and the Great War**
- Aulberos grew up in the shadow of the Great War, raised on stories of valor and righteousness.
- In his youth, he defied his family and left home to join what he believed was a holy crusade to end evil forever.
- Fought alongside soldiers, wizards, and heroes of every race.
- Experienced the horrors of true warfare — devastation, betrayal, and senseless death.
- When the war ended, Aulberos believed peace would follow, but within decades the same corruption, greed, and zealotry reemerged under new names.
- The realization broke his faith in mortals and their endless cycles of destruction.

**[[The Stonebloom Enclave]]**
- Seeking purpose, Aulberos joined [[The Stonebloom Enclave]], a druidic order dedicated to healing the scars of the war and preserving the wilds.
- Found solace in their teachings for many decades and rose to some prominence within the order.
- Over time, a faction within the Enclave began selling their powers and services to nobles and warlords, abandoning their neutrality and ideals.
- Aulberos openly opposed them, clashing with the new leadership -particularly the ruling council that included his old friend [[Aelan]].
- Disillusioned, he left the Enclave rather than watch it decay from within.

**Exile in the Desert**
- Fled east into the endless dunes outside [[Velrosk]], where he became a hermit.
- Settled near [[Kareth's Crossing]], a caravan outpost, but kept to the outskirts, interacting only when necessary.
- Over centuries, he nurtured life where there should be none — coaxing plants from dry stone and summoning rain where the air was dead.
- Most travelers who cross his territory feel the wind grow gentle and cool, the sands less harsh — a sign of his subtle guardianship.
- [[Sadiya]], a desert-born trader, once saved his life when he was stricken with sand fever. She remains one of his few trusted friends.

Motivations
- Now feels something stirring in the balance — creatures more violent, the winds restless, the land uneasy — and knows he cannot remain idle.
- His reawakening purpose is not born of hope but of duty. If this is to be his final season, he will make it count.

Notable Locations
- [[Kareth's Crossing]]: Aulberos occasionally visits this sprawling trade camp to barter herbs or rainwater. Avoids the crowds, trading only with Sadiya or other trusted nomads.
- [[The Cradle of Thorns]]: Once home of [[The Stonebloom Enclave]], a lush valley born from a battlefield scorched to glass.
	- He remembers it with deep longing and grief — the ideal it represented, and the betrayal that followed.
	- He has not returned in centuries, though part of him still calls it “home.”

**Rumors**
- *Lies*
	- Some say he is not a man at all, but a desert spirit made manifest. If you cross him, the sand will swallow you whole. Some nomads believe he is a ghost.
	- He betrayed his order and was cursed by [[The Stonebloom Enclave]] druids to wander the sands in exile until his death. This rumor was started by some in the Enclave, the same people that drove him to leave with their treachery and scheming.
	- He protects an ancient relic he has hidden in the desert. A stone heart that keeps him alive. Treasure hunters have gone looking for it but none have returned, though the desert is much more to blame for their disappearances then he is.
- *Truths*
	- He fought in the great war centuries ago, some say he was even there when it ended.
	- He lives out in the dunes where no life should be yet the wind smells of rain. Caravan guides claim there is a stretch of desert that's never as harsh as it should be.
	- He burned a city to the ground once, few know the truth though, that it was not out of hate but to keep a plague of rot from spreading, and troubled him deeply.

**Current Motivations**
- *Balance*: Investigate the disturbances plaguing the desert and the growing unease of its creatures.
- *Legacy*: Ensure his final years serve the natural order rather than fade into obscurity.
- *Reconciliation*: Confront [[The Stonebloom Enclave]] if fate forces their paths to cross again — whether to forgive or to condemn.

Six centeries is a long time to watch the world repeat itself, but this has been Aulberos's fate. When he was young, he left his home against the wishes of his family and friends to fight in what the elders called, the great war. A war he believed was destined to end evil in the world forever. He joined soldiers, wizards, and heroes of every race. His heart was filled with conviction that they were forging a new age. When victory came Aulberos believed for a moment that the world might finally know peace, but that peace was fleeting. The kingdoms he bled for, lost friends for, all fell to infighting. Nobles sucumbed to greed and couruption and faiths twisted into zealotry. Within a few decades the same cruelties returned, just with different names and faces. For a long while Aulberos wandered the world, watching generations bloom and wither like wild flowers. He saw cities rise from the dust and return to it. His faith in mortals waned. To him, they became like ants, endless in number, restless, and blind to the greater balance of the world. He seeked purpose, so he joined The Stonebloom Enclave, a druidic order dedicated to preserving the wilds. For a time this gave him solace until even there he found ambition, politics, and pride. Disheartened, he left his position in the order and withdrew into the deserts outside Velrosk where he lived as a hermit. He spent centuries, coaxing life from the sand, urging stubborn roots to take hold, and calling rain where none should ever fall. Now, in his final years, something stirs in him again. A memory of the fire that once burned in him. For the first time in an age, Aulberos feels the pull of the world beyond his dunes. Perhaps he cannot save it, but he cannot leave it as it is. If this is to be his final season, he intends to make it matter.
