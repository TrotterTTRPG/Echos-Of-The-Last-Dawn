**Basic Information**
- *Species:* Drake
- *Gender:* Male
- *Original name:* Peng (“friend, vigorous” in Chinese)
- *Renamed:* Kai (“triumph, victory, music”) after Chein’s late father [[Kai Yang]]
- *Bonded to:* [[Chein Po]] (formerly Chein Yang)
- *Relationship type:* Imprinted and magically bonded — inseparable emotional and spiritual link

**Origin and Early Life**
- Chein discovered his egg in the foothills of the [[Gray Mountains]] at age 13 after feeling an irresistible pull to explore.
- She secretly took the egg home and hid it from her father.
- When it hatched, Chein and the hatchling imprinted instantly, forming an unusually strong, empathic bond.
- His birth caused ecological disturbance around [[Helmut]] — animals became scarce and hunts failed — drawing attention from his mother.

**The Mother Drake and the Fire**
- The mother drake, searching for her lost offspring, eventually found Chein’s home.
- She attacked [[Helmut]], burning Chein’s house and killing her father, [[Kai Yang]].
- Despite the maternal call, the hatchling refused to leave Chein, hiding instead of rejoining his mother.
- After the attack, Chein fled into the mountains; the young drake followed her soon after, solidifying their bond.

**Life in the Wilderness**
- Kai helped Chein survive and recover emotionally after her father’s death.
- Over time, he became a teacher and protector, helping her:
	- Learn to hunt, track, and live off the land
	- Understand the balance of nature
	- Study dragon behavior and instincts
- The pair were hunted for months by the mother drake.
- Working together, they trapped and killed the mother drake using strategy and terrain — Kai triggered a landslide, and Chein delivered the killing blows.
- This victory forged their lifelong partnership and mutual trust.

**Growth and Abilities**
- Over decades, Kai grew large and powerful, capable of:
	- Surviving independently in the wild
	- Tracking and communicating with Chein telepathically or empathically
	- Understanding (and later teaching) Draconic language
- While he cannot speak Common, he and Chein share an intuitive, near-verbal communication through looks, body language, and emotion.
- Chein likely learned Draconic from him during their long years of isolation.

**Current Relationship**
- Their bond is permanent and mutual, rooted in affection, survival, and shared loss.
- Kai dislikes civilization and prefers wilderness; he waits nearby when Chein visits towns or cities.
- Comes when summoned, aiding her in battle or danger.
- Acts as both companion and conscience, keeping Chein grounded and reminding her of her father’s teachings.
- Despite his draconic pride and independence, Kai is deeply loyal to Chein and would defend her to the death.

**Symbolism and Role**
- *Represents:* Chein’s link to her past, her guilt, and her redemption.
- His name change from Peng → Kai mirrors Chein’s transformation from a naïve child to a survivor honoring her father.
- Serves as a living reminder of nature’s duality — danger and beauty, destruction and balance.
- Provides Chein with emotional balance and an anchor during her struggle to reconnect with society.

**Rumors and Legends**
- Locals associate Kai’s sightings with misfortune or theft, due to Chein’s criminal years:
	- “The green drake of the mountains heralds the Robbing Shadow.”
	- “Where the green drake flies, coinpurses empty overnight.”
- In truth, Kai avoids harming innocents and stays hidden from most mortals unless provoked.
