**Character Overview**
- *Race/Class*: Eladrin Bladesinger
- *Hometown*: [[Solstice]] — a serene, forest town at the base of twin mountains southwest of [[Dornthalm]].
- *Affiliation*: [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]] — a bladesinger order dedicated to preserving and protecting knowledge.
- *Mentor*: [[Caelen Quickfrost]], Head of the [[Monastery of the Sacred Scroll]]
- *Recent Assignment:* Formerly stationed at the [[Cloister of the Sharpened Quill]] in [[Dornthalm]] under [[Elyndra Sunweaver]].
- *Current Path*: Answered a summons to [[Thalyra]] to attend [[The Adventurer's Docket]].

**Appearance & Traits**
- *Seasonal Aspect*: Born a Winter Eladrin, Aelar’s appearance shifts with his moods and spiritual attunement to the seasons, though few have seen his Autumn form in years.
- *Demeanor*: Calm, introspective, and analytical; carries himself with quiet confidence and deliberate grace.
- *Telltale Habits*: Always carries a book or journal. Frequently pauses mid-conversation to jot notes or observations.
- *Combat Style*: Elegant and precise — pairs arcane skill with swordplay in the style of the bladesingers.

**Personality & Motivations**
- *Curious Scholar*: Aelar’s defining trait is his endless curiosity. He seeks understanding in all things — history, language, culture, and the hidden mysteries of magic.
- *Devoted Preserver*: He believes knowledge is sacred and must be protected from misuse or loss.
- *Adventurous Spirit*: Though scholarly by nature, he yearns to see the world firsthand — to live the stories he once only read.
- *Self-Reflective*: He often second-guesses himself, wondering if his pursuit of knowledge comes at the cost of personal connection or tradition.
- *Moral Code*: Guided by the Oath of the Protectors — to never use knowledge for harm, to preserve wisdom, and to share learning with others.

**Childhood in Solstice:**
- Born under a full moon in deep winter; grew up surrounded by stories, legends, and lore.
- Obsessed with books and knowledge from a young age — constantly asking “why.”
- Traveled often between [[Solstice]] and its sister town [[Equinox]], visiting the Glades to honor the seasonal traditions of the Eladrin.

**Discovery of the Monastery:**
- As a youth, Aelar was fascinated by the strange sounds from the Monastery of the Sacred Scroll — blades clashing and arcane chanting.
- Curiosity led him to meet members of the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes, who recognized his potential.
- Mentored by [[Caelen Quickfrost]] and formally inducted into the order.

**Life as a Protector:**
- Studied tirelessly, mastering both spell and sword to defend repositories of knowledge.
- Traveled widely on assignments to document distant libraries, ruins, and cultures.
- Served under [[Elyndra Sunweaver]] in [[Dornthalm]] at the [[Cloister of the Sharpened Quill]], impressing her with his diligence — despite an early tavern incident that nearly got him expelled.

**Call to Adventure:**
- Upon returning to [[Solstice]], Aelar received a summons to [[Thalyra]] to participate in [[The Adventurer's Docket]].
- Remembering his childhood wanderlust and his oath, he saw this as a chance to explore again — to preserve and spread knowledge beyond monastery walls.
- The night before he left, a spark from his torch nearly burned the oath scroll — an omen he took as both warning and reminder of his purpose.

Three Truths
- Nobody has seen Aelar transform into his Autumn form in quite some time. It is said that it is because Aelar missed his pilgrimage to [[Equinox]] nor has he been seen there for quite some time.
- When Aelar first came to the city of [[Dornthalm]], he got into quite the tavern brawl when a debate went too far while enjoying a night out. The sign of the tavern is still scorched with fire to this day from the brawl.
- Aelar is more studious than most members of the [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]]. He always carries a book around with him, and is always writing down information all the time about everything he sees.

Three Lies
- The [[Protectors of the Ancient Tomes]] are nothing more than pompous know-it-alls that are given a blade, and use their prestige and position in all manners of unscrupulous and corrupted ways.
- After Aelar left to go the city of [[Thalyra]] to answer summons for a skilled adventurer to join the guild, some books from the library at the [[Monastery of the Sacred Scroll]] went missing. It is said Aelar took them without permission
    - Note: The book is missing, but Aelar did not take it.
- Aelar never excelled at the blade, and his only strength was in the intellectual and magical. It is said Aelar has a habit of losing his swords, dropping them constantly, accidentally hurting many people wherever he goes with his clumsiness.

Oath of the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes
	We promise in summer and with the power of fire,
	to never use it against the knowledge we acquire.
	We promise in winter where we pen knowledge to scroll,
	to use our powers to keep the library whole.
	We promise in spring and the returning of light,
	to protect [[Dornthalm]] from its plight.
	We promise in autumn where the trees look to burn,
	to spread our knowledge and help people learn.

**Actual Text**

Aelar Aurelius was born in Solstice, a remote town to the southwest of Dornthalm at the base of two mountains, amongst the forest. Solstice was a place of calm serenity for the most part, with a mix of stone and wooden structures nestled amongst the forest, with their sweeping roofs and elegant structures poking through the forest canopy. Like a majority of people in this town, Aelar Aurelius was an eladrin elf, meaning he was an elf of the seasons, changing his appearance at will overnight. This mysterious power was a power all eladrin were born with and to further understand their powers and as part of tradition, would need to visit either the Verdant Glade in the sister town of Equinox south of Dornthalm around the Rockmyst Valley during or as close as possible to the Spring or Fall Equinox, or the Glade of the Sun and Moon in Solstice during or as close as possible to the Summer or Winter Solstice. This pilgrimage by tradition should happen around every 10 years in a rotating cycle of seasons, where they would perform rituals and celebrations in and around the respective glade, and would renew and reavow their birthright and powers as eladrin. Aelar was born under the full moon during the deepest depths of winter, appearing first as a winter eladrin.

Growing up in Solstice, Aelar would spend large portions of time listening to stories and legends being told by his family, neighbors, and other members inside the town of Solstice. He would constantly be asking for new stories and adventures about far off lands and people to be told to him by all those he met. If he was ever given a book, Aelar would devour it as fast as he can, reverently reading every page, and drinking in every word the book had to say. This nature of eagerly drinking in knowledge naturally meant Aelar was curious, always asking questions: who, when, where, how and more often than the others, why. Because of these traits and also because Aelar was ever keen to just learn and document in general, Aelar excelled in anything anyone taught him...at least from a knowledge perspective. What he lacked in physical strength he more than made up for in dexterity and intelligence.

Of course, while Aelar was growing up he would travel with his parents multiple times back and forth between Solstice and Equinox, visiting both glades, partaking in the celebrations and rituals, letting Aelar see some of the world. Aelar loved travelling almost as much as he loved reading books, and hearing adventures and tales about people places and things across the realm. While travelling, Aelar would see it as himself living his own magical adventure, and during this time living out his own adventure, he would experience all sorts of new sights, sounds and smells, which he eagerly documented in a journal he always kept on him at all times. He built up his own little collection of things he wrote down which he viewed as interesting, including people, places, things, and even ideas. This information he built up of his own accord was a large part of the reason why eventually he was inducted and invited to the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes, whose main headquarters was located in a large stone monastery at the edge of his hometown in Solstice.

Aelar came to join the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes due to the natural gravitation he had towards the place and his extremely curious nature. During his younger years he would wander towards the monastery, and hear the clangs of metal on metal, the sounds of loud yelling, and also the sound of arcane effects, and would quickly retreat when he heard any of these sounds. When he started entering his teenage years, Aelar grew more bold, staying by the monastery, listening to these strange disturbing sounds not often heard in Solstice, and would contemplate what those sounds actually were. Eventually one day, the monastery doors flew wide open while Aelar was waiting outside, listening to the clang of blade on blade, revealing a pair of robed figures with blades strapped to their belts, and wearing large travel bags. As they stepped out of the monastery, mid conversation, their eyes would meet Aelar’s. Aelar, unsure of himself and nervous after seeing these figures he never met before in his life, paused for a short uncomfortable period of time, before his curiosity finally got the better of him, and he immediately started peppering them with all sorts of questions about what was going on in the monastery, where they were going, and why they had swords at their belts. The pair of figures, recognizing a curious soul, guided Aelar inside to the Monastery of the Sacred Scroll, introduced him to the head of their order here, an Eladrin named Caelen Quickfrost. Quickly recognizing Aelar’s thirst and respect for knowledge, Aelar was quickly introduced and inducted as an initiate to the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes, giving Aelar access to an even greater repository of knowledge and information, which Aelar would often be found pouring over books inside the library inside the Monastary of the Sacred Scroll. Aelar finally felt he found a home here, and amongst his brothers and sisters, they had a shared interest and goal: to preserve, protect and document knowledge. Coming with the territory of preserving and protecting knowledge came with it the teachings of how to wield both blade and magic, further helping in the pursuits of protecting knowledge.

Being a member of the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes also came with several assignments, which included traversing the realm to explore distant cities, explore their libraries, and also to document the land itself, and all sights sounds and smells that was encountered while traversing the land, very similar to what Aelar did in his younger years. Another such assignment was to travel to the city of Dornthalm and a Cloister there called the Cloister of the Sharpened Quill, a location owned by the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes dedicated not only to exploring and cataloguing the ancient library in Dornthalm, but also helping protect and serve the city itself to keep this massive repository of knowledge safe. This library was where a good portion of the books the Monastary of the Sacred Scroll came from. Aelar was assigned for some time to visit Dornthalm, and stay at the Cloister of the Sharpened Quill, where he spent most of his time cataloguing and reading books within the ancient library of Dornthalm. During this time here Aelar would work underneath Elyndra Sunweaver, the Head Archivist of Dornthalm, and would leave a rather good impression of himself to Elyndra as someone who is quick to memorize, absorb, and transcribe knowledge. That is, after a first uncomfortable encounter Elyndra had to bail Aelar out of (See Truth #2). Eventually, however, Aelar would be sent back to the monastery due to the call of the traditions of the eladrin, and would venture back to Solstice.

Aelar would resume his quiet life back at the Monastery of the Sacred Scroll, pouring through books brought in from other members of the order all across the world and most importantly from the library in Dornthalm, alongside keeping in practice with his blades and magic. Eventually, though, one day, a traveler arrived at Solstice, bearing a summons for an eladrin to travel to Thayla to form a new Adventuring Guild. Aelar, hearing of this began to comb through the library for information about Thayla and its surrounding areas, thinking of the time when he was younger, and his first amateur collection of journals documenting his travels between Solstice and Equinox. Thinking back to this time brought fond memories, and perhaps, he thought, it is time to venture out in the world once more, explore new lands, meet new people, and most importantly, catalogue and discover the mysteries of the world outside of Dornthalm, Solstice and Equinox. Standing alone in the library late at night alone with torch in hand, thinking these thoughts, Aelar looked at the last line of the oath he swore when he first got inducted to the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes, and thought to himself that perhaps he can also look to spread both his learnings and the knowledge the Protectors of the Ancient Tomes taught him to others as well, and help contribute to this new Adventuring Guild. As he came to this conclusion, he was interrupted by a spark from his torch jumping off the torch and glancing off onto the oath scroll he was staring at. Cursing himself for being so careless, and realizing just how tired he was, Aelar brushed the spark off the scroll, extinguished his torch, and went to bed. The next morning, he volunteered to accept the summons to this newly formed adventuring guild, and began his journey to Thayla.
