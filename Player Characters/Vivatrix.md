**Character Overview**
- *Full name*: Vivatrix (surname unknown)
- *Race/Class*: Half-Elf Warlock (The Undying)
- *Hometown*: Unknown — grew up on the outskirts of a forested village
- *Familiar*: [[Edgar]], a loyal and unusually intelligent raven
- *Age*: Mid-20s (appears young, but her lifespan may now be extended)

**Current goals:**
- Discover the fate of her childhood friend [[Astra]]
- Master her growing power and understand her connection to death
- Build her own fortune and freedom beyond the shadow of [[Brumble]] and [[Aloria World]]

Early Life
- Vivatrix grew up near a quiet riverside forest where she spent her days playing with her best friend, [[Astra]].
- During one of their adventures, [[Astra]] slipped into the river and disappeared beneath the current.
- Vivatrix dove in after her but couldn’t save her — [[Astra]]’s body was never recovered.
- Haunted by guilt and dreams of [[Astra]] whispering “find me,” Vivatrix became obsessed with the idea her friend might still be alive.
- A raven she had befriended, [[Edgar]], stayed by her side from that day onward, sensing her grief and protecting her ever since.

**The Performer of [[Aloria World]]**
- As she grew older, Vivatrix traveled to [[Aloria World]], a fantastical amusement park founded by the enigmatic [[Aloria]].
- There she met [[Brumble]], a shrewd goblin entrepreneur who offered her work as a fortune teller.
- Using a deck of tarot cards she didn’t actually know how to read, she became the park’s most popular attraction — charming crowds with dramatic “prophecies.”
- When [[Aloria]] mysteriously vanished and left the park to [[Brumble]], rumors spread that Vivatrix had foreseen her disappearance.
- Disillusioned by [[Brumble]]’s greed and tired of performing lies, Vivatrix eventually left [[Aloria World]] to seek real power — and real answers.

**Retreat into Darkness**
- Vivatrix retreated deep into the forest, seeking solitude and clarity.
- One night, a dream led her to a cave illuminated by a pulsing red light. Inside, she met [[Legion]], a shadowed being who taught her to embrace her darker instincts instead of burying them.
- [[Legion]]’s teachings awakened something powerful — and dangerous — within her.
- Before she left, he gifted her his grim reaper’s cloak, a relic steeped in shadow magic.

**The Pact of the Undying**
- Soon after leaving the forest, Vivatrix was visited in a vision by [[The Undying]], a deathless entity who offered her eternal life in exchange for souls.
- He gifted her a small crystal vial — a soul jar — to collect the essence of the fallen.
- Each soul she gathers strengthens her bond with her patron and extends her own lifespan.
- She hides this object in a small satchel she never lets out of sight.
- The soul jar’s faint glow after battles has sparked countless rumors and superstitions.

**Life of Rumor and Restlessness**
- Vivatrix’s eccentric performances and shadowy aura have made her both famous and feared.
- She is rumored to see the future, though her visions are dreams — not prophecies.
- Locals claim [[Aloria]] fled after Vivatrix warned her of doom.
- Others whisper that her satchel holds cursed relics, forbidden tomes, or even the trapped spirits of the dead.
- Despite her notoriety, Vivatrix is ultimately driven by loneliness and hope — clinging to the belief that [[Astra]] still calls to her.

**Joining [[The Adventurer's Docket]]**
- Hearing rumors of [[The Adventurer's Docket]] in [[Thalyra]], Vivatrix saw an opportunity:
- To find new purpose in the wake of her patron’s demands
- To collect powerful souls and strengthen her connection to [[The Undying]]
- To seek answers about [[Astra]]’s fate, which her dreams suggest is somehow linked to [[Thalyra]]’s lands

**Current Motivations**
- Closure — Discover what truly happened to [[Astra]] and end her haunting dreams.
- Eternal Life — Continue gathering souls to fulfill her pact with [[The Undying]].

Other Details
- Combat style: Uses necrotic and illusion magic, often enhanced by the presence of [[Edgar]].
- Familiar: [[Edgar]] acts as both a companion and emotional anchor; in combat, he vanishes when struck down, returning later unscathed.
