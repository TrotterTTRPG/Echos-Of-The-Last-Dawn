**Character Overview**
- *Full name:* Chein Po (formerly Chein Yang)
- *Nicknames:* The Robbing Shadow
- *Race/Class:* Wood Elf Ranger (Drakewarden)
- *Hometown:* [[Helmut]], a remote village near the [[Gray Mountains]]
- *Drake companion*: [[Kai]] (originally named Peng)
- *Age:* Approx. 120 years (matured but still young for an elf)
- *Current goals:*
	- Live honestly and leave behind her criminal past
	- Learn the truth about her estranged mother, [[Shun Yang]]

**Early Life**
- Born in Helmut to [[Kai Yang]] (father) and [[Shun Yang]] (mother).
- At age 3, her mother left suddenly, leaving Chein with her father.
- Her father taught her kindness and honesty, but her mother’s abandonment created:
    - A buried resentment toward her mother
    - A mild distrust of women
    - A resentment of “whole” families

**The Egg and the Fire**
- At age 13, Chein felt an irresistible urge to explore the wilderness east of [[Helmut]].
- Disobeying her father, she discovered and secretly took home a large strange egg.
- The egg hatched into a drake, which she named Peng ([[Kai]]).
- Unknown to her, the mother drake was nearby and began terrorizing [[Helmut]] searching for its lost offspring.
- The mother drake eventually attacked Chein’s home, setting it ablaze.
- Chein’s father was killed while telling her to run.
- The town assumed Chein had also died and that the drake fled afterward.

**Survival and Transformation**
- Chein fled into the mountains, accompanied by her drake (now renamed [[Kai]] after her father).
- She fell into despair but was comforted and aided by [[Kai]].
- Over months of being hunted by the mother drake, Chein:
	- Learned survival skills and hunting
	- Mastered use of the bow
	- Studied draconic behavior and weaknesses
- Eventually, she and Kai trapped and killed the mother drake using a landslide in [[Rockmyst Valley]].

**Life in Exile**
- With the danger gone, Chein began reintegrating into society, though fearful of being tied to Helmut’s tragedy.
- Changed her name to Chein Po to hide her identity.
- Lived on the fringes of society as a thief, smuggler, and spy — earning the moniker “The Robbing Shadow.”
- [[Kai]] avoided cities but stayed bonded to her, answering her summons when needed.
- She became skilled in:
	- Tracking and archery
	- Covert movement and infiltration
	- Understanding and communicating with her drake

**[[Faris Harith]] and [[The Adventurer's Docket]]**
- Met [[Faris Harith]], a courier and information broker in [[Velrosk]], who became a mentor and paternal figure.
- He respected her privacy and encouraged her to live honestly.
- Told her of [[The Adventurer's Docket]] in [[Thalyra]], a chance to start anew.
- Also mentioned sightings of her mother near [[Thalyra]].
- Chein decided to travel east to pursue both redemption and truth.

**Current Motivations**
1. *Redemption* - Honor her father's memory by living honestly and leaving behind crime
2. *Closure* - Find her mother, [[Shun Yang]] and learn the reason for her abandonment
3. *Companionship* - Maintain her lifelong bond with [[Kai]] while cautiously reconnecting with society.

**Other Details:**
- *Combat style*: Prefers bow and nature-based magic.
- *Language*: Likely learned Draconic from [[Kai]] over decades.
- *Rumors about Chein / “The Robbing Shadow”:*
	- Misfortune follows any female white-haired wood elf
	- Sightings of the green drake of the mountains results in any carried money / goods as lost if one were to sleep in the wilderness 
	- White-haired, green-eyed wood elves are trouble
	- The Robbing Shadow will not hesitate to take a life if it stands between what it desires
	- Green drake are a sign that The Robbing Shadow is in the area
	- Suspicion rises in the criminal / spy network piques when a white-haired elf woman is seen, knowing there is a connection to Velrosk is present

**Actual Text**:

Chein's family lived in a town far to the northeast of Dornthalm, just at the foothills of the gray mountains. The village her family lived in had a close-knit marketplace, while residences tended to be few and far between in the surrounding territory. The town had been named Helmut by collective agreement of the representatives of the residents, as many of the families that settled there were hardy, resourceful, and thrived in times of hardship. Even the houses closest to the heart of the market of Helmut were a half-day's journey from the market. Chein's home was the northernmost residence outside of the main marketplace, which ended up being a full-day’s journey on foot.

Shortly after Chein had turned three, a fight between her parents ended with her mother, Shun Yang, packing her bags and leaving in the middle of the night. She gave no indication of where she intended to go or as to why she decided to leave her husband and only child behind.

Chein had been so young at the time, that her father, Kai Yang, told Chein simply that Shun Yang had somewhere more important that she needed to be, and that her absence was wholly justified. Kai did his best to keep Chein from being resentful of her absent mother, yet the seed had been planted, and, despite his best efforts, a hatred for her own mother seethed under the surface. This, unintentionally, resulted in a mild distrust of women in general and a resentment toward complete families. While he never spoke a foul word of her mother, he never gave much in the way of detail, leaving Chein to fill in gaps with her own imagination. That being Chein had been the true cause of the separation and Shun simply was not ready to be a mother.

One night, not long after Chein had turned thirteen, she had an unknown and irresistible urge to explore the unsettled lands to the east of their village. When she had expressed this desire to her father over breakfast the next morning, he vehemently forbade her from exploring alone, still being so young and unable to protect herself from danger. Her childish and stubborn nature drove her to defy her father's wishes, and she snuck out the very same night. Packing only necessities that would go unnoticed in her absence, she set out after her father had gone to bed, promising herself that she would return before morning.

As she explored closer to the foothills of the gray mountains, she stumbled upon a large, strange egg. When she brought it home, Chein was too afraid to tell her father, too ashamed that she had defied his wishes. During the time in her care, animals that lived in the area she had discovered it in had become skittish and acted strangely. Hunts of other townspeople that ventured to the foothills ended in far less success than previously. Because Chein kept the egg a secret from everyone, no one quite knew why food and resources had started to dwindle.

Not a month had passed before the egg hatched. She had been with the egg when it finally hatched, and, to her surprise, a baby drake arose. They imprinted on each other instantly and quickly built upon their extremely close bond. Chein did everything she could to hide the creature from her father, too scared that he would be angry and make her return it to the wild. Following the hatching, the huntsmen of Helmut almost always returned empty-handed.

With the drake now outside of its protective shell, the mother drake that had been searching for her kin and scaring away the game that the townspeople relied on, it found Chein's home. From the scent and maternal instinct that her child was nearby, the mother drake scoured the town of Helmut for days. It terrorized the citizens with the threat of its presence, launching no outright attacks, yet refused to leave. Once it traced the scent of its kin to Chein’s home, she finally launched a full out attack to retrieve it.

Yet, because the pair had imprinted on one another, the infant drake didn't want to leave, and it hid from its mother. When the mother drake arrived, but didn’t attack, Chein did her best to keep it on the outskirts of her home, yet it still returned to her bedroom window every night.

When the mother drake attacked Chein’s home, she led her drake to the foothills of the gray mountains and tried to have it remain there. However, its scent had been established to one specific location and the mother drake refused to relent and set her home ablaze in the middle of the night. When she and her father were trying to make their escape, he became trapped and told her to save herself. She tried to free him from a fallen wall that crushed his legs beneath it, but still so young, Chein was unable to provide any aid to the one parent that had not forsaken her. He repeated desperately: “GO!” and she, reluctantly, did. This was the last thing Kai Yang had ever said to Chein Yang. She fled from her smoldering home in tears and wracked with guilt.

The townspeople of Helmut banded together to fight the mother drake off. Pooling all their resources and weaponry – including farming tools and anything else that could be used to defend their homes but, to their surprise, once they arrived at the destroyed Yang home, the mother drake was already gone. They then searched the wreckage, only finding her father’s remains, and assumed that Chein had been eaten or taken by the mother drake and presumed her dead.

After fleeing, her drake companion followed not far behind initially, but she noticed that, as the mother drake continued her pursuit of them, it began keeping a greater distance from Chein. Only a few days after the fire, Chein’s drake tracked her down in the western foothills of the snowy mountains. When he found her, she was a broken girl that had lost any will to live and was lost in despair, believing that even her companion had left her.

Her father had never learned why their home had been suddenly attacked by the beast, so even to this day Chein carries a sense of responsibility that she has buried so deeply that she is extremely adverse in discussing her family life. Instead, she tends to avert conversation by any means necessary or will simply refuse to answer.

Although Chein had originally named her drake Peng because of its playful and mischievous personality, after reuniting following the fire, she then renamed him after her father: Kai. In the next few weeks, he helped her to interpret his instincts and understand the careful balance of nature. They remained in the mountains for a short while, always on the move to flee from the careful tracking of the mother drake. He was special to her because they seemed to have a kinship and understanding of the unspoken. Even though they had no direct way to communicate, there always existed an understanding between them.

During their time together isolated in the mountainous wilderness, the mother drake found them time and time again for months. As time passed, Chein learned more about the strengths and weaknesses of dragons and, with the aid of Kai, they were eventually able to turn the tables and hunt down the mother drake. They had purposefully led her to the valley within the central brown mountains (not sure what they are going to be named) and waited until she settled for a rest. Kai started a landslide close enough that the mother drake wouldn’t be able to wake and escape in time. Once encased in the fallen stone, Chein dealt the fatal blows using her bow – her preferred method of combat. Spells that she will utilize will revolve around harnessing the strengths within nature.

Now with the confidence they are no longer being hunted, Chein was much more willing to enter areas of civilized societies, having been terrified that her presence within society would result in them being burned down by the mother drake that hunted her. Despite enjoying the company of Kai, there had always been a sort of emptiness in their years alone living and growing within Chein.

Because of her time in isolation with Kai, she became proficient in tracking and hunting with a hand-crafted bow and perfected the art of whittling arrows. Kai grew larger and stronger, and their bond permanent. However, whenever Chein visits cities or other populace, he would linger in the nearby wilderness and await her call. Their current relationship allows for Kai to thrive in the wild and only join Chein when summoned. Although always bonded, they recognize that he dislikes civilizations and will avoid them while Chein has been gradually rejoining them.

However, terrified of being tied to the tragedy of Helmut, and the knowledge that the only casualty had been her father, Chein opted to change her name to avoid being tied to his death. She introduced herself as Chien Po, as the meaning behind the new surname held no real association, meaning, and was common enough to not rouse suspicion. Although her given name was unique, she couldn't find the heart to change it.

With few traits suited to civilized life, she tended to resort to petty crime in order to make her way when outside of the wilderness. She entered the world as a criminal / spy because it was a lifestyle that was more well-suited to her anti-social and secretive personality. Although her father would disapprove of her lifestyle, she gradually began to make friends with the sketchy underground who seldom asked questions about her past. Because of the stubborn nature that she failed to shed from her youth, Chein will opt for silence to a question that she doesn’t want to answer.

One contact in particular, Faris Harith who is a currier of small goods, connections, and information, is fully accepting of her silence when certain questions are asked. He provides her with requested things to help her throughout her travels, he can only be found in Velrosk, as that is where they originally met. In their last meeting, and as he was the only person Chein opened up to about her past, he sympathized with her conflict of acting outside of the way her father had raised her, and he encouraged her to try and live honestly while reassuring he would always be around if needed for consultation or advice. He recognized the vacuum in her life and, reluctantly, stepped into a pseudo-paternal role for Chein.

He told her of an Adventurer’s docket in Thalyra that could be her first honest, non-criminal opportunity to step away from the world of the criminal / spy lifestyle. He also mentioned that she may find more details about a woman named Shun had been sighted in Thalyra. Unable to resist the desire to find and question her mother for her absence, Chein decided to journey eastward.

Currently, Chein holds two main goals / driving force to live more honestly within society and, more discretely, learn more about her mother. She made a promise to herself and her late father to be more amiable and strive to live a better, more honest life.
