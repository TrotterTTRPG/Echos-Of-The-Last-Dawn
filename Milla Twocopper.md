**Title:** Desk Officer at the [[Thalyric Crown Bank]] in the Gold District in [[Thalyra]]  
**Race:** Halfling
**Gender**: Female
**Age:** 42  

**Appearance:**
- Small even by halfling standards, which means she is barely visible above the desk without the raised platform installed for her workstation
- Warm brown skin and curly dark hair worn pinned back with simple brass clips
- Round, pleasant face that defaults to a professionally neutral expression that could be read as friendly
- Moves quickly and quietly — clients are sometimes startled to find her already standing beside them

**Personality:**
- Disarmingly approachable in a way that is entirely intentional
- Excellent memory for faces and names; greets returning clients by name without checking her records
- Cheerful in a way that is genuine but disciplined — she controls when it appears
- Notices everything and files it away without appearing to pay attention

Milla Twocopper is the desk officer most clients specifically request, which she finds professionally flattering and personally useful. People relax around her. Relaxed people talk more than they intend to. She has reported more inconsistencies and potential fraud attempts to [[Edran Braxton]] than any other officer on the floor — always in writing, always without drama, always accurate.
